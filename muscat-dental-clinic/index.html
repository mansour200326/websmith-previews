After your visit

تعليمات بعد العلاج

What to do at home after treatment. If something doesn't feel right, call us.

After an extraction

بعد خلع السن
  1. Your lips, teeth and tongue stay numb for a while, so don't chew for 1 to 2 hours.
  2. Bite firmly on the gauze for about 45 minutes.
  3. Don't rinse hard for at least 24 hours, and don't keep spitting. Swallow the saliva.
  4. Take your medicine as directed.
  5. Eat something cold an hour after the procedure, then soft food, and drink plenty.
  6. When you brush the next day, keep away from the extraction site.

After a filling

بعد الحشوات
  1. Some sensitivity to hot and cold drinks is normal and fades gradually.
  2. If you can't bite properly, come back and we will adjust it.
  3. Avoid sticky food, especially gum, and hard food. They can loosen the filling.

Crowns and bridges

التلبيسات والجسور
  1. The gums may be sore or sensitive for a while. This settles gradually.
  2. Brush normally and floss carefully.
  3. Don't bite on hard objects or chew sticky food. If a crown or bridge comes off, it can usually be cemented back in place.
  4. Keep your mouth clean and see your dentist every six months for a cleaning.

After gum surgery

بعد جراحة اللثة
  1. Don't eat until the numbness has worn off.
  2. Don't rinse hard or drink through a straw.
  3. Eat soft, nourishing food. Avoid hot and spicy food, tobacco and alcohol.
  4. A little bleeding is normal. If it continues, hold an ice pack on the outside of your face and keep your head raised on pillows when lying down.
  5. Come back after one week to have the stitches removed.
