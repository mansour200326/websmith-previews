01

Concrete pavers

The most popular paver going, because of the colours and styles you can get. There are two kinds: dry pressed suits smaller jobs; wet cast suits bigger, busier areas and lasts a lot longer under traffic.

From Austral, Boral, Villastone, ECS Masonry, Baines Masonry and PGH, at wholesale prices, quoted to the size of your project.

Flagstone
Natural stone look, extra thick. Mostly for driveways: they take heavy equipment and keep their looks.
Large pavers
For commercial and big outdoor areas. Up to 400 × 400mm and up to 60mm thick.
Masonry
Clean, modern pavers and tiles, from brick size to large squares.
Cobblestone
For walkways and gardens. Simple to lay, picked up from the yard.
Bullnose
Curved, smooth edge for pools and patios, cut to the shape of the pool.

02

Clay pavers

Made from clays and shales fired hot in a kiln; the clay decides the colour, which is why they look warm and earthy. Very versatile, easy for the do-it-yourselfer to lay, and they last forever.

Once you've picked your paver you don't want to wait for it, so we keep clay pavers in stock at the showroom for quick pickup or delivery.

Brands
Austral, Claypave, PGH and more
Sizes
Standard brick size through to 400 × 400mm

Travertine
Cool underfoot, chlorine and salt safe: a natural for pools. Bullnose to match the edge.
Granite

Sandstone
Pavers 30mm, tiles 20mm, matching bullnose. 400 × 400, 600 × 400, 500 × 500mm. Seal it near chlorinated pools; no acid cleaners.
Limestone
Range of colour, density and finish, from rustic split face to Kota Blue. Takes foot and vehicle traffic.

05

Wall cladding & capping

Cladding and capping give a boundary wall some class, and they're cut to your job so the height stays even all the way round. Styles run from classic country stone to more modern hand-cut looks.

Countrystone Fascia 3D
Course heights 185, 250 and 300mm, many lengths, about 25mm thick. Soft yellow or white as standard; any colour can be made. Lay lengths at random or all one size.
Quoins
L-shaped corner pieces in all three heights. The 250 lines up with 3 courses of brickwork, like solid stone blocks.
Capping
For walls and piers: smooth, rock face or pyramid. Standard and custom sizes, 3-sided end cappers, 1-sided for window ledges.

07

Accessories

The full range for paving, retaining, block, brick and Countrystone jobs. Trade-quality brands at discount prices; call or email us for technical advice.

Sealers
For natural stone, clay and concrete pavers and walls, plus anti-graffiti. New paving or bringing old paving back.
Noskum
Removes insoluble white stains on fired clay bricks, and vanadium, manganese, iron, copper, timber, mortar and smoke stains.
Pave-Lok
Lightly bonded jointing sand. Resists wash-out after 30 minutes, slows weeds, cuts wind erosion of joints.
Grout guns
Svenic for the tradesman, Faithful for the intermediate user. Grout pavers without smearing.
Adhesives
CTA, including Prohesive Proflex, a single-part flexible adhesive for Countrystone fascia.
