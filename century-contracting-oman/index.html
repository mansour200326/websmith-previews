01 · Who we are

Who we are

Century Contracting belongs to one of the pioneer business groups in Oman, founded over a century ago. We have been in civil construction for more than forty years.

Our mission is not to be the largest, but to be the best: a single source for the industry's best ideas, best quality, best safety record and best value. Over the years we have built a record of excellence, financial strength and operational flexibility.
