About Century Contracting

“Trust is always earnedand not commanded.”

That is a philosophy we stand by. With these guiding principles, my family has been a fabric of Omani business society for over a century. Today our interests span construction, trading, manufacturing and service industries. Although our business portfolio is diversified, we stand very focused when it comes to quality of service throughout our organisation.

Century Contracting Co LLC belongs to this group, one of the pioneer business groups established in Oman over a century ago, and has been in civil construction for over 40 years.

Our mission

Not the largest. The best.

Our mission is not to be the largest, but to be the best: a single source for the industry's best ideas, best quality, best safety record and best value.

Through our history we have built and maintained a record of excellence, financial strength and operational flexibility.

HSE policy

It is the policy of Century Contracting Co LLC to achieve the safest possible working conditions for all our employees, and to protect the environment, prevent pollution and ensure a safe and healthy working environment. Our objectives:

  • To ensure, as far as possible, protection from foreseeable work hazards for all employees and the public.
  • To ensure that all employees have the training, information, supervision and common sense to avoid all foreseeable hazards.
  • To ensure all employees know it is their responsibility to take reasonable care for the health and safety of themselves and others.
  • To ensure all employees, management and workforce cooperate and consult on matters of safety.
  • To ensure healthy and safe places and systems of work.
  • Safeguard the natural environment.
