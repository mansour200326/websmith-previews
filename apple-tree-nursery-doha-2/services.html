Learning through play

Our philosophy is learning through play. It sits in our mission: a unique, secure, nurturing setting that develops each child’s pre-school abilities on an emotional, social, intellectual and physical level.

So your child’s days are full of structured activities that make sure each developmental milestone is met, with plenty of laughter, one of our four core values. We follow the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) of the British curriculum.

Our highly trained team makes sure children have fun while they learn, so they leave Apple Tree happy, confident and ready for the journey ahead.

  • British EYFS
  • Qualified Arabic & Islamic teacher
  • Monthly themes
  • Library time

Health & safety

Health and safety have always been paramount at Apple Tree. Our standard is exceptionally high, and as a team we work hard to keep it that way, every day.

We are licensed by the Qatar Ministry of Family & Social Affairs and Qatar Civil Defence. Our full-time qualified nurses, our nutritionist and our visiting paediatrician are licensed by the Qatar Supreme Council of Health. Our nutritionist adds notes on your child’s snacks and food.

  • Surveillance cameras in key areas
  • UK-certified paediatric first aid
  • Civil Defence fire warden training
  • Chubb-certified fire equipment
  • Monthly in-house fire drills
  • Weekly in-house team training

Enrolment & terms

We have an appointment-based show-around policy, in keeping with our child-centred approach: our children come first, so we ask you to book a time that suits all of us. Show-arounds run Sunday to Thursday.

  1. Complete an enquiry form (we’ll send it to you).
  2. We invite you to one of our Open Days.
  3. A payment secures your child’s place.

Registration includes a non-refundable registration fee and a refundable deposit. For July and August enquiries, email maeve@appletreedoha.com.
