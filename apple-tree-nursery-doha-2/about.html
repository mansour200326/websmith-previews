About us

Apple Tree Nursery was established in January 2008 and opened its doors to the Qatar community on 9 March 2008.

Our story Mission & vision Aims Accreditations Our team

Sowing the seeds

We are proud of the reputation and standards the nursery has built in Qatar, and of the awards we have been nominated for and won along the way.

Your child’s nursery is a huge stepping-stone for both of you. Our policies and procedures are there to guide and inform you through it, and our team brings love, care, knowledge and professionalism to every day.

Parents are vital to our nursery. We thrive on our relationship with you, and we believe in your own development too, all through your time at Apple Tree.

Mission

Apple Tree Nursery is committed to providing the highest standard of childcare within Qatar. We believe that children need to be carefully nurtured to allow their potential to be fully realised. We are proud of our strong reputation within Qatar and we enjoy working with our children and their parents to make their time at Apple Tree of the highest standard.

Vision

To create a unique, secure, nurturing environment that will develop each child’s preschool abilities on an emotional, social, intellectual and physical level, by providing a safe, child-centred setting where the key focus is learning through play.

Aims & objectives

And our four core values: Empathy, Time, Respect and Laughter.

  1. To provide loving care for your child in a safe place.
  2. To provide a stimulating learning environment that suits your child’s age and stage of development.
  3. To recognise that each child is an individual who learns at a personal pace, with a wide variety of learning experiences.
  4. To promote, by positive example, respect and care for people, their feelings and property, for our nursery, ourselves and the world around us.
  5. To create a positive, co-operative community of the Apple Tree team, children and parents.
  6. To promote the joy of learning and encourage children to have fun.
  7. To provide a clean and hygienic environment with high standards of safety.

Accreditations & awards

Qatar Ministry of Family & Social Affairs Excellence Award, March 2015, presented by the Deputy Minister.

  • Qatar Ministry of Family & Social Affairs licence
  • Qatar Civil Defence licence
  • Qatar Supreme Council of Health licence for our full-time nurses, nutritionist and visiting paediatrician
  • Qatar Chubb certification, fire safety equipment
  • UK-certified paediatric first aid training
  • Qatar Civil Defence authorised fire warden training
  • Internationally qualified childcare leaders
  • Qualified Arabic and Islamic teacher
  • Weekly in-house team training and monthly fire drills
  • Early Years Foundation Stage of the British curriculum
