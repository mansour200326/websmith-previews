Apple TreeNursery

A British Early Years nursery for children from 10 months to under 4, in a corner villa off Al Waab Street, Doha. Our doors have been open since 9 March 2008.

Book a show-around → Sun–Thu, by appointment
“Sowing the seeds” for your child’s future
Al Waab, since 2008

A welcoming place that engages each child.

Nursery is a huge stepping-stone, for your child and for you. Our days are full of structured activities, so each child meets their developmental milestones, and full of laughter too.

We follow the Early Years Foundation Stage of the British curriculum, which gets children ready for their first school years: the skills and the confidence to learn and discover on their own.

Health and safety come first.

We are licensed by the Qatar Ministry of Family & Social Affairs and Qatar Civil Defence. Our nurses, nutritionist and visiting paediatrician are licensed by the Qatar Supreme Council of Health.

  • Qualified full-time nurse
  • Qualified full-time nutritionist
  • Visiting paediatrician
  • CCTV in key areas
  • UK-certified paediatric first aid
  • Monthly fire drills, Chubb-certified equipment
  • Civil Defence fire warden training
  • Weekly in-house team training

Enrolling your child

We take children from 10 months to under 4 years. Come and see us first: show-arounds are by appointment, Sunday to Thursday, so our children always come first.

  1. Send us an enquiry

    Call or email the nursery administrators and we’ll send you the enquiry form.

  2. Come to an Open Day

    We’ll invite you to an Open Day or a show-around at a time that suits you.

  3. Secure your place

    A payment secures your child’s place for the term.
