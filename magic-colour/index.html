Magic Colour Technical Services LLC is an interior, exterior, exhibition, event and kiosk production and fit-out company, with our head office in Dubai, UAE, since 2003. The zeal to excel hasn’t changed — we keep pursuing newer and better goals, aiming to be one of the best exhibition stand fabricators and interior fit-out contractors in the market. We’re among Dubai’s premium exhibition stand producers, offering business-to-business solutions for a complete job.

At the Magic Colour design desk, ideas turn into design concepts, then move to a crew of carpenters, electricians, painters and other skilled workers — backed by marketing, technical and management staff, all working from the same desk.

Magic Colour Technical Services is committed to delivering high-end production that meets client expectations through collaboration and exceptional service. We aim for perfection, and the client’s satisfaction is how we measure the job.
