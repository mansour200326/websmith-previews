The workshop

Everything starts at the Magic Colour design desk. Once the client signs off the design, it goes straight to our own crew in the warehouse.

We don’t pass the job to someone else to build. Our carpenters, electricians and painters make it under one roof, and the same team takes it to site and installs it.
