Magic Colour / About

Since 2003

Magic Colour Technical Services LLC is an interior, exterior, exhibition, event and kiosk production and fit-out company, registered in Dubai since 2003. We work business to business, from the first idea to the finished build.

Head office and workshop: Block 38, Warehouse 1, Al Quoz Industrial Area 4, behind Emirates Glass.

The desk and the crew

At the Magic Colour design desk, ideas become design concepts. The team behind it brings together marketing people, technicians, and people from management and public relations.

Behind the desk is a large crew of carpenters, electricians, painters and other skilled workers. They build every job in our warehouse and take it to site.
