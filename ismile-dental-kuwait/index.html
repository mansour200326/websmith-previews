iSmileDental Center

Implants, veneers, braces and everyday dentistry on the 11th floor of AlMutawa Medical Complex, Midan Hawalli. Six doctors, one clinic, since 2012.

Call 2227 8949 or mobile 6633 3650

The waiting lounge, floor 11AlMutawa Medical Complex, near Al Shaab Park

Dr. Badri Al-Rayes opened iSmile in 2012 so patients could get every kind of dental treatment in one place, without the stress.

Our surgeons, orthodontist and general dentists work side by side, so an implant, a crown and a check-up can all happen under one roof. Nervous patients and small children can be treated under laughing gas.

We sterilise to World Health Organization standards, and the whole team keeps up continuing professional training.

  • 3D cone-beam X‑ray 3D images of teeth and jaw
  • Intra-oral scanner digital impressions
  • CAD/CAM and 3D printing crowns made at iSmile
  • Laser dentistry in the clinic
  • Nitrous oxide laughing gas sedation

Treatments

01Dental implantsReplacing missing teeth with a fixed tooth

Our oral surgeons replace a missing or broken tooth with a screw-like metal post and a new tooth on top. Planned on a 3D cone-beam scan, so we see the bone before we start.

More on implants →
02Veneers and Hollywood smileA new smile, tooth by tooth or the whole row

Thin veneers for a few teeth, or a full Hollywood smile: crowns and veneers on every visible tooth in both jaws, up to the premolars.

More on veneers →
03BracesWith Dr. Mazen Aboud, specialist orthodontist

Orthodontic treatment planned and followed by our specialist orthodontist, Dr. Mazen Aboud.

More on braces →
04Crowns and bridgesMade with CAD/CAM and an intra-oral scanner

For badly broken or missing teeth. We scan your mouth digitally instead of taking a putty impression, and the crown is designed on screen.

More on crowns →
05Gum treatmentSurgical and non-surgical, by our periodontist

Treating gum disease, restoring healthy gums and saving teeth that gum disease would otherwise loosen.

More on gum treatment →
06White fillings and bondingMatched to the shade of your own teeth

Tooth-coloured fillings, and bonding to repair small chips and close small gaps, often in one visit.

More on cosmetic work →
07Treatment under laughing gasFor dental anxiety and young children

Conscious sedation with nitrous oxide. You stay awake and calm through the treatment.

More on sedation →
08Sports dentistryCustom mouthguards with the player's name

Flexible, comfortable mouthguards in any colour, printed with the player's name or photo. Suitable for players with braces or a Hollywood smile.
