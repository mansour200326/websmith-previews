Who we are

Dr. Badri Al-Rayes opened iSmile in 2012, on the 11th floor of AlMutawa Medical Complex in Midan Hawalli.

Six doctors work here now. Dr. Badri and Dr. Chadi Khanji do the surgical and cosmetic work. Dr. Mazen Aboud is our orthodontist. Dr. Taha Birakdar, Dr. Kinda Mustafa and Dr. Mohammed Ashraf do general dentistry and root canals.

Implants, veneers, braces, crowns, fillings and check-ups are all done in this clinic, so you are not sent somewhere else halfway through. If you are nervous, or you are bringing a small child, we can treat under laughing gas.

We sterilise to World Health Organization standards. If you work in the private or government sector, ask about our Ibtasem card.

In the clinic
