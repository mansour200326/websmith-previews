01

Dental implants

Surgical dentistry: Dr. Badri Al-Rayes and Dr. Chadi Khanji. Dr. Taha Birakdar holds the German Board of Oral Implantology.

An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth with a screw-like metal post, and a new tooth is fixed on top. It looks and works much like a real tooth, and it is an option when a denture or a bridge doesn't fit well.

Our surgeons follow the latest advances in implantology, and the clinic has its own 3D cone-beam X-ray.

Call to book a consultation

02

Veneers and Hollywood smile

Cosmetic dentistry: Dr. Badri Al-Rayes, Dr. Chadi Khanji.

Our name says it: we put a special emphasis on your smile. Veneers are thin shells that cover the front of a tooth, for a few teeth or many.

A Hollywood smile is a full makeover: crowns and veneers, and often implants, on every visible tooth in both jaws, up to and including the premolars.

06

White fillings and bonding

We fill cavities and build up broken teeth with white filling material matched to the exact shade of your teeth.

Bonding puts an enamel-like material on the tooth surface to repair small chips and close small gaps, often in one appointment.

Treatment under laughing gas

Our team is trained to give dental treatment under conscious sedation with nitrous oxide (laughing gas). It is for patients with dental anxiety, and for young children who find treatment in the chair difficult.

08

Sports dentistry

Mouth injuries are the most common facial injuries in sport, and many athletes don't notice them. We prevent and treat them, and make custom mouthguards:

  • Flexible, stable and comfortable
  • Easy to talk, breathe and drink with
  • Any colour, printed with the player's name or photo
  • Suitable for players with braces
  • Full protection for a Hollywood smile
