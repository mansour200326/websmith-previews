Dries farms too.

Being a farmer himself since 1986, Dries knows cattle, goats, sheep, horses, free-range animals and game. Today he farms organically, with no hormones or chemicals: lucerne, nuts, fruit and vegetables.

So when you look at a farm with him, you talk about water, soil and grazing, not just the view. And after the sale he puts you in touch with people he trusts:

  • Water divining and water testing
  • Drilling boreholes
  • Fencing
  • Earth moving and making roads

The Little Karoo, from the inside.

Montagu, Robertson and Bonnievale are friendly, peaceful towns surrounded by mountains and unspoilt fynbos, with hiking trails and endless skies for stargazing and bird watching. The climate and the soil suit olives, nuts, fruit, vineyards, figs and herbs.

Montagu sits on the R62 wine route, with 4x4 routes, rock climbing and the hot springs on your doorstep. A place to return to, and for some, never to leave.
