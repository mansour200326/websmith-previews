Buying, selling and farming it after.

Dries Barnard Properties specialises in farm and country estates, commercial property and businesses around Montagu. This is what that means in practice.

01

Selling your farm

Many of the farms on our books are with us on an Exclusive Sole Mandate. Our successes speak for themselves: we sell more farms than any other estate agent in the area.

Each listing carries a full set of photos, the facts a farmer asks first (hectares, water rights, dams, boreholes, buildings, rates) and, on some farms, a video.

02

Finding you a farm

If we do not have the property for you, we will find it.

Tell us the area, the size, the budget and what you want to do on the land: fruit, livestock, game, a guest farm or a weekend retreat. Right now we have 33 farms and smallholdings on the books, from R 750,000 for 3 ha near Oudtshoorn to R 28,500,000 for 1,470 ha in Montagu.

  • Small farms with infrastructure
  • Operating farms, sold as going concerns
  • Undeveloped mountain farms
  • Guest farms and lodges
  • Game and hunting land
  • Lifestyle smallholdings near town

After the sale

Our service carries on after the sale is concluded. From decades of farming, Dries has qualified contacts across the whole spectrum of farm work, and arranges them for you.
