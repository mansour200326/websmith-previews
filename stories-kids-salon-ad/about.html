The salon & team

A salon built for children, so a haircut feels like an outing, not a chore. Here is the room, the people, and the small things parents ask about.

Why a salon just for kids

We want children to walk out more confident than they walked in: a good cut or a style they like, in a room that feels friendly, with a team that knows how to handle little ones.

So everything is set up for them. Bright arches and colour instead of a grown-up barbershop. A purple wash chair under a glowing green arch. Black barber chairs and cream styling chairs. Short appointments: most cuts take fifteen minutes.
