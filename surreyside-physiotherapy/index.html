Your first visit

We start with a thorough assessment to find the cause of your symptoms. That's how we know whether physiotherapy will help you.

Your physiotherapist explains what they found and works out a treatment plan with you, around your goals.

Treatment starts the same visit. At each session we check how you're responding and adjust the plan from your feedback.

If you're not progressing the way you expected, your treatment is re-evaluated, or we refer you to the appropriate consultant.

Questions

Anything else, call 604-597-4100 and speak with the physiotherapist.

How long is the wait for an appointment?

Same-day appointments are available, seven days a week.

Will physiotherapy help me?

The assessment will tell us. If physio isn't appropriate for you, rest assured we will refer you to the appropriate medical personnel.

How long is the first visit?

The initial assessment and treatment take 45 minutes to 1 hour.

How many sessions will I need?

Your physiotherapist will discuss this with you after the initial assessment. Every condition is different and responds differently to treatment. On average our clients have four physiotherapy treatments.

How often will I need treatment?

It depends on the condition and the diagnosis. The earlier we treat your condition, the better the outcome and recovery.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, MasterCard, Interac debit, cash and cheque.
