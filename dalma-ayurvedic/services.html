Therapies

The classic Kerala treatments, done on traditional wooden droni tables.

Sirodhara

Also written Shirodhara

Medicated oil, medicated buttermilk or a herbal extract flows in a continuous stream over the forehead while the therapist gently massages the scalp. We use it for sleep problems, stress, chronic headaches, poor concentration and high blood pressure. The oils absorbed through the scalp also help with hair fall and early greying.

Session
1 hour
Course
Daily, 7 to 21 days
Price
AED 200

Full body oil massage

Elakizhi

Leaf bundle massage

Medicinal leaves, cooked in medicated oil, are tied in a muslin bundle, dipped in warm oil and pressed over the whole body. Used for joint pain, stiffness and muscle pain.

Podikizhi

Herbal powder bundles

Cotton bags filled with a mix of herbs are dabbed rhythmically over the body with massage at the same time. Very effective for back pain, arthritis and neuromuscular problems.

Kativasthi

For the lower back

A ring of herbal paste is built on the lower back and filled with warm medicated oil, which is kept there for the session. It gives relief for all kinds of back pain and spinal problems. We do the same for the neck (Greeva vasti) and knees (Januvasti).

Nasya

Also called Nasyam

After oil and warm fomentation on the face, medicated oil or herbal extract is put into the nostrils. It clears the sinuses, helps the eyes, nose and ears, brings sound sleep and strengthens the neck and shoulders.

Session
30 minutes
Price
AED 50

Netra Tarpanam

For the eyes

A pool of medicated ghee is held over each eye for 15 to 30 minutes, depending on the condition. It strengthens the eyes and eases strain from stress or long hours on screens. It is done after the body has been prepared with other treatments.

Session
15 to 30 minutes

A full body herbal steam in a wooden steam box, with your head outside. It follows Abhyanga or another oil massage, helping the body clear out and the oils work.
