Our work

We work on all general cars, whatever the make. Being independent, we give you straight advice on what your car needs and what it will take.

  • Body denting and painting

    Dents, scratches and accident damage. Panels are put right in our workshop and painted to match.

  • Auto modification

    Changes to the body and look of your car. Tell us what you have in mind and we will tell you what can be done.

  • Fibre glass modification

    Fibre glass body work and modification, done here in the same workshop.

Spies Hecker paintWe use Spies Hecker company paint products on the cars we paint.

"Quality, not quantity."Our motto in the work. Mr. Ebrahim Jameel Rinno, proprietor
