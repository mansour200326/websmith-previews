Tyre fitting

The car goes up on the lift, the old tyres come off the rims and the new ones go on. Wheel nuts go back on with the impact gun and are checked by hand.

  • Fitting and balancing together
  • Ask us before you come if your rims are oversized

Wheel balancing

A wheel that is out of balance makes the steering wheel shake at speed and wears the tyre unevenly. Each wheel is spun on our electronic balancer and weights are added where it needs them.

  • Electronic wheel balancing
  • Worth doing with every new tyre
  • The fix for a steering wheel that shakes at 100+ km/h
Ask on WhatsAppCall 02 558 4603
04

Wheel alignment

If the car pulls to one side, the steering wheel is not straight, or the tyres wear on one edge, the alignment is out. Clamps go on all four wheels and we set the angles from the readings on screen.

  • Clamps on all four wheels, readings on screen
  • Steering wheel straight again
  • Good to check after new tyres or a hard pothole
Ask on WhatsAppCall 02 558 4603
05

Tyre rotation

Front tyres wear faster than rear ones on most cars. Swapping them round every few services makes a set last longer and wear evenly.

  • Wheels moved front to rear
  • Pressures set after
  • Easy to do with an oil change
