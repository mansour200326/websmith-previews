Our story

From one shophouse selling used cars to vehicle imports and exports, a performance workshop, parts distribution and a sourcing office in Tokyo.

2002

MB Motors Pte Ltd was founded in 2002 and started as a used vehicle retail outlet, operating out of a small shophouse in the east of Singapore.

In late 2003 it branched out into importing and selling new vehicles, and exporting quality used vehicles.

In September 2005 we formed MB Performance Pte Ltd for a growing client base that needed specialised support for their cars. Its main focus is performance enhancement, while keeping each car in proper health.

Today MB Motors deals in vehicle imports and exports, runs workshop services, and holds sole distributorships for a number of well-known aftermarket parts brands.

We would not exist, or have grown through the years, without the support our customers have given us. For this, we keep pushing to meet what our customers need as it changes.

Our existence lies in the belief that we are here to serve with pride.
