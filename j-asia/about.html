About J-Asia

Founded in 2006, J-Asia (Singapore) Pte Ltd is a specialist in home and commercial wellness. We supply saunas, steam baths, hot tubs, spas, bathroom products and kitchen fixtures from international brands.

J-Asia has a team with expertise in the design, installation and customisation of spa pool systems, outdoor hot tubs, and modular sauna and steam rooms.
