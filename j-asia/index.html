From the first visit to the after-sales call

Our team designs, installs and customises spa pool systems, outdoor hot tubs and modular sauna and steam rooms.

01

Design

We look at the space with you, a rooftop, a balcony, a garden deck or a bathroom, and design the spa, sauna or steam room around it.

02

Customise

Spa pool systems and modular sauna and steam rooms are made to fit: the model, the size, the controls and the finish.

03

Install

Our own trained technicians do the installation, on time, and hand it over running.

04

After-sales

We believe in strong after-sales service. For any technical question, write to technical@j-asia-sg.com.
