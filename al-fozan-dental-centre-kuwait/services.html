01

Check-ups & cleaning

فحص وتنظيف الأسنان

A full exam at your first visit, then regular check-ups with your dentist and the hygienist.

At a check-up we look at

  • Diagnostic X-rays: decay, cysts, bone loss, root positions
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Gums and bone, for signs of gum disease
  • Every tooth surface for decay
  • Your existing fillings and crowns

A cleaning includes

  • Removing tartar above and below the gum line
  • Removing plaque
  • Polishing away stains

02

Gum disease diagnosis

تشخيص أمراض اللثة والعظام الداعمة للأسنان

Part of every check-up. A small probe measures the pocket between tooth and gum: a healthy one is three millimetres or less and doesn't bleed. From the pocket depths, bleeding and any loose teeth, the dentist tells you which stage you are at.

  • Gingivitis: tender gums that bleed easily
  • Periodontitis: tartar build-up, gums pulling away from the teeth
  • Advanced periodontitis: teeth losing support from the bone

03

White fillings

حشوات الكومبوزيت (البيضاء)

Tooth-coloured composite fillings, matched to your own teeth, so they suit front teeth and anywhere that shows. Usually done in one appointment while the tooth is numb.

  • Chipped teeth
  • Closing a gap between two teeth
  • Cracked or broken teeth
  • Decayed teeth
  • Worn teeth

Book a filling: 1801033 →

04

Root canal therapy

علاج قنوات جذور الأسنان

When decay or infection reaches the nerve, we remove the pulp and bacteria, clean the canals and fill them, so you keep the tooth instead of losing it. Our specialist endodontist does this work.

Signs you may need one

  • A severe toothache
  • Strong sensitivity to hot and cold
  • A small abscess on the gum
  • Swelling or tenderness
  • Sometimes nothing, and it shows on an X-ray

Toothache? Call 1801033 →

05

Extractions & wisdom teeth

الخلع

The area is numbed first. Afterwards we tell you how to look after the socket: no smoking, hard rinsing or straws for 24 hours, a cold pack for swelling, and brush the other teeth as usual. Wisdom teeth that come in sideways, partly, or stay trapped under the gum are checked on X-ray and removed when they cause trouble.

Ask about a wisdom tooth: 1801033 →

06

Crowns & fixed bridges

تيجان وجسور السيراميك الثابتة

A porcelain crown covers and protects a weak tooth. A fixed bridge replaces a missing tooth: two crowns on the teeth either side hold a false tooth in the gap. It stays in, it doesn't come out at night. Usually two or more visits.

  • Fill the space of a missing tooth
  • Stop the other teeth drifting
  • Chew and speak properly again
  • Move up from a removable partial denture

Ask about a bridge: 1801033 →

07

Dentures

أطقم الأسنان الكاملة والجزئية

Complete dentures when all the teeth in a jaw are gone; partial dentures when some remain, which also stop the others from shifting. Immediate dentures are made in advance and fitted the same day the teeth come out, so you are never without teeth while the gums heal.

Ask about dentures: 1801033 →

08

Implants

زراعة الأسنان

A titanium root placed in the jaw, with a natural-looking tooth on top. It replaces a missing tooth without touching the ones beside it, or holds a bridge or denture firmly in place.

How it goes

  • X-rays and impressions to check the bone
  • The implant is placed while the area is numb
  • It heals into the bone, usually 4 to 8 weeks
  • The new tooth is fitted on top

Ask about implants: 1801033 →

09

Porcelain veneers

قشور السيراميك

Thin porcelain shells made in a dental laboratory to your shape and shade, bonded to the front of the teeth. Two visits: the first to shape the teeth, take a mould and pick the colour; the second to fit them.

  • Stained or discoloured teeth
  • Crooked or misshapen teeth
  • Uneven gaps
  • Worn or chipped teeth

Ask about veneers: 1801033 →

10

Lumineers by Cerinate

لومينيرز من شركة سيرينيت

Very thin porcelain veneers that go over your existing teeth with little or no reshaping, for stained, chipped or slightly crooked teeth. They can sometimes go over existing crowns and bridges too. Usually 2 to 3 visits.

Ask about Lumineers: 1801033 →

11

Whitening

تبييض الأسنان

Two visits. At the first we take moulds to make clear trays that fit your teeth; at the second you try them on and take them home with the whitening gel. Whitening works on natural enamel only, so old fillings or crowns may be replaced afterwards to match. It isn't permanent: coffee, tea and smoking bring the colour back over time.

Ask about whitening: 1801033 →

12

Orthodontics

معالجة تقويم الأسنان

Four of our dentists are orthodontists, including Consultant Orthodontist Dr. Tahani Al Musallam. Braces move the teeth slowly with gentle pressure. Fixed braces stay on until the end of treatment; removable ones come out for eating and cleaning. Results come fastest while children are still growing, and many adults have braces too.

  • Crooked or crowded teeth
  • Overbite and underbite
  • Missing or extra teeth
  • Jaw position and jaw joint problems

Book an orthodontic consultation: 1801033 →

13

Children's dentistry

طب أسنان الأطفال

We want your child's first visit to be a pleasant one, and to catch cavities and bite problems while they are still small. Children wait in their own room and are seen in a treatment room made for them.

  • Exams
  • Diet sheets and assessments
  • Oral hygiene and prevention
  • Topical fluoride trays
  • Fissure sealants
  • Fillings
  • Pulpotomy and stainless steel crowns
  • Inhalation sedation

Book your child's visit: 1801033 →

14

Inhalation sedation

كمام أكسيد النيتروس

Nitrous oxide, often called laughing gas, for nervous patients and children. You breathe it through a small mask; it works within a few minutes, the dose can be adjusted during treatment, and it wears off quickly afterwards. The mix is at least 30% oxygen.
