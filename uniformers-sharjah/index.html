Your logo, put on properly

Six ways to put a name or logo on cloth and giveaways. Which one fits depends on the fabric, the item and how many you need.

01

Embroidery

Your logo stitched in thread. The usual choice for shirts, polos, jackets and caps.

02

Screen printing

Ink pressed through a fine mesh, one colour at a time. Suits larger runs of T-shirts and bags.

03

Dye sublimation

The design is heat-set into polyester fabric, so full-colour and all-over prints are possible.

04

Heat transfer

A printed film pressed onto the garment with heat. Handy for names and numbers.

05

Contour cutting

Lettering or a logo cut along its outline, then applied to the garment.

06

Pad printing

For small or curved items such as pens, USB drives and mugs.
