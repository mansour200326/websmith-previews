Embroidery & printing

Six methods for names and logos. Not sure which one suits your garment or quantity? Send the logo and ask us.

01

Embroidery

Your logo stitched in thread straight onto the garment. The usual choice for shirts, polos, jackets and caps, and for a neat, raised finish on a chest logo.

02

Screen printing

Ink pressed through a fine mesh screen, one colour at a time. Best for larger runs of T-shirts and bags with a logo in a few solid colours.

03

Dye sublimation

The design is heat-set into polyester fabric rather than sitting on top of it, so full-colour and all-over prints are possible.

04

Heat transfer

A printed film pressed onto the garment with heat. Handy for names, numbers and smaller quantities.

05

Contour cutting

Lettering or a logo cut along its outline, then applied to the garment.

06

Pad printing

A soft pad lifts the ink and presses it onto small or curved items: pens, USB drives, mugs.
