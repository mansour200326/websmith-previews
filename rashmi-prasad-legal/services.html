01

Arbitration & disputes

Our attorneys represent clients in complex disputes that are settled by arbitration, where both sides agree to an arbitrator instead of a court. For each case we look at the jurisdiction first, then decide whether it is best handled in-house or by an outside attorney we work with.

International arbitration is for parties in different countries, usually because their contract says future disputes go to arbitration. Awards can be enforced in the many countries that have signed the New York Convention, which is one reason businesses choose it over the courts.

What we help with

  • Representing you in commercial arbitration
  • Choosing the institution and rules: DIAC, ICC, LCIA, SIAC and others
  • Enforcing an award in another country

Mergers & acquisitions

A merger joins two companies into one; an acquisition is one company taking over another's shares or assets. The legal structure you choose decides which liabilities, contracts and staff come with the deal.

What we work through with you

  • Asset purchase or share purchase, and why
  • Buying a division or unit that is not a separate company
  • Separating the assets and liabilities that belong to that unit
  • Transferring permits, licences and employees
  • Making sure the seller doesn't compete with you afterwards

Private equity & venture capital

08

Wills & succession

A will says how your property is shared after your death and names the executor who manages it until then. Without one, the law of each country decides.

Kinds of will

  • Notarial: made in public form and attested by a notary
  • Self-proved: signed with witnesses' affidavits
  • Mirror wills: for couples, each leaving to the other
  • Holographic: in the testator's own handwriting

If you own assets in more than one country, and one of them doesn't recognise international wills, it is usually wiser to have a separate will for each country.
