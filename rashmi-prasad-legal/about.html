Legal costs, plainly.

The first time you hire a lawyer can be unnerving, and the fees are often the biggest worry. We aim for high-quality work at a reasonable, value-for-money price, because we want clients who stay with us for years.

So we offer an unconditional, no-obligation first half-hour consultation. In it we find out what you need, explain how we work and the timeframes involved, and give you an estimate of the cost.

How we work

Business first

We get to know how your business runs before we advise. You get strategic, commercial answers, not endless legal parlance.

The right attorney for the case

In arbitration, we look at the jurisdiction and decide, in your interest, whether our in-house attorneys or an outside attorney should take it on.

Associate law firms

For disputes that need local advocates in court, we work with associate law firms who can act at every stage of the courts in the region.

Bankers at the table

On private equity and venture capital deals we work alongside investment and private bankers, so the legal and financial structure fit together.
