Had an accident?

Being in a collision is scary for anyone. Here's the step-by-step guide we give our customers. Stay calm, don't argue, and don't sign anything at the scene.

Call 204-727-8585 after →

  1. Stop.

    If your vehicle is involved in an accident and you don't stop, you may be subject to criminal prosecution.

  2. Call 911 if anyone is hurt.

    Also if damage to all vehicles looks to be more than $1,000, or you suspect a Criminal Code offence. Don't move anyone who is injured.

  3. Get off the road safely.

    If it's safe, move to the side. If the car can't be driven, use hazard lights, cones, triangles or flares. Take photos of the scene before the vehicles are moved.

  4. Write down the other drivers' details.

    Names, addresses, phone and licence numbers, plates, registered owners and insurance for every other vehicle.

  5. Get witnesses' details.

    Names, addresses and phone numbers of passengers and witnesses.

  6. Note the scene.

    Jot down specific details about the scene using the accident report sheet.

  7. Report it to Manitoba Public Insurance.

    Then call us about the repair. We help with Autopac claims.
