Painting & paint matching

Your vehicle is painted in a down draft paint booth. Powerful turbo fans make sure the finish is clean and dust free before the paint goes on, and the fluorescent lighting is placed to give a shadow-free painting area.

Why waterborne paint

  • Waterborne paints use water as the solvent to disperse the resin, and contain up to 80% water.
  • Low toxicity and flammability thanks to low VOC and HAP emissions; regulations require less than 2.5 pounds of VOC per gallon of water.
  • Less coating is needed to cover the same surface than with solvent-borne paint.
  • Good primers, with good resistance to heat and abrasion.
  • Our paint guns clean with water or water-based solutions: no thinner, acetone or methyl acetate.

Glass repair & replacement

Car makers now design the windshield as a major part of the support for the roof, and an airbag opens against it at 150 to 200 miles an hour. So the right glass matters, and so does the way it's installed.

eGlassClaim: skip the call to MPI

eGlassClaim links participating glass shops to Manitoba Public Insurance's systems. When you contact us about a glass claim, we register the claim, confirm your coverage and schedule your appointment.

What to bring

  • The driver's licence document number of the registered vehicle driver
  • The vehicle registration
  • The date and approximate time the damage happened
  • The licence plate number and Autopac policy number, any other insurance you have, and your customer number
  • If the vehicle is registered to a company, the customer number on the registration

How we replace a windshield

  1. Remove all mouldings, clips and other restraints around the glass.
  2. Check the area for rust, which can compromise a good installation.
  3. Vacuum the area, then cut the old adhesive to remove the glass.
  4. Check the new glass for fit before installation.
  5. Cut the remaining old adhesive for better access and fit.
  6. Check again for rust and prime any scratches.
  7. Clean the inside of the new windshield and prime it for better adhesion.
  8. Apply a bead of urethane to the pinch weld and set the windshield on it.
  9. Replace all clips, screws, mouldings and fasteners.
  10. Reinstall the wipers and test them for proper positioning.
  11. Clean the new glass inside and out, vacuum again, and let the urethane cure for the right time.
