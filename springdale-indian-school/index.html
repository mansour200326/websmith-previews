Joining Springdale

Admission is open to Indian students on merit, and to other nationalities when seats are available. Most new admissions start in April, at the beginning of the academic year.

  1. Step 1Call or email the officeCheck seats for your child's grade on 06 524 3335 or info@springdaleindianschool.com.
  2. Step 2Fill in the applicationKG admission is by a chat for verbal skills; from Grade 1 there's a short written test.
  3. Step 3Bring the documentsPassports with visa pages, photos, last report card, and an attested leaving certificate where needed.
