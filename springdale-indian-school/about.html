About the school

A CBSE school in Al Azra, Sharjah, that grew from a nursery opened in 1991.

Our storyVision & missionThe PrincipalThe team

How we grew

  1. 1991: a nurserySpringdale started as a nursery school for children aged two to four.
  2. Two schools taken onWith the help of the Ministry of Education, Sharjah, the school was permitted to adopt two institutions that were struggling.
  3. Up to Grade XThe Ministry gave permission to run the school up to Grade X, and the building was upgraded for the older classes.
  4. CBSE todayAffiliated to CBSE (affiliation No. 6630069, school code 90217), teaching KG 1 to Grade X in a fully air-conditioned building.

The Principal's message

Welcome to Springdale

“Education is not merely the acquisition of knowledge, but the nurturing of character, confidence, and compassion. At Springdale Indian School, we are committed to providing a holistic learning environment where every child is encouraged to explore their potential, develop critical thinking skills, and grow into responsible global citizens.

Our school believes in balancing academic excellence with strong moral values, creativity, and physical well-being. We place great emphasis on discipline, respect, and inclusivity, ensuring that every student feels valued and empowered.

In today’s rapidly changing world, education must go beyond textbooks. We aim to equip our students with 21st-century skills, digital awareness, and ethical responsibility, preparing them not only for examinations, but for life itself.

I strongly believe that education is a partnership between the school, parents, and the community. I invite you to explore our school, engage with our vision, and be an active part of this meaningful journey.”

Mr. Ratnadeep B. SuryavanshiPrincipal, Springdale Indian School
