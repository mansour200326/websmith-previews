Admissions and facilities

How to apply, the right grade for your child's age, the papers to bring, how to pay fees, and what's on campus.

Applying

Admission is open to all Indian students on merit, according to the seats available, at the discretion of the Principal and under the rules of the Ministry of Education, Sharjah. Students of other nationalities are also welcome when seats are available.

New admissions are generally accepted at the beginning of the academic year, in April. Call the office first to check seats for your child's grade.

How we decide

  • Parents fill in the application form with correct details. Incomplete forms aren't considered.
  • From Grade 1 onwards there's a written test. Admission to KG is based on verbal skills.
  • Applying early doesn't give preference, and passing the test doesn't guarantee a seat. Brothers and sisters of current students also go through the normal process.
  • All documents are checked at the administrative office and must be attested to Ministry rules before admission. The seat is cancelled if documents and fees aren't in by the date given.
  • The Principal has the final say on every application.

Ready to ask? Call 06 524 3335 or email info@springdaleindianschool.com with your child's name, age and current grade.

Documents to bring

  1. Passport of the student and the parent with a valid visa page (original and 2 photocopies).
  2. Birth certificate, for KG and Grade 1 (original and 1 photocopy).
  3. School Leaving Certificate or Transfer Certificate, attested as the Ministry requires (original and 1 photocopy).
  4. 5 passport-size photographs.
  5. Progress report or statement of marks from the last exam (original and 1 photocopy).
  6. Conduct certificate from the last school (not needed for KG).

Coming from a school in India

The School Leaving Certificate must be countersigned by the education authorities in India (AEO, DEO), attested by the Embassy or Consulate General of India in the UAE, and attested by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Coming from another school in the UAE

Get the School Leaving Certificate attested by the Ministry zone your current school belongs to.

Leaving Springdale

Parents fill in the withdrawal form at the office. Transfer certificates can also be registered online through the school's T.C. registration.

Paying fees

All fees are in AED and subject to SPEA approval. Books and uniforms are charged separately, and VAT applies as per UAE law.

CashAt the school office, during office hours.
Credit or debit cardAny bank's card, at the school office.
Bank transfer (EFT)To Springdale Indian School at Emirates Islamic Bank, Rolla branch. Ask the office for the account details.
ChequeIn favour of Springdale Indian School.

After a transfer or cheque, email the slip to accounts@springdaleindianschool.com with the student's name, Emirates ID number and SIS ID, so it's matched to the right child.
