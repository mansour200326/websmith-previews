A quiet room in Muwaileh Commercial

Outside is Muwaileh Commercial. Inside it's white walls, oak shelves, palms and linen. Nap Foot Spa is a touch of Bali in Sharjah, starting with the authentic Balinese foot massage.

Come in, slow down

Through the arch is the lounge: deep reclining chairs behind floor-length curtains, low light and plants all around. Foot massages happen here. Back, shoulder and head treatments and the full body massage are booked by time, from 30 minutes to an hour and a half.

At reception there's lemon water waiting and a few chairs if you're early. On the wall: “Have a NAP day.”
