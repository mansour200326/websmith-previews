A touch of Bali in Sharjah.

Every king deserves a nap.

It's written on our wall, next to the water and the flowers.

NAP is a Balinese-style foot spa in Muwaileh Commercial, Sharjah. We brought the foot massage of Bali here: slow, warm, done properly, in a quiet room away from the traffic outside.

Sit back in the lounge behind the linen curtains for a foot massage, add your back, shoulders and head, or book a full body massage. We also do manicures and pedicures, so you can come in for one thing or the whole hour and a half.
