About Amel

The story behind Amel Africa, a ladies' salon in Mahboula, Kuwait.

Founded by an Ethiopian businesswoman

An Ethiopian businesswoman, a salon in Kuwait

Amel Africa was founded by Amel, an Ethiopian businesswoman who lives in Kuwait with her family.

Over time the salon has grown into one of the hair salons women of all nationalities in Kuwait come to: for braids, hair extensions, wigs, styling and colour, and for makeup, waxing, nails and spa.

We keep a full selection of human and synthetic wigs and hair extensions in the salon, so you can see the hair before you choose.

As we say: beauty starts with the soul.
