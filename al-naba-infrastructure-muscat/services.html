01

Construction, turnkey

Our Construction Division builds commercial and residential buildings, from concept to completion. You deal with one contractor for the whole job: design, structure, finishing and handover.

Our quality system for the construction of commercial and residential buildings was audited by TÜV SÜD to ISO 9001:2015.

  • Any project from design to finishing
  • Our own qualified engineers and support staff
  • Skilled masons, carpenters, plumbers, electricians and other technicians
  • Capital equipment hired or leased for short-term projects
  • Sub-contracting made easier and cheaper as part of a large group

02

Planning, drawings and cost

Most of the savings on a building are made before the first block is laid. We help with site selection, local infrastructure, municipal codes, the schedule, and choosing and buying materials and systems.

We coordinate with your architect, sub-contractors and suppliers to settle problems on the drawings, keep a strict budget, and use our buying power and critical-path purchasing to keep idle time down.

  • Pre-construction advice: site, codes, schedule, materials
  • Drawings coordinated with architects, sub-contractors and suppliers
  • Budget control and purchasing
  • Concurrent phasing of design, engineering and construction
  • One system for estimating, project management, scheduling and change management

Property management and maintenance

We manage and maintain property of any size and value. We work with our sister company Al Nab’a Services, Oman’s largest integrated facilities management company, so there are people and equipment behind every contract.

  • Leasing residential and commercial property to tenants
  • Regular maintenance of residential buildings, offices, showrooms and labour camps
  • Housekeeping, landscape maintenance, pest control and waste disposal
  • Civil and electro-mechanical maintenance of residential buildings
