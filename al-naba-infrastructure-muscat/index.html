02 / What we do

Two divisions, one team

We build it, and our maintenance division can look after it once it’s handed over: leasing, upkeep and repairs.

1

Construction Division

Building and contracting work of different sizes, taken on turnkey: from concept to completion.

  • Any project from design to finishing
  • Our own qualified engineers and support staff
  • Skilled masons, carpenters, plumbers, electricians and other technicians
  • Capital equipment hired or leased for short projects, on the strength of our name
  • Sub-contracting made easier as part of a large group
2

Property Management & Maintenance

We manage and maintain properties of any size, working with Al Nab’a Services, Oman’s largest facilities management company.

  • Leasing residential and commercial property to tenants
  • Regular maintenance of residential buildings, offices, showrooms and labour camps
  • Housekeeping, landscape maintenance, pest control and waste disposal
  • Civil and electro-mechanical maintenance of residential buildings

03 / How a job runs

From the plot to the keys

01

Before we build

We get involved early: site selection, local infrastructure, municipal codes, the schedule and which materials to use. Getting these right is where most of the savings are.

02

Drawings and design

We work with your architect, our sub-contractors and suppliers until the drawings are right, and sort out problems on paper before they reach the site.

03

Cost under control

A strict budget, our buying power with suppliers and sub-contractors, and critical-path purchasing, so the money goes into the building and not into waiting time.

04

Handover on time

Design, engineering and building run side by side where they can. One integrated system for estimating, scheduling and changes keeps everyone on the same page until handover.

04 / Quality, health, safety

Checked, not promised

TÜV SÜD audited our quality system for the construction of commercial and residential buildings to ISO 9001:2015.

  1. All accidents are preventable.
  2. Practise and work safely, every day.
  3. Teamwork and collaboration.
  4. Training and knowledge sharing to grow the team.
  5. The right procedures and work instructions on every job.
  6. Auditing, monitoring and review.

Our workers get regular safety training, good living quarters, proper food and medical care. Internal audits run independently of the site teams.
