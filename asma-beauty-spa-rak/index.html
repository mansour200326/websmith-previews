Inside the salon

Four rooms under one roof, so you can do your hair, nails and a massage in the same visit.

01

The pedicure lounge

Deep armchairs on a raised, lit platform, each with its own footbath. This is where you sit for a pedicure, Moroccan foot care or a foot massage.

Pedicure AED 80 · Moroccan foot care from AED 105
02

The nail bar

A double manicure table and a wall of polish colours to choose from. Manicures, nail shaping, paraffin for the hands, and polish for the little ones too.

Manicure AED 60 · With Thai treatment AED 110
03

Hair

Styling chairs, lit mirrors and a backwash basin. Blow-dry, cuts, colour, wavy styling, ceramic and the gold ampoule treatment for hair fall.

Blow-dry from AED 70 · Haircut AED 50
04

The massage room

A fully closed room with warm light and oils on the shelves, kept quiet for massage. Classic, hot stone, herbal or deep tissue, an hour each.

Classic massage AED 175 · Hot stone AED 220
