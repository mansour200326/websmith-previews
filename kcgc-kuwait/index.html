What we do

We build tensile fabric coverings, roofs and architectural structures in PTFE and PVC fabric.

Fabric gives an architect the freedom to reach the design they have in mind without giving up quality or safety, where many conventional building materials hit their limits. We are proud to be part of this industry, and our job is to help our customers carry these designs through to a finished building.

Shapes conventional materials can't make

PTFE and PVC fabric gave architects the breakthrough they were looking for. Stretched between masts, cables and frames, fabric takes forms like these.

Cone

Fabric pulled up to a high point on a mast or ring.

Saddle

Four corners, two high and two low, so the fabric holds its shape.

Arch

Fabric stretched over a row of arches to cover a long space.

Drawings are illustrations of common tensile forms, not KCGC projects.
