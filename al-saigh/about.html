About us

Al Saigh International LLC is a limited liability company in the Sultanate of Oman that specialises in earthworks. We were registered in 2002 and have grown into a quality earthworks contractor in Oman.

The Earthmoving Division

Bulk earthmoving has long been one of the company's core activities, and it is where our expertise lies.

Our professional approach to site management keeps projects under control. On-site teams of trained and experienced managers and operators carry out each project to the customer's requirements, on schedule and within budget.

The company has an extensive, well-maintained modern fleet of specialist earthmoving equipment and has completed a number of prestigious contracts, including substantial works in the Oil & Gas and Civil sectors.

Our policies

Health, Safety & Environment

Al Saigh International LLC is devoted to prevention: reducing and eliminating all undesired events that could, or do, result in loss. Our safety and environmental programme covers all sections and areas of our operations.

Our goal is to achieve and maintain HSE performance excellence as measured by our clients, industry peers and government regulators.

Quality

The directors and management of Al Saigh International LLC are committed to running every aspect of the business to the standards that offer the highest possible quality of service to all clients.

This is supported by a progressive management style that encourages a quality culture throughout the company.
