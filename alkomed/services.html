01 · High Voltage Power Projects Department

Substations and super tension cables, 21kV to 400kV

Installation, testing and commissioning of high voltage transmission projects, for statutory utility providers and private industrial and commercial developers.

Substation installation

Transport of heavy equipment from port to site and off-loading; power transformers, reactors, GIS and AIS switchgear and bus ducts; substation ancillaries; HV and LV cabling and terminations; control, protection, ICS and SCADA; testing and commissioning.

HV power cable installation

Route survey and setting out; trenches, joint bays, pits and the civil works with them; cable transport to site; laying super tension cable systems; post-installation tests.

Jointing & termination

Factory-trained jointing teams supervised by cable engineers. GIS terminations (pre-moulded, dry type, silicone oil filled), outdoor terminations, pre-moulded, heat shrink, taped and site-vulcanised joints, oil-filled cable joints, link boxes, bonding and earthing.

HV cable testing

Independent third-party testing across the Middle East. Trailer-mounted HighVolt WRV 83-260/80T AC test system for XLPE cables up to 500kV, to IEC 60840 and IEC 62067. Also DC and sheath tests, insulation resistance, impedance, fault location, pilot cable and cross bonding tests.

Fibre optic cables

Installation, splicing and testing for communication, DTS and OPGW, with a Fujikura FSM-50S splicer, OTDR, light source and power meter. Fibre fault location too.

02 · Electrical Projects Department

Electrical building services, any voltage, any load

Our Grade 1 Electrical Licence lets us work on any electrical project in the Kingdom of Bahrain, with no limit on system voltage or electrical load. We have done electrical works for the major government ministries and most of the big industrial, commercial and private companies here.

  • Complete building services installations
  • LV and MV power distribution and control: switchgear, motor control centres, control panels, cabling, raceways, outlets
  • Indoor and outdoor lighting with lighting controls
  • Earthing and lightning protection
  • Fire detection, fire alarm and voice evacuation
  • Security: CCTV, access control and PIR alarms
  • Audio visual systems
  • Telephone, data and structured cabling
  • Standby and emergency power: generators, UPS, DC batteries
  • Electrical testing and commissioning
