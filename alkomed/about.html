About Alkomed

Alkomed Engineering Services Co. W.L.L. is a joint Bahraini–Cypriot company, incorporated in Bahrain in 1978.

شركة الكومد للخدمات الهندسية

History

We spent our first three years as the mechanical and electrical division of Alko Construction Company. In 1978 we were incorporated in Bahrain as an independent company, and we have been working here without a break since.

The founding shareholders, Al-Alawi Factories of Bahrain and the Mastro Group of Cyprus, have not changed, and they still give the support and guidance the company is built on.

We started out in power distribution cabling, overhead lines and street lighting. Over the years we added departments as the market needed them, and the workforce grew in size and in technical skill alongside Bahrain's economy and industry.

“Our vision is to be the leading electrical and mechanical engineering and contracting services company in Bahrain, in quality and reliability, with the capability to expand our activities around the Gulf.”

John Chr StrongylosFounder. From his message: our customers are “our most valuable asset”.
