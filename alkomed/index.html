We are an electro-mechanical contractor in Bahrain. Each project is run by its own team from the department that knows the work, from design and supply to testing, handover and maintenance.

1978Incorporated in Bahrain as an independent Bahraini–Cypriot company.

We began as the mechanical and electrical division of Alko Construction Company. After three years we were incorporated on our own in 1978, and we have worked in Bahrain without a break ever since.

Our founding shareholders, Al-Alawi Factories of Bahrain and the Mastro Group of Cyprus, are the same today. We started in power distribution cabling, overhead lines and street lighting, and grew department by department as the market asked for more.

500kV

On-site AC testing of XLPE cables up to 500kV, anywhere in the Middle East.

IEC 60840 and IEC 62067 ask for an AC voltage test on extruded cables once installation and jointing are done. We own a trailer-mounted HighVolt Prüftechnik test system and work as an independent third-party testing agency.

  • Test systemHighVolt WRV 83-260/80T, trailer mounted
  • AlsoDC and sheath tests, fault location, cross bonding tests
  • FibreFujikura FSM-50S splicing, OTDR testing
