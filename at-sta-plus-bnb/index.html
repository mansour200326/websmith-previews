Port Elizabeth

Most visitors only know PE for its airport, where the Garden Route starts. Stay a few days. The beaches are golden, the weather is mild all year, and the game parks are malaria free.

Walk along the beachfront in the morning and you may see dolphins playing in the surf. Sardinia Bay is good for long walks, and you can fish or just sit and watch the sunset.

In town, the Donkin Heritage Trail takes you 5 km through the Old Hill area, past 47 historical sites from the 1820 Settlers. St Georges Park has the Port Elizabeth Cricket Club and the oldest bowling green in South Africa. Settlers Park is 54 hectares of flora and fauna in the city centre.

For game viewing, Schotia runs combined day trips with Addo. Ask us and we'll book it for you.
