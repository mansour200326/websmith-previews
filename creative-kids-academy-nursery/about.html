Dear Parents,

You are embarking upon the serious task of choosing just the right child care center. We know this process can be overwhelming, so we hope the information here makes difficult decisions easier for you.

The first five years of a child's life have an incredible impact on their development and on lifelong learning. We pride ourselves in creating a healthy learning environment where a child's wellbeing is at the core of everything we do, supported by a highly skilled and committed team of early years educators and teachers.

Professionalism and attention to detail, in security, curriculum, enrichment programmes and hygiene, are the characteristics of our center. You want the best for your child, and so do we.

Please feel free to call or stop by to talk to our Center Director with any questions.

Our mission

To deliver high quality childcare and exciting opportunities for learning that give every child a head start as they prepare for school, with the chance to explore, discover and invent, and to develop emotional, intellectual and physical skills in a happy, friendly and supportive atmosphere.

What we care about

We dedicate our energies to creating a fun and caring environment while delivering the best in childcare and education.

An exceptional facility
A bright, spacious learning environment with vast indoor and outdoor spaces, built to support every child's learning and development.
Outstanding childcare
Qualified, caring practitioners committed to high quality care for every child with us.
Cultural diversity
An inclusive space where children learn about the culture of the UAE.
Play first, without pressure
Play-based activities combined with formal learning lead to happy, confident children. Children grow in confidence without feeling pressured.

Our staff

Our practitioners inspire children and plan learning that makes the child the leader. Their role is to observe, listen to children's questions and stories, find what interests them, and help them explore it further.

Our friendly, appropriately qualified and experienced staff are the backbone of the nursery. We are committed to staff training, with a proper induction and ongoing training at every level.

Staff are trained in first aid, health and safety and basic food hygiene, and are encouraged to extend their training and qualifications with our full support.

When your child joins, their Class Teacher and Key Worker make sure the introduction to nursery goes as smoothly as possible. You can stay with your child until they settle, and we ask about their sleeping and feeding routines, likes and dislikes, so their day reflects what they're used to at home.
