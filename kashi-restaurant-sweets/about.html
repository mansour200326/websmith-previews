Welcome to Kashi

Sweets, namkeen and an eatery under one lit-up sign on 17A Street, Al Karama. Here is how we tell it on the first page of our menu.

We grew up chasing the last piece of kaju katli. Saving the crispy corner of the kachori. And stealing sweets before the guests arrived.

At Kashi, we've wrapped all those delicious memories into a sweet and snack eatery that feels just like home, only with better lighting and no one yelling, "Bas ek hi le lo!"

Made with love, and with zero preservatives: every bite is just how your dadi would make it (if she ran a kitchen in Dubai).

From classic favourites to fusion sweets with a twist, our menu is a joyful celebration of India's flavours, reimagined for today.

Whether you want to sit down and savour, take a little something home, or surprise someone with a thoughtfully packed hamper, Kashi is here to make it special.

Inspired by the soul of India and served with a whole lot of heart, our food honours childhood flavours, family recipes, and the kind of joy that only comes from fresh, honest food.

So come hungry. Come curious. And most importantly, come ready to fall in love, one bite at a time.
