About Midori

A quiet spa near Palm Jumeirah, with a team that has more than eight years at the table each.

Our therapists come from Thailand, Vietnam and the Philippines. Tell us where it hurts and how firm you like it: soft Swedish strokes, a medium aromatherapy massage, dry Thai stretching, shiatsu or warm stones. Our most popular is the Midori Signature, which blends a little of each.
