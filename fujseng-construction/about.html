FujEng

FujSeng Construction L.L.C. is a subsidiary of Fujairah Engineering & Manufacturing Holdings, which is why many people know us as FujEng. Our motto, on our logo: Building Dreams.

  1. 2006

    Founded in Fujairah as a contracting company, licensed in the Unlimited Category. Later registered in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Ras Al Khaimah too.

  2. 2007

    Our first building handed over. We kept a maintenance team on it, and that team grew into today's ISO 41001 facility management unit.

  3. 2016

    The duct manufacturing unit opens on 13,525 sq ft, alongside our own façade, steel and rebar factories.

Safety: a way of life

Our QEHS policy, in short. One integrated management system, checked by regular internal audits and management reviews.

ISO 9001:2015ISO 14001:2015ISO 45001:2018ISO 41001:2018
  1. Execute projects in full compliance with the contract.
  2. Strictly comply with all applicable laws and regulations.
  3. Eliminate accidents, injury and property damage with a safe, healthy workplace.
  4. Protect the environment and keep the harm from our operations to a minimum.
  5. Follow a customer-focused approach and give the best value for money.
  6. Build our team's skills through training and development.
