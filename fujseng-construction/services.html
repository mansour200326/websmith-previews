Civil engineering

Licensed in the Unlimited Category, so we take on Design & Build (EPC) and normal contracts, from high-rise buildings to infrastructure.

Four project units, each led by a senior projects manager: a civil engineer with 25+ years of site work. Each unit has its own construction, project and site engineers, foremen, technicians, storekeepers and a document controller.

Behind the units

  • Our own rebar cut & bend yard, working to each project's BBS
  • A design wing with a structural design engineer, draftsmen and surveyors
  • The other divisions for specialist works

What we've delivered

  • Residential and commercial buildings: high-rise towers, malls, hotels, hospitals
  • Infrastructure: roads, culverts, tunnels, storm-water channels
  • Warehouses and factories, one recently of 25,000 m²

Some projects

Fujairah Investment Towers · G+35 Comm. & Res. Building · Dibba Stadium & Cultural Club · Al Orooba Stadium · Century Mall & Hotel · Mina Al-Fajar Resort · Airport Tunnel at Fujairah International Airport · FOIZ Emergency Response Center · Fruit & Vegetable Market

Electro-mechanical

All mechanical, electrical and plumbing works on EPC, Design & Build and other contracts. The division is headed by a mechanical engineer with 30+ years' experience: a LEED Green Associate, ASHRAE member and Lean Six Sigma Green Belt.

Three MEP project units, each led by an engineer with 15+ years and their own electrical and mechanical project engineers, site engineers, foremen and technicians.

Inside the division

  • Duct factory: fire-rated (Civil Defence approved) and non-fire-rated ducts
  • Facility management unit
  • Energy Management, Testing & Commissioning Unit (EMTCU)
  • A design wing and a contracts wing with QS engineers

Aluminium & façade

Curtain walls, doors and windows (including systems authorised by known system brands) and cladding, fire-rated (Civil Defence approved) and non-fire-rated. EPC façade work for our own projects, or as a subcontractor to other main contractors.

The factory runs precision cutting lines, four-axis CNC processing machines and vertical and horizontal bed CNC routers, with six independent lines for cladding assembly. Three project units, each run by an engineer with 15+ years.

of aluminium extrusions (curtain wall, door and window systems) processed per shift, per month, plus 7,500 m² of façade cladding.

04

Steel fabrication

Conveyor belt system and steel works for oil.

A cut-and-weld factory and a strategic partnership with Roofing Middle East: together about 800 MT of hot-rolled (400 + 400) and 3,000 MT of pre-engineered steel structures a month, fabricated and erected.

We design, fabricate and erect

  • Clear spans 10 m to 100 m
  • Heights 4 m to 50 m
  • Multi-span, multi-gable
  • Lean-to, single-shaped
  • C, Y and T-shaped car canopies
  • Crane, mill and hangar buildings

Delivered

Oil pipe racks for terminals in the FOIZ area and for ADNOC through the Port of Fujairah, belt-conveyor structures, aircraft hangars, pedestrian bridges, a briquette plant building and the FIE Twin Towers steel works.
