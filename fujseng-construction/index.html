Since 2006

We started in Fujairah in 2006 as a building contractor. Over the years we set up our own factories for the façade, the ductwork, the steel and the rebar, so most of what goes into our buildings is made by our own people.

Today FujSeng, also known as Fujairah Engineering or FujEng, takes on Design & Build (EPC) and normal contracts: high-rise towers, malls, hotels, hospitals, mosques, roads, culverts, tunnels and storm-water channels, oil and gas steel, warehouses and factories.

Registered in Fujairah (CR 100044), Dubai (691474), Abu Dhabi (1325495) and Ras Al Khaimah (54549), in the Unlimited Category in all four, so we can work as main contractor in each. ISO 9001, 14001, 45001 and 41001.

What we make ourselves

We set up our own factories over the years, so the façade, the ducts and the steel on our sites come from our own lines and work to one schedule. They also take orders from other main contractors.

01

Aluminium & façade

Curtain walls, doors, windows and cladding, fire-rated (Civil Defence approved) and non-fire-rated. Cut on precision lines and four-axis CNC machines, put together on six independent assembly lines.

  • 25,000 m² of aluminium extrusions per shift, per month
  • 7,500 m² of façade cladding per shift, per month
02

Ductwork

Our duct unit opened in 2016 on 13,525 sq ft. Galvanised, mild and stainless steel up to 1.2 mm, pre-insulated phenolic and aluminium cladding, fire-rated and non-fire-rated, to DW 144, SMACNA and ASHRAE.

  • About 3,000 sq ft of duct per shift
03

Steel

Hot-rolled cut-and-weld work and pre-engineered buildings: clear spans from 10 m to 100 m, heights from 4 m to 50 m, crane buildings, mezzanines and hangars. Rebar is cut and bent in our own yard to each project's bar-bending schedule.

04

MEP & upkeep

Three MEP project units, each run by an engineer with 15+ years. Our facility management team has looked after buildings since our first handover in 2007: HVAC, electrical and plumbing, 24×7, ISO 41001 certified.

Who runs your site

Our projects division has four units. Each is led by a senior projects manager, a civil engineer with more than 25 years of site work.

Every unit has its own construction, project and site engineers, foremen, technicians and storekeepers. Behind them sit a design wing with a structural engineer, draftsmen and surveyors, and the other divisions for the specialist work.
