About the lounge

A ladies-only beauty lounge at Shop 14 on Al Rayat Street, Al Dhait, Ras Al Khaimah.

We do hair: a trim or a blow-dry, colour and highlights, keratin and protein treatments, braids, extensions and wigs, and hairstyles for an occasion or a wedding.

We also do nails, lashes, make-up, facials, threading, bleach and waxing, and there's a separate room for the Korean bed spa.

We're open every day from 10am to 9pm. Message us on WhatsApp, call, or book on Fresha, where you get an instant confirmation and can pay in the app.
