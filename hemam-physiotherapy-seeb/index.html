A physiotherapy centre in Seeb. Our three consultant physiotherapists treat adults, mothers-to-be and babies, on the mat and in the pool, and we bring physiotherapy to your home when getting here is hard.

Therapy in the water

In the pool, the body weight is reduced by up to 90%.

Hydrotherapy treats some diseases and injuries through swimming and exercises in water. Sessions on our list run in blocks of 15 minutes: water pool therapy with therapeutic exercises, for one or more areas.
