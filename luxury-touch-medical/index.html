Candela GentleMax Pro laser hair removal for women and men

Two laser wavelengths in one machine, so the settings can match your skin tone, your hair and the area we treat. Your laser technician checks all three before the first session.

A laser room at the clinic, 10th floor.Separate laser rooms
Wavelength one755 nm

Alexandrite. Usually chosen for lighter skin tones and different types of dark hair.

Wavelength two1064 nm

Nd:YAG. Goes deeper and can be adjusted for darker skin tones.

CoolingDCD

A short cooling spray around each laser pulse protects the skin's surface and makes the session more comfortable.

Laser questions

asked every week

Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Is Candela GentleMax Pro suitable for dark skin?

The device includes the Nd:YAG 1064 nm wavelength, which can be adjusted for darker skin tones. A skin assessment is required before treatment.

Is Candela laser hair removal painless?

The DCD cooling system helps improve comfort, but the sensation varies depending on the area, hair thickness and individual sensitivity.

Can men use Candela GentleMax Pro?

Yes. Laser hair removal appointments are available for men for areas such as the beard line, neck, back, shoulders, chest and full body.

Can I have laser treatment after tanning?

Recent tanning can increase skin sensitivity and may affect the selected laser settings. Tell the laser technician about recent sun exposure before treatment.

How many sessions will I need?

Laser hair removal normally takes a series of sessions because hair grows in stages. The number depends on your hair, skin, the area and how you respond.
