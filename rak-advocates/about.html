The officeعن المكتب

Ras Al Khaimah Advocates is a law firm licensed to practise in the United Arab Emirates. Advocates, legal consultants and administrative staff, on the second floor of NS Tower on the Corniche.

Advocate Salem Al Kait

For years, Advocate Salem Al Kait has given legal services and legal advice. The office grew around him into a complete team.

Today it brings together advocates and legal consultants who each work in their own field of law, with the managers and staff who keep every client's file in order.

How your case runs

Once you sign with the office, this is what happens.

  1. 01

    An electronic file, from day one

    We open an electronic file for you holding every document related to the case.

  2. 02

    A copy of the court file

    We get a copy of the papers submitted in the case file at court.

  3. 03

    Studied, then discussed with you

    We study them and consult with you about what they say.

  4. 04

    Kept up to date

    We tell you about every new development.
