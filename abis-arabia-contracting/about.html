Since 2010

ABIS Arabia started in 2010 as a specialist contractor. Year by year the projects grew, the turnover grew, and with it the range of work we can take on.

Construction, maintenance and supply for industry, mainly oil and gas, and for the infrastructure around it.

We have completed major projects in the industrial sector and keep adding services for oil and gas clients. Behind us is a network of partners that backs the technical and financial side of every job, so each phase of a project is carried through to completion.

Our aim is simple: a qualified, professional team that our clients trust, and relationships that last because the work is done right, by every member of the team.

We want to be friendly, supportive and responsive to what customers need, and to deliver in a sustainable, cost-effective way. That's what the line on all our service sheets means: your satisfaction is our motivation.
