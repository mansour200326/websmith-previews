05

HVAC and fire fighting

Before any HVAC work starts we prepare the detailed duct layout, submit it and get it approved. We review the approved materials with the client, buy only after approval, then install, inspect against a checklist and close out the punch list. We also maintain and repair heating, ventilation and cooling units for commercial and residential customers.

On fire fighting we install, maintain, supply and test the systems below. And we put up scaffolding and insulation for domestic and commercial jobs, with qualified crews who work safely.

Fire fighting

  • Fire alarm systems
  • Fire building furnishings
  • Building plumbing
  • Water and foam extinguishing systems
  • FM-200 suppression
  • High and low pressure CO2 systems
  • High and low pressure water mist

Also

  • HVAC duct design submittals and approval
  • Duct installation and inspection
  • HVAC maintenance and repair
  • Scaffolding erection
  • Insulation
