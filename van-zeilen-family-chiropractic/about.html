Dr. Kris Van Zeilen

Doctor of Chiropractic, owner

Dr. Kris is a Rochester native and a graduate of Life University in Marietta, GA. His treatment plans begin with a correct diagnosis and treat the underlying problem, not just the pain, so the person can get back to their best health.

He takes the time to learn as much as possible about each patient through a complete history, a thorough exam and x-rays, and honest education about the condition. A newborn needs a different technique from a 20-year-old athlete, and moving between light-force and more traditional adjusting is a skill built over years of continuing education.

Sports
Team chiropractor for the Rochester Edge Tier 1 Girls Hockey organization. Has treated professional, college and amateur athletes from the US, Canada, Europe, New Zealand, Australia and South Africa.
Rugby
Member of the 2000 USA Rugby Super League Champions, the Life University Running Eagles. Also played for the All Army Rugby Team and the Combined Services All Star Team. Now on a long break; his wife calls it "retirement".
Family
Married to Robyn, with four children.

Kara

Office Manager

Kara earned her bachelor's degree in athletic training and kinesiology from the College at Brockport in 2014, then her physical therapist assistant degree from Genesee Community College in 2016, working as a certified athletic trainer along the way.

She has worked in outpatient, sub-acute and long-term care, and brings case management and billing experience to the front office. Outside work: running, weightlifting, her husband, son and two dogs.

Kaycee

Chiropractic Assistant

Kaycee is an expert at first impressions. Over 8 years of medical front-desk experience and 3 years as a Licensed Massage Therapist, and she goes out of her way to build a relationship with everyone who walks in.

In her free time: road trips with her husband, her golden retriever, the garden, and something good in the kitchen.
