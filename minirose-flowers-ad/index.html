One arrangement, up close

Flowers, money and a gift, from our October reel. Here is how it comes together, piece by piece.

01

The shape

Tall at the back, red amaranth spilling over one side, all of it set in a white fluted bowl on a stand.

02

The money

Banknotes rolled one by one and tied in neat rows, standing in the front where they're seen first.

03

The gift

The present goes in whole, still wrapped and tied, with the flowers built up around it.

04

The finish

Deep red blooms, white lace flowers and fresh green to tie it together. Then it's ready to go.
