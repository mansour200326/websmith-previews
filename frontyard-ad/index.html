What people order

The things our guests keep naming in their reviews, from the first coffee at 7 to dessert after dinner.

  • Matcha

    Salted vanilla and strawberry are the two people come back for.

  • Spanish latte, dirty espresso

    From the espresso bar, or to go in the red Frontyard cup.

  • Hot chocolate

    A favourite in the reviews.

  • Aleppo eggs and breakfast plates

    All-day favourites, next to the toast and croissants.

  • Aseeda

    The Emirati classic, and one of the desserts reviewers single out.

  • Tiramisu

    Named in review after review.

  • Lava cake, cheesecake

    For sharing, or not. Served on the terrace until 1am.
