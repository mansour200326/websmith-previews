About & parents

Al Reyadah is a nursery in Al Khaledia, Sharjah. Our aim is simple: to enrich children’s lives in a place where they learn the basic skills, safely, in English and Arabic.

Partnership with parents

What we work for

Six goals from our partnership with parents, in plain words.

  1. Encourage every child, with ambition, towards high standards.
  2. Care and guidance that build independence, self-confidence and respect for others.
  3. A chance for every child to take part in nursery activities, with real enthusiasm.
  4. Help children understand their place in society, here at the nursery and in their families.
  5. Supervisors who enjoy working with children and are good role models.
  6. Work with parents so the time here is happy, and prepares them for what comes next.
