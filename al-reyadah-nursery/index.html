Al Reyadah Nursery

An EYFS nursery on 77th Street in Al Khaledia, Sharjah, for children from two months to four years. Lessons are in English and Arabic, in small groups.

Six stages, one building

Children join us from two months old and stay until they are four. They move up through six stages, and each stage has its own carers who know what that age needs.

Arts and crafts

Paper, paint and glue at the craft table. Ladybirds, the letter of the week, things to take home.

Aerobics and sport

Moving every day: aerobics, games on the grass and the slide in the shaded yard.

Computer principles

First steps on the classroom touch screen, with a teacher beside them.

Concerts and birthdays

Birthdays, National Day celebrations and concerts through the year.

Workshops

Planting day, the farm corner and hands-on days that change through the year.

Storytelling

Story time on the cushions in the reading corner, in English and Arabic.

Potty training

We work on it together with you, at your child’s pace.

EYFS, taught in English and Arabic

We follow the Early Years Foundation Stage. Children work in small groups on short and long projects, and every activity grows one of five areas:

  • Cognitive
  • Language
  • Social
  • Emotional
  • Physical
