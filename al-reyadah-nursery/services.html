Programmes

What your child does at Al Reyadah, from the first stage at two months to the last before school. Ask about any of it on WhatsApp.

01

Six stages, 2 months to 4 years

Babies can start with us from two months old, and children stay until they are four. The nursery is split into six stages by age, and each stage has its own room and its own carers.

Tell us your child's age and we'll show you the room they would join.

Ask on WhatsApp →or call 06 528 3010

02

EYFS in English and Arabic

We follow the Early Years Foundation Stage, the British framework for the early years. It is taught in English and Arabic, with some French.

Children work in small groups, mixing abilities and interests, on short and long projects that get them thinking creatively and solving problems.

  • Cognitive
  • Language
  • Social
  • Emotional
  • Physical

03

Arts and crafts

Something to cut, paint and stick, and often something to take home: ladybirds, the letter of the week, seasonal displays.

Aerobics and sport

Aerobics and different sports and games, indoors and outside. The yard has artificial grass, a slide and swings under coloured shade sails.

05

Computer principles

A first, gentle start with screens: children use the classroom touch screen together, with a teacher, as part of a lesson.

07

Workshops

Storytelling
