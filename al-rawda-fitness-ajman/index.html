Walk the floor

One big hall of machines, weights and benches, a second level above it, and an open matted floor for pad work and classes.

01

The machine rows

Plate-loaded and pin machines run down both sides of the hall, with benches in between. Chest, back, shoulders and legs, all on one floor.

02

Weights and benches

Plate trees, benches and cable stations under the red lights. This is where weight training and bodybuilding happen.

03

The upper level

A second floor runs above the hall behind glass, lit in blue. You see it the moment you walk in.

04

The open floor

A matted floor with room to move: pad work with a trainer, stretching, aerobics and Zumba.
