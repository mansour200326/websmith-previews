Services

Everything you can do at Al Rawda Fitness, for ladies and gents. Open Monday to Saturday, 5am to 2am. Prices and timings change, so the fastest way to know is a WhatsApp message.

Personal training

Train one-to-one with a trainer who builds your plan around you: losing weight, putting on muscle, or starting again after a long break. The trainer shows you each machine and keeps you on track.

  • A plan made for you
  • Machines, free weights or pad work
  • Ladies and gents: ask us about trainer timings

02

Weight training & bodybuilding

The main hall is lined with plate-loaded and pin machines, with benches, plate trees and cable stations down the middle. Come early before work or late after a shift: the floor is open until 2am.

  • Plate-loaded and pin machines
  • Benches, plates and cable stations
  • Open 5am to 2am, Monday to Saturday

Cardio
