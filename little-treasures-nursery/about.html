A home away from home

Little Treasures is a happy, caring, nurturing place for the children of Ras Al Khaimah, in a relaxed and secure setting in Al Hamra, Jazeera.

We follow the British Early Years Foundation Stage, and add our own core values and programmes on top.

The EYFS sets the standards for learning, development and care from birth to 5. Children learn by playing and exploring, being active, and thinking creatively, indoors and outside.

Every child's learning journey is tracked through continuous assessment. With close monitoring and plenty of talking to parents, there's challenge for the more able and support for children with additional needs.

We're approved and licensed by the Ministry of Education. All our preschool teachers hold relevant qualifications, and every member of staff is Paediatric First Aid trained and certified. We keep training too, at Early Years workshops and seminars.
