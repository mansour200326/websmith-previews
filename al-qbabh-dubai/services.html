Trays for the table

Come as a group and order a tray. It's how most tables here eat.

  • Mixed seafood trayصينية مشكل بحري

    Grilled fish, prawns, crab and mussels over spiced rice, served on one big tray. Tell us how many of you are coming.

  • Crabقبقب

    Gabgoob, the local crab. It isn't always written on the menu, so ask.

Order a tray ahead →

Fish

The fish changes with what came in. Fish is priced by the day: ask on WhatsApp.

Ask what's fresh today →
  • Grilled fishسمك مشوي

    A whole fish from the day's catch, over charcoal, with white rice.

  • Hamour machboosمچبوس هامور

    Hamour with machboos rice, cooked with dried lime and spices.

  • Tunaتونة

    The fish the restaurant is named after: Al Qbabh, a local tuna.

From the sea

Ask how it's cooked today →
  • Prawnsربيان

    Prawns off the grill, on their own or on the tray.

  • Squidخثاق

    Squid. Ask how it's cooked today.

  • Seafood in creamبحري بالكريمة

    Mixed seafood in a cream sauce, for when you want something rich.

  • Jesheedجشيد

    Minced baby shark cooked with onion and spices. An old Emirati dish, eaten with white rice.

Soup, rice, sweet

Order ahead →
  • Nokhatha soupشوربة نوخذة

    Seafood soup named after the nokhatha, the captain of the old pearling boats.

  • White rice with saminعيش أبيض وسمن

    There's a jar of samin (melted ghee) on every table. A little over the rice is how it's done.

  • Hareesهريس

    Wheat and meat, slow-cooked until smooth.

  • Luqaimatلقيمات

    Small fried dumplings in syrup with sesame, to finish.

Order ahead, then drive out

  1. Message us

    The day, the time and how many of you.

  2. Say what you'd like

    A tray for the table, the day's fish, a private room. We'll confirm on WhatsApp.

  3. Come to Al Aweer

    Your table is ready when you arrive. Open every day, 11am to 2am.
