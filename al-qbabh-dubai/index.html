What to orderشو تطلب

  1. 01Mixed seafood trayصينية مشكل بحري

    The one people drive out for. Grilled fish, prawns, crab and mussels over spiced rice, on one tray for the whole table.

  2. 02Hamour machboosمچبوس هامور

    Hamour with machboos rice, cooked with dried lime and spices the Emirati way.

  3. 03Grilled fishسمك مشوي

    Whole fish from the day's catch over charcoal, with white rice. Ask which fish came in today.

  4. 04Nokhatha soupشوربة نوخذة

    Seafood soup named after the nokhatha, the captain of the old pearling boats.

  5. 05Prawns, squid, tunaربيان، خثاق، تونة

  6. 06Jesheedجشيد

  7. 07Harees and luqaimatهريس ولقيمات

    Harees, and luqaimat in syrup with sesame to finish.

The menu at the restaurant is in Arabic. There's samin (melted ghee) on the table: a little over the white rice is how it's done here.

It started on the beach

Mohammed Al Rashidi used to grill seafood on the beach at weekends for his family and friends. Word got round, more people came each weekend, and the little grill grew into this restaurant in Al Aweer.

It still feels like a weekend by the sea: palm-frond roofs, the fish on show when you walk in, and the whole family round one tray.

Netflix's Somebody Feed Phil finished its season 7 Dubai visit at our tables.
