About DUSK

Danah Universal School of Kuwait is an American school in Salwa, Block 1, teaching from KG1 to Grade 12. Our crest carries the line we work by: Education for Tomorrow's World, تربية لعالم الغد.

Our vision

“To educate each individual to reach his/her full potential in a sustainable and caring community that fosters excellence and responsibility.”

Our vision, as set out in the principal's letter. We want students to think critically, solve problems, create and enjoy learning, and to grow into responsible members of the wider community.

Cognia

Our accreditation

DUSK is accredited by Cognia, a non-profit, non-partisan organisation and the largest community of education improvement professionals in the world. Cognia carries out rigorous on-site external reviews of Pre-K to 12 schools.

We teach to the American Common Core Standards, use NGSS for science and NWEA MAP for external testing.
