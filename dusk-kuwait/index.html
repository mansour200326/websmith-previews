What we teach

DUSK is an American school. Lessons are in English, and Arabic is taught as part of the Language Arts, following Ministry of Education guidelines.

  • Common CoreCommon Core State Standards (CCSS) for literacy and mathematics.
  • NGSSNext Generation Science Standards for science.
  • NWEA MAPExternal testing that measures each student's progress over the year, so teachers can plan for every child.
  • CogniaThe school is accredited by Cognia, which carries out on-site external reviews of Pre-K to 12 schools.
  • French
  • Emotional Intelligence
  • Visual and Creative Arts
  • IT
  • Music
  • Robotics
  • Physical Education
  • Arabic
  • Islamic Studies

Around the campus

Everything below is inside our building on St 100, for KG to Grade 12.

01

A 25-metre pool

DUSK has its own 25-metre swimming pool on campus, indoors, with marked lanes.

02

Two semi-covered sports grounds

Two covered sports grounds and recess areas for PE and break time. Elementary, middle and high school each have a lunch canteen.

03

Robotics lab and three IT labs

A fully equipped robotics lab, and separate IT labs for the elementary, middle and high school.

04

Two art rooms and a music room

Art and music are taught in their own rooms, with keyboards, guitars and percussion in the music room.

05

Indoor gym and auditorium

Used for PE, assemblies, the book fair, Islamic Day and the visits we host, like the Kuwait Fire Force awareness day.

How to register

New families are welcome at every stage, KG1 to Grade 12. This is how registration works.

  1. 1

    Call or email us

    Tell us your child's age and current grade. We'll send the registration form and the full process.

  2. 2

    Meet the stage supervisor

    Your child has an interview with the supervisor for their stage, which may include short tests. Bring a copy of their latest report card.

  3. 3

    Confirm the place

    Pay the KD 100 registration fee and the first instalment before the first day. Ask us about the school bus and uniforms at the same time.
