Our story

We've been operating from this spot for over 35 years. It started as a brake and clutch centre.

The current owners took over more than 23 years ago and widened the business into a bigger range of products and services, and it has kept growing since.

We take pride in giving you the right information, the correct parts at competitive prices, services that aren't readily available in most areas, and a high standard of service.

We offer a highly trained staff and flexible service times, and we guarantee your complete satisfaction.
