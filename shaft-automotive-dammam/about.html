Sharing pride to your ride.

SHAFT began with people who couldn't find a garage they trusted for their own BMWs. This is how it went.

Our story

  1. 1999

    A home garage

    We struggled like everybody else to service and repair our own cars: there were no good BMW garages, and the local dealership cost us a fortune every time. So we worked on our BMW and MINI cars in our home garage.

  2. Then

    Friends, then their friends

    Friends and others trusted our clean work, and how fast we found the real root cause of a problem without changing parts that didn't need changing. That is how we built our reputation.

  3. 2006

    Tuning and racing

    Car and motorcycle tuning came along together. We had a winning career, from the national autocross races to the car motor shows.

  4. Now

    The service centre

    With over 19 years of experience, custom tuning and certified engineering behind us, we opened an automotive engineering service centre in Dammam, for first-class car and motorcycle service and tuning.

OEM standards, OEM tools.

SHAFT works to OEM service standards, with OEM-approved tools, in the hands of passionate mechanics and engineers. Integrity and quality are our values: high-end professional service at reasonable cost.

What we hold to

Professional. Committed. Reliable.

Our mission is simple: an uncommonly great experience for anyone who needs auto service and repair.
