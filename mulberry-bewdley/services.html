Parties and wedding receptions

The restaurant is available any time for party bookings, with special party and wedding reception menus. Birthdays, anniversaries, a christening lunch or the whole family at Christmas: tell us the date and the numbers and we'll put a menu together.

We're normally closed on Mondays and Sunday evenings, but we will open for a private party of 20 or more.

Booking

To avoid disappointment, it's always best to book a table in advance. Call us on 01299 404040 or email enquiries@mulberryrestaurant.co.uk.

For tables of 6 or more we ask for a 50% deposit when you book, for evenings and lunchtimes alike. Until the deposit is paid the booking is provisional, and provisional bookings are held for up to 5 days.

Many thanks, Jonathan

Allergies or special diets? Ask a member of the team for an allergen card when you arrive, so the kitchen can take extra care.
