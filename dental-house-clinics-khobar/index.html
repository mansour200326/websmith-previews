Dental House

Specialist dentists on the Khobar Corniche: braces and clear aligners, implants, root canals, gums, whitening and children's dentistry. Fahad Center, 4th floor.

Book on +966 13 811 9996 Toll-free 920 002 721
Open now Saturday to Thursday, 2pm to 10pm

Dental House is a group of Saudi consultants and academics who set up one clinic so people in the Eastern Province could see the right specialist close to home.

Each of our doctors works in one field: braces and aligners, crowns and implants, root canals, gums, children and special needs, or family dentistry. Between them they bring experience from North American universities.

We built the clinic with up-to-date equipment and rooms made for our guests to be comfortable. That is what we call our patients at Dental House: guests.

Treatments

Ten kinds of care, each handled by the doctor trained for it. Tap one for the details and who treats it.

All treatments in detail →

  1. Orthodontics

    →

    Clear aligners (Invisalign), metal braces and tooth-coloured ceramic braces, for children and adults.

  2. Dental implants

    →

    A fixed tooth for one or several missing teeth. Placed under local anaesthetic; the final crown goes on after about three months.

  3. Crowns, bridges and dentures

    →

    Replacing missing teeth to bring back how they work and how they look.

  4. Root canal treatment

    →

    Removing the infected nerve and sealing the canal, to save the tooth.

  5. Fillings

    →

    Repairing damaged surfaces with the right type of filling, so you keep the tooth.

  6. Teeth whitening

    →

    At home with trays made for your teeth, or in the clinic under a special light.

  7. Gum treatment

    →

    Treating disease of the gums and the bone around the teeth, and replacing what is lost.

  8. Children and special needs

    →

    Prevention and treatment suited to the child's age.

  9. Oral and jaw surgery

    →

    Buried wisdom teeth, bone grafts, and conditions of the jaws and face.

  10. Cleaning and oral health

    →

    Removing build-up from every surface. We advise a cleaning every six months.

Your visit, room by room

Everything happens on one floor of Fahad Center, from the first conversation to the last check-up.

01

Reception

Fahad Center on Prince Turki Street, fourth floor. Tell us who you're seeing, or that it's your first visit, and we'll take it from there.

02

Your treatment plan

We have a room just for talking through treatment plans. You sit down with the doctor and go through the steps before anything starts.

03

X-rays

X-rays are taken in our own X-ray room on the same floor.

04

Treatment

Treatment rooms have big windows and daylight over Al Khobar.

05

Sterilisation

Instruments are cleaned and sealed in pouches in a separate sterilisation room.

Children and patients with special needs

Two of our dentists specialise in children and patients with special needs. They look after prevention and treatment in the way that suits each age, and the clinic has laughing gas (nitrous oxide) on hand.
