Orthodontics

We diagnose and treat teeth that don't bite or line up properly, and jaws and faces that are out of balance, in children and adults.

With Dr. Suliman Shahin, Dr. Ibrahim Al Arfaj and Dr. Ayed Alqahtani

01

Clear aligners (Invisalign)

One of the newest ways to straighten teeth, without it getting in the way of your life. The aligners are clear and come out, so you eat comfortably and clean your teeth properly, which lowers the chance of decay and gum inflammation during treatment. It also needs fewer visits.

02

Metal braces

The traditional way, and it can treat every kind of case. Metal brackets are fixed to the teeth and move them into line. Stable, and a good choice if you'd rather not have a removable appliance.

03

Ceramic braces

Fixed like metal braces, but made of ceramic close to the colour of your teeth, so they are hard to notice. For adults who want to look neat during treatment.

Dental implants

For anyone who has lost a tooth, or several, or is about to.

With Dr. Firas Alqarawi, Dr. Faris AlShahrani, Dr. Hamad AlRumaih, Dr. Mohammed Alqahtani and Dr. Abdulkareem Alhumaidan

  1. Day one

    The implant is placed under local anaesthetic, and you leave with a temporary crown.

  2. ~3 months

    The bone grows around the implant and holds it firmly.

  3. Final crown

    A fixed crown goes on that looks and works like your natural teeth.

Teeth whitening

One of the most common ways to a bright, natural smile, done by a cosmetic dentist. Two ways to do it:

With Dr. Firas Alqarawi, Dr. Faris AlShahrani and Dr. Hamad AlRumaih

At home

10 to 15 days

We take impressions of your teeth and the dental lab makes trays that fit them. At home you put the whitening gel in the trays and wear them for a set time. With regular, correct use, results usually show after 10 to 15 days.

In the clinic

Under a special light

We put whitening gel on your teeth and activate it with a special light, and repeat it until you reach the shade you want.

Children and special needs

We look after the teeth of children and of patients with special needs, with prevention and treatment suited to their age. The clinic has laughing gas (nitrous oxide).

More treatments

  • Crowns, bridges and dentures

    We repair teeth, keep them working and improve how they look, replacing missing teeth and parts of the mouth with prostheses.

    With Dr. Firas Alqarawi, Dr. Faris AlShahrani, Dr. Hamad AlRumaih

  • Root canal treatment

    We diagnose and treat the nerve of the tooth, usually by removing the infected nerve and filling the canal with special materials.

    With Dr. Manal Alsaadoun

  • Fillings

    We keep the tooth and repair the damaged surfaces with different types of fillings, so it still works for eating and looks right.

  • Gum treatment

    We diagnose disease of the soft tissue and bone around the teeth, treat it and keep it healthy, and replace what is lost with permanent implants.

    With Dr. Mohammed Alqahtani, Dr. Abdulkareem Alhumaidan

  • Oral and jaw surgery

    Surgical treatment of conditions, birth defects and injuries of the head, neck, face and jaws, including buried wisdom teeth and bone grafts.

  • Cleaning and oral health

    A healthy mouth matters for your general health. A cleaning removes the build-up from the outer and inner surfaces of the teeth; we advise one every six months.

    Family dentistry with Dr. Yousef Alhomaid
