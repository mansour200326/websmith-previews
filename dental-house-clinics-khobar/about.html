About Dental House

A clinic started by Saudi dentists for the people of the Eastern Province, on the fourth floor of Fahad Center in Al Khobar.

How it started

Dental House began when a group of highly qualified dentists in Saudi Arabia, with experience from North American universities in every field of dentistry, decided to work together in one place.

We set out to give the people of the Eastern Region dental and cosmetic care to the highest standard, close to home.

The clinic has a modern design and up-to-date equipment and technology, so that every guest has a comfortable visit. Yes, guests: that's what we call our patients.

We brought together doctors, assistants and administrators who share one goal: giving the best in dentistry in a welcoming place. That is our ambition and our promise to every guest of Dental House.

Vision, mission and values

Vision

Excellence and leadership in dentistry in the Middle East.

Mission

The highest standard of dental and cosmetic care, using the latest technology, from the most capable people in the region.

Values

  • The highest standards in every treatment.
  • Earning our guests' trust through care, accuracy and honesty in our work.
  • Keeping up with the latest developments in every field of dentistry.

What we aim for

  • Meeting every dental and cosmetic need of our guests with evidence-based treatment and modern equipment.
  • Integrity at work, and making sure our guests are happy with the care they get.
  • Community projects that raise awareness of oral and dental health, nationally and in the region.
  • A good place to work, where our team keeps growing professionally.
