About MRB

In the Saudi market since 2008.

MRB Contracting Co Ltd is an ISO 9001 certified company and a subcontractor to Saudi Aramco, SABIC and its affiliates, Saudi Electricity Company, Maaden and many national and multinational companies. We provide construction, contracting and maintenance services, and we take on shutdown and turnaround jobs.

01 / How we work

Our own people, on every job

Our contracting and maintenance divisions are staffed by qualified, experienced engineers, technicians and skilled labour. They handle electrical, instrumentation and mechanical construction, plant maintenance and shutdowns, whatever the size of the project.

Our engineering department gives you the facts to choose the most reliable and economical specification for your project. Our projects run from short-term jobs to long-term, multi-discipline contracts.

Many of our jobs are repeat assignments from the same clients. We answer fast and look after the details, and we keep to our clients' production deadlines with our own construction crews and labour supply.

02 / Quality and safety

ISO 9001, inspected as we go

Quality assurance follows the ISO international standards, with strict inspection procedures and quality control on site. We work to each client's own requirements and to every regulatory authority that applies, with documented evidence.

The safety and health of our employees and the protection of the environment come first. Our safety programme is part of how we work: each employee knows his responsibility for himself, the people around him, the equipment and the property.
