08 / HRSG

Doosan HRSG service agreement

Doosan Heavy Industries' HRSG division builds heat recovery steam generators for combined-cycle power plants. It named MRB its local distributor for the HRSG plants in Saudi Arabia and Bahrain, where 34 of its units run.
