02 / Speciality

Non-metallic piping: GRP, RTR, GRE and HDPE.

It's the work we're best known for. Pipelines in the desert and process piping inside the plant, with our own fitters, riggers and FRP laminators.

01

Stringing and lowering in

Lengths are laid out on sandbags along the route, then lifted and set down by sideboom, with the crew guiding every length.

02

In the trench

Our fitters line up and joint each length in the trench. GRE and GRP lines run the same way inside gas plants such as Harad and Fadhili.

03

Making up the joints

Threaded RTR joints are made up by hand with chain tongs on pipe stands. Bonded and wrapped joints are done by our own lamination and FRP technicians.
