01Litigation & international disputes

We look for the quickest and most cost-effective way to resolve a dispute. The firm handles every form of dispute resolution and settlement, including mediation, and our lawyers draft the memos, petitions and appeal papers for civil, commercial and criminal cases, up to the Court of Cassation.

Call about a case →

02Arbitration & alternative dispute resolution

Our arbitration lawyers know the UAE arbitration provisions and the international rules on conciliation and arbitration. We advise from the contract stage, when the arbitration clause is written, through to the hearing. Forums we work in:

  • Dubai International Arbitration Centre (DIAC)
  • Abu Dhabi Commercial Conciliation and Arbitration Centre (ADCCAC)
  • DIFC–LCIA Arbitration Centre
  • ICC International Court of Arbitration
  • Ad hoc arbitration tribunals in the UAE
  • UAE court-appointed arbitration tribunals

Cases include joint venture and corporate disputes, land and property ownership (freehold, SPAs, leasehold), Islamic finance, infrastructure, sale of goods, distributorship and agency agreements, and construction disputes between employers, main contractors and subcontractors.

Call about arbitration →

03Police & criminal cases

We handle police complaints and criminal cases, including complaints against customers who have defaulted. A department inside the firm works on cases and investigations with the police and the public prosecution.

Call about a police case →

04Insurance

We advise insurers and their customers on insurance and reinsurance law, from new products to disputes, and we litigate for and against insurance companies and the insured. Areas we cover:

  • Life credit risks
  • Professional liability
  • Product liability
  • Construction risks
  • Property & personal injury
  • Marine policies

05Company set-up & commercial agencies

We set up companies, establishments, branches and representative offices of foreign companies, joint ventures and reorganisations. That covers the paperwork (memorandum of association, powers of attorney, side agreements, MoUs, shareholder and board resolutions, local service agent agreements) and the permissions, approvals and licences.

For foreign companies that do not want a formal presence in the UAE, we advise on commercial agency, franchise and distribution agreements.

Call about a company →

06Corporate & commercial affairs

Setting up a business under the different UAE jurisdictions, free zones included, from the first question to the licence. We negotiate, draft, review and interpret agreements: distributorship, franchise, construction, joint venture and more.

07Finance & banking

Banking and finance matters of every kind, acting for borrowers, lenders and corporate trustees.

08Construction

For developers, employers, contractors and consultants: joint venture agreements, project financing, engineering, design, procurement, construction and management contracts, performance bonds, collateral warranties and guarantees.

09Shipping

Advice across the shipping and transport industries: bareboat, demise, time and voyage charter parties, bills of lading and ship management.

10Hospitality

For operators, owners, developers, banks and investors in hotels, restaurants, clubs, marinas and yacht clubs, serviced apartments and spas. Hotel management, technical services and pre-opening agreements, franchise, lease and concession, trademark licences, sale and purchase, acquisitions, due diligence, and court cases for the industry.

11Trade marks & intellectual property

We register trade marks for companies and individuals so nobody else can trade on them. After the Ministry of Economy examines the mark and the fees are paid, the owner receives a certificate of ownership. We also register intellectual property in the owner’s name with the Ministry, to protect it from forgery and unauthorised copies.

Call about a trade mark →

For you & your family

Work, home, inheritance and everyday law.

  1. Labour
  2. Real estate
  3. Wills for non-Muslims

12Labour relations

For employers and employees, under UAE labour and employment law: employment contracts, pay, benefits and pensions, termination and resignation, confidentiality, non-compete and restrictive covenants. We draft and review employee policies, codes of conduct, offer letters and settlement agreements, represent you before the labour authorities, and take labour cases to court. We also run seminars on the UAE Labour Law and its amendments.

Call about a work problem →

13Real estate

Contracts for sale and purchase, land investment, development, leasehold and freehold, reservations, agency and mortgage financing; registration procedures with the authorities; and property disputes in court or arbitration, for and against developers, tenants and landlords.

  • Ending, expiry and renewal of tenancy contracts, residential and commercial
  • Eviction, execution and claims for unpaid rent
  • Rent increases and maintenance disputes
  • Advice to developers and investors on freehold, Musataha, long leases and mixed-use schemes
Call about a property →

14Wills for non-Muslims

How the UAE courts apply inheritance law worries many expatriates. We draft wills for non-Muslims, notarised in Dubai, and help with attesting them before a notary public. Court precedent shows these wills can be applied in any emirate, at the court’s discretion.

Call about a will →

15Family, civil & penal law

Beyond the areas above we advise on employment and consultancy contracts, patents and copyright, labour disputes, family law, the penal code and civil law.
