The name on the door

Mohammed Abdulla Al Braiki has worked in law and legal consultancy since 2000. He specialises in international law and is a member of the UAE Association of Lawyers and Legal Advisors.

Our head office is in Abu Dhabi, we take cases in every emirate, and where we have no office abroad we work with correspondent firms. Our lawyers know the local customs and the court procedures, and they draft the memos, petitions and appeal papers, up to the Court of Cassation.
