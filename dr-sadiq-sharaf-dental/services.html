01 · Preventive dentistryCheck-ups and cleaning

Looking after your teeth so they stay healthy: no cavities, no gum disease, less wear. Come for an exam at least once a year, more often if you're prone to problems. We find small things early, when they're quick and cheap to fix.

Scaling and polishing
Removes the plaque and tartar that brushing can't reach.
Fissure sealants
A thin coat on children's back teeth to keep decay out of the grooves.
Fluoride application
Strengthens enamel, especially for growing teeth.

02 · Cosmetic dentistryHollywood smile and whitening

If your teeth are stained, discoloured, worn, chipped or have gaps, we can make your smile look better with one treatment or a few together. We plan it with you first.

Veneers (Hollywood smile)
Thin shells over the front teeth. Dr. Sadiq's speciality.
Teeth whitening in the clinic
The fastest, most effective whitening, done after we check there's no plaque or tartar.
Home bleaching kit
To keep the colour after whitening. Home gels are weaker than clinic gels, so we show you how to use them.

Ask for Dr. Sadiq Sharaf or Dr. Maryam Madan.

03 · Dental fillingsFillings and bonding

We numb the area, remove the decay, clean the tooth and fill it in a colour that matches your teeth. Fillings also repair cracked teeth and teeth worn down by grinding or nail-biting.

Tooth-coloured fillings
Composite resin, matched to the shade of your teeth.
Gap closure (bonding)
Closes small gaps between front teeth with tooth-coloured material.

04 · EndodonticsRoot canal treatment

A root canal saves a badly damaged or infected tooth instead of pulling it out. We remove the infected pulp, clean and disinfect the canals, then fill and seal the tooth. With today's anaesthesia it feels much like a filling.

Tooth pain, swelling or tenderness? Come in soon. An infection left alone can turn into an abscess.

Root canal
Cleans, disinfects and seals the inside of the tooth.
Apicoectomy
A small procedure at the tip of the root when a root canal alone isn't enough.

Ask for Dr. Bhargav Penubadi, endodontist, or Dr. Zahraa Alkhabbaz.

05 · Oral surgeryImplants and extractions

Most of our surgery is done under local anaesthetic. An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth with a small post, then a crown goes on top. The bone has to heal around the post, so the whole process takes a few months.

Dental implants
An alternative to dentures or a bridge that doesn't fit well.
Tooth extraction
Including wisdom teeth.

Dr. Sadiq Sharaf holds the NHRA Dental Implant Privilege Licence.

For replacing missing teeth or protecting weak ones. Fixed or removable, on your own teeth or on implants.

Crowns
A cap over a damaged tooth. Porcelain or ceramic to match your teeth.
Bridges
Fills a gap using the teeth on either side.
Dentures
Full or partial, removable.

07 · OrthodonticsBraces and Invisalign

Crooked teeth are harder to clean and put strain on the jaw. We check with a full exam, X-rays and photos, then suggest the right option for you, at any age.

Braces
For crowding, crooked teeth, overbites and underbites.
Invisalign
Clear aligners you change in order, usually over one to two years. You take them out to eat and brush.

Ask for Dr. Manjula, orthodontist.

08 · PaedodonticsChildren's dentistry

Children have smaller mouths, changing teeth and short attention spans. We introduce them to the dentist slowly so they feel safe, and we like to see them every six months. Good habits now protect their adult teeth.

White fillings
Strong, tooth-coloured fillings for milk teeth.
Pulpotomy and pulpectomy
Saving a milk tooth when decay reaches the nerve.
Space maintainers
Keeps the gap open when a milk tooth is lost early.
Extractions
Only when the tooth can't be saved.

Ask for Dr. Maryam Madan.

Call East RiffaCall Budaiya

09 · Periodontics and laserGums and laser treatment

Plaque hardens into tartar under the gum line and only a professional clean removes it. Left alone it causes gingivitis, then gum disease. We also use a dental laser for several gum procedures.

Gum treatment
Deep cleaning of the roots for bleeding or receding gums.
Laser gingivectomy
Removes or reshapes extra gum tissue, usually 30 to 60 minutes.
Gingival curettage and operculectomy
Laser cleaning of infected gum, and removing the gum flap over a wisdom tooth.
Ulcer healing
Laser treatment for mouth ulcers (canker sores).

10 · TMJJaw pain

The jaw joint works like a sliding hinge on each side of your face. Pain, clicking or locking can come from grinding, clenching, arthritis or an injury. Most cases settle with simple, non-surgical care.
