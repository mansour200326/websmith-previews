A small clinic in East Riffa that became two branches and eight dentists. Here is how Dr. Sadiq tells it.

A message from our director

Message from Dr. Sadiq Sharaf

We opened in 1999 as a small clinic in Riffa, and gradually added more dental services and more staff. Because of that demand, we opened our second branch in Budaiya in 2015.

Our goal is to give every patient high-quality treatment at a reasonable price, with the best equipment and materials, and with as little pain as possible.

Before we start any treatment, we go through the plan and the procedure with you, so you know exactly what's going to happen.

Feel free to book a consultation. We're always here to help.

Dr. Sadiq Sharaf

Your rights here

Patients' rights

  1. Confidentiality of all information about your dental care.
  2. To make decisions about your dental care.
  3. To accept or refuse treatment.
  4. Privacy during consultation and treatment.
  5. Safety and security while inside our centre.
  6. To know the name and professional status of everyone treating you.
  7. To know everything about your case and have all your questions answered.
  8. To know the reason if we refer you to another dentist.
  9. To know the price of your treatment.
  10. A wheelchair for patients with special needs.
