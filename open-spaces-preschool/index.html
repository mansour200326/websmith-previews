About Open Spaces

Rather than cotton-wooling them, we let children find their own challenges. We believe children are their own best risk managers.

Open Spaces is a small private centre on the outskirts of Whangarei, nestled on a farm with a significant area of native forest. Much of our children's learning takes place in nature: lush gardens, flowers, bush walks, water, animals, sticks, stones and mud.

The Wild Woods

The heart of our program. A paddock with several stands of native bush, plus a track down to the creek at the back of the farm. Our 'outside classroom'.

01 · The walk out

Every week, a small group

Wild Woods trips happen every week with up to 8 children and 2 kaiako, a 1:4 ratio. Tamariki in our Under 2s area often come along on the walks too.

02 · In the bush

Climb, explore, build huts

Puriri trees over 400 years old, trees to climb and a maze of old trails. The children learn to respect the natural environment: all part of our philosophy of Kaitiakitanga.

03 · Down at the creek

Real big eels

The older children occasionally get to go on a big adventure to feed our local Tuna (eels) in the Waitangi stream that runs behind the Woods.

04 · Back at the centre

The engine room

Learning experienced in the bush is brought back to the centre and developed further. Our connections to Papatūānuku are part of our daily practice.

"We hope that the dirt they get under their nails travels to their hearts and ignites a passion that will endure as long as life lasts."

Little ones and big kids, together

We're a mixed-age centre and actively encourage the age groups to mix, so Tuakana / Teina relationships can flourish. We value interdependence over independence.
