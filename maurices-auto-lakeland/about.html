About the shop

Call 863-646-4479

Family-owned, on Drane Field Road in south Lakeland, and open every day of the week.

Maurice's has been a family-owned auto shop for more than 34 years, and a member of the Lakeland Area Chamber of Commerce since 1990.

We're open 7 days a week, including major holidays, because cars don't break down on a schedule. The lot is a secure facility with camera surveillance, so your car is safe while it's with us.

We've expanded: the body shop is now across the road at 2940 Drane Field Road, and the repair shop and towing desk are at 3025.

Our promise: 100% satisfaction, or we'll look at your vehicle again.
