Auto repair & maintenance

Routine maintenance is the best way to keep your car out of the shop. We follow the service schedule in your owner's manual, and when something does wear out or break, we fix it: from custom headlights to radiators.

  • Minor engine repair
  • Automatic transmissions

  • Clutch and driveline
  • Brakes
  • Steering and suspension
  • Air conditioning
  • Cooling system and radiators
  • Electrical
  • Gas and diesel engine diagnostics
  • Hybrid powertrains
  • Muffler and exhaust
  • Hitch systems

Maintenance & inspections

  • Lube, oil and filter service
  • Factory-recommended maintenance
  • Fluid replacement
  • Tire and wheel service
  • In-house alignment
  • Emission and safety inspections
Working to a budget? In some cases we can fit good used parts, including engines and transmissions. Ask when you call.

Warranty: 12 months or 12,000 miles on repairs as standard, with extended warranties available. We also do extended-warranty work and deal with most insurance companies.

Towing

Having your car towed is nerve-wracking, especially after an accident, and most people don't know a tow driver by name. When you call, our dispatcher takes your information, answers your questions and sends the right truck to you as quickly as possible.

Our tow trucks are late-model, kept clean and maintained like new, and each one carries a full emergency equipment package. We treat your car with care on hook-up and on the road.
