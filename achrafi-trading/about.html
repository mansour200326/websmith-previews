About us

A Sharjah trading house for industrial products and spare parts, since 1996.

Since 1996

Adel Achrafi Trading Est. was set up in 1996 in Sharjah as a trading company for industrial products and spare parts. It has grown steadily every year since.

We specialise in Italian industrial equipment, and keep working with more of the best Italian makes as customers ask for them: electric motors, gearboxes, ventilators and many other parts for industry.

Our customers are in iron and construction, production lines, brick manufacturing and engineering. For crusher equipment we supply the spare parts.

"We put between your hands twenty years of experience in the field of industrial tools."From our website, written in 2016.
