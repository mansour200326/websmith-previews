  • Preventive care for the whole family

03Root canal treatmentEndodontics

When the nerve inside a tooth is infected or inflamed, root canal treatment removes the infection and saves the tooth, instead of taking it out.

On the team: Dr. Hasan Fateel, Specialist Endodontist

  • Root canal treatment
  • Treatment of painful or infected teeth
  • Re-treatment of older root canals
  • Crown afterwards when needed
Call to book: 1731 1180 →or email us about root canal treatment
04White fillings, crowns & bridgesRestorative dentistry

On the team: Dr. Ameera Radhi, Consultant Prosthodontist

  • Composite (white) fillings
  • Crowns
  • Bridges
  • Dentures
Call to book: 1731 1180 →or email us about fillings, crowns or bridges
