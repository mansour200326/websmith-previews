Doctors & centre

Mission and vision

Our mission

To stay at the forefront of dental innovation, so we can keep giving our patients a level of quality unique in Bahrain. We work hard to make sure only top professional care is used, and that patients leave knowing they had the best care we can give.

Our vision

To bring international standards of dental care within reach of our patients, and to offer high-quality, cost-effective treatment in a comfortable setting, with modern dental technology.

Patients' rights & responsibilities

Your rights

  1. To receive information about your diagnosis, investigations, treatment plan, risks, benefits and likely outcome of any treatment or procedure.
  2. To choose a particular healthcare provider, and to know the name and specialty of any doctor or other person caring for you.
  3. To receive screening and stabilisation in case of severe pain, injury or sudden illness without prior authorisation.
  4. To know all treatment options available to you and to take part in decisions about your care.
  5. To receive considerate and respectful care, and to express your spiritual and cultural beliefs as long as they do not interfere with others' privacy or the centre's work.
  6. To complete privacy during your treatment.
  7. To have your health information kept confidential. It may only be shared with your informed consent, except when required by law or a court order.
  8. To a fair, fast and objective review of any complaint about your doctor, a member of staff or the centre.
  9. To seek and receive all the information you need to understand your condition.
  10. To understand and receive information on the cost of treatment.
  11. To receive information about any proposed treatment, including benefits, risks, alternatives and recovery time, so you can give informed consent.

Your responsibilities

  1. To give complete personal information whenever required.
  2. To give accurate and complete information about your illness, history and medicines.
  3. To keep your appointments.
  4. To follow the treatment plan your doctor recommends.
  5. To take part in your pain management and tell the doctors and nurses how well it works.
  6. To be responsible for your actions if you refuse the treatment offered.
  7. To take care of your valuables and personal belongings.
  8. To treat other patients, visitors and staff with courtesy and respect, and follow the safety rules.
  9. To understand all instructions before signing consent forms.
  10. To give accurate information about your health insurance and pay your bills on time.
  11. To follow the no-smoking policy.
  12. Women of reproductive age: to tell the doctor if you may be pregnant, especially before an X-ray.

Toothache on a Friday?

On Fridays the centre opens for emergencies only: pain, swelling, a broken tooth or a knocked-out tooth. Please call before you come.
