The cafe

100th St. Corner Cafe is a casual place to sit down for breakfast or lunch. We serve Urbandale and the surrounding areas, and we keep it friendly and warm.

Pull up a booth, have a cup of coffee with free refills, and take your time.

Coming with a group? We take reservations for weekday parties of more than 10. Call (515) 331-2343.
