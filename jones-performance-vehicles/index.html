Shane built rally Evos for Ralliart.

Evo 3Evo 4Evo 5Evo 6Evo 7

Before the workshop in Bathurst, Shane Jones built Group A to Group N Mitsubishi Evos for international teams at Ralliart Australia. At Ralliart UK he worked on Evo models 3, 4, 5 and 6, and the first WRC car, the Evo 7 shape, driven by Tommi Mäkinen.

Mäkinen won four world championships while Shane was there. Back in Australia, Shane built cars with Ralliart Australia for Ed Ordynski and Spencer Lounds, then came home to Bathurst and started Jones Automotive Performance Vehicles.
