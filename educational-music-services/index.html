Try before you buy

Take an instrument home for three months, then three more. Up to six months before you decide, and everything you've paid in rental comes off the price.

Months 1 to 3

15%

An initial payment of 15% of the price covers the first three-month trial.

Months 4 to 6

25%

A second payment of 25% of the price covers another three months.

Then

Buy or return

Buy it, with all rental charges deducted from the price, as long as the expiry date hasn't passed. Or bring it back.

For example: Buffet 100 Series alto sax, €750

€112.50 for the first three months, €187.50 for the next three. Keep it, and those €300 come off the €750. The purchase price is guaranteed for the whole rental.
