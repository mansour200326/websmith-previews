On this road for more than three decades.

Our aim has always been the same: a proud name for good work and good service, for light and heavy vehicles alike. Over the years that has built us a loyal base of customers, many of whom have been bringing their vehicles to Thoban for a long time.

Long-serving staff, latest testers.

We are known as a reliable maintenance and service garage because our staff stay. Mechanics, denters, painters and electricians, fully trained, many with us for years, working with the latest diagnostic tools and system testers.

The garage and service station cover 40,000 square feet: a light-vehicle workshop, a heavy-vehicle workshop with fabrication and welding, a denting section, a temperature-controlled paint room and a water-service bay.

“Our experience and knowledge is geared to our valuable clients.” That line has been on our wall and our website for years, and it still holds.
