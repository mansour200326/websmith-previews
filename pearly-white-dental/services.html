Treatments

Six kinds of care, all at the clinic in Al Khuwair. Dr. Raj sees you at every appointment, explains the options with photos from the intraoral camera, and gives you the cheaper option when it's the better one. Tap any treatment to read more.

01

General dentistry

Care for the whole family in one place. Dr. Raj herself handles all cleaning appointments, so you get her eye whether you need a routine clean or an implant.

Complete check-up

Twice a year is what we recommend. We check the soft and hard tissues and magnify each tooth with the intraoral camera so decay, cracks and worn old fillings aren't missed. You see the live picture on the screen.

Old metal fillings, inlays, onlays and crowns can be replaced with tooth-coloured ones.

Gum disease treatment

Gum disease usually doesn't hurt, so many people have it for years without knowing. Left alone it destroys the gums and the bone that hold the teeth. We check your gums at every cleaning and treat it early.

Root canal treatment

Most damaged or infected teeth can now be saved instead of pulled. Dr. Raj uses an endo motor with an apex locator for every root canal: it cleans the canals more thoroughly and usually means one to three visits.

Post and core

Even when most of the tooth crown has broken or decayed, the tooth can often be rebuilt: a root canal, a custom post, a core built around it, then a crown.

Tooth-coloured fillings

Nano-hybrid resin that looks and feels like tooth, in front and back teeth. Also used to replace black metal fillings, rebuild chipped teeth and close small gaps.

Night guard

Grinding at night (bruxism), often from stress, wears teeth flat and makes them sensitive to cold. A night guard made for your bite protects them while you sleep.

02

Cosmetic dentistry

Often a single appointment or two, with no time taken away from your week.

A Pearly White whitening patient, from the clinic's gallery.

Teeth whitening

About an hour in the clinic, then a home kit if you want to keep the result up. Teeth can feel a little sensitive for around 48 hours; we give you a gel for that.

Veneers, inlays and onlays

Thin porcelain, ceramic or composite shells bonded to the front of the teeth to change their shape, colour or length, in as few as two visits.

Crowns

When a filling would be too big, a crown (cap) covers and protects the whole tooth, often after a root canal. Zirconia (Cercon), all-porcelain, gold or porcelain fused to metal.

Re-contouring of teeth and gums

Too much gum when you smile, or an uneven gum line? Gum reshaping fixes it, on its own or with veneers or implants. Results are immediate and usually permanent.

Tooth jewellery

A small gem bonded to the enamel in 10 to 20 minutes. Harmless, no sharp edges, and it comes off quickly whenever you like.

03

Missing teeth

A gap lets the neighbouring teeth tilt and drift, traps food and throws off your bite. Dr. Raj is accredited by the Ministry of Health, Oman as an implant specialist. Every replacement is matched to the colour of your own teeth.

All-ceramic crowns, from the clinic's gallery.

Dental implants

A titanium implant in the jawbone carries a crown, a bridge or a denture. One implant per tooth, an implant-supported bridge, or a full arch on implants. Looked after well, they can last a lifetime.

Bridges, on implants or teeth

A fixed row of replacement teeth held by crowns on the teeth or implants either side. Metal-ceramic or metal-free. A Maryland bridge is bonded to the neighbouring teeth with minimal drilling.

Dentures

Partial dentures held with discreet clasps, complete dentures for a full arch, and chrome-cobalt dentures for a stronger, longer-lasting partial.

Braces and alignment

For straightening teeth and fixing the bite. Dr. Raj completed a two-year programme with Progressive Orthodontics, California, and an MSc in Specialised Orthodontics at the University of Duisburg-Essen, Germany.

Orthodontics

For straight teeth and a proper bite, in children and adults. Ask Dr. Raj at your check-up and she'll go through the options with you.

05

Children's dentistry

We see children from two years old, earlier if you notice a problem. We teach them to brush and floss, first with your help, then on their own. There's a toy corner in the waiting room.

Fluoride application

A gel, foam or varnish painted on the enamel for a few minutes. Varnish suits very young children who can't hold a tray.

Pit and fissure sealant

A thin clear coating on the back teeth that keeps sugar out of the grooves. Quick, and it saves a lot of fillings.

Milk tooth restoration

Milk teeth hold the space for the adult teeth. Decayed ones are treated so the decay doesn't spread and the adult teeth come in straight.

Pulpectomy

When decay reaches the nerve of a milk tooth, we clean it out to save the tooth and avoid an abscess.

Space maintainer

If a milk tooth is lost early, a space maintainer stops the other teeth drifting into the gap.

Re-implantation

A knocked-out tooth can often be put back. Come straight away: the first 20 to 40 minutes decide whether it can be saved.

06

Oral surgery

We do every oral surgery procedure that can be completed under local anaesthetic, here at the clinic.

Simple and surgical extractions

When a tooth can't be saved with a root canal, it comes out before the infection spreads. Includes impacted wisdom teeth. We'll talk about a bridge or implant for the gap.

Cyst excision

Removal of a mucous cyst (mucocele), most often on the inside of the lower lip, when it doesn't go away on its own.

Apicoectomy

If infection stays at the tip of a root after a root canal, the root tip is removed and sealed, to keep the tooth.
