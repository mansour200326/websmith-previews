You see the same dentist every time. Even for a cleaning.

Dr. Raj handles every appointment herself, from a routine scale and polish to a dental implant. She listens first, shows you what she sees on the screen in front of the chair, and explains the options before anything starts.

"My efforts are always directed to try and save existing teeth, since there cannot be any real substitute for natural pearly whites."

What's in the room

  1. 01

    Intraoral camera

    A small camera shows the inside of your mouth, enlarged on the screen in front of the chair, so you see what the dentist sees.

  2. 02

    Digital X-rays

    Up to 90% less radiation than film, and the image is on the screen straight away. No ten-minute wait.

  3. 03

    Laser dentistry

    Used for cavities, whitening and gum disease. Less invasive, faster healing.

  4. 04

    Smart endo motor

    Root canals with an apex locator: more accurate cleaning, and fewer visits.

  5. 05

    Treatment videos

    Short animations explain a procedure before you agree to it.
