Dr. Raj and the clinic

Pearly White opened in Muscat in 2008 with one aim: a healthy smile for you and your family that lasts. We don't advertise much. Most of our patients come because a friend or relative sent them.

Dr. Richa Raj

Director and founder · Implant specialist, accredited by the Ministry of Health, Oman

"There cannot be any real substitute for natural pearly whites."

Dr. Raj graduated in Dental Surgery in 2001 from CCS University, India, as Gold Medalist, and did her house job at PGI, Chandigarh. She then took a degree in Hospital Administration from Apollo Medvarsity and a one-year course in Implant Dentistry in the USA.

She completed a two-year programme in Orthodontics with Progressive Orthodontics, California, and an MSc in Specialised Orthodontics at the University of Duisburg-Essen, Germany.

Her aim is always to save the tooth you have. Keeping the original tooth structure is better for your teeth in the long run, and saves you time and money too.

  • Life Member, Indian Dental Association
  • Member, American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID)
  • Member, Oman Dental Society
  • Member, ITI International Team for Implantology

Renugadevi Ramkumar

Nurse · at Pearly White since 2016

She trained at Kasturba Gandhi Nursing College, India (BSc Nursing, 2008), started at Mahatma Gandhi Medical College & Research Institute in Pondicherry, and has held a Ministry of Health, Oman nursing licence since 2012.

She'll keep you comfortable through the whole treatment.

"I believe in providing the best care possible for all patients."
