Our history
1975

We started as the Sporting Goods Division of Sound General Trading Establishment, a small sports shop in Muscat.

1985

The division became its own company, Supa Sportsman LLC, trading only in sports goods and equipment.

5

Shops in Muscat: our multi-brand showrooms in Al Hamriya and Al Masa Mall, and Adidas brand stores at Muscat City Centre, Qurum City Centre and Muscat Grand Mall.

Who we supply

  • Ministries
  • Associations
  • Sports clubs
  • Hotels
  • Civil defence
  • Military
  • Companies

Besides our shops, our sales team works directly with government and private clients: ministries and associations, sports clubs and companies.

For gyms in hotels, civil defence, the military and clubs, we have designed the layouts, supplied the equipment and carried out the maintenance ever since installation.
