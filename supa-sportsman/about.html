About us

A Muscat sports company since 1975, trading only in sports goods and equipment: from our own showroom in Ruwi to gyms for hotels, clubs, civil defence and the military.

1975

A sports shop in Muscat

We started as the Sporting Goods Division of Sound General Trading Establishment, a small sports shop in Muscat.

1985

Supa Sportsman LLC

The division became an independent limited liability company under the name Supa Sportsman LLC, trading only in sports goods and equipment.

Brands

New to Oman, then household names

We brought many products into the Omani market that weren't available here before. Many of them are now household names in sports circles.

Adidas, Life Fitness, Babolat, Yonex, Mikasa, Paramount, Body-Solid, Concept2 Rowing, LeMond Bikes, Pavigym flooring, Sartor Trophies, Polar, 2XU and Nathan are marketed exclusively in Oman by us. We don't just collect agencies: every brand we represent gets real marketing support and after-sales service.

Ruwi

Our own building

Our head office in Ruwi is in a building owned by the owners of Supa Sportsman. It is also one of our main multi-brand showrooms, and many of our customers simply walk in.

Shops

Showrooms, brand stores and a sales team

We sell through outlets and brand stores owned by the company: the Ruwi showroom, a multi-brand outlet in Al Masa Mall, and three Adidas brand stores at Muscat City Centre, Qurum City Centre and Muscat Grand Mall.

Our sales team follows up with government and private clients, such as ministries and associations, sports clubs and companies.

Gyms

Layouts, equipment and maintenance

We have designed and finalised layouts and supplied equipment for gyms in hotels, civil defence, the military and clubs. We have carried out maintenance on that equipment continuously since installation, and our clients and customers have thought highly of us for it.
