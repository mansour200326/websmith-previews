12

Radiology

Digital X-ray, ultrasound, bone density and mammogram, read by our radiologist.

What we see

  • X-ray: bones, joints, chest and abdomen
  • Ultrasound: liver, kidneys, thyroid, pregnancy, veins and arteries
  • DEXA bone density: hip, spine, forearm
  • Screening and diagnostic mammogram

Before your visit

  • Most X-rays need no preparation; remove jewellery and metal
  • Tell us if you might be pregnant
  • DEXA: lie still on a padded table, no discomfort
  • Mammogram: about 15 minutes, yearly from age 40
