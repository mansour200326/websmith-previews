Our story

On 15 October 1994, Dr. Latifa Al-Duwaisan opened a private endocrinology clinic. Over ten years her patients came to trust her across Kuwait.

On 15 October 2004 she moved to a larger building, the clinic that is now Salmiya Clinic, and added nutrition, obstetrics and gynaecology, and dermatology and cosmetology.

In 2007 the clinic added dentistry, a laboratory, radiology, paediatrics, ENT, internal medicine and urology and andrology, so that a family could see most of the doctors they need at one address.

Endocrinology is still at the centre of the clinic. Today nine doctors work here, with our own lab, X-ray and the Jawharat Al Salmiya Pharmacy, open every day from 8am to 9pm.

What we hold ourselves to

With us, quality is not an option. It is a must.

From the clinic's mission

  1. Family spirit. Ethics and family spirit are our two core values. We want every patient to feel at home, as if with their own family.
  2. Right the first time. We aim to do things right from the first visit, so patients don't pay for tests and visits they didn't need.
  3. Evidence first. Our doctors work from current, evidence-based medicine, as one team with our nurses and front desk.

Humane care is more than healthcare.
