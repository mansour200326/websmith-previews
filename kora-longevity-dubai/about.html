AboutKORA

A beauty and longevity lounge on the ground floor of Aswaaq Mart, Al Wasl Road. On Instagram we're KORA, Conscious Beauty & Longevity. Before that, we were Privèe Dubai.

The key to longevity

KORA is more than just aesthetics – we endeavor to take care of you in a way that is in harmony with your body, safely and effectively.

From organic facials to non-toxic manicures, every treatment is designed with your long-term wellness in mind.

Next to the usual salon menu (nails, hair, lashes and brows, waxing, make-up, facials and massage) there is a longevity corner: a full spectrum infrared sauna, photobiomodulation, a PEMF mat, vibration therapy and compression boots. See the longevity menu
