Services

Everything below is booked the same three ways: WhatsApp, a call to 058 642 9972, or our Fresha page, where each service shows its times and price.

Prices on Fresha run from AED 12 to AED 384. Open every day from 8am to 10pm at 21 14B Street, Al Quoz Industrial 3.

01 · Recovery

Red light and contrast therapy

Contrast therapy is hot and cold: the wooden sauna with its salt-brick wall, then the cold plunge tubs set among the palms right outside. Red light therapy is booked on its own, or put together with other services as a combo.

  • Red Light Therapy
  • Contrast Therapy sauna + cold plunge
  • Combo Service
