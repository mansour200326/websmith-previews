The space

A wellness studio in Al Quoz Industrial 3, at 21 14B Street. Yellow walls, black ceilings, turf on the floor and plants in every room.

A team of 9+ practitioners runs pilates and yoga, red light and contrast therapy, massage, hair, nails and beauty, every day from 8am to 10pm.

01

The lobby

You walk in to yellow walls and a big window printed with three words: Pause, Breathe, Reflect. There's a sofa on the turf and a lounge corner by the glass.

The lobby. The window lettering is printed to be read from the street.

02

Sauna and cold plunge

The sauna is pale wood with a wall of salt bricks. Step out and the cold plunge tubs are right there, among the palms. Together they make our contrast therapy.

03

Recovery room

A green plant wall, lounge chairs with footstools, and more turf underfoot.

04

The studio

Where yoga and pilates happen. Turf floor, tall plants in white pots and black curtains to close the room off.

05

Treatment room

For massage therapy and the signature massages. The table is made up with our own Hyphen towels.

06

The salon

Hair care, blowouts, nails, threading and waxing. Black chairs, tall mirrors and a wash basin, at the same address as everything else.
