The firm

The lawyer Khaled Al-Abduljaleel opened The Legal Center in 1986. The idea was a firm that covers every branch of the law, in Kuwait and abroad, with the same care for a single client as for a large company.

Today the firm carries the name of Omar M. Al-Omar & Partners and works from four floors on Ahmad Al-Jaber Street in Sharq. Lawyers, legal consultants, paralegals, administrative staff and a translation section all work under one roof.

Why we started

Our vision

When The Legal Center was founded in the mid-eighties, the aim was a legal practice that goes beyond the usual way of working, and beyond the usual limits of what a law firm does for its clients. A leading national firm, run to modern international professional standards.

Our mission

The founders didn't want to be just one more law office. They wanted a firm that serves every branch of the law, at home and abroad, with the same quality, and that uses every legal means to settle disputes quickly, without ever giving up on the rule of law.

How we are organised

All practice areas →

  • Local department

    All litigation in Kuwait. Each client has a qualified, experienced lawyer, backed by paralegal staff through every step before the courts.

  • Debt collection department

    Collection of debts and arrears, shares, profits, claims, compensation and insurance. More than a dozen staff, each on assigned files, supervised by a senior manager.

  • Execution and collection

    Our dedicated enforcement team of more than 50 staff: summons, travel bans, salary and car attachment, and the seizure of property and funds.

  • International department

    Advice and action for international clients in Kuwait and abroad: contracts, agreements and deeds, company formation, and opinions on Kuwaiti law and regulation.

  • Translation section

    Our own translators, so contracts, pleadings and documents move between Arabic and English without leaving the firm.
