The LegalCenter

The firm

A Kuwaiti law firm, started by the lawyer Khaled Al-Abduljaleel in 1986. Lawyers, legal consultants, paralegals and our own translation section work together under one roof.

Every client gets a lawyer who sits down with them, goes through the case and explains the options before anything is filed. The paperwork and court procedure are handled by our paralegal staff from start to finish.

For companies we also work outside Kuwait, through a network of independent business law firms in the major cities, so you get local counsel wherever the other side is.

Three departments, one file

01

Local department

Every case in the Kuwaiti courts runs through here. A lawyer is assigned to you, discusses the case with you and works through every legal angle before the first hearing. Qualified paralegals follow the procedure from filing to judgment.

02

Debt collection department

Debts and arrears, shares, profits, claims, compensation and insurance payouts. More than a dozen staff on this alone, each with assigned files, supervised by a senior manager.

03

International department

For international clients and companies, in Kuwait and abroad. We draft and review contracts, agreements and sale deeds for moveable and immoveable property, form companies, and advise on what Kuwaiti law and regulation require of both sides.
