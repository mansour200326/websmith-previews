The chiropractors

TJ

Dr. Terry Jacobs

B.Sc. Human Physiology, University of SaskatchewanWestern States, Portland, OregonRegina practice since 2008

Dr. Terry was raised on a Century Farm near Stoughton, where his endless theories on modern farming and unique methods of avoiding work got him fired. He was politely told to attend university and theorize there, and somehow ended up with a degree in Human Physiology, then chiropractic college at Western States in Portland.

He practised in La Ronge for 7 years, worked a year in Ireland, and has been in Regina since 2008. He's a member of the Canadian Chiropractic Association, the Chiropractors' Association of Saskatchewan, the Planetary Society, and the Sport Medicine and Science Council of Saskatchewan, and was inspired to coach soccer after watching Ted Lasso.

JK

Dr. Jason Kraft

Accepting a limited number of new patients

A long-time fixture of NW Regina, Dr. Kraft brings his knowledge and enthusiasm to Crossroads. He volunteers with his kids' sports teams and helps sponsor community school events, when he isn't teasing the staff.

He keeps his training current with courses for a wide range of conditions, but claims most of his health knowledge came from his mother: sit up straight, eat your vegetables, and go to bed on time!

CB

Dr. Charla Borowski

Western States Chiropractic College, 2004Accepting new patients

Dr. Charla has practised in Regina and in England. A mother herself, she takes a special interest in conditions that come with pregnancy and small humans, and treats patients from young to old with methods from traditional to modern.

We love seeing the little ones start treatment in their baby seats, and show up years later with sports mishaps.

DM

Dr. Dave Morton

20 years in private practice, TorontoCCA, CAS and Ontario Chiropractic Association

Dr. Dave joined us from Toronto, where he also worked with personal injury and disability ratings. That makes him invaluable, since both Drs. Kraft and Jacobs are quite accident-prone. Drop-table techniques are his main treatment method.

He enjoys camping, travelling and motorcycling, and has embraced Saskatchewan winters.

KG

Dr. Karisa Groff

B.Kin., University of ReginaD.C., Palmer West, San Jose, Summa Cum LaudeMondays, Wednesdays and Fridays

Born and raised in Regina. After several close calls and broken shower curtain rods, her family enrolled her in gymnastics, and she wound up on the elite circuit. Before they could lose her to Cirque du Soleil, she went to university.

She combines adjustments with muscle release and at-home rehab programs. CCMI Concussion Management certification helps her keep treating athletes, and ART, Activator and Graston give her other options alongside traditional chiropractic. Elite or beginner, young or elderly.

AG

Dr. Aarti Goyal

B.Kin., University of ReginaD.C., Northwestern Health Sciences University, 2019Away November 3 to December 7

Born in Winnipeg, Dr. Aarti made the correct decision to grow up in Regina. She has worked with children, pregnant women, college and professional athletes and the elderly, and is a member of the International Chiropractic Pediatric Association. She also holds Activator Methods (Basic) certification.

She plays the dhol, a north Indian drum, and has performed on stage with well-known international artists. We'll beat our own drum in saying she's a great addition.

The Sensational Soft-Tissue Squad

RM

Rose Melnychuk RMT

Western College of Remedial Massage Therapy, 2014 · MTASRCMP, SGI, WCB and prenatal patients

Rose knew she wanted to be a therapist years ago, but life took a short detour with marriage and two strapping boys. As long as she's making people feel better and getting them back to function, that's all she needs.

She enjoys pregnancy treatments, deep tissue, hot stone and cupping, has taken a doula course and a course in caring for cancer patients, and goes to Rider games with her buddies.

MB

Mary Boys RMT

With Crossroads since 2016 · MTASSGI, WCB and RCMP patients

Mary moved from spa relaxation work to deep tissue therapy and loves seeing her patients improve on a regular treatment plan. She uses cupping in her sessions if you let us know ahead of time.

Mary speaks Labrador and Terrier, understands but does not speak Rottweiler or Weimaraner, and is brushing up on her baby lingo.

AL

Anya Le Cren M.P.H., RMT

19 years in Europe and North America · MTASPolish and English

Raised in Busko-Zdrój, Poland, Anya holds a bachelor's and master's in Public Health, and has worked in spas and medical clinics: deep tissue, relaxation, hot stone and aromatherapy massage. She has been at Crossroads since September 2016.

She cooks traditional Polish dishes and knows several ways to pronounce bratwurst.

TP

Tasos Panagiotopoulos RMT

Western College of Remedial Massage, 2019 · MTASSGI, WCB and RCMP patients

Although Tasos has a hard name to pronounce, he is very easy-going. Soft tissue work is his passion, and he's studying toward Sports Massage Therapist certification, Trigenics, and the McGill Method for difficult back pain.

Out of the clinic: aviation, motorcycles, technology, and cheering for the wrong team.

JP

Jaime Parrott RMT

Graduated 2010 · MTAS member since 2010Welcomes new patients

Jaime worked on the east side of Regina for several years before bringing her soft tissue talents up to sunny North West Regina. She sees RCMP and SGI patients and does prenatal massage.

Her son is really into fishing, so she has been seen standing in cold rivers up to her armpits. On her own time: technical hiking, snowshoeing, trail running, skiing and rock climbing.

DS

Dawn Schroeder RMT

WCRMT, 2018Jade stone massage · upper body Rapid Neurofascial Reset

Dawn changed careers in 2016 and wanted a clinical setting doing restorative work, not the relaxation vibe. After much badgering from one of the owners, she moved her practice to Crossroads.

Ask her about fantasy football (don't bet against her). Don't ask about her golden doodle, Wrigley, or you will regret it.

SA

Stephen Abrametz RMT

B.Kin., University of Regina, 2016 · MTASBowen therapy

Stephen grew up in the Parkland forests dreaming of being a boxer. Kinesiology taught him how people move; massage therapy turned out to be the best way to help them, since it was too late to go pro.

He works with Swedish massage, trigger point, deep tissue, RAPID Neurofascial Reset and soft tissue release, and has finished his Bowen therapy training.

AR

Alex Roy RMT

Prairie College of Massage, honours · NHPCRCMP and prenatal patients

After a career in the automotive sector, Alex came to a crossroads: become the Patrick Swayze of Pilot Butte, or work in health care. Thankfully for Northwest Regina, he chose massage.

Outside the clinic he rides his skateboard and hangs out with his dog Duke.
