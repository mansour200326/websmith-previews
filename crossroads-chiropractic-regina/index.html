Before you book

Anything else, call the front desk at 306-205-7777 or email info@crossroadschiro.ca.

Do you bill my insurance directly?

We bill SGI, WCB, RCMP and Veterans Affairs directly. We don't direct-bill other benefit plans at this time, but our system can email you a PDF receipt to submit. Most major companies pay in 0 to 3 days if you use their app.

I was hurt in a car accident or at work. Can I come in?

Yes. Tell us it's an SGI or WCB claim when you book, and bring your claim number. For a new WCB claim, call us first to check we can take you.

Do you see babies, kids and pregnant patients?

Yes. Dr. Charla Borowski takes a special interest in pregnancy and young children, and Dr. Aarti Goyal works with pregnant patients and kids too. For prenatal massage, book with Rose, Jaime, Dawn, Anya or Alex, and note on the booking that you're prenatal so the right cushions are ready.

Is online booking instant?

It's a request. The front desk reviews it and approves it, you get a confirmation by email (or text, if you've opted in with the staff), then a reminder the day before. New massage patients: fill in the new patient forms first, or arrive 15 minutes early.

What if I can't make it?

Call at least 24 hours ahead, and leave a voicemail if no one picks up. A missed appointment without 24 hours' notice is billed at the full cost, because someone else could have had that time.

Do you offer physiotherapy or acupuncture?

No. We offer chiropractic care and registered massage therapy only.
