Chiropractic

Six chiropractors. Each one has their own style, so ask which methods your doctor uses.

Request an appointment →

Your first visit

Your chiropractor takes a detailed health history and examines you, paying particular attention to your spine, joints, muscles and nervous system. If you need X-rays, we refer you to the medical clinics next door. A valid Sask. Health card is needed for that coverage.

If chiropractic treatment is right for you, you'll leave with a plan built for your needs. If what you have should be treated, or co-treated, by another health professional, your chiropractor will refer you.

How we treat

  • Hands-on adjustmentsDiversified technique.
  • Drop-piece adjustmentsThompson technique. Dr. Morton's main method.
  • Hand-held instrumentActivator Methods. Dr. Groff and Dr. Goyal.
  • Muscle releaseIncluding Active Release Technique (ART) and Graston with Dr. Groff, plus at-home rehab programs.
  • Concussion careDr. Groff holds CCMI Concussion Management certification.

Follow-up visits

Your chiropractor may use one or more kinds of adjustment and manual care. Treatment often includes exercises, and advice on diet and healthy living.

Pregnancy, babies and kids

Dr. Charla Borowski takes a special interest in pregnancy and young children, and sees patients from babies up. Dr. Aarti Goyal is a member of the International Chiropractic Pediatric Association and works with pregnant patients, children and athletes.

Claims, payment and policies

The small print, in plain words.

SGI, WCB, RCMP and Veterans Affairs

We bill these directly. If it's a car accident or a work injury, tell us when you book (it's important we know at the time of booking) and bring your claim number. For a new WCB claim, call first to check we can take you.

Other insurance

We don't direct-bill other benefit companies at this time. Our system can email you a PDF receipt to submit, and most major companies pay in 0 to 3 days if you use their app.

Paying

Cash, debit, e-transfer, Visa and Mastercard.

Booking online

Online booking is a request, not a live booking. The front desk reviews and approves it. You get an email (or a text, if you opt in with the staff) when it's received, another when it's confirmed, and a reminder, usually the day before. Not seeing our emails? Check your junk folder.

Cancellations and no-shows

When you book, that time is set aside for you and only you. We never double book. If you can't make it, call at least 24 hours ahead, or leave a voicemail. A missed appointment without 24 hours' notice is billed at the full cost. We know sudden illness and family emergencies happen; your practitioner may make an exception.

What we don't do

We don't offer physiotherapy or acupuncture.
