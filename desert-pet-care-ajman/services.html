Services.

Five services from our shop in Al Yasmeen, Ajman.

01

Boarding for cats and dogs.

When you travel, they stay with us. The cats have their own spaces: the Villas, and the cat room with Ghaf wood climbing and turf. The dogs have a room of their own.

Choose the space that suits your pet's comfort and personality, and we'll keep you posted every day.

  • Separate spaces for cats and dogs
  • Photos, videos and messages every day of the stay
  • Clean, sanitised rooms
  • For Eid and school holidays, book early: spaces are limited

Grooming for dogs, cats and rabbits.

Send us a photo and the breed on WhatsApp. We'll tell you what the coat needs and the price before you come in.

Shampoos and grooming products for between visits are on our shelves too.

  • Dogs, cats, and rabbits too
  • Book your time on WhatsApp or by phone
  • Shampoos to take home

Pet day care.

For the days you're at work or out: your dog or cat spends the day with us, playing on the turf and resting, and you collect them later.

Tell us the days and times on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.

  • Cats and dogs
  • Indoor turf and play rooms
  • Ask about days and times

Dog walking and pet sitting.

Some pets are happier at home. We walk dogs and look after pets while you're busy or away.
