Started for Coco and Bella.

Desert Pet Care is a small pet place in Al Yasmeen, Ajman, opposite Rahmaniya and Sustainable City. We look after cats and dogs: they stay with us, spend the day with us, get groomed here and go home with food and litter from our shelves.

The place was inspired by our own two, Coco and Bella, so it's built the way we'd want it for them: comfortable, eco-friendly and close to nature.

Ghaf woodThe cat play areas are custom-made from the wood of the Ghaf, the UAE's desert tree.
