About the school

Nibras Al Iman American Private School, NIPS for short, is an American-curriculum school in Al Azra, Sharjah. We combine strong academic foundations with new ways of teaching, and we care about the whole child: learning, well-being, social skills and global citizenship.

A message from the Principal

“We welcome each student and ensure that he or she receives a high quality of education.”

Nibras Al Iman Private School is an American-based curriculum school where a unique educational environment exists. We have committed ourselves to raising achievement, raising aspirations and enriching partnerships, from developing teaching and learning to the extra-curricular opportunities.

There is no doubt that the achievement of our students is built upon the partnership between students, parents and staff. We create success through the quality of teaching and learning, the student support and commitment to extra-curricular activities.

The school prides itself on its TIMSS results, where our school achieved the sixth position in Sharjah and students scored higher than the world average in 2019 in math and science. We pride ourselves as well on the Cognia accreditation, with an excellent rate, accrediting the school for five years.

Although we value academic performance in core subjects, we deeply focus on Quran teaching for all students starting from KG. We foster values of courtesy, consideration and individual responsibility, and offer a high level of challenge accompanied by equally high levels of support.

I firmly believe that a strongly led school is strongly led by many. With the excellent staff team we have here, we have the ability to ensure that your child achieves the very best.

Ms. Sara Tarek AbdelfattahThe School Principal

Vision, mission and values

Innovative education for a knowledge, pioneering and global society.

Develop an innovative education system for a knowledge and globally competitive society, that includes all age groups and meets future labour market demand, by ensuring the quality of the Ministry of Education's outputs and providing the best services for internal and external customers.

  1. Citizenship and responsibilitiesStrengthening national identity and social responsibility.
  2. Principles and values of IslamDialogue, tolerance, moderation, peace and volunteering.
  3. Commitment and transparencyProfessionalism and transparency in performance.
  4. Contribution and accountabilityPartnering with society in education, accountable for students' performance.
  5. Right to education for allEqual opportunities for everyone in society.
  6. Quality and innovationPreparing people who contribute to sustainable development and compete globally.
