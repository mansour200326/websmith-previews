Learning & admissions

Kindergarten, elementary and a girls' school up to Grade 12, all on the American curriculum, with the Quran taught to every child from KG. Below: what each stage looks like, and how to register.

Kindergarten

Pre-KG, KG1, KG2 · boys and girls · ages 3 to 5

The youngest children start in Pre-KG at three. Kindergarten is where Quran lessons begin, and where children go on their first school trips. KG2 ends with a graduation in caps and gowns: our latest was the 13th graduating class of KG2.

  • Quran lessons from KG
  • KG trip to Sharjah Aquarium
  • KG2 graduation ceremony

Elementary, Grades 1 to 4

Boys and girls · ages 6 to 9

Boys and girls learn together through Grade 4. Our curriculum balances strong academic foundations with creativity, critical thinking and character, and we take learning out of the classroom too.

The girls' school, Grades 5 to 12

Girls only · Grade 5 to Grade 12

From Grade 5 Nibras is a girls' school. Our students sat the international TIMSS study in 2019, placed the school sixth in Sharjah and scored above the world average in maths and science.

Admissions

  1. 1

    Ask for a place

    Call 06 562 7288 or WhatsApp 050 506 7954 with your child's age and the grade you need.

  2. 2

    The assessment

    If a place is available, the school contacts you and arranges an appointment for your child to sit an assessment.

  3. 3

    Registration

    If your child passes, you complete the Application of Admissions (parent or guardian only) and visit the accounts department to arrange the fees.

Documents to bring

  • 6 recent passport-size photographs
  • A copy of your child's passport (with UAE entry stamp or valid residence visa if non-Emirati)
  • A copy of the father's or guardian's passport (with UAE entry stamp or valid residence visa if non-Emirati)
  • A certified copy of your child's last school report, with the school stamp
  • Birth certificate
  • A copy of the inoculation record
  • A copy of the child's and the father's or guardian's Emirates ID
  • School transfer letter
