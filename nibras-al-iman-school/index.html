The Quran, for every child, from KG

We teach the American curriculum, and alongside it every student learns the Quran, starting in Kindergarten. The school runs a Holy Quran competition, with a ceremony to honour the children who take part.

How to join

Three steps, from the first call to your child's first day.

  1. 1

    Ask for a place

    Call the office or send us a WhatsApp with your child's age and the grade you need.

  2. 2

    The assessment

    If a place is available, the school contacts you and books a time for your child to sit an assessment.

  3. 3

    Registration

    If your child passes, you come back to fill in the Application of Admissions (parent or guardian only) and arrange the fees with our accounts department.
