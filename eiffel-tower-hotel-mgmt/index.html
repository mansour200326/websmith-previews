03 · Causeway RestaurantKing Fahad Causeway

A restaurant in the middle of the sea.

We run the first-class restaurant on the causeway that connects Bahrain and Saudi Arabia. Thousands of visitors come every year, most of all in the festival seasons. It is quiet and away from town, so it also works well for business meetings.

From the top of the tower you can see the Arabian Sea and the border of Bahrain and Saudi Arabia.

04 · Gulf Toursالخليج للسياحة

Around Bahrain by coach, by boat, and out to the islands.

Gulf Tours is one of the leading tour operators in Bahrain. We run trips all around the country and its waters, out to islands like Jeradah and Hawar.

  1. 01

    Land Tour

    Luxury coaches to the archaeological sites of the Kingdom.
