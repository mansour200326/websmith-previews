Ezra Isaacs

Hairdresser since 1976. Owner of Ezra Coiffure and Ezra Nails & Beauty in Ottery, stage artist, competition judge, and the long hair specialist.

Book with the salon: 021 704 1162 →

In Ezra's words

01

Setting the stage

My career started in 1976 as an apprentice hairdresser with Salon Emile in Cape Town, where I qualified and stayed until 1986. Then I trained and worked as a beauty consultant until 1991, with leading cosmetic brands.

In 1991 I went back to my first love and bought Way-Ahead salon in Lotus River. In 1994 I sold it and opened Coiffure Ezra in Ottery. In 2011 I opened Ezra Nails & Beauty in the premises next door.

02

The paperwork

My training includes the Gents, Ladies and Afro hairdressing trade tests and the Cape Master Hairdressers Association's Master Certificate.

I keep up to date with courses, diplomas and seminars with Goldwell, Schwarzkopf (Bronze, Silver and Gold), Paul Mitchell, Joico, Wella, L'Oreal, Revlon and Redken, and I've travelled to OMC Hairworld in London, Russia, Chicago and Paris.

03

Curtain up

Since 1996 I have presented live hair shows for Wella, GHD, Twincare, Hands Down and Goldwell at major centres around South Africa, and for Intercoiffure Australia at their Golden Jubilee in Melbourne.

My work has been published in South African Hairdressing journal, Estetica (including their first Afro front cover), Image Magazine, Glamour, Marie Claire and Fair Lady.

04

Production values

When you reach a certain level, you pay back to your industry. I sit on industry committees and bargaining councils, and I've been an assessor for trade tests and colleges since 2000 (Services SETA).

I am Vice President (South Africa) of Intercoiffure Interbeaute, Paris, and Fashion Director for the Western Cape. I have judged Western Province and national competitions since 2002.
