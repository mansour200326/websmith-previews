About Ezra

“Whereas my hairdressing expertise is broad-based, I have built a reputation as The Long Hair Specialist.”

Ezra Isaacs started as an apprentice at Salon Emile in Cape Town in 1976 and opened this salon in Ottery in 1994. Nine qualified hairstylists work here with him.

On stage and in competition

Since 1996 Ezra has presented live hair shows for Wella, GHD, Twincare, Hands Down and Goldwell at major centres around South Africa, and for Intercoiffure at their Golden Jubilee in Melbourne.
