Since 1993

Al Diyar General Contracting Company L.L.C. started its construction business in Abu Dhabi in 1993 and holds the 1st class contractor category.

Under the guidance of our chairman, Mr. Hamad Al Junaibi, and the teamwork of Mr. Kamil Sarieddine, Mr. Nadim Sarieddine, Mr. Najib Sarieddine and Eng. Naji Darwish, the company earned its classification in the 1st class category.

Since the first day we have built multi-storey commercial buildings, office buildings, palaces, luxury villas, groups of commercial and residential villas and military projects, under the supervision of qualified, experienced staff and skilled labourers.

We work to international standards of practice, and we aim to serve quickly and efficiently so our clients keep their trust in us, project after project.
