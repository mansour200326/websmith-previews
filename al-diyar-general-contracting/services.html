Services

Al Diyar works in two divisions: building contracting, and general maintenance. Our civil engineers, electrical engineers, quantity surveyors and quality engineers stay on the job from the first block to the annual maintenance visit.

  1. 01

    Building construction

    Multi-storey commercial buildings, office buildings, palaces, luxury villas, groups of commercial and residential villas, and military projects, completed on time.

    • Commercial towers and office buildings
    • Residential and furnished buildings
    • Villas, villa groups and palaces
    • Military and government projects
    • Civil engineering works
    Call about a building project →
  2. 02

    Civil works

    The trades that turn a structure into a finished building, done by our own skilled labour.

    • Block work and plastering
    • Painting works
    • Interlock and tiling works
    • Design for special types of flooring
    • Crack repair with special chemicals
    Email us about civil works →
  3. 03

    Electrical & plumbing

    Supply and installation of modern wiring and electrical equipment to the authority standard, for new buildings and existing ones.

    • Repairing the existing facility
    • Upgrading existing power
    • New electricity and water connections
    • Installing and repairing all plumbing accessories
    Call about electrical or plumbing work →
  4. 04

    Maintenance contracts

    Our general maintenance division looks after commercial and residential buildings and villas once they are handed over.

    • AC maintenance: gas filling, repairing and cleaning
    • Package units, from buying the units to installation and maintenance
    • Household electrical work
    • Annual maintenance contracts for buildings and villas
    Ask for a maintenance contract →
  5. 05

    Marble, stone & interiors

    Supporting divisions inside the group: a marble and stone factory and an interior decoration division, with our sister company Al Diyar Marble Factory.

    • Marble and stone supply
    • Interior decoration
    • Sister companies: Sarieddine Trading, Al Diyar Marble Factory
    Email us about stone or interiors →
  6. QA

    Quality and safety on site

    Technology, quality assurance and customer service are what have kept our major projects division going. Every employee knows their health and safety responsibilities, and anyone can report an unsafe condition to their supervisor without fear of being disciplined.

    • ISO 9001:2008 quality management
    • ISO 14001:2004 environmental management
    • OHSAS 18001:2007 health & safety
    See the certificates →
