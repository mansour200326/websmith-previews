Services

Al Diyar works in two divisions: building contracting, and general maintenance. Our civil engineers, electrical engineers, quantity surveyors and quality engineers stay on the job from the first block to the annual maintenance visit.

Building construction

Multi-storey commercial buildings, office buildings, palaces, luxury villas, groups of commercial and residential villas, and military projects, completed on time and to the drawings.

  • Commercial towers and office buildings
  • Residential and furnished buildings
  • Villas, villa groups and palaces
  • Military and government projects
  • Civil engineering works
