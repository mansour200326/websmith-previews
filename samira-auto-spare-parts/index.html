Say no to counterfeit parts

Everything on our shelves is the maker's own part, in the maker's own box.

A copy filter or brake part can look the same on the counter. It does not last the same on the road. Since 1980 we have only dealt in genuine parts, and our customers keep coming back for that, from across the UAE and from abroad.
