How to ask for a part

  1. Find your chassis number

    It is the 17-character VIN on your registration card (mulkiya). It tells the counter exactly which model and year you drive.

  2. Say what you need

    The part name, or the number on the old part if you have it. A photo of the old part helps too.

  3. Call or email the nearest branch

    Use the part request slip on the home page, or call any of the four counters directly.
