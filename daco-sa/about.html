Who we are

Dar Alkawashif Co. (DACO), شركة دار الكواشف, was founded in 1996. It started in Riyadh, concentrating on fire fighting systems, and now covers projects all over the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

DACO is a slim organisation that thinks globally but acts locally. Our agility is what impresses our clients: how we handle their requests and how quickly we respond.

Our office is in Al Malaz, near Al Ahsa Street, Riyadh.

What we do

  • Fire detection and protection supplies and installations
  • Power generation, transmission and distribution overhauls and supplies
  • Construction and property development
  • Engineering consultation for each of these industries
  • Management consultation and NFPA training
