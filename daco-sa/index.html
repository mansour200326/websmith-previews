About DACO

DACO started in Riyadh with fire fighting systems, beginning with the maintenance of fire equipment. Step by step it grew into a company that designs, supplies and installs complete fire fighting and alarm systems, with projects all over the Kingdom.

We are a slim organisation. We think globally and act locally, and our clients notice how quickly we answer and act.

A fire system, start to finish

One company from the first drawing to the day the system is handed over and running.

01

Design

We have designed fire protection and fire fighting systems around the Kingdom. Our system designers from the USA are NFPA certified, so large projects can be reviewed or designed to NFPA standards.

  • Hydraulic calculations, unlimited nodes
  • Design to GCC Fire Code, IFC 2009, IBC 2009 or NFPA
GCC Fire CodeNFPAIFC 2009IBC 2009
02

Civil Defense approval

DACO is certified by the Saudi Civil Defense and can get your plans approved to the GCC Fire Code. Our subsidiary TACO is licensed by the Saudi Civil Defense to approve fire plans.

03

Supply & install

We supply and install the full range of fire fighting and alarm systems.

  • HFC-227 (FM200) clean-agent systems
  • Water sprinkler systems
  • Foam systems
  • Smart fire detection systems
04

Commissioning & maintenance

We commission what we install and hand the system over working. DACO itself began by maintaining fire equipment, so we know these systems after handover too.
