01

Fire & safety

DACO began with fire equipment maintenance and grew step by step from there. Today we offer the complete range of services and supplies for fire fighting and fire alarm systems.

We have designed many fire protection and fire fighting systems around the Kingdom. Our system designers from the USA are NFPA certified, so large projects in Saudi can have their designs reviewed or made to NFPA standards. We are certified by the Saudi Civil Defense and can get your plans approved to the GCC Fire Code.

  • Hydraulic calculations, unlimited nodes

  • Sales, installation and commissioning

02

Power generation, transmission & distribution

DACO has long experience in power generation projects. We have worked as a subcontractor to SEC, SWCC and utilities for more than a decade, and taken on key reinforcement and replacement projects as a subcontractor to the National Grid company in Saudi Arabia.

Our work covers turbine and generator services, overhauls and parts supply. We service and supply parts for most of the gas turbine fleet in Saudi, sell refurbished turbines to other countries, and our partners in Europe deliver reverse engineering solutions.

Construction & property development

Our construction work starts with the plan study and project set-up and runs to turn-key handover. We build for private owners and for business real-estate developers, and DACO shares ownership in some of the projects.

04

Engineering consultation

DACO acts as a consultant on a number of projects through its subsidiary, TACO. TACO is a licensed firm for approving fire plans with the Saudi Civil Defense.

TACO has given property owners sound technical advice on serious structural problems in their buildings, and can advise on settlements between contractors and owners.

  • Fire protection and detection
  • Construction: plumbing, wiring, structural and insulation issues
  • Power generation projects, in joint venture with German firms
