Who we are

We began with around fifteen people doing general contracting. Now we run our own trucks, plant, materials store and camp as well. From the first excavation to the last kerb stone.

1989

First established in Abu Dhabi, with general contracting as the main focus. The materials store followed in 2007.

2004

Discovery General Transporting Est. founded in Musaffah: our own tippers, low loaders, tankers and earth-moving plant.

1,000+

Employees today, management and workers, with a branch office in Dubai. Engineers and supervisors run every site.
