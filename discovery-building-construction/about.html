About the group

Founded in Abu Dhabi in 1989, with general contracting as the main focus. From about fifteen people to over a thousand, still run from Musaffah.

Our story

Our first work was mostly infrastructure. Over the years we added transport, excavation, building materials, prefab houses and offices, labour camp management, interior décor, facilities management, real estate, gas and oil field services and equipment rental.

We grew by being straight with clients and by the people who work here. The company went from an initial staff of around 15 to more than 1,000 employees today, management and workers, with a branch office in Dubai and qualified engineers and supervisors on every project.

The group stays loyal to its customers by adapting to what each one needs, and by taking on new kinds of work when they ask for it.

How we work

Quality

"To seek performance excellence and always sustain our customers' trust, respect and satisfaction."

  1. Finish on time, within budget and to the requirements.
  2. Use modern construction techniques and the best materials.
  3. Keep training our staff and upgrading their knowledge.
  4. Build good relationships with suppliers and subcontractors.
  5. Keep improving our quality system and objectives.

Health, safety & environment

Every incident that injures people, damages machines or harms the environment can be prevented with the right precautions.

  1. A safe system of work and a healthy workplace for every employee.
  2. A formal risk assessment for every activity.
  3. HSE is line management's job; HSE staff help every site meet it.
  4. Local HSE law and contract terms are always followed.
  5. Our HSE management system follows OHSAS 18001:2007.
