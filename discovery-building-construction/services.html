01 · Discovery General Contracting LLC

Building contracting

High-rise residential and commercial buildings, villas and palaces, industrial sheds, warehouses and factory buildings, and labour camps. We take the job from foundations and excavation up to slab on grade, through the structure, to the interior fit-out.

  • Buildings: G+M+7+Roof towers at MBZ City for Mr. Bakheet and Mr. Hamdan Al Mazrouei; G+G+4 Traffic & Patrol Police Station, Tarif; Emirates ID Card buildings with JC Maclean in Barsha, Ajman, UAQ and RAK; Nakheel sales office with KCPC, Palm Jumeirah; Jebel Ali Airport building (structure).
  • Villas & palaces: 5 villas for Mr. Ahmed Mohd Mhanna, 3 for Mr. Ali Ahmed Marzooki and 3 for Mr. Ahmed Nasib, all MBZ City; Majlis for Al Maquam Palace, Al Ain.
  • Warehouses & factories: Wet Wipes office, Dubai; factory buildings, Jebel Ali Free Zone; warehouses in Musaffah for Mr. Ahmed Yousuf Al Hosani; Discovery Group offices, Musaffah.

02 · Discovery General Contracting LLC

Road contracting

Roads and infrastructure are the core of our contracting work. We have the machines, equipment and people for any range of it: new roads, interlock paving, kerb stones, guard rails and every type of chain-link fence.

  • Expansion of Ruwais Refinery project, Ruwais
  • Footpath paving, Abu Dhabi city
  • Road railing works, Western Region
  • Frisco Ridge road works
  • Airport security fencing at Abyad Island, and at Al Qseewarth, Al Ain
  • Security fencing, Al Maktoum International Airport, Dubai
  • Road construction in Mohammed Bin Zayed City
  • Site perimeter fencing, Sahara Kingdom, Dubai

06 · Discovery General Utility Services LLC · Ruwais

Catering & camp management

We run our own accommodation for 6,000 people in the Ruwais area, in Senior, Junior and Regular categories, for companies working with the ADNOC group. Rooms range from VIP to Regular, and the kitchen cooks for the different nationalities on site.

We can also take over the catering, housekeeping, camp management and laundry for your own camp.

  • Camp clients include Larsen & Toubro, Kaefer, TUV Middle East, Cape East, Alpine Bau Deutschland, Deutsche Babcock, Velosi, Kettaneh Construction, Gulf Contracting Company, Amana Steel Buildings, Exterran Belleli, Al Asab General Contracting and more.

Prefab & camp construction
