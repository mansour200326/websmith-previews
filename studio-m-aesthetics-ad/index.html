Your first visit

What happens when you come to the villa for the first time.

01

Park and come in

Studio M is Villa 4 in Compound 181 on Al Tashreef Street, with parking available. There is a lounge to wait in when you arrive.

02

Face mapping first

A free 15-minute skin analysis. Mandy looks at your skin, asks what bothers you, and recommends the treatments and products that suit it.

03

Your treatment

Facials, peels, Dermapen microneedling or Gold RF, in the treatment room. Most treatments take an hour to an hour and fifteen minutes.

04

Keep it going at home

Before you leave, ask about the products used in your treatment. A gua sha is AED 100, or AED 150 with the oil.
