About Studio M

We're a small, woman-owned beauty and wellness clinic in a villa on Al Tashreef Street, Khalifa City A. Your treatment is with Mandy.

In a villa in Khalifa City A

Studio M is Villa 4 in Compound 181 on Al Tashreef Street, with parking available. There's a lounge to wait in, and a treatment room with a facial bed, a treatment chair, an LED lamp and shelves of the products used in treatments.

It's a quiet, private place, and your comfort comes first.
