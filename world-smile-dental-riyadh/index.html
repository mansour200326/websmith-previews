Before you come in

  1. 1

    Book by phone or WhatsApp

    Call our unified number 920007882, or 050 075 4387, or send a WhatsApp to 058 111 1974. Tell us if it's for teeth or skin and which doctor you'd like.

    Already booked? Enter your mobile number on our appointment checker to see today's and upcoming visits.

    Our children's dentistry includes fissure sealants, fluoride and space maintainers, and nitrous-oxide sedation (SR 400 per visit) for children who find the chair hard.
