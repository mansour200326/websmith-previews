A proper British salon in Cluster V, with a proper cup of tea.

Our British stylists specialise in blonde and dark hair, colour and extensions. Next to them sits our skilled Filipino and Sri Lankan team, doing nails, lashes and brows properly.

It's a warm, relaxed, home-from-home kind of place: friendly faces, the kettle on, and nobody rushing you out of the chair.
