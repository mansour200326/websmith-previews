The salon

The British Salon JLT is a British-owned, woman-owned salon in Cluster V. Hair, nails and beauty under one roof, with a proper cup of tea and a relaxed, home-from-home feel.

British stylists

Our British hair stylists specialise in blonde and dark hair, colour and extensions. Tell them about the last bad experience; they're good listeners.

  • Nails, lashes, brows

    Our skilled Filipino and Sri Lankan team look after nails, lash extensions and lifts, brow lamination, waxing and threading.

  • The kettle's on

    Friendly faces, a proper cuppa and nobody rushing you. Parking is available and public transport is close by.
