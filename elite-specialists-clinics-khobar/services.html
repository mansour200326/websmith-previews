Orthopaedic clinics

Spine clinic

Fractures, slipped discs and curvature of the spine.

  • Recent and old fractures of the spine
  • Osteoporotic fractures of the vertebrae, treated by injection through a needle, without surgery
  • Curvature of the spine (scoliosis), corrected surgically
  • Slipped discs anywhere in the spine, including needle treatment under local anaesthetic, without surgery
  • Spinal inflammation, with medicine or surgery

Doctors in this field: Prof. Abdullah Al-Othman, Dr Sulaiman Al-Mousa

Book this clinic 013 894 0004

Sports medicine clinic

Knee ligaments, torn cartilage, shoulder and ankle injuries, mostly by arthroscopy.

  • Sports injuries affecting the joints
  • Torn anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments (ACL, PCL)
  • Torn inner and outer meniscus
  • Torn ligaments around the hip
  • Torn shoulder ligaments and dislocated shoulder, by arthroscopy
  • Ankle ligaments and worn ankle cartilage, by arthroscopy
  • Tennis and golfer's elbow

Doctors in this field: Prof. Abdullah Al-Othman, Dr Sulaiman Al-Mousa

Book this clinic 013 894 0004

Osteoporosis clinic

Diagnosing and treating thinning bones, in older and middle-aged men and women.

  • Accurate diagnosis of osteoporosis in older men and women
  • Evidence-based treatment of all kinds of bone thinning
  • Finding the cause in middle-aged patients, and treating it
  • Fractures caused by osteoporosis, including vertebral fractures treated by injection without surgery

Doctors in this field: Prof. Abdullah Al-Othman, Dr Sulaiman Al-Mousa

Book this clinic 013 894 0004

Children's orthopaedics

Hip dysplasia, club foot, short limbs and children's fractures.

  • Hip dysplasia from birth (developmental dislocation of the hip)
  • Club foot, treated with the Ponseti method without surgery
  • Surgery for difficult congenital foot deformities
  • Limb lengthening for a shorter arm or leg
  • Children's fractures, with modern fixation
  • Bone infections and rickets in children
  • Congenital limb deformities, using the Ilizarov method

Doctors in this field: Prof. Abdullah Al-Othman, Dr Sulaiman Al-Mousa

Book this clinic 013 894 0004

Joint wear clinic

Knee, hip and ankle: from injections and arthroscopy to cartilage grafts.

  • Arthritis treated with medicine
  • Modern joint injections
  • Worn joints treated by arthroscopy
  • Worn joints treated with cartilage grafting
  • Correcting and strengthening misaligned joints
  • Joint fixation when it's needed

Doctors in this field: Prof. Abdullah Al-Othman, Dr Sulaiman Al-Mousa

  • Stimulating ovulation when it is weak
  • Artificial insemination (IUI)
  • IVF and ICSI
