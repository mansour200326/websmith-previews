Video game software and hardware supplier, trading out of Deira since 1984.

In the gaming business since 1984. From 1994 to 1999 the company was the sole distributor for SNK Corporation — now known as Playmore — supplying SNK games across Asia. Branches followed in the UAE in 1999 and in Saudi Arabia in 2010. Today 3G Electronics supplies video game software and hardware to a wide range of customers across the Middle East.
