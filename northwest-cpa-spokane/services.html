Tax strategy, planning & preparation

We handle individual and business taxes, with special depth in business and real estate. Someone who doesn't know your industry can easily miss the deductions that matter. We know exactly what to look for.

For businesses we start with your entity type, how the IRS treats it, how it affects your personal taxes, and the pitfalls to avoid. We compare C-corp, S-corp and sole proprietorship side by side, so you know which is the most tax-efficient for you, and we review recent tax law changes and new strategies with you.

  • Business tax preparation and filing
  • Planning ahead to lower your tax bill
  • Entity selection and structure
  • Real estate and investment tax strategy
  • Credits, deductions and incentives
  • Rental properties and Schedule K-1s
  • RSUs, ESPPs and incentive stock options
  • 1031 exchanges and real estate professional status
  • QBI deductions and AMT
  • Multistate returns
