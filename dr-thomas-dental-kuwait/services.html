Crowns and bridges

A crown is a cap made over a tooth, especially one that has had root canal treatment, so it doesn't fracture. A bridge replaces missing teeth by fixing onto the teeth next to the gap.

This is Dr. Thomas's speciality. We talk through the options with you before deciding, including implant-supported work where it suits you better.

  • MaterialsZirconium, ceramic, E.max, In-Ceram or white gold
  • FitColour and shape matched to your own teeth, bite checked
  • CareWe show you how to look after them so they last

Smile designing and veneers

When teeth are out of place or darker in colour, we design the smile and cover the teeth with thin veneers so they sit in harmony. Hollywood smile, laminates and Lumineers are different names for the same idea.

Dr. Thomas has been doing veneers for 15 years. You see a mock-up or temporary veneers first, so you can try the new smile before the final ones are made.

  • OptionsTraditional, minimal-prep or no-prep veneers
  • MaterialsPorcelain or E.max
  • FirstA full smile analysis: face, colour, shape and alignment
03

Root canal treatment

When decay reaches the nerve inside the tooth, a filling is no longer enough. We remove the infected part, clean and disinfect the canals, then fill and seal them.

With modern anaesthesia it is usually no more uncomfortable than a filling. We use an endo motor, which makes the treatment more precise and quicker.

Ask about this on WhatsApp →or call 6997 5041

  • VisitsUsually one or two, 30 minutes to an hour each
  • AfterSome tenderness is normal; avoid chewing on it until it is restored
  • ThenMost back teeth need a crown to protect them
04

Removable dentures

When all the teeth are missing, a full denture is the economical answer. When some teeth are still there, a partial denture replaces the missing ones. Cast partial dentures in Cr-Co alloy are the stronger, longer-lasting option.

Dentures are part of Dr. Thomas's speciality. We do a wax trial first, so you can see how you will look before the denture is made.

Ask about this on WhatsApp →or call 6997 5041

  • TypesFull, partial, cast Cr-Co, implant-supported overdentures
  • TrialWax try-in before the final denture
  • InsuranceWe help with certain insurance claims

Extractions and wisdom teeth

10

Crown lengthening, post and core

Post and core: when a tooth is broken or decayed but its roots are healthy, a post goes into the root canal and a core is built on it to carry a new crown.

Crown lengthening: when a tooth needs a little more height for a crown to fit, the gum (and sometimes bone) around it is reshaped.

Teeth whitening

Professional whitening is safe when it is supervised and used as directed. It works on natural teeth only: crowns and veneers keep their colour, so we plan it with you if you have them.
