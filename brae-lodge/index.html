Find us

Free parking for up to 5 cars

By bus from the city centre

Take Lothian bus 37 or 7. Both come straight to us with no change, in about 15 to 20 minutes. From Princes Street the bus goes over North Bridge and South Bridge, then out through Newington and Craigmillar Park.

After the stops on Liberton Road you cross the lights onto Liberton Brae. Get off at the next stop, Orchardhead Road. If in doubt, ask the driver for the first stop after Liberton Road. We're about 100 metres away, on the opposite side of the road.

From Waverley Station

Take the Princes Street stairs exit and turn right, past the Balmoral Hotel. Cross North Bridge and walk up to South Bridge, where you'll find the stop for the 37 or 7.

From the airport

Take the Airlink 100 bus from stand A to its last stop on South St. David Street, or the tram to St Andrew Square. Walk back to Princes Street, turn right, and catch the 37 at the stop by Marks & Spencer.

By car

From the airport take the A8 to the Gogar roundabout, then the A720 city bypass towards Edinburgh South & East. Leave at Straiton and turn left onto the A701 for the city centre. Go straight through two sets of lights; we're about half a mile further on the left, just after a right-hand bend. Park free in front of the house.
