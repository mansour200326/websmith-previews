Treatments

Straightening teeth is what we started with. Everything else your family needs is done here too: implants, veneers, root canals, whitening and check-ups.

01Braces and InvisalignMetal braces or clear aligners, for teenagers and adults

Full braces usually go on once most adult teeth are in. Invisalign uses a series of clear trays made for your teeth; each one moves them a small step.

  • Planned by an orthodontist
  • Retainers and check-ups
  • Invisalign clear aligners
  • Braces for teenagers and adults

Ask about braces on WhatsApp →

02Implants and graftsReplacing missing teeth for good

An implant replaces a tooth at the root and can last many years. Where the bone or gum needs building up first, our periodontist and implant doctors do the grafting here.

  • Single and multiple implants
  • Bone and gum grafts
  • Impacted teeth
  • Implant crowns

Ask about implants on WhatsApp →

03Hollywood smile and veneersSmile design, crowns, veneers and bonding

We plan the whole smile first, then use what it needs: thin porcelain veneers matched to your colour, composite bonding, crowns, implants or whitening.

  • Porcelain veneers
  • Crowns and bridges
  • Composite bonding
  • Gum contouring

Ask about a smile makeover →

04Root canals and fillingsSaving the tooth you have

When the nerve inside a tooth is inflamed or infected, a root canal treatment removes it and keeps the tooth. Small cavities get a filling in a tooth-coloured material.

  • Root canal treatment
  • Tooth-coloured fillings
  • Porcelain restorations
  • Crowns after a root canal

Toothache? Message us →

05Whitening and laserLighter teeth, gentler gum treatment

Whitening lightens teeth by several shades. Laser treatment on the gums means less bleeding and swelling, and on cavities it keeps more of the healthy tooth.

  • In-clinic whitening
  • Laser gum treatment
  • Gum depigmentation
  • Laser cavity treatment

Ask about whitening →

06Check-ups for the familyCleaning, children's dentistry, gum care

Regular cleaning and check-ups for adults and children, with the focus on preventing problems rather than drilling them.

  • Scale and polish
  • Children's check-ups
  • Gum disease treatment
  • Dentures

Book a check-up →
