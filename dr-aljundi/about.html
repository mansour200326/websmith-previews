About the clinic

Since 2005 on Al Karama Street, Abu Dhabi. Started by an orthodontist, grown into a family dental clinic with ten doctors.

How it started

Dr. Azzam Al Jundi studied dentistry at the University of Plovdiv in Bulgaria and took his Master of Orthodontics at Saint Louis University in the United States. In 1997 he became a clinical professor, teaching orthodontics to undergraduate and postgraduate students at Western Reserve University in Cleveland, and later ran the clinic of the Orthodontic Graduate Program at Saint Louis University.

In the UAE he was consultant and head of the Division of Orthodontics at Al Tawam Hospital in Al Ain. In 2005 he opened his own practice in Abu Dhabi, which is now Dr. Aljundi Orthodontic and Dental Center. Over the years general dentists, prosthodontists and a periodontist joined, so families can have everything done in one place.
