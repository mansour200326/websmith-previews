01 · OrthodonticsBraces and Invisalign

This is where the clinic began. Full braces usually go on the top and bottom teeth once most of the adult teeth have come in. If you'd rather not wear metal, Invisalign uses a series of clear trays made for your teeth; each one moves them a small distance until they're where they should be.

  • Metal braces
  • Invisalign clear aligners
  • Retainers and retainer check-ups
  • Orthodontic consultations

Who: Dr. Azzam Al Jundi and Dr. Elie Khouri, orthodontists

02 · ImplantsImplants and grafts

An implant is a permanent way to replace a missing tooth, and it can last many years; most other options need replacing sooner. If the bone or gum isn't thick enough to hold one, we build it up first with a graft.

  • Single and multiple implants
  • Bone grafts and bone augmentation
  • Gum grafts
  • Impacted teeth

Who: Dr. Mohammed Alkahlout, periodontist and implantologist (ITI certified), and Dr. Skander Chelli, who holds implant privileges from the Abu Dhabi Department of Health

Ask about implants →Call 02 666 5357

03 · CosmeticHollywood smile and smile design

We look at the whole smile first: the shape and colour of the teeth, the gum line, how they sit with your face. Then we use what's needed, which can be veneers, composite bonding, implants, whitening or a mix.

  • Porcelain veneers, colour-matched
  • Composite bonding
  • Gum contouring
  • Smile makeovers

Who: Dr. Yazan Mustafa, prosthodontist (smile design and digital dentistry), and the general dentists

Ask about a Hollywood smile →Call 02 666 5357

04 · ProsthodonticsCrowns, veneers, bridges and dentures

Crowns and veneers both restore a tooth and improve how it looks. A veneer is a thin layer of porcelain, matched to your colour and bonded to the front of the tooth. Bridges and dentures replace teeth that are missing.

  • Porcelain crowns
  • Veneers
  • Bridges
  • Full and partial dentures

Who: Dr. Yazan Mustafa and Dr. Abdalla Ghonimi

Ask on WhatsApp →Call 02 666 5357

05 · EndodonticsRoot canal treatment

Inside each tooth is the pulp, the soft nerve tissue. When it gets inflamed or infected, it can cause pain, swelling and in time bone loss. A root canal treatment removes it, cleans the canals and seals them, and with normal care you keep the tooth.

Who: Dr. Islam Samy and the general dentists

Toothache? Message us →Call 02 666 5357

06 · RestorationsFillings

We take out the decayed part of the tooth and fill the space. Fillings come in several materials; porcelain and tooth-coloured fillings are close to invisible.

Book on WhatsApp →Call 02 666 5357

07 · WhiteningTeeth whitening

Whitening bleaches the teeth and lightens them by several shades. We'll be straight with you: it makes teeth lighter, not paper-white. If you want more than that, we'll talk about veneers.

Ask about whitening →Call 02 666 5357

08 · Laser and periodonticsLaser treatment and gum care

Compared with the drill, a dental laser can mean less pain and less worry. On the gums it cuts down bleeding and swelling, and on cavities it keeps more of the healthy tooth. Our periodontist also treats gum disease and does gum contouring and depigmentation.

  • Gum disease treatment
  • Gum contouring
  • Gum depigmentation
  • Laser cavity treatment

Who: Dr. Mohammed Alkahlout, periodontist

Ask about gum treatment →Call 02 666 5357

09 · PreventionCheck-ups, cleaning and children

Good brushing and flossing at home stop bad breath, decay and gum disease. At a check-up we clean what the brush misses and catch problems early. For children we focus on prevention and on teaching good habits early, and we talk parents through it.

Who: Dr. Alsafa Al Zuhairi (former clinical instructor in paediatric clinics), Dr. Salma Abu Odeh and Dr. Jamal Tafankji
