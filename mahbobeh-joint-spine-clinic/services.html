Muscle and joint pain

Acute and chronic musculoskeletal pain is most of what we see. Mahbobeh combines dry needling, musculoskeletal acupuncture, manual therapy, electrotherapy, exercise therapy and taping, depending on what you need.

Head and face
Headaches and migraines, dizziness and vertigo, jaw pain, Bell’s palsy
Neck and back
Neck pain, back pain
Arms
Shoulder, hand and elbow pain
Legs
Knee pain such as osteoarthritis, foot pain such as plantar fasciitis and heel spur, leg pain
Also
Sports injuries, rehab after a fracture, lymphatic problems, pelvic pain
