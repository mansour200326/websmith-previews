The space

A recovery studio for athletes, active people and anyone who wants to move better, feel better and recover better. Suites, stretching, sauna, ice bath and contrast recovery, all in one place in Muweileh.

What we're here for

Whether you're recovering from training, dealing with everyday stiffness and soreness, spending long hours at work, or just taking time to reset, the goal is the same: you walk out feeling refreshed, restored and ready for what's next.

That's why the Recovery Suites, Stretch & Mobility, the sauna, the ice bath and contrast recovery are all in one place, built around what your body needs that day.

Built for performance. Made for everyone.
