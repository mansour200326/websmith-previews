Look for the red-rose bags

Our rusk and bread sticks come in clear bags printed with red roses, stacked from the floor to the top shelf. Plain rusk, brown rusk, long bread sticks: take a bag with your tea, or a few for the week.

Not sure which one you had last time? Send a photo of the bag on WhatsApp and we'll put the same one aside.
