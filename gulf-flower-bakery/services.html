What we bake

Everything here is on our counter and shelves in Al Uwaid. What's out changes through the day, so the quickest way to check is a WhatsApp: tell us what you're after and we'll answer from the counter.

Cream cakes

Whole round cakes on silver boards in the glass counter, ready to take home for the evening or a family visit. Smaller cakes and single slices sit boxed on the shelves below.

  • White cream with a chocolate drip and red cherries
  • Orange piping with a cherry and sprinkles
  • Brown cream strands under white cream rosettes
  • White cream with red jelly and sprinkles
  • Slices and small cakes in clear boxes

Pastries

On trays at the bottom of the counter: puff pastry horns filled with cream, and eclairs topped with chocolate. Take one for now, or a box for the table.

  • Cream horns

Croissants

The red croissant rack, packed and ready to grab. Each shelf has its own filling, labelled in English and Arabic so you can pick without asking.

Rusk & bread sticks
