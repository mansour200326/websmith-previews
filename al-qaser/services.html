Steel fabrication

Supply, fabrication, blasting, painting and erection of structural steel, large and medium jobs. Mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium.

  • Steel structures for sheds and car sheds
  • Mezzanine floors
  • Staircases, metal stairs, spiral staircases
  • Roofing sheets, fascias, gutters
  • Hand rails, platforms, walkways
  • Ladders, gates and grills
  • Rolling shutter doors
  • Storage tanks
  • Trolleys and tents
  • Trench railings
  • S.S and brass hand rails, foot rails
  • Stainless steel work for joinery, to your drawing
  • Cladding to columns, frames

03

Rubber moulds

Our rubber division started by making moulds for the rubber manufacturers in Dubai. Later it began making rubber products of all types for industrial, marine and construction use.

Ask on WhatsApp → Call 04 340 6220

Moulds we make for

  • Rubber speed humps
  • Corner guards
  • Wall guards
  • Wheel stoppers
  • Moulded and extruded rubber products
  • Rubber fenders
  • Bridge bearings

Send the drawing, a sample or a photo of the part. We make the mould (die) in our own machine shop.
