About us

A fabrication and engineering company in the heart of the industrial zone of Al Quoz, serving the industrial sector in the region.

Headed by Mr. Murli Cheruvakkat, Managing Director.

Al Qaser Fabrication Works LLC is a fabrication and engineering company set in the industrial zone of Al Quoz, Dubai. We work in mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium, we run our own machine shop, and we have a full rubber mould division.

What we keep coming back to is improving how we work. Competitive pricing, world class workmanship, a quick turnaround and prompt delivery: that is what we promise every customer, from a single handrail to a plant structure.

We also provide designer partnership and CAD facilities, so you can come to us with an idea, a sketch or a full drawing.

Quality and machines

We run an ISO 9001 quality system (per our company brochure), with quality control and procedural systems in place.
