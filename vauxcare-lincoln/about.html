About us

We're a family-run, independent Vauxhall garage in Lincoln. We offer an alternative to the main dealers for servicing and repairs: small enough to care, large enough to cope.

Established 1994. Known as Lincoln's independent Vauxhall specialists, in the city, throughout the county and beyond.

1994

A modest workshop

Vauxcare started in a modest-sized workshop in the south of the city of Lincoln.

Then

Moving north

The business grew soon after we started, so we moved to larger premises north of the city. Today we're at 4 Wavell Road on the Allenby Industrial Estate, off Monks Road.

Word of mouth

Six of us now

As the customer base got larger, so did the staff. We're a six-strong team, and our customer base has grown mainly through word of mouth.

The workshop

Any service, any repair

A fully equipped workshop, from a simple bulb change to an engine and gearbox rebuild. Our main work is servicing and repairs, plus MOT testing and tyre fitting.

Equipment

Vauxhall Tech 2

We've invested in the Vauxhall Tech 2 for fault finding, ECU reprogramming, key reprogramming and electrical faults.

Old school

Every 10,000 miles

We service the old-school way: every 10,000 miles or once a year.

Your Vauxhall warranty stays intact.

Block Exemption means an independent garage can service your Vauxhall without invalidating its warranty. We use OE quality parts and stamp your service book every time.
