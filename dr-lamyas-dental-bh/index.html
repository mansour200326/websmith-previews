Laser, where the drill and the needle used to be.

Laser treatment is usually less invasive than the traditional way. In Dr. Lamya's words: no drilling, no needles, and no pain for most cases. We were one of the first clinics to bring laser dentistry to Bahrain, and our centers carry the largest number of laser systems in the country.

The waiting lounge

Nervous about the dentist?

Tell us when you call. We carry nitrous oxide for patients with high anxiety, and Dr. Lamya and Dr. Nooran are certified to give it. Many treatments can be done with laser instead of a drill or an injection.

For children, Dr. Nooran recommends the first visit within six months of the first tooth, or by the first birthday.
