The name you can trust

Dr. Lamya Mahmood opened the first laser specialist dental center in Bahrain and the GCC. Today Dr. Lamya's has three branches, in Muharraq, Riffa and Janabiya, with all dental specialties under one roof.

Our love for the work shows in our smile, and yours.

Dr. Lamya is the first senior female dentist in the GCC with a Master's degree in Laser Dentistry from Aachen University, and holds a European Master in Oral Laser Application (EMOLA). She treats across laser dentistry, surgery, implants, emergencies, restorative work, prosthodontics and cosmetic cases, and is a certified nitrous oxide provider for patients with high anxiety.

Our different advanced laser therapies set us apart from other dental centers in the country. Laser is usually less invasive than the traditional methods: for most cases, no drilling, no needles and no pain.

Infection control matters a great deal here. We follow set guidelines and policies for sterilisation and infection control that keep both patients and staff safe. And by building a relationship of trust with every patient, our staff take away the usual stress of a visit to the dentist.

We look forward to having you join the Dr. Lamya's Care Family.
