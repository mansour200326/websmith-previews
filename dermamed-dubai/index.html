How booking works

The quickest way to book is WhatsApp. Tell us what it's about and the doctor you'd like, and we reply with a time.

WhatsApp 04 385 2222 info@dermamed.ae
  1. Message or call us

    WhatsApp or call 04 385 2222, Saturday to Thursday, 9:00 to 20:00. Ask for a doctor by name if you have one in mind.

  2. Hold your time with 25%

    When you book a treatment we take 25% in advance. It comes off your final bill on the day.

  3. Need to change it?

    Tell us at least one week before and the advance goes back to the card you paid with.

  4. Your details stay with us

    Card details and personal information are never stored, sold or shared with anyone else.
