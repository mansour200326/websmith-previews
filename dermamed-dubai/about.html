Founder · Consultant Dermatologist · American and Canadian Board

The clinic

A spacious dermatology clinic on the first floor of Wafi Mall, opposite Boots Pharmacy, near Dubai Healthcare City metro station. Medical and cosmetic treatments are done here by a board-certified team who look at each patient on their own and plan treatment around them.

Booking policies

Advance payment

25% of the payment is taken as an advance when you book. It comes off your final bill when you have the procedure at the agreed time. If you don't come at the booked time, it isn't refunded.

Cancelling

Tell us at least one week before your treatment date and the 25% advance is refunded, to the card or method you paid with.

Privacy

Everything between you and the clinic stays confidential. Card details and personal information are never stored, sold, rented or leased to anyone.
