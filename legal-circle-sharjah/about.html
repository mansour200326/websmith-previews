About us

We have a reputation for being agile, nimble, passionate and fierce about finding practical answers for our clients.

Who we are

We are legal specialists who look for practical legal and commercial solutions, efficiently and at a reasonable cost.

Our advice is made for each business, whether it is a start-up, a small or medium enterprise, or a multinational corporation. We have local expertise and a global footprint through our legal network, so we can support clients wherever they do business.

Our lawyers qualified in Australia, the UK, the US and other common law countries, and bring both local and international experience. Our office is at the Sharjah Research, Technology and Innovation Park (SRTIP).

Women in business

As a majority female law firm, we are passionate about supporting women in business with the right legal advice to make informed decisions.

We have worked with women from all cultures, ages and disciplines, and we understand the needs of women who are often juggling entrepreneurship and family. Women who have the support of other women in professional roles can build very successful businesses.

Our goal is to become the UAE and GCC's preferred law firm for women in business.

From incorporation to exit

What we do for start-ups, step by step. We designed the Legal Circle Membership to keep this affordable for start-ups and SMEs.

  1. 01Incorporation

    The right legal structure and licence for how you operate, including operating entities and holding companies.

  2. 02Corporate structure

    Set up so founders' and investors' rights are clear and liabilities are understood.

  3. 03Governance

    Best practice for how the company is run, with the policies and documents to match.

  4. 04Compliance and risk

    Which laws and regulations apply to you, and how to keep risk low.

  5. 05Investor agreements

    Agreements written for your round that protect the start-up and its investors.

  6. 06Growth

    Ongoing support as you expand, so the business stays compliant and protected.

  7. 07Exit

    Mergers and acquisitions, IPOs and other options, to get the best outcome for what you've built.
