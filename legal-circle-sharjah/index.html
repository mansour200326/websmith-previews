Why Legal Circle

Practical legal and commercial answers, sized to the business in front of us: a two-founder start-up, an SME or a multinational.

  1. 01
    Lawyers qualified abroad, working here

    Our team qualified in Australia, the UK, the US and other common law countries, with local and international experience, and a legal network for clients who do business across borders.

  2. 02
    Fees you can plan for

    Competitive hourly rates, capped fees for each stage of the work, or a mix of both. Budget certainty, and costs you can predict.

  3. 03
    A firm led by women

    We are a majority female law firm. We work with women from all cultures, ages and disciplines, and our goal is to be the UAE and GCC's preferred law firm for women in business.

Who we help

Five kinds of client, one team. Each gets advice written for their situation, not a standard pack.

01

Start-ups and SMEs

Incorporation, the right licence and structure, founder and investor agreements, governance and compliance, then support as you grow and when it's time to exit. Our Legal Circle Membership keeps a lawyer on the phone for a fixed monthly fee.

The membership →
02

Investors and private clients

Corporate and commercial work, tax, wealth and inheritance, real estate, banking and finance. We protect the investment and keep the risks small, across real estate, healthcare, F&B, technology and energy.

03

Women in business

Legal advice from women, for women building companies, often while juggling a family as well. Women who have other women behind them, professionally, build stronger businesses, and we want to be part of that growth.

04

In-house legal counsel

We take the corporate secretarial load off your desk: licensing, company set-ups, board resolutions and agreements, attestation and notarisation, share transfers. An extension of your team, with up-to-date local and regional law.

05

Multinationals and international firms

Set-up, acquisitions, expansion or dissolution in the UAE and GCC, with advice written for the GM, CEO or MD who has to decide. Overseas law firms use us to understand the local detail for their own clients.
