Questions founders ask us

Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp or call 04 583 0716.

Do I need to set up in the mainland to operate in the UAE?

It depends on the type of business, your target market and what you sell. Many businesses choose the mainland for access to customers across the country, but there may be other options for your situation.

Is my corporate structure right for my business?

That depends on the nature of the business, who owns it, where it operates and what you want it to achieve. Reach out and we can go through it in detail.

Are SPVs and holding companies allowed in the UAE?

Yes. An SPV is a subsidiary set up for a specific purpose, such as holding assets. It can be an LLC or another type of entity, and the rules vary by jurisdiction and type of business.
