Restore your energy.

It's written on our front window, and it's the whole idea.

ANZO is a spa for men in Al Ramlah, Umm Al Quwain. We do massage, properly: hot stone, traditional Thai, Swedish, aromatherapy, deep tissue and sport massage, and foot reflexology. For hands and feet there's a full manicure and pedicure.

Book ahead on WhatsApp and your session starts on time. There's plenty of parking, and we're open every day until midnight, so after work is never too late.
