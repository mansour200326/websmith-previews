Since 1989

We started in 1989 with a single clinic in an apartment complex, one of the first private dental clinics in Kuwait. Thirty-seven years on, we're still here, and still growing.

We look after the long-term health of your teeth, not just today's problem. That is why the practice kept growing: full-service dentistry from check-ups to children's dentistry, implants, root canals, gums and orthodontics, for everyone.

The clinic

Bright rooms, painted ceilings and green chairs. Our hygienists take care of cleaning and scaling so the dentists can give their time to the harder work, and our receptionists look after everything else you need on the day.
