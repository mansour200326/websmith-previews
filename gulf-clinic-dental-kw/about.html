Since 1989

One of the first private dental clinics to open in Kuwait, owned by a woman who has worked in dentistry since 1989.

One clinic in an apartment complex

Gulf Clinic began in 1989 as a single clinic in an apartment complex, and quickly became a pioneer in what was then an emerging field. We've been looking after patients ever since, led by experienced dentists who care about doing the work properly and putting the patient first.

We think about the long-term health of your teeth, not only today's appointment, and that is why the practice has kept growing. Today we offer full-service dentistry, from primary care to children's dentistry, implants, root canals, gums and orthodontics, at two branches: Salmiya and Sabah Al-Salem.

Our own lab

The clinic has its own laboratory with skilled technicians, supervised by our dentists, so the crowns and veneers we fit look right and feel comfortable. Our philosophy there is simple: the doctor won't look good unless the lab does good. That's why the lab works with up-to-date technology, to support our dentists and our patients.

Hygienists, reception and nervous patients

Our trained hygienists look after oral hygiene and scaling, which lets the dentists concentrate on the more demanding work. Our receptionists are there to help with whatever you need on the day.

If you're nervous, you're not the only one. Our staff are used to dental fear and to each patient's own needs. We work with a range of insurance companies in Kuwait, and the clinic is easy to reach, with parking spaces for patients.
