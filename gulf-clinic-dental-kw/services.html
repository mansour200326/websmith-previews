Braces and Invisalign

03

Root canal therapy

The pulp inside a tooth can be damaged by decay, a knock, a large filling, gum disease, or even grinding. When that happens, root canal therapy stops the problem spreading to the tissue around the tooth.

Signs you may need one

  • Pain with hot or cold drinks that lasts a few minutes
  • Throbbing pain that gets in the way of your day
  • A tooth that's tender when you bite
  • One tooth changing colour

Sometimes there's no pain at all, when the pulp has already died and the tooth drains through a small opening in the gum (a fistula). Some soreness after treatment is normal for 3 to 4 days; it's controlled with painkillers, not antibiotics.

Done by Dr. Fouad Moussallem, endodontic specialist, and Dr. Mohamed Rafiq Jaber, endodontist.

04

Veneers and crowns

Veneers are thin shells of porcelain made to fit exactly over the front of your teeth. They repair damage, cover stains, and let light through so they look natural. They're bonded in place, they're very strong, and you eat and speak as usual.

Crowns and veneers come from our own lab, where the technicians are supervised by our dentists.

Done by Dr. Anisa Al-Rasheed, cosmetic and operative dentistry consultant, prosthodontists Dr. Mohamed Barakat and Dr. Tamara Khaleel Alasali, and Dr. Bankima Malhotra (GP dental and prosthodontics).

Children's dentistry

Children need a different approach, and a dentist who understands how they feel. Ours are trained to treat children who are scared of the clinic. With a child who's had a bad experience, we don't rush into treatment: we go one step at a time until they trust the dentist again.
