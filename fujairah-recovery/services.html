Breakdown recovery

When the car won't start, won't move, or isn't safe to drive any more. Call with where you are and we come out with the flatbed.

We load it, strap it down and take it where it needs to go: home, your garage or the agency.

  • Cars that won't start or won't move
  • Cars that aren't safe to drive
  • Taken to home, a garage or the agency
02

Low and sports cars

A low front and big wheels need care. The car goes up the flatbed and is strapped at the wheels, so it sits flat for the whole trip.

  • Loaded flat on the bed
  • Strapped at the wheels

4x4s and pickups

Big and heavy goes up the same way. This Ford F-150 4x4 rode on our flatbed, with a strap over the back wheel.

  • 4x4s and SUVs
  • Pickups
