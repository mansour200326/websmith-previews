Treatments

Main treatments

01Chiropractic

A natural approach that treats the cause of the pain, not just the symptoms. When the spine is aligned, the nervous system works as it should and the body can heal itself.

The chiropractor moves a "fixated" joint back to its correct position with a quick, precise force. This is the adjustment. Dr. Danya uses the Gonstead technique and diversified methods, and may also use an activator, drop tables, a cox flexion table or SOT blocks.

  • Back pain
  • Disc problems
  • Neck pain
  • Joint pain
  • Scoliosis
  • Headaches, dizziness, children's bed-wetting
Book on WhatsAppor call 011 466 1664

02Physical therapy

Our physiotherapists work to improve your mobility and your ability to do daily things, or to rehabilitate you after surgery. We treat women and men of all ages, and children, with muscle, bone and joint conditions.

Every patient gets their own plan, using electrotherapy, ultrasound, paraffin, infrared, cold or heat therapy, band and balance exercises, cold laser therapy, shockwave therapy and more.

  • Low back pain
  • Arthritis
  • Chronic pain
  • Disc, neck and joint pain
  • Scoliosis
Book on WhatsAppor call 011 466 1664

03Sports injuries

Physical therapy for athletes: we manage and help prevent common sports injuries, with exercises that help healing, rebuild strength and bring back flexibility, so you can get back to what you enjoy. For the whole family.

  • ACL injuries
  • Ankle sprains
  • Ligament and muscle tears
  • Post-operative cases
Book on WhatsAppor call 011 466 1664

04After surgery

Post-op physical therapy after knee surgery, shoulder surgery and other operations. We guide you and your family every step of the way. After back surgery, chiropractic care can also help keep the pain from coming back.

  • Knee surgery
  • Shoulder surgery
  • Back surgery
Book on WhatsAppor call 011 466 1664

05Women's health

A physiotherapist who specialises in women's health treats muscle and joint problems during pregnancy and after birth, and runs education and care before and after delivery.

  • Low back pain, SIJ, diastasis
  • Scar management
  • Labour preparation classes
  • Exercises during and after pregnancy
  • Pelvic floor orientation and posture
  • Postnatal check-up, 2–6 months after natural birth or caesarean
  • Breastfeeding consultations
Book on WhatsAppor call 011 466 1664

06Pelvic floor rehabilitation

Private, one-to-one treatment with our pelvic floor rehabilitation specialist.

  • Pelvic organ prolapse
  • Pain during intercourse
  • Incontinence
  • Constipation

Equipment and therapies

07Laser foot scanner

The laser foot scanner finds imbalances in the foot, abnormal pressure points and arch problems that contribute to foot, ankle, knee and hip problems.

From the result we can prescribe stabilising orthotics made to fit you, to bring your body back into alignment and support you when you stand, walk or run.

Book a foot scanor call 011 466 1664

08Foot orthotics

Orthotics for knee pain, foot pronation, flat feet, back pain and neck pain. We work with Foot Levelers, a company in the USA, for high-quality orthotics made for each patient.

  • Knee pain
  • Foot pronation
  • Flat feet
  • Back and neck pain
Ask about orthoticsor call 011 466 1664

09Shockwave therapy

A non-invasive treatment that uses pressure waves on long-standing muscle, tendon and joint problems.

  • Plantar fasciitis
  • Heel pain
  • Tennis elbow
  • Golfer's elbow
  • Shoulder and rotator cuff injuries
  • Morton's neuroma
  • Knee tendonitis
  • Muscle knots
  • Muscle and tendon sprains and strains
Ask about shockwaveor call 011 466 1664

10RockTape taping

We have a certified RockTape practitioner. The tape goes over the muscles to reduce pain and swelling, relax overused muscles and help them move. It is very elastic, so you keep your full range of motion.

  • Acute and overuse injuries
  • Back and disc pain
  • Better circulation and healing
  • Better muscle function
  • Support for a joint
  • Injury prevention
Ask about tapingor call 011 466 1664

11Super pulsed laser

Red and near-infrared light on the injury stimulates the cells that repair tissue, and reduces inflammation and pain. It takes several sessions before you see a lasting effect; how many depends on the injury and how long you've had it.

  • Sprains and strains
  • Back and neck pain
  • Osteoarthritis
  • RSI, frozen shoulder, chronic low back pain
  • Nerve pain from disc injuries
  • Post-operative care and tendon repair
Ask about laser therapyor call 011 466 1664
