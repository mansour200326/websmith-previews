The clinic

Who you'll see at Muroona, what they trained in, and straight answers to the questions we get every day.

We opened our doors in Sulimaniya in 2014 as a chiropractic and physical therapy clinic, and we're still in the same place on King Abdulaziz Road.

Chiropractic looks for the cause of the pain instead of only treating the symptoms. With a spine that moves properly and a nervous system without interference, the body is better able to heal itself. Our physiotherapists build on that with rehabilitation, sports injury care, recovery after surgery and women's health.

We treat women, men and children. There is no age restriction: from newborns to grandparents, everyone has a spine.

Questions and answers

Something else on your mind? Call 011 466 1664 or email Contact@Muroona.com.sa.

What is chiropractic?

A natural method of treatment that focuses on the causes of physical problems, not just the symptoms. With a spine that works normally and a healthy nervous system, your body is better able to heal itself. Your chiropractor makes sure your vertebrae are aligned and your nerves have no interference. It helps with back pain, disc problems, neck pain, joint pain, scoliosis and more. Our chiropractor uses the Gonstead technique and diversified methods.

What will the treatment be like?

At the consultation we examine you and look at your x-rays and lab work. We make a diagnosis and set a treatment plan. Treatment starts on the first visit and is similar each time, with small changes: a chiropractic manipulation to align and free the joint, plus physiotherapy such as electrotherapy, ice or heat, ultrasound and exercises to strengthen the area. Depending on the case, you might be taped, and foot orthotics might be ordered to stabilise your spine.

Do I need a referral?

Yes. As per the Ministry of Health's regulations, please bring a referral for physical therapy from a medical physician before starting your treatment.

What is an adjustment, and does it hurt?

An adjustment is a specific, gentle force applied by the doctor in a precise direction to a joint that is fixated, "locked up", or not moving properly. You might hear a popping sound: that is nitrogen gas in the joint being released, not your bones breaking, and some of our methods don't cause a pop at all. It doesn't hurt. If you are tense, you may feel some muscle spasm on the day and the day after, and then it settles.

Can chiropractic help pregnant women?

Yes. Pregnant women find chiropractic care eases back pain, neck pain, sciatica and other spine-related pain. We use special techniques for pregnant women.

Can I have chiropractic care after back surgery?

Yes. "Failed back surgery syndrome" is common, and symptoms can return months or years after surgery. Chiropractic care can help prevent that pain from coming back.

Is there an age limit? Can children be treated?

There is no age restriction. From newborns to the elderly, everyone has a spine and the right to a healthy life. We use different methods depending on age and diagnosis.

Does chiropractic only help back and neck pain?

No. It can also help soft tissue problems of the shoulders, knees and other joints, and some kinds of headaches, ear aches, dizziness, bed-wetting in children and scoliosis. See the treatments page.

Will I need chiropractic forever?

Just as you see a dentist and an eye doctor for check-ups, regular spine check-ups help keep your spine healthy. Your chiropractor can spot problems before they turn into complicated health issues. Prevention is the best therapy.

Do you offer home visits or other branches?

Not at the moment. We don't provide home care, and our only location is on King Abdulaziz Road in Sulimaniya, Riyadh.
