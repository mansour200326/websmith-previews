Muroona, مرونة, means flexibility. Since 2014 we have treated back pain, disc problems, neck and joint pain and scoliosis in Sulimaniya, looking for what causes the pain, not only easing it.

Chiropractic and physiotherapy under one roof. Our chiropractor uses the Gonstead technique and diversified methods; our physiotherapists take care of rehabilitation, sports injuries, recovery after surgery and women's health.

There is no age limit. We see newborns, children, adults and grandparents, each with a plan that fits their age and diagnosis.

One clinic, on King Abdulaziz Road. We don't do home visits at the moment.

Your first visit

What happens from the moment you walk in, in three steps.

01

Referral and examination

Bring your physical therapy referral from a medical physician; Ministry of Health rules ask for it before treatment starts.

We examine you, go through your x-rays and lab work, make a diagnosis and set a treatment plan with you.

02

Treatment starts the same day

An adjustment to align and free the joint, then physiotherapy such as electrotherapy, ice or heat, ultrasound and exercises to strengthen the area.

You may hear a small pop. That is gas leaving the joint, not a bone. It doesn't hurt.

03

Taping and orthotics if you need them

Depending on your case we may tape you with RockTape or order foot orthotics to keep your spine stable between visits.

Later visits follow the same plan, with small changes as you improve.

Questions patients ask

The ones we hear most at reception. All questions →

Does an adjustment hurt?

No. An adjustment is a specific, gentle force in a precise direction on a joint that is locked up. You might hear a pop, which is nitrogen gas being released, not your bones. If you're tense you may feel some muscle soreness that day and the next, and then it settles.

Can children be treated?

Yes, there is no age restriction. From newborns to grandparents, everyone has a spine. We use different methods depending on age and diagnosis.

Can chiropractic help during pregnancy?

Yes. It can ease back pain, neck pain and sciatica in pregnancy, and we use special techniques for pregnant women. Our women's health physiotherapist also runs labour preparation classes.

Do I need a referral?

Yes. Following Ministry of Health regulations, please bring a referral for physical therapy from a medical physician before starting treatment.
