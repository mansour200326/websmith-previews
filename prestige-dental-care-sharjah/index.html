Treatments

From a six-monthly clean to a full set of dentures. Tap a treatment to see what it involves.

01CleaningScaling and polishingfrom AED 130

The most common reason people visit us. We recommend a clean every six months: a toothbrush at home never reaches what the dentist's tools do.

Book a cleaning on WhatsApp →

02FillingsFor cavities, usually one visitfrom AED 130

Most cavities need a filling, and most fillings take about an hour. You may feel some pressure while we work, but it shouldn't hurt.

Ask about a filling →

03Root canalWhen the nerve is infected and it hurtsfrom AED 350

Usually comes after real tooth pain. The tissue inside the tooth is infected, so the dentist removes it, cleans the canal and seals it, and you keep your own tooth.

Tell us about the pain →

04CrownsPFM or zirconia, two visitsfrom AED 400

When a cavity is too big for a filling, or the top of the tooth has broken down. We take a mould, a lab makes the crown to fit, and we fit it on your second visit.

Ask about crowns →

05ExtractionsDone gently, with aftercare advicefrom AED 150

Extractions sound worse than they are. The dentist does everything to keep you comfortable, and tells you how to look after the area afterwards.

Ask about an extraction →

06Laser whiteningA lighter shade, checked for sensitivity first

An easy treatment for most people. A few are sensitive to the whitening agent, so the dentist checks first.

Ask the price on WhatsApp →

07VeneersFor crooked or discoloured front teeth

A thin covering bonded to the front of the tooth. Mostly used to correct shape, and it whitens too. Simple and painless for most people.

Ask about veneers →

08Braces and InvisalignStraighter teeth, metal or clear

Both straighten crooked teeth. Metal braces work faster; Invisalign is almost invisible and slower. Expect a little ache when they go on.

Ask about braces →

09DenturesRemovable, made to fit you

Not only for older patients. Fitting takes a few visits, and at the end you have a full set of working teeth again.

Ask about dentures →

Your first visit

  1. Walk in, or send a WhatsApp

    Come in during opening hours, or message 056 550 7129 and we'll give you a time. Fridays are by appointment.

  2. A full examination

    The doctor checks every tooth and your gums, not just the one that hurts.

  3. X-rays, if they're needed

    Included in the AED 60 consultation, so you know exactly what's going on.

  4. A plan, with prices

    The doctor explains what needs doing, in what order and what it costs. Nothing starts until you agree.
