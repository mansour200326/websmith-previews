Your first visit

  1. Walk in, or send a WhatsApp

    Come in during opening hours, or message 056 550 7129 and we'll give you a time. Fridays are by appointment.

  2. A full examination

    The doctor checks every tooth and your gums, not just the one that hurts.

  3. X-rays, if they're needed

    Included in the AED 60 consultation, so you know exactly what's going on.

  4. A plan, with prices

    The doctor explains what needs doing, in what order and what it costs. Nothing starts until you agree.
