Cleaning

fromAED 130

Scaling and polishing is by far the most common reason people visit a dentist, and we recommend it every six months. Your toothbrush will never reach what the dentist's tools do. Scaling removes plaque and tartar; polishing lifts stains for a smoother, brighter finish.

  • Cleaning from AED 130
  • Cleaning + polishing from AED 160

Fillings

fromAED 130

Cavities are common and easy to get. For most of them a filling is the answer, and it usually takes about an hour. You might feel pressure while the dentist works, but it shouldn't be painful. The price includes the consultation and X-rays.

Ask about a filling →

Root canal

fromAED 350

A root canal usually comes after some pretty bad tooth pain. It means the tissue inside the tooth is infected and inflamed. To stop the pain, the dentist removes that tissue, cleans the canal and seals it, so you keep your own tooth.

  • From AED 350 for a front tooth
  • Includes consultation, X-rays and composite filling
  • Root canal + crown from AED 600
Tell us about the pain →

Crowns

fromAED 400

If a cavity is too big for a filling, or the top of the tooth has decayed, a crown covers and protects it. It's usually two visits: we take a mould of your tooth, a lab makes a crown to fit, and we fit it the second time. Like a filling, it shouldn't be painful.

  • PFM (porcelain fused to metal) from AED 400
  • Zirconia (all ceramic) from AED 600
Ask about crowns →

Extractions

fromAED 150

Extractions sound scary, but the dentist does everything to keep you comfortable, and you go home with aftercare advice for a smooth recovery. There are many reasons you might need one; the consultation and X-rays are included in the price.

Veneers

priceon WhatsApp

For teeth that are crooked or discoloured. A veneer is a thin covering placed over the front of a tooth or a set of teeth. Mainly used to correct shape, and it whitens too. Simple and painless for most people.

Braces & Invisalign

priceon WhatsApp

Both straighten crooked teeth, and straighter teeth are easier to keep clean. Classic braces use metal to move the teeth back into place. Invisalign is almost invisible and slower, but just as effective. Having them fitted can leave a little ache, nothing extreme.

Ask about braces →

Dentures

priceon WhatsApp

Dentures replace missing teeth in a natural way and are usually removable. They aren't only for older patients. Fitting takes a few visits, and at the end you have a full set of working teeth again.
