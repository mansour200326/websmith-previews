What we do for owners, tenants and buyers

Sunrise started by taking properties from owners in volume and sub-leasing them out unit by unit. That is still the heart of the business, alongside management for landlords, buying and selling, and building our own warehouses, labour camps and villas.

01

Investment contracts, via sub-leasing

We take different types of properties in big volume, on a short-term or long-term investment contract, and sub-lease them in retail to individual tenants. The developer or owner gets a good return in rent and doesn't have to deal with the day-to-day, because we take care of the property.

Today we have more than 5,000 tenants under these contracts, in three groups: residential, commercial and industrial.

02

Leasing and management for landlords

Peace of mind for landlords, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. We run a 95.8% occupancy rate across the leasing units we manage. Pick the level you need: Leasing only, Lease management or full Property management. Between them they cover:

  • Finding a tenant
  • Drawing up the tenancy contract
  • Tenancy contract registration
  • Collection of rent
  • Check-in of the tenant at the start of the tenancy
  • Re-marketing while the property is empty between tenancies
  • Property information pack with professional photos
  • Detailed property inspections
  • Banking
  • A dedicated property supervisor
  • Managing the check-out
  • Arranging repairs and maintenance
  • Arranging payment of outgoings
  • Reporting
  • Paying and clearing electricity and water deposits
