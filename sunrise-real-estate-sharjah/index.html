A Sharjah family firm, two generations in.

Mr. Haresh Khubchandani started Sunrise Real Estate Enterprises in Sharjah in 1979. His son, Mr. Ashish Khubchandani, joined as a Director in 1995–96. Our work is mostly sub-leasing: we take properties from owners and developers, let them out unit by unit, and look after them, so both the landlord and the tenant have peace of mind.
