About Al-Mana

Al Mana Engineering & Contracting has been a contracting company under the laws of the State of Qatar since 1963, and is part of the Al Mana Group.

Most of our work is for Qatar Petroleum in Dukhan: laboratories, a training centre, a fire administration building, two mosques, a primary school, the downtown roads and the sewage treatment plant upgrade. We also build private villas in Doha and at The Pearl.
