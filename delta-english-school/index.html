The school

Delta started as a nursery in Sharjah and grew, class by class, into a full K-12 school. Since September 2009 we have been in our own purpose-built building on Sheikh Khalifa Bin Zayed Road, It has the Ernest Hemingway Library, the DES Media Hub and a student activity centre.

Admission

First-time applicants and transfer students go through the same four steps with the Office of the Registrar. If anything is unclear, come in and speak to an Admissions Coordinator, or call or email us.

01

Ask

Call, email or book a campus visit. We answer your questions before you apply.

02

Apply

Apply online through Helios, our admissions portal, any time.

03

Assess

The Registrar’s office takes you through the next step for your child’s grade level.

04

Accept

You hear back from us and complete registration.

How we got here

  1. 1989

    Dennis and Anita D’cruz open Delta Nursery in Sharjah. Later that year it adds a Pre-K kindergarten program.

  2. 1992

    The Ministry of Education grants permission for Delta English School. It opens in Ghafiya, close to Al Shaab Sports Club.

  3. 1996

    DES moves to a bigger building in Maysaloon, opposite the Sharjah Municipal Health Clinic.

  4. 2009

    In September we move into our own custom-built campus in Muwailih.

  5. 2020

    Grade 11 and 12 classes begin with the 2020–2021 school year.
