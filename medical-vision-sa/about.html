From a division to a company

Medical Vision Est. is the dental company of the Al‑Sanie group, based on Dabab Street in Riyadh.

How we started

  1. 1998

    Al-Sanie Trading Est. sets up its medical and dental division.

  2. 2007

    The dental business keeps growing, so the dental division is separated as its own company: Medical Vision Est.

  3. Today

    25 people covering the whole of Saudi Arabia, with showrooms in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam.
