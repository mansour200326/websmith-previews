Packages abroad

Three trips we have planned day by day. Our representative meets you on arrival and handles every transfer.

Honeymoon in Malaysia

9 nights, 10 days · 2 nights Petaling Jaya, 2 Langkawi, 2 Penang, 3 Kuala Lumpur

Sunway Resort Hotel & Spa, Berjaya Langkawi Beach Resort, Golden Sands Resort by Shangri-La and Swiss-Garden Hotel & Residence.

Day 1Kuala Lumpur arrival

Met at Kuala Lumpur International Airport and taken to Sunway Resort Hotel & Spa.

Day 2Genting Highlands, 8 hours

A 90-minute drive through tropical forest to the cable car, then the highlands: the casino, amusement centre, bowling, and a boat ride on the lake. Photo stop at Batu Caves.

Day 3Fly to Langkawi

Free morning, then the flight from KLIA to Langkawi and check-in at Berjaya Langkawi Beach Resort.

Day 4Langkawi discovery, 4 hours

Rainforest and waterfall in the north of the island, Malaysia's largest crocodile farm, Oriental Village and the Langkawi cable car up Gunung Mat Cincang.

Day 5Ferry to Penang

A 2 hour 45 minute ferry to Penang and check-in at Golden Sands Resort by Shangri-La.

Day 6Penang round-island, 4 hours

A batik workshop, a fishing village, a fruit plantation, the Snake Temple from 1850 and the OE jewellery factory.

Day 7Coach to Kuala Lumpur

Five hours on a double-deck luxury coach with a lounge and cabin crew. Check-in at Swiss-Garden Hotel & Residence.

Day 8KL city tour and cultural show

Photo stop at the Petronas Twin Towers, the King's Palace, National Monument, National Mosque and Chinatown, then a Malaysian cultural show with dinner.

Day 9Kuala Lumpur, private car

A private car for 8 hours for the places you missed and last-minute shopping.

Day 10Departure

Check-out and transfer to KLIA for your flight home.

Educational trip, Malaysia

4 days · Kuala Lumpur and Genting Highlands

For schools and colleges. All tours and transfers by private air-conditioned van.

  • 3-star hotel (Hotel Sentral Pudu or similar), daily breakfast
  • Return airport transfer
  • 2 factory visits and 2 university visits
  • KL city tour (6 hours) and Putrajaya tour on the way to the airport
  • Genting with return cable car and indoor theme park tickets
Day 1Arrival and KL city tour

Met at the airport, check-in, then a 6-hour Kuala Lumpur city tour in the afternoon.

Day 2Factory visit

A full day of factory visits, 8 hours. Visits need permission arranged before arrival.

Day 3University visit

A full day visiting Malaysia's top universities.

Day 4Genting Highlands

Batu Caves photo stop, the cable car and Genting Highlands, with indoor theme park tickets.

Educational trip, Malaysia and Singapore

5 nights, 6 days · student package

Orkid Inn Pudu in Kuala Lumpur, Hotel 81 Orchid in Singapore. Twin-sharing rooms, breakfast, lunch and dinner, all entrance fees included.

Day 1Putrajaya and Kuala Lumpur

Putrajaya (Perdana Putra, Putra Mosque, Dataran Putra, the lake cruise), then the Twin Towers, National Museum, King's Palace, KL Tower, Chinatown and the National Science Centre. Evening at i-City, Shah Alam.

Day 2Proton and University Malaysia

A visit to Proton, the Malaysian car maker, then University Malaysia in the afternoon.

Day 3Genting Highlands

Batu Caves on the way, then Genting by cable car.

Day 4Coach to Singapore

Transfer by coach from Kuala Lumpur to the hotel in Singapore.

Day 5Sentosa

Cable car from Mount Faber to Sentosa Island, Underwater World and the dolphin lagoon, and the Merlion.

Day 6Departure

Transfer to the airport.
