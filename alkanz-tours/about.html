About Alkanz Tours

A Sharjah travel agency started by a group of professionals from the service industry, with experience across the GCC.

We are here to help you plan cultural, historical, adventure, desert safari, city, modern-attraction and sport tours of Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the Northern Emirates.

If you want a beach resort or a city hotel with amazing architecture, we have options for business and leisure travellers. Our relationships with hotels around the world let you choose your hotel anywhere in the UAE, and abroad, at good rates.

We run every kind of tour: desert safaris, trips to the mountains and beaches, bird-watching and sightseeing, golf and water sports, shopping, horse and camel riding. And if none of those fit, we put together an itinerary for your interests and budget.

For companies, our MICE specialists plan meetings, incentives, conferences and events, with venues and hotels across the UAE.

What makes it work is the team: multilingual, experienced, and taking care of everything from the airport to the last transfer.
