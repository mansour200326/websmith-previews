About MEP Home Maintenance

2012Incorporated in Dubai, July 2012

We started in July 2012 to make home maintenance in Dubai simple: one company you can call for the AC, the electrics, the plumbing and everything else, at a fair price.

One roof, every trade.

Finding a good maintenance company at a reasonable cost is harder than it should be. Before, you'd call several companies, get estimates, check references and insurance. We set up MEP so a resident could get the whole home looked after under a single roof.

Our tradesmen cover Dubai, so we're close to wherever the job is, and urgent work can often be done at very short notice. They are uniformed and insured, and we don't charge for the inspection.

We work for homes, villas and apartments, and for offices and shops, on single call-outs and on annual contracts.
