What the contract covers.

In plain words, from our contract terms. Labour and consumables are included; spare parts are charged at cost plus 10%.

Day-to-day repairs

  • AC: water leaking through ducts, blocked filters
  • Electrical: fuses, short circuits, board problems, light fixtures
  • Plumbing: toilet mechanisms, blockages, broken taps and valves, small leaks, low pressure
  • White goods connected and disconnected

Emergencies

  • AC stopped completely
  • Total power failure
  • Water pressure system failure
  • Major leaks or flooding that put the people or the building at risk

Scheduled checks

  • AC filters, evaporator and condenser coils cleaned
  • Gas and oil levels checked
  • Condensate drains and pipes tested and cleaned
  • Controls tested, electrical switchgear checked

Away for the summer?

Our Home Care visits look after empty and vacant properties: regular scheduled checks while you're away, so you don't come back to a dead AC or a leak that's been running for weeks.

We can add internal cleaning, floors included, before you come home.
