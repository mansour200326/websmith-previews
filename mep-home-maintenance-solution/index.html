What each letter covers.

The three letters in our name are most of what goes wrong in a Dubai home. One call covers all three.

M

Mechanical and AC

  • AC not cooling, blowing hot air or dripping water
  • AC service, cooling coil and duct cleaning
  • Chillers and HVAC systems
  • Water pumps and motors
  • Nest thermostat installation
E

Electrical

  • Fuses, short circuits and distribution boards
  • Light fittings, bulbs and ballasts
  • Sockets, switches and dimmers
  • Extractor fans
  • Fault finding when the power keeps tripping
P

Plumbing

  • Blocked toilets, basins and sinks
  • Dripping taps, leaking pipes and valves
  • Low or no water pressure
  • Water tank cleaning
  • Washing machines connected and moved

And the rest of the house: carpentry and gypsum, painting and wallpaper, tiles and masonry, locks and doors, pest control, cleaning, swimming pools.

Room by room.

Most calls are small: a tap, a light, a door that won't close. Here's what we look after in each room.

Kitchens

Leaking sinks, tap washers, extractor fans, cabinet doors and handles, appliances connected.

Living rooms and bedrooms

AC service, lights and dimmers, sockets, blinds and curtains, shelves, paint touch-ups.

Bathrooms

Flushes, blocked basins, broken tiles, re-grouting and fresh silicone around the bath.

Behind the walls

Pipes, wiring, water pumps and tanks: the things you only notice when they stop.

Annual maintenance contracts.

For apartments, villas and town houses, 1 to 4+ bedrooms. The annual fee depends on the property; ask us for yours. Payment by cash or cheque.
