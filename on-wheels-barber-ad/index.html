How a visit works

No driving across town and no waiting room. You message us, we drive over, and you sit down in the chair a few steps from your front door.

  1. 01

    Message us

    Send your area and a time on WhatsApp, or book online on Fresha.

  2. 02

    We drive over

    The van parks outside your house at the time you picked.

  3. 03

    Step in

    A proper barber's chair, wash basin, mirror and lights. The tools come out of a steriliser.

  4. 04

    Back inside

    A haircut takes about 30 minutes, haircut and beard about 45.

Inside the van

Everything you'd find in a good barbershop, fitted into a van. You step in from the pavement and sit down.

  1. 01The chairA full barber's chair, the same as in a shop.
  2. 02Wash basinBuilt into the counter, for a wash before or after the cut.
  3. 03Steriliser cabinetCombs and tools are kept in a UV steriliser.
  4. 04Lit wood-slat wallsBright inside at night too: the van is out until 2am on weekend nights.
