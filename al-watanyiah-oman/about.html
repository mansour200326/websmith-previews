Al Watanyiah started in the Sultanate in 1992 as National United Engineering & Contracting (NUEC) and took its new name in early 2013. It is an Omani company and has worked on the country's roads, utilities and public buildings ever since.

Today we cover civil construction, highways and highway structures, airports and concrete pavements, sewerage and STPs, water networks, HT electrical works, electromechanical works, GRC and GRP, steel fabrication, aluminium and glazing, and interiors.

Safety & quality

We are certified to ISO 9001:2015, and hold the HSE-MS certificate from OPAL, the Oman Society for Petroleum Services.

Our QA/QC department is independent of the projects it checks, and a QA/QC team is assigned to every job. Senior management meets every month to review HSE performance, and everyone on site is empowered to stop unsafe work.

We run on an ERP system covering estimation, accounting, procurement, quantity surveying, subcontracting and HR.
