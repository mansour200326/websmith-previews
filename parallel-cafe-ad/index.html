PARALLEL

Parallel Cafe. Coffee, all-day breakfast and the ice-cream bun, on Al Khaleej Al Arabi Street in Al Bateen.

Spanish cortados and flat whites on Brazilian beans. Sit in with a laptop, or message your order and pick it up outside. Every day, 7am to 11pm.

Order on WhatsApp or call 050 132 9224

What people come for

The things regulars order again and again. The full menu, coffee to cheesecake, is on the next page.

  1. 01

    The ice-cream bun

    A warm bun, fresh out of the oven, split and filled with ice cream that melts as you eat it.

  2. 02

    Spanish cortado

    Served in a small glass. Also toffee nut lattes, cappuccinos and flat whites on Brazilian beans.

  3. 03

    Iced, to take away

    Iced lattes and cold coffee to take away. Message ahead and it’s ready when you pull up.

  4. 04

    Açai and beetroot bowls

    Fruit, granola, chia and coconut. Breakfast is served all day, so is beetroot toast with a poached egg.

  5. 05

    Something sweet

    Kinder brownies, açai cheesecake, Nutella cookies, banana pudding and fresh pastries.

White walls, rattan and a lot of plants

Cane chairs, round mirrors and tall windows onto the street. Seats inside and out, Wi‑Fi, and room to sit with a laptop or a book for the afternoon.

Quietest: mornings. It fills up from about 5pm.

Good to know: wheelchair accessible, cards accepted.
