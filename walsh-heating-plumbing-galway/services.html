Heat pumps & boilers

Daikin Altherma · oil and gas boilers

Our dedicated Air to Water showroom is open. For a new house we do the full system design: our design engineer works out each room and picks the heat pump, underfloor, radiators and controls to meet the building regulations, with designer sign-off where needed. For an existing home there are oil and gas boilers too, and SEAI grants towards a heat pump retrofit.

  • Altherma 3 Low Temperature, single fan4, 6 and 8kW, R-32. Up to 500% efficiency at 35ºC, runs down to −25ºC, 44dBA.
  • Altherma 3 Low Temperature, twin fan11, 14 and 16kW, R-32. Integrated, wall-hung or solar-ready indoor units.
  • Altherma High TemperatureWater up to 70ºC, so it suits existing radiators. Runs down to −28ºC.
  • Reversible and solar-ready modelsFor cooling in summer or pairing with solar.
  • Oil and gas boilersFor existing homes, with radiators, towel rails and controls to match.

Stoves

Stanley · multifuel, wood burning and pellet

A wood burner can be as much as 80% efficient against 20–25% for an open fire, and the wood costs a fraction of electricity, oil or gas per kilowatt. Come in and see them on display.

  • Solis F800 GrandeFreestanding multifuel and wood. 8kW to the room, 83% efficient, 150mm flue.
  • Solis I900 CassetteDedicated wood-burning insert, 9kW, large viewing glass, chrome detail.

  • Oisin solid fuel stoveFreestanding, removable ashpan, top and rear flue exit.
  • ReginaldFreestanding central heating stove with window-wash air.

Underfloor heating

Plans and spec for your builder

Underfloor runs at a lower temperature than radiators, so it costs less to run, and it frees up the walls. We work with you or your builders to spec and draw detailed plans for the new system. Send us your house details and we'll do the rest.

  • Detailed plans and specificationDrawn by our team and our underfloor partners, ready for the builder.
  • Pipe, panels and manifoldsEverything to lay the system.
  • Zone controlsHeatmiser thermostats room by room (see controls below).
  • Any heat sourcePairs with a heat pump or a boiler.
