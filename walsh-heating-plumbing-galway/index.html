Our Air to Water showroom is open

Come in and see the Daikin Altherma heat pumps working before you decide. They take up to 75% of their energy from the outside air and give you all your heating and hot water.

Building new? Our design engineer does the room-by-room calculations and picks the system to meet the building regulations, with sign-off where you need it. Already in your home? SEAI gives grants for fitting an air to water heat pump, and we'll help you pick the right one.

Underfloor heating, from your plans

We work with you or your builder. Tell us about the house and we'll do the rest.

01

Send us the plans

Call us on 091 757 248 or drop the drawings in. Let us know the house details: new build or existing, floor types, how you'll heat the water.

02

We draw and spec it

Our team and our underfloor partners draw up detailed plans and the specification for your builder, room by room.

03

Zones you control

Heatmiser thermostats run each zone on its own. The UH8 wiring centre takes up to 8 zones, wired or wireless.

04

Collect it here

Pipe, manifolds, controls and the fittings to finish the job, all from the one counter on the Tuam Road.
