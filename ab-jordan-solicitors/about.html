The firm

AB Jordan Solicitors has over 30 years’ experience advising and representing both businesses and individuals.

We aim to give each client advice and guidance that adds value, in a practical “can do” manner, bringing know-how, discretion, confidentiality and practical experience from our team.

We are also Commissioners for Oaths, available to see the public during office opening hours. Please bring original national photo ID, such as a passport or driving licence.

Bernard Jordan

Principal · BA NUI

1976Began in general practice.

Bernard is the Principal of the firm and has over 30 years’ experience in general practice, having started practice in 1976.

His clients include voluntary organisations, religious organisations, charities, small and medium enterprises, local employers, professionals and individual private clients.

  • Personal injury cases and litigation
  • Defamation
  • Family law
  • Wills, estate planning and administration of estates
  • Tribunals of enquiry
  • Property: landlord and tenant; buying and selling residential, agricultural and commercial property
