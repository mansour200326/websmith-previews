01

Property law

We advise on commercial, agricultural and residential property transactions.

  • Buying, leasing and selling properties
  • Mortgaging and releasing security
  • Advising charitable and religious organisations, clubs and trusts on clubhouses, community centres, schools, churches and sports grounds
  • Advising families on the transfer of agricultural property
  • Compulsory Purchase Orders

Residential conveyancing: Charlotte Egan. Agricultural and commercial: Bernard Jordan.Email about a property →

02

Wills, probate and estate planning

Clear instructions in a will matter, not least for tax planning, so that proper provision is made for the next generation.

  • Wills and testamentary dispositions
  • Enduring Powers of Attorney
  • The creation of trusts
  • Probate and administration of estates
  • Estates under the law of intestacy, where there is no will

We know we are working with people at a difficult and sad time. We aim to make the process as clear and straightforward as we can.

Administration of estates: Charlotte Egan and Bernard Jordan.Call about a will or estate →

03

Family law

Confidential advice when a family is going through a change.

  • Judicial separation and divorce
  • Mediation and collaborative law
  • The rights of co-habitants under the Civil Partnership legislation
  • Child law: custody, access, maintenance and guardianship

Family law: Bernard Jordan.Call 059 914 2157 →

04

Litigation & dispute resolution

Disputes across a broad range of courts, tribunals and disciplinary bodies, with a focus on getting a result.

  • Personal injury litigation*
  • Debt collection in the District, Circuit and High Court
  • Enforcement proceedings
  • Family law
  • Employment law
  • Court applications in licensing matters
  • Road traffic offences (District Court)
  • Mediation and alternative dispute resolution

*In contentious business, a solicitor may not calculate fees or other charges as a percentage or proportion of any award or settlement.

Court work: Bernard Jordan. District Court licensing: Charlotte Egan.Email about a dispute →

05

Employment law

We advise companies and private clients on their rights and obligations, in contentious and non-contentious matters.

  • Drafting and reviewing employment contracts
  • Handbooks and office policies
  • Grievance and disciplinary procedures
  • Disciplinary investigations
  • Redundancies and business restructuring, including TUPE
  • Claims under the Unfair Dismissals Acts and equality legislation
  • Representation at tribunals and in the Circuit and High Court
  • Bullying, harassment and equality in the workplace
  • Leave, holidays and parental entitlements
  • Health, safety and welfare at work

Local employers and employees alike.Email about an employment matter →

Company law

From forming the company to the everyday running of the business.

  • Company formation and start-up
  • Charities, voluntary organisations and clubs
  • Employment contracts
  • Employer obligations
  • Licensing, distribution and franchising agreements

Small and medium businesses in Carlow and around.Call about your company →

Voluntary organisations, clubs & charities

  • Clubhouses, community centres and sports grounds
