What we do

We started in equipment rental and site services for the big international contractors. In 1986 we became a construction company. Most of the work is done with our own people, equipment and workshops.

  1. 01

    General contracting

    Commercial buildings, residential complexes and industrial buildings, including site development, infrastructure and utilities, plant civil works, surveying and drafting. We also do drilling and blasting for mines, earth moving and road construction, Kingdom-wide.

  2. 02

    Marine works

    Marinas, jetties, breakwaters and access bridges. Since 1989 we have produced SF Marina concrete pontoons in our Jeddah yard under licence from SF Marina System AB, Sweden, for Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar and Yemen. Gangways, mooring fingers, cleats and service pedestals are supplied with them.

  3. 03

    Steel fabrication & erection

    Structural steel for buildings, fuel and water tanks, piping networks, warehouses, supports for industrial machinery and custom structures, fabricated in our workshop on Usfan Road and erected on site. Kiln installation and repair and cement mill gearbox work as well.

  4. 04

    Compound services

    Villas for European and Arab families at the Azzam Compound next to Jeddah International Airport, labour and senior staff camps in Jeddah and Yanbu, and plots in our yard leased to companies for their offices and warehouses.

Azzam Compound

Next to Jeddah International Airport. We built the first villas in 1985 for European staff of the international contractors, and extended it over the years.

European & Arab villas

1 to 5 bedroom villas with their own gardens, in European and Arab standard sections. Mini supermarket, medical clinic, health club with gym, sauna and steam rooms, squash, floodlit tennis, basketball and football courts, a large pool and a children's pool, restaurants, school and shopping buses, broadband and satellite TV.

Labour & staff camps

In Jeddah and Yanbu. Furnished rooms, cleaning, maintenance and pest control, mess hall, laundry for supervisors and engineers, basketball court, with a mini market and bakery next door. Plots in the yard are also leased to companies who build their own offices and warehouses.

Company

Founded by the late Abdul Aziz Ali Al-Ahmed, who called his clients respected friends. Our chairman today is Tarek Abdul Aziz Al-Ahmed.

  1. 1973

    Formation

    Started as an equipment rental company, supplying manpower, materials, demolition and site clearing to large international contractors.

  2. 1985

    Accommodation

    Built a secure European compound for the foreign contractors' staff, later adding Arab standard villas and a labour camp.

  3. 1986

    Contracting

    Re-organised as a construction company: civil, electro-mechanical, steel, precast, drilling and blasting, with facilities set up across the Kingdom.

  4. 1989

    Marine division

    Production licence from SF Marina Systems, Sweden. Concrete floating pontoons made in the Jeddah yard from then on.

  5. 1998

    Yanbu camps

    Labour camp and senior staff villas built at Yanbu for the projects in the area.

  6. 2010

    Bahrain branch

    Office opened in Manama to handle marina products and services for Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar and Yemen.
