About us

Grand Auto Master is a husband and wife team, both from the motor trade. We started in California in 2007 and opened our doors in Dubai in 2017.

How it started

Like every good story, ours began with determination and a dream. Since we opened in Dubai we've earned a name for fixing the most difficult problems, and for restoring classics that nobody in town knew how to work on.

Today we work from a 4,000 square foot workshop in Al Quoz Industrial Area 4, with five mechanical service bays and eight full-time staff.

How we work

Finding the right mechanic is hard, and a lot of people put it off. We know trust has to be earned, so we keep it simple:

  1. 01We never do anything to your car until you've authorised it.
  2. 02We only fix what needs fixing. You'll know what has to be done now and what can wait.
  3. 03We stay in touch at every step, so you're never left wondering where your car is.
  4. 04We treat every customer like family, and your car like our own.

The workshop

Our technicians keep training on new automotive technology and work on both European and other makes. With current diagnostic equipment we find the fault quickly, so your car spends less time with us. Appointments are best, walk-ins are welcome when the schedule allows, and most services are done the same day.
