A dry mix factory in Mesaieed

Qatar Plaster (QP) manufactures dry mix products in Mesaieed Industrial City. We offer a wide range of products based on cement and gypsum for the construction industry.

Silos and machines

We run a modern silo system with pneumatic dry mix conveying, and automatic application machines for the construction industry.

Factory produced dry mix mortar is more reliable and productive than mortar mixed on site. Each product is accurately produced to keep the quality and consistency. We deliver to site in silos or bags.

Our application machines come from PFT, Germany. Mixing by machine gives an even, accurate mix with water and removes the errors that come with hand mixing.

Quality policy

QP products are designed and manufactured to BS / EN / DIN / ASTM specifications.

A modern manufacturing facility and strict quality control keep the products consistent. Our fully equipped testing laboratory works round the clock, and quality control runs from the selection of raw material to the finished product.

We can design, develop and manufacture custom products to a customer's requirements, and our sales, QC and site engineers support you as and when required.
