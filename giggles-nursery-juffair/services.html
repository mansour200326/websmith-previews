The programme

Giggles takes children aged 1 to 4. This is how we teach, the session times on our fee schedule, and what to know about fees before you enrol.

How we teach

01

British EYFS

Our education programme is based on the UK's EYFS (Early Years Foundation Stage) framework. EYFS is a UK government framework that sets welfare and development goals for children aged five and under: the standards for learning, growth, development and care from birth to five.

Getting these areas right early matters for healthy growth and for the learning that comes later.

02

Montessori tools, fun, care and learn

We blend EYFS with Montessori tools, all under one idea: fun, care and learn. Lesson plans, plus mental and physical activities, look after the whole child.

Children build basic recognition skills, like numbers, letters, shapes and colours, and join in fun activities that develop fine motor skills. Time with children of the same age and with teachers helps them communicate.
