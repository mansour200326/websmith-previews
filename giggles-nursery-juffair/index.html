About Giggles

Every child is special and brings bundles of imagination and happiness. At Giggles, we want these tender years to be as joyful and memorable as possible.

We look after toddlers in a secure, neat and hygienic villa, and we follow one idea: learning with fun. Children pick up numbers, letters, shapes and colours, and the time with other children and teachers helps them talk and play together.

More about us →

EYFS, with Montessori tools.

Our programme is based on the UK's Early Years Foundation Stage, the framework that sets the standards for learning, growth and care from birth to five. We blend it with Montessori tools, and plan every day around lessons plus mental and physical activities.
