About Giggles

A nursery and daycare in a villa in Block 324, Juffair, for children aged 1 to 4.

Who we are

Learning with fun

Every child is special and brings bundles of wonderful imagination and happiness. At Giggles, we wish to make these tender years as joyful and memorable as possible.

We follow the philosophy of learning with fun. For children, it's a magical land of their own dreams, a place of love, joy and discovery. Our kids and their families are part of an adventure full of learning, exploration and skill development.

Why it matters

The early years count

Early childhood is a critical time for a child's physical, cognitive, emotional and social development, and it shapes the adult they will become. A child learns to talk at home, but time with children of the same age and with teachers in a preschool helps them build their communication skills.

At Giggles we work to make a child's first learning experience meaningful and full of fun. Our teaching is designed to nurture creativity and develop cognitive and language skills.

Our staff

The team

Our core strength is our team. They have the passion and drive for taking care of toddlers, with excellent qualifications and experience in preschool education.
