How a job works

No booking forms. You ring, we talk it through, and you hear the price before any spanner goes on.

  1. Ring us or email Dan

    Tell us the make, the model and what it's doing. 01202 735826 or dan@libraryroadgarage.co.uk.

  2. Bring it in, or we fetch it

    We're on Library Road, just off Ashley Road. Can't get to us? Collection and delivery are free within a 4 mile radius.

  3. We look it over and quote

    We get a free, no-obligation quote to you as quick as we can.

  4. Nothing extra without asking

    We never carry out additional repairs without telling you what they cost first. No nasty surprises when you collect.

  5. Collect it, paperwork included

    A service comes with your own copy of the checklist for your car, so you can see exactly what was done, and a stamped service book.
