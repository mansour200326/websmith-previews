A three-bay workshop just off Ashley Road in Parkstone, owned and run by Danny Parker.

How we got here

From technician to owner in the same workshop.

  1. 2010

    Library Road Garage opens.

  2. 2011

    The garage joins the Good Garage Scheme in March. Danny Parker starts here as a technician, with many years' experience already gained elsewhere.

  3. 2013

    Danny is appointed manager, and carries on studying to reach Level 4 in advanced diagnostics and management.

  4. 2016

    In late 2016 Danny takes over as owner.

  5. Today

    Rated 98.7% from 337 reviews on the Good Garage Scheme.Source: goodgaragescheme.com, November 2026

How we work

We aim to give every customer a polite, professional answer when it comes to servicing and repairing their car or light commercial vehicle, whatever the make or model.

We check our prices regularly to make sure we're giving great value as well as a top quality service, and we only fit good quality parts.

As a Good Garage Scheme member, the work we do is open to feedback from every customer, and you can read all of it on our profile.
