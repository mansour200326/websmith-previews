Who we are

My Umbrella has over 20 years of experience in the manufacture and export of quality umbrellas, made in Malaysia and China.

We are a direct manufacturer, importer and wholesaler of umbrellas. Buying direct from the maker saves you money and helps you keep your margin, whether the umbrellas are for your own use, for selling or for promotions.

Our range runs from fashion umbrellas to jumbo double canopy and vented golf umbrellas, auto open and foldable umbrellas, tents and canopies, and parasols. They are mainly exported to Europe, the USA, Korea and Southeast Asia.

All of our umbrellas are available plain, or custom silk-screen printed with your company name and logo. A team of designers works on new products all the time, and we take on custom and OEM orders.
