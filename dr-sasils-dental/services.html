Orthodontic care

Where the clinic began in 1993. Straightening teeth and correcting the bite, for children and adults.

01

Braces and orthodontics

Orthodontic treatment moves crooked or crowded teeth into place and corrects how the jaws meet. We use fixed appliances, the braces glued to the teeth, and functional appliances, which guide the growth of the jaws in children.

  • Fixed appliances (braces)
  • Functional appliances

General dentistry

Check-ups, fillings, root canals, crowns and implants: the work that keeps your own teeth working.

02

Check-ups

A routine examination of your teeth and gums, with intra-oral periapical X-rays so the dentist can see the roots and the bone around them. You hear what was found and what, if anything, needs doing.

  • Full dental examination
  • Intra-oral periapical X-rays

Call to book a check-up →

03

Fillings

When decay has made a hole in a tooth, we clean it out and fill it. You can choose a tooth-coloured composite filling or an amalgam filling.

  • Tooth-coloured composite fillings
  • Amalgam fillings

Call to book →

04

Root canal treatment

When the nerve inside a tooth is infected or inflamed, root canal (endodontic) treatment cleans out the inside of the tooth and seals it, so you can keep the tooth instead of losing it.

  • Root canal treatment

Dr. Louis PerumalilEndodontist, B.D.S, M.D.S

Call about a root canal →

05

Crowns and bridges

A crown covers a broken or weak tooth; a bridge fills the gap where a tooth is missing by resting on the teeth either side. We make all-ceramic crowns in Empress and zirconium, and porcelain-fused-to-metal crowns and bridges.

  • All-ceramic crowns: Empress, zirconium
  • Porcelain-fused-to-metal crowns
  • Bridges

Dr. Geetha SasilM ClinDent (Prosthodontics) U.K.

Call about a crown →

06

Implants

An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth. On top of it we fit a crown, a bridge, or an overdenture that clips onto the implants so it stays put.

  • Implant-supported crowns
  • Implant-supported bridges
  • Implant-supported overdentures

Dr. Geetha SasilCert. Implantology

Call about implants →

Cosmetic dentistry

Replacing missing teeth and improving how your smile looks.

07

Dentures and veneers

Dentures replace several or all missing teeth: acrylic, chrome-cobalt (a thin metal frame) or flexible. Veneers are thin ceramic or composite facings bonded to the front of teeth to change their shape or colour.

  • Acrylic dentures
  • Chrome-cobalt dentures
  • Flexible dentures
  • Ceramic and composite veneers

Call to ask →

08

Teeth whitening

For teeth that have become discoloured, we offer bleaching and in-office whitening at the clinic.

  • Bleaching
  • In-office whitening
