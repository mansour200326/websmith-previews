Since 1993

Dr. Sasil Poonnen opened the clinic in 1993 as one of the first orthodontic practices in Sharjah. More than thirty years on, we are still on Al Arouba Street in Rolla.

How the clinic grew

1993

An orthodontic clinic in Rolla

Dr. Sasil Poonnen, an orthodontist with a B.D.S and M.D.S, opens Dr. Sasil's Dental Center on Al Arouba Street. It is one of the first orthodontic clinics in Sharjah.

Then

More specialities under one roof

The clinic adds endodontics (root canals), prosthodontics (crowns, bridges and dentures) and implantology, so patients no longer need to be referred elsewhere for them.

Today

Four dentists

An orthodontist, an endodontist, a prosthodontist with implant training and a dental surgeon, each seeing patients for their own speciality.

Every day

The front desk
