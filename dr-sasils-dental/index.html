1993The year Dr. Sasil Poonnen opened the clinic in Rolla.

We started as an orthodontic clinic and grew with our patients.

Dr. Sasil's was one of the first orthodontic clinics in Sharjah. Over the years we added specialists for root canals, for crowns, bridges and dentures, and for implants, so most families can have everything done here instead of being sent across town.

  • Orthodontics braces and functional appliances
  • Endodontics root canal treatment
  • Prosthodontics crowns, bridges, dentures
  • Implantology implant crowns and bridges
