Before your screening

The questions participants ask us most. Anything else, call 6746 6033.

/01Do I have to fast before the screening?

Yes. No food or drink, except plain water, for 8 hours before. Fasting makes your glucose and cholesterol results more accurate.

/02Can I still be screened if I'm on medication?

Yes. Take your medication as your doctor prescribed. If you are diabetic, please leave out your anti-diabetic drugs that morning.

/03What if I have a fever, or I'm expecting?

Please don't take part. We advise participants with a fever, and expecting mothers, not to join the screening.

/04Can I go ahead during my period?

Yes. If a urine sample is needed, we will arrange to collect it 7 to 10 days after the screening. We'll give you the details on the day.

/05I donated blood yesterday. Is that a problem?

No. Regular blood donors can go ahead; it will not affect your results.

/06Can I take tests other than the basic screening?

Yes. Optional tests are available on top of the basic screening, entirely at your own choice and expense. See the tests

/07How long does it take, and when do I get my report?

About 30 minutes on the day. Reports go to your organisation within 14 working days, each one individually sealed, private and confidential.

/08What if I can't make the on-site day?
