Screening packages

Prevention is better than cure

Health screening finds early disease in people who otherwise feel well, so it can be followed up and treated before it causes serious complications. That's why it is worth being screened even when you feel perfectly healthy. Packages run from basic to comprehensive, plus pre-marital and individual tests. Only 10 cc of blood is needed.

Comprehensive health screening

Eight profiles, each with what it is used for.

Liver profile

Total bilirubin, alkaline phosphatase, SGPT (ALT), SGOT (AST), gamma GT (GGT), total protein, albumin, globulin, alb/glob ratio

  • Screens liver function.
  • Liver function may be affected in Hepatitis B carriers, or by long-term medication or alcohol.

Kidney profile

Urea, creatinine, potassium, sodium, chloride, urine FEME

  • Shows how well the kidneys are working.
  • Raised urea and creatinine may be early signs of kidney failure.
  • Kidney damage often starts with small amounts of albumin leaking into the urine (microalbuminuria), an early sign of nephropathy.

Endocrine profile

Free thyroxine (FT4), thyroid stimulating hormone (TSH)

  • Monitors thyroid hormone function.
  • TSH is made by the pituitary gland and controls the thyroid.

Bone & joint profile

Calcium, phosphate, uric acid, rheumatoid arthritis

  • High calcium and phosphate can come from a range of disorders, including bone disease.
  • High uric acid may lead to gout.
  • Diagnosis and prognosis of rheumatoid arthritis.

Cancer markers

Alpha-fetoprotein (AFP), carcinoembryonic antigen (CEA)

  • Blood test for the cancer marker alpha-fetoprotein.
  • Raised CEA, a protein found in many cells, may be linked with certain tumours.

Venereal profile

VDRL, Syphilis TP Ab

  • Screens for syphilis.

Full blood count

Hb, total WBC, RBC, PLT, ESR, DC, PB

  • Screens for anaemia (low Hb), clotting disorders and infection.

Hb indices

PCV, MCV, MCH, MCHC

  • Screens for anaemia, clotting disorders and infection.

Additional tests

Added to your screening from the same sample. No extra blood is taken. Optional tests are your own choice and at your own expense.

  1. 01

    Ovarian screening (CA125) 卵巢检验

    Many women with ovarian cancer have abnormally high CA125 in their blood, though some non-cancerous conditions can raise it too.

  2. 02

    Prostate screening (PSA) 前列腺检验

    PSA is a protein made by prostate cells, used to screen men for prostate disorders.

  3. 03

    Nasopharyngeal screening (EBV) 喉鼻检验

    Epstein-Barr virus is common and can be linked to several rare cancers, including nasopharyngeal carcinoma.

  4. 04

    Pancreatic screening (CA19.9) 胰腺检验

    The pancreas makes enzymes to digest food, and hormones including insulin to regulate blood sugar.

  5. 05

    Cancer antigen screening (CA15.3) 乳腺检验

    Used with other clinical and diagnostic procedures to monitor the course of disease and therapy in metastatic breast cancer.

  6. 06

    Beta HCG 人绒毛膜促性腺激素

    May be raised in germ cell tumours of the testes, ovaries, liver, stomach, intestines, kidneys, pancreas and lungs.

  7. 07

    C-Reactive Protein (CRP) C 反应蛋白

    Also known as a cardiovascular disease risk factor. It measures general inflammation in the body. Recent research shows a high CRP increases your risk of a heart attack or stroke twice as much as high cholesterol does.

  8. 08

    3gAllergy™ testing (Total IgE) 总免疫球蛋白 E

    Measures the antibodies in blood and tissue that cause allergies, an abnormal reaction to something normally harmless.

  9. 09

    Pre-marital screening

    Women: Rubella IgG antibody and HB electrophoresis (thalassaemia screen). Men: HB electrophoresis (thalassaemia screen). Rubella is often mild but can badly harm an unborn baby; thalassaemia is a genetic blood disorder that can be serious if both parents carry the gene.

  10. 10

    Hepatitis A & B A 和 B 型肝炎

    Both can lead to chronic hepatitis, liver cirrhosis, liver failure and liver cancer.

    “80% of liver cancer patients in Singapore are Hepatitis B carriers.”
