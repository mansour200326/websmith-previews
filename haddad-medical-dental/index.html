To Mum & Dad

Oral health starts young. Our advice for parents, from our children's page.

  • Brush two to three times a day, with a soft children's brush.
  • Use toothpaste the size of your child's little fingernail.
  • Brush at the same time as them. It motivates them.
  • Check their teeth after brushing, and floss every day.
  • Rinse with water after eating or drinking.
  • After brushing at night, only water before bed.
  • Avoid constant snacking and bottle feeding.
  • Bring them for a check-up every six months.

Nursing bottle syndrome: a bottle of milk or juice at bedtime or nap time leads to fast decay and crooked teeth. Give water instead.
