Grooming,and a good coffee

Trim & Latte is a men's grooming, beauty and coffee lounge on Saadiyat Island. Barbering, skin, body, nails and massage under one roof, with an espresso bar in the lounge.

One roof, the whole visit

Whether you're in for a fresh cut, healthier skin, an hour of massage or just a proper coffee, it's all in the same place on the ground floor of Sunrise Residence 4.

The barbers do classic cuts, fades, beards and blade shaves. Karem looks after skin, body and scalp treatments, Cha does hands and feet, and there's a free consultation for hair or skin if you're not sure what you need.

Kids are welcome, and there's a father-and-son booking on the menu. We're open every day from 9am to 9pm.
