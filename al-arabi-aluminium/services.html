What we make

Our in-house engineers handle each job from problem analysis and design to fabrication, installation, testing and technical help after installation. Five areas of work:

01

Aluminium & glazing works

We make and install aluminium and glass work for towers, offices, show rooms, palaces and villas. Our technical engineers help from the start of the project, with guidance through to completion.

  • Curtain walling
  • Doors, windows and hand railings, with automation systems
  • Radar controlled entrance doors and fire rated doors
  • Aluminium and galvanized aluminium skin plates
  • Skylights

02

Rolling shutters

Our technicians make and install rolling shutters made to suit your exact requirements, manual or electrical, with special shutters built for special applications.

Used for reception counters, display areas, serveries, shop fronts, homes and cupboards.

  1. Aluminium rolling shutters
  2. Galvanized rolling shutters
  3. Grill rolling shutters with aluminium tubes
  4. Perforated rolling shutters
  5. Polycarbonate rolling shutters

03

Steel & wrought iron works

Our own wrought iron factory makes decorative work for palaces and private villas, galvanized and powder coated. In steel we make structures for mezzanines, sheds and towers, spiral staircases, style gates for embassies and banks, and electrical barriers for car parks.

Gates

Designed to the client's requirement, from villa gates to main majlis gates.

Grills, railings, fences

Window grills, balcony railings, balustrades and boundary fencing.

Staircases

Spiral staircases and staircase railings.

Sheds & furniture

Car parking sheds, and wrought iron chairs, tables and dining sets to choose from.

04

Automatic systems

We are the sole agent in the UAE for Automatismi Beninca, Italy, an ISO certified maker of automation products. We sell the branded systems and our trained technical team installs them.

  • Swing door operators
  • Sliding gate operators
  • Rolling shutter operators
  • Automation for leaf gates
  • Automation for road barriers
  • Automation for sensor doors

Fitted at: Palace of Ruler of RAK (gate barrier, sliding gate, swing gate), Al Hamra Fort, Sharjah court, Abu Dhabi port.
