We started in 1975 on a 2,200 m² plot in Ras Al Khaimah. Our engineers draw it, our factory cuts, welds and finishes it, and our own teams install it: aluminium, glass, steel and wrought iron, for villas, palaces, towers and shops across the Emirates.
