Since 1975

A Ras Al Khaimah manufacturer of aluminium, rolling shutters, steel and wrought iron, with a factory in Sharjah and an office in Abu Dhabi.

We started in 1975 on a 2,200 m² plot in Ras Al Khaimah, making aluminium for the Gulf.

Later we opened another factory in the emirate of Sharjah, with modern facilities and technically skilled, experienced staff for special work. Then came our office in Abu Dhabi, so we can stay close to our clients and follow the progress of work on site.

Today we take on major projects from all over the Emirates: curtain walling, skylights, doors and windows, radar controlled entrance doors, fire rated doors, rolling shutters with manual gear or remote control, and hand railings.

We make steel structures for mezzanines, sheds and towers, spiral staircases, style gates for embassies and banks, and electrical barriers for car parking. Our own wrought iron factory makes gates, hand railings, furniture and fences for palaces and private villas, galvanized and powder coated. And we have long experience making kitchen cabinets.

Mr. Hassan Jamil Chemeissani Al Arabi was established under his leadership. He is qualified in light metal industries.

How the company is set up

01Design

Our engineering design department works on CAD/CAM. We have our own die equipment to design, fabricate, service and repair all our dies on the premises, and we design new profiles. Each project is checked for structural stability, air tightness and water tightness.

  • CAD/CAM
  • Own dies
  • New profiles

02Fabrication

Fabricated parts, finished products and machined parts in aluminium, with air and water tight hairline joints. Thorough quality checks so the product reaches international standards.

  • TIG/MIG welding
  • Sandwich panelling
  • Sheet bending
  • Rolling

03Projects

Our engineers and technicians on site take a job from design and work execution through installation to handing over to the client, with clear lines of accountability along the way.

  • Extrusion
  • Tooling and welding
  • Quality assurance
  • Metallurgy
