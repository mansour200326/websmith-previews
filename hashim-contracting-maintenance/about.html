Since 2000

Hashim Contracting & General Maintenance was established in Abu Dhabi in 2000 as a general contracting company. It grew into interior design and landscaping, real estate, industrial supplies, transport and logistics, and carpentry and joinery.

Who runs the work

The company is managed and operated by qualified technical staff and a skilled workforce of its own. When a job needs it, we bring in certified outside specialists.

Clients need exact estimating, proficient scheduling, solid construction and real service. We follow professional procedures at the tendering stage, keep to strict quality and safety standards and use current planning and project management methods, so the balance of quality, time and price holds on every building we deliver.

We believe there is more to value than the lowest price. We want to be the one contractor you call for every job, and to keep that relationship through honesty and professionalism.

Chairman's message

We, Hashim Contracting, focus on building the nation and contribute by developing a safe and comfortable infrastructure and living environment.

"Construction is our business and employees are our strength. Hashim Contracting's accomplishments are our inspiration for the company's success. We will aim more, guided by integrity, quality, time, fairness, efficiency and a safe environment.

We are committed to excellence and client satisfaction. We believe that only through outstanding workmanship, superior quality control, effective safety methods and continuous development of our management and skills will our goal of customer satisfaction be achieved."

Vision and mission

Our vision

To be one of the UAE's premier engineering, construction and project management companies: the contractor of choice for large, complex and demanding projects. A company clients want to work with, customers can rely on and employees are proud to work for.

Our mission

Quality construction, technical and management services, and long-term relationships with our clients built on safety, quality, timely service and anticipating what they need. We treat every employee fairly and involve them in improving quality.

QHSE

A zero-accident workplace, with strict compliance to every authority regulation. Published policies: QHSE, OSH and Welfare, plus our ADOSH / OSHAD approval.
