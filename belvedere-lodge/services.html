Breakfast

There are few things more delicious than a quality Irish breakfast, cooked to order.

Or fresh free-range scrambled eggs with delicate swirls of smoked Irish salmon. Vincent McGrath heads up the talented, hard-working team in the kitchen. The breads are baked in the house and the jams are made here, and much of the rest comes from great local producers, among them Clonakilty Puddings and, of course, Barry's Tea.
