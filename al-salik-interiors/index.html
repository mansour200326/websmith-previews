Al Salik Interiors

About us

We started in Dubai in 2003 as an interior decoration and exhibition contractor. Today we make and fit interiors for hotels, restaurants, shops, offices and homes across the Emirates.

We are makers as well as contractors: the wood, metal, glass and acrylic work in our projects is ours, so the ceiling, the counter and the doors fit together.

What we hold ourselves to: good quality, skilled hands, and suppliers who deliver on time, so we do too.
