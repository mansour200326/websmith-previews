Who we are

Al Salik Interiors is an interior decoration and exhibition contracting company based in Dubai. We started in 2003 and we make high quality products from wood, metal, glass and acrylic.

A good part of what we do is hospitality and leisure: restaurants, cafés, kiosks and showrooms. We also fit out offices, flats and villas, and build stands for exhibitions.
