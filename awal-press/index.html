From your file to a finished copy, under one roof

Each stage runs on our own machines in Tubli, from the plate to the shrink-wrap.

  1. 01

    Plates

    Files go straight to plate with CTP, computer-to-plate, inside the Heidelberg workflow.

    CTPHeidelberg workflow
  2. 02

    Print

    4-colour and 5-colour presses with on-line varnishing and infrared drying, and the 8-colour perfecting press.

    4 & 5 colour8-colour perfector
  3. 03

    Cut & fold

    Polar programmable cutters take paper, card, board and plastic board. The MBO combi-folder folds precisely with short make-ready.

    PolarMBO
  4. 04

    Bind

    The Kolbus Ratio gathers, binds, trims and packs on one line, and can shrink-wrap each copy. The Stitchmaster saddle-stitches medium and large editions.

    Kolbus RatioStitchmaster
  5. 05

    Finish

    Spot and full UV coating, hot foil stamping, high-speed lamination, digital die-cutting with camera registration, and screen printing.

    UVHot foilDie-cutting
