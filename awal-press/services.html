The machines, stage by stage

The equipment in our Tubli plant, as our company profile describes it. Open a stage for the detail.

01Prepress and platesHeidelberg workflow, CTP

The new plant runs the fully commissioned German Heidelberg workflow system. With CTP (computer-to-plate), plates come straight from your files, which improved our quality and turnaround for simple jobs and the most complicated ones.

  • Heidelberg workflow system, fully commissioned
  • CTP, computer-to-plate

Ask about this stage →Call +973 77 711 777

02Printing4, 5 and 8 colour

A range of presses from the latest 4-colour and 5-colour machines, with on-line varnishing and infrared drying, to our 8-colour perfecting press, which prints 15,000 sheets an hour. The new facility tripled our printing capacity.

  • 4-colour and 5-colour presses with on-line varnishing
  • Infrared drying
  • 8-colour perfecting press, 15,000 sheets an hour

Ask about this stage →Call +973 77 711 777

03Cutting and foldingPolar, MBO

Polar programmable cutting machines cut paper, cardstock, paper board and plastic board with precision. The MBO combi-folding machine is flexible, with short make-ready, little downtime and precise folds, which is what gives a clean binding finish.

  • Polar programmable cutters
  • MBO combi-folding machine

Ask about this stage →Call +973 77 711 777

04BindingKolbus Ratio, Stitchmaster, spiral, eyelets

The Kolbus Ratio binder gathers, binds, trims and packs on a single production line, and can shrink-wrap printed work so each copy is sealed on its own. Catalogues, paperbacks and directories made in-line are an Awal Press speciality. The Heidelberg Stitchmaster saddle-stitches medium to large editions, with stapling and trimming built in, run from one touch-screen.

  • Kolbus Ratio: gathering, binding, trimming, packing, shrink-wrap
  • Heidelberg Stitchmaster saddle stitching
  • Spiral binding and eyelet machines

Ask about this stage →Call +973 77 711 777

05FinishingUV, foil, lamination, die-cutting, screen

A fully automatic digital die-cutting machine cuts accurate, delicate shapes, fed automatically and kept in register by a camera. Alongside it: high-speed lamination, hot foil stamping, hand-fed die-cutting, and a UV coating machine for spot and full UV at 1,000 sheets an hour. We also screen print.

  • Digital die-cutting with camera-guided registration
  • UV coating, spot and full, 1,000 sheets an hour
  • Hot foil stamping and high-speed lamination
  • Screen printing
