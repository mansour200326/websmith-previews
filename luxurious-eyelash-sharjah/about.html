A ladies salon in Muwaileh

Luxurious Eyelash is a woman-owned beauty salon for ladies in the ASAS Q4 Building, Muwaileh Commercial, Sharjah.

Lashes are in the name, and the menu goes on from there: nails, brows, waxing, hair and massage, seven days a week.

Who you'll see

Four of us take bookings on Fresha. Ask for anyone by name when you book.

  • GulManicures and pedicures, including the Russian method. Clients book her by name.
  • NafisaPart of the team, bookable on Fresha.
  • GulizaLash technician: extensions, refills and lifts. Brow lifts too.
  • AnjeeAll-rounder, across the menu.
