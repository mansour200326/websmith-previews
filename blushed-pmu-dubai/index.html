Blushed is a small studio in office 110 of Park Towers A. Nano stroke brows, ombré lip blush, lash line, lash lifting and brow lamination, and removal when old permanent makeup has to go.

Book how you like. Message on WhatsApp, or book on Fresha with instant confirmation and pay in the app.

Adults only. Prices run from AED 100 to AED 2,000 depending on the treatment.
