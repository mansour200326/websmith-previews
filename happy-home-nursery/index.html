Classes and times

Which class is my child in?

Children start with us from one year old and stay until their fourth birthday, plus a few months depending on the school term. Lessons are in English. Arabic and Islamic studies are available on request.

Playgroup18 months to 2 yearsCare and learning planned around what toddlers need and are interested in at this age.
Nursery2 to 3 yearsA programme built to support each child's development during an important year.
Preschool3 to 4 yearsGetting children ready for school: the knowledge and skills they need for what comes next.

Keeping them safe

  • A nurse on site, full timeA qualified, DHA approved nurse runs our own clinic. She keeps an eye on each child's health, eating and safety, and a visiting doctor checks on the children's general health.
  • School bus across Sharjah cityOur buses have CCTV and seat belts, with a trained female bus attendant and driver. Seats are limited and go first come, first served, so ask the office early.
  • Trained staffEvery teacher is trained in early childhood, child protection and safety, and fire fighting, with in-house or outside training every quarter.
  • Written policiesPolicies and procedures for child protection, security and safeguarding, available from the office.

Registering your child

Admissions are open for Playgroup, Nursery and Preschool. Collect the registration forms from the nursery office and bring these with you:

  1. Completed registration forms, with the terms and conditions signed
  2. Copy of your child's passport with the UAE residence visa page
  3. Copy of the sponsor's passport with the UAE visa page
  4. Copy of your child's Emirates ID
  5. Copy of your child's birth certificate
  6. Six passport-size photos of your child
  7. Copy of your child's immunisation record
  8. Child medical history form (from the nursery)
  9. Signed welcome circular
  10. Emergency contact and pick-up authorisation form

Want to see the classrooms first? Call the office, or send us a WhatsApp with your child's age.
