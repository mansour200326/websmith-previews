How we work

A specialist for each job

Endodontists for root canals, orthodontists for braces, oral surgeons and implantologists for implants, a prosthodontist for crowns and bridges, a children's dentist for the little ones.

Teeth and skin together

At Al Muntazah you can see the dentist and the dermatologist at the same address.

Steps explained first

Many people are nervous at the dentist. Our doctors explain each step from start to finish, and reception, nurses and doctors try to make the visit as relaxed as possible.
