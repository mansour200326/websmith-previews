01Servicing and repairsGeneral servicing and repairs for all makes and models

  • General servicing
  • Repairs and maintenance to keep it MOT-ready
  • New and pattern parts supplied and fitted
  • All makes and models

02MOT preparationWe get it ready, then submit it for its MOT

We check the car over and do the work to MOT standard first, then submit it for the test, so it has the best chance of passing first time.

  • Pre-MOT check
  • Repairs to MOT standard
  • We take it for the test

04Cam beltsReplaced on time, with good quality belts

Most cars now run a toothed cam belt instead of a chain. It has to be changed at the maker's time or mileage interval, or it can snap and wreck the engine. We do the full job with high quality belts.

Slipping, juddering or a pedal that bites high: we replace clutches on all vehicles.
