Getting here

424 Smollett St, Albury NSW 2640, opposite OfficeWorks.

Driving from Sydney

Leave the Hume Freeway at the South Albury East Street exit. Turn right at the lights, go under the freeway to the roundabout and turn right. Keep to the right lane and turn right at the lights by the Good Guys. Turn left at the next lights and we're right there, opposite OfficeWorks.

Driving from Melbourne

Leave the Hume Freeway at the city centre East Street exit. Turn left at the lights, then right at the roundabout. Get into the right-hand lane and turn right at the lights by the Good Guys. At the next lights turn left and we're right there, opposite OfficeWorks.

By train or bus

We're a two-minute walk from the railway station, the bus station and the information centre. Cross at the traffic lights and we're just opposite OfficeWorks.

Albury sits on the NSW and Victoria border, on the Murray River, and is a handy stop on the Hume Highway between Sydney or Canberra and Melbourne.

Around Albury

Closest first, measured from the motel.

  1. under 1 kmSundays

    Albury Wodonga Community Market

    Everything from worm farms to woollen socks, flowers to fishing tackle, pottery to produce. Every Sunday, 9am to 12.30pm, at the Wilson Street car park. Entry is by donation, and the money goes to local and international Rotary projects.

  2. 1.3 km

    Albury Botanic Gardens

    Four hectares in the heart of the city near the Murray River and the riverside parks, with more than 1,000 native and exotic plant species.

  3. 3 km

    Monument Hill War Memorial

    The monument was designed by architect Louis Harrison in 1923. Below it is a Memorial Bowl honouring the men and women who served in World War II, and walking tracks run to and around the site.

  4. 5 km

    Albury Racing Club

    Albury's first race meeting was held in 1840, and the club is proud of its long and colourful history.

  5. 17 km

    Hume Dam and Lake Hume

    The major dam across the Murray River, and home to the Lake Hume Cycle Challenge, which raises money for the Albury Wodonga Regional Cancer Centre. Lake Hume is a favourite for water sports.

  6. under 200 kmVictorian Alps

    Falls Creek and Mt Hotham snowfields

    Up in the Victorian Alps, at 1,862 m. Many skiers drive up for the major events, and Albury is the natural overnight stop on the way.

Also nearby: wineries and vineyards, the Performing Arts Centre, the art gallery, theatre and cinemas, clubs, historical sites, canoe hire, the wetlands, children's play centres, sport and leisure facilities, antiques and collectables, and the 'Man From Snowy River' Bush Festival.
