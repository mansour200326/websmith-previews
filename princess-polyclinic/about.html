The clinic

A polyclinic for beauty, health and wellness on the third floor of Blue Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road.

Who we are

Princess Polyclinic is owned and run by Jonalyn Cordova Pagal, who has worked in this field for five years.

We opened the clinic to give people good healthcare, beauty and wellness treatment in a place that feels welcoming, with the standards you expect from a medical clinic. Our aim is simple: to be a clinic people in Dubai trust and come back to.

Treatments are led by our Medical Director and Aesthetic GP, Dr. Rediet. Every patient is checked first: blood pressure, pulse, temperature, oxygen, breathing rate, weight and BMI.
