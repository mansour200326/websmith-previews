Inside

Everything at Kanz Gym is on one long floor in Al Butain. Here it is, area by area, with real photos from inside. Want to know about membership or come and see it? Message us.

02 / 07

Plate-loaded machines

A long line of black plate-loaded machines. You load the plates yourself, so the weight feels like a barbell, but the machine keeps the path steady. Good for pushing heavy without a spotter.

  • Chest press and incline press
  • Lat pulldown and high row
  • Seated row
  • Plates kept right by the machines

03 / 07

Racks & barbells

Power racks with pull-up bars for squats, presses and pull-ups, a Smith machine, and a full rack of straight and EZ-curl bars. Bench stations sit close by.

  • Power racks with pull-up bars
  • Smith machine
  • Straight and EZ-curl barbells
  • Flat and incline bench press stations
Ask about the racks on WhatsApp →
04 / 07

Leg day

A 45-degree leg press and a hack squat stand side by side against the yellow wall, on rubber floor tiles. The racks are a few steps away when you want to squat with a bar.

  • 45-degree leg press
  • Hack squat
  • Rubber floor tiles
  • Power racks close by

05 / 07

Cables & machines

A cable crossover station in the middle of the floor and pin-loaded machines where you set the weight with a pin. Easy to learn if you're new, and good for finishing off a session.

  • Cable crossover station
  • Pin-loaded machines
  • Set the weight with a pin, no plates to carry

06 / 07

Cardio

Treadmills in a row facing the big windows, a stair climber, and recumbent bikes under the wall mural. The whole floor is air-conditioned.

  • Treadmills along the windows
  • Stair climber
  • Recumbent bikes
  • Air-conditioned floor

07 / 07

Open floor

A rubber-tiled open space with medicine balls along the wall and a big mirror. Room for warm-ups, stretching, core work and anything that needs floor space.

  • Medicine balls
  • Mirror wall
  • Open rubber floor
