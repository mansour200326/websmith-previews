WorkshopHow an order goes through our shop.

  1. Cutting & forging

    We heat the end of the round bar by induction and forge the head on National Upsetters. The heads can go on before or after threading, and on an upsetter the bolt length isn't limited the way it is on a press.

  2. Threading

    Roll threading up to 1″ diameter, which is quicker and often cheaper, and cut threading up to 6″ diameter. Our threads don't gall or fight the nut, even after galvanizing.

  3. Bending

    J bolts, bent anchor bolts, square and round U bolts, open and closed eyes, square, round and custom hooks.

  4. Galvanizing

    Our galvanizing line is set up for threaded fasteners, so the threads come out clean and the nut fits without a fight. Everything is hot dipped to ISO 1461, comparable to ASTM A-153, under our own QC department.

  5. Testing & certificates

    Rotational capacity, mechanical and hardness tests at regular intervals in a climate-controlled facility. Operators check with tri-roll gages, ring gages, thread micrometers and a thread comparator. Every certificate is stored electronically and we email you the PDF as soon as your order ships.

  6. Packing & shipping

    Strong boxes, palletized, banded and shrink wrapped. Large, clear labels, and the boxes holding the certification documents are marked so your store keeper finds them.

CoatingsFinishes we apply or arrange through our coating partners.

Hot dip galvanized ISO 1461

Full immersion in molten zinc at around 400°C, spun to keep the threads clean. Done in-house.

Electrolytic zinc plating 5–40 µm

Smooth, bright zinc layer from our in-house plating facility, so it doesn't hold up delivery.

PTFE / Xylan coated Whitford, UK

Fluoropolymer topcoat for low friction and corrosion resistance, often over zinc plating.

Zinc yellow bichromated 5–40 µm

Electrolytic zinc with a yellow chromate finish.

Dacromet

Thin zinc and aluminium flake layers, good for small fasteners.

Sherardising 15–25 µm

Zinc dust in a heated barrel at about 420°C forms a zinc-iron alloy layer.

Mechanical zinc plating 3–80 µm

Tumbled with glass beads and zinc dust. No hydrogen embrittlement.

Electroless nickel plated 20–50 µm

Hard, corrosion resistant nickel-phosphorus layer, matt, bright or semi-bright.

Cadmium plated

Listed with our special coatings; ask us for details.

Chromium plating

Over a nickel base, for a decorative finish with corrosion protection.
