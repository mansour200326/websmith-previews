06

Rental terms

A short summary. The full terms are in the rental agreement you sign.

Age
Renters must be 25 or over. For drivers between 21 and 25, an extra deductible of 10% of an insurance claim applies, on top of the insurance excess.
Licence
A valid licence for the area, held for at least one year.
Rental day
24 hours, with two hours' grace for returning the car. After that, an extra full day is charged.
Drivers
Only the renter and any additional driver named in the agreement.
Not allowed
Carrying people or goods for hire, towing, races or tests, driving under the influence, carrying more people than the car is permitted, driving lessons, and leaving Oman without our agreement.
Traffic fines
Paid by the renter as and when they are received, with the service charge for settling them.
Tyres
Tyre repairs and replacement are the renter's responsibility, whatever the insurance.
Accidents and theft
Report to the police immediately and to us within 24 hours, or any CDW or theft protection you took becomes void.

Driving in Oman

From our driving tips and safety notes.

  • The roadsMotorways link Muscat with all major cities and towns in Oman, and there are excellent roads leading to Dubai. Vehicles drive on the right.
  • Wadis and desertA four-wheel drive is highly recommended for wadis. For off-road desert trips, go with an experienced guide and a well-equipped 4WD.
  • RainEven light rain can make the smooth roads very slippery. Wadis can become impassable in heavy rain.
  • Camels and sandstormsWatch for them on highways outside the cities. Slow down or stop and let camels cross.
  • Seat beltsEveryone in the car should be buckled up. Driving without a seat belt is fined.
  • AccidentsStay with the car and report to the police straight away. Leaving the scene before the police arrive is an offence. Royal Oman Police: 999.

Hear an unusual noise from the car? Report it to the Al Maskry Rent-A-Car office straight away.
