Before you drive

Age
Renters 25 or over. For drivers between 21 and 25, an extra deductible of 10% of an insurance claim applies, on top of the excess.
Licence
A valid licence, held for at least one year. An international driving licence is valid in Oman.
Rental day
24 hours, with two hours' grace when you return the car.
Leaving Oman
Only with our agreement first.
Traffic fines
Paid by the renter, as and when they arrive.
999

Royal Oman Police hotline. Call it for emergency help on the road or after an accident. Stay with the car, and report the accident to us within 24 hours.

From our driving tips: Watch out for camels and sandstorms especially when you are on the highways outside city limits.
