About Al Maskry

“People are our business.”Amar Suleiman Said Al Maskry, Managing Director

Al Maskry & Partner Co. LLC is one of the oldest car hire and leasing companies in the Sultanate of Oman, and also runs a transport division and inbound tourism services.

Who we work for

We provide car hire, leasing and travel services to individual and corporate customers. Part of our work is for a premium clientele: leading corporate houses and businesses, international businessmen, and tourists visiting the Sultanate of Oman.

Our fleet runs from executive saloon cars to luxury vehicles, four-wheel drives and commercial pick-ups. Our flexible leasing options, comprehensive maintenance and 24x7 roadside assistance are there so you get more from every rental.

Al Maskry also has a transport division, with a fleet of buses of 14, 25, 30 and 60 seats.
