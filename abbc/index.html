About

Active in building construction since 1977.

AB Building Contracting Co. L.L.C. has been executing projects — towers, multi-storey residential and commercial buildings, villas, mosques, sheds, showrooms and warehouses — across the United Arab Emirates since 1977.

The confidence clients and consultants have placed in the company helped it complete more than 310 projects by the year ending 2009.

A team of experts headed by General Manager Eng. Kasem M. Sayed is available to execute and complete projects on time. Should a project need more manpower, the company can draw on its group's resources to supplement the work force.
