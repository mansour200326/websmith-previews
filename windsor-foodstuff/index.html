HACCP certified

Certified by BM TRADA, the UK certification body accredited by UKAS. Every operation follows Good Manufacturing Practice.

  1. Our own laboratoryStaffed and equipped to test every incoming raw material and all packaging before it is used.
  2. Quality assuranceProgrammes in place so products meet the properties and specifications agreed with the customer.
  3. Clean storageVentilated, hygienic stores, and temperature-controlled rooms for the materials that need them.
  4. Made and packed hereManufacturing and packaging lines under one roof in Industrial Area 3, Sharjah.
