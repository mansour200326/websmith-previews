About Windsor

Windsor Foodstuff Factory makes and supplies some of the Gulf's most popular food products, from our premises in a busy industrial estate in Sharjah.

1995

Our story

We started in 1995. Today our factory in Industrial Area 3 has its own manufacturing and packaging lines, with HACCP certification.

From beverages to desserts, condiments to flavourings, we are equipped to serve the retail trade, private labels and the food service sector. Our own brands are Kenbee, Vivita, Sunquench, Rotana and Jus-C.

Quality policy

We are a HACCP-certified manufacturer, certified by BM TRADA in the UK, a certification body accredited by UKAS (United Kingdom Accreditation Service).

Everything in the factory runs to Good Manufacturing Practice, with quality assurance programmes in place so products meet the properties and specifications agreed with the customer.

  1. LaboratoryOur own well-equipped, professionally staffed laboratory tests all incoming raw materials and packaging for safety and quality.
  2. StorageAll materials are kept in ventilated, hygienic stores, and in temperature-controlled rooms where they need it.
  3. Good Manufacturing PracticeEvery operation follows GMP.
  4. Agreed specificationsQuality assurance programmes hold every product to the properties and specifications we promised.

New product development

With our own production lines and know-how, we can change a recipe to suit a customer or a market. We were a pioneer in producing rose water in the GCC.

We keep growing and look for new chances to learn, work together and build food businesses. If you are taking the next step with a food product, talk to us.
