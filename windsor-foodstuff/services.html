Contract manufacturing & private label

Research and development runs through the whole factory: raw material sourcing, product development, the production process and the marketing plan. Every department works together on the quality of the product, the service and the success of the brands we make.

We change recipes to suit a customer or a market. We were a pioneer in producing rose water in the GCC, and we keep looking for new partners to develop products with.

Distributors & agents

We make and distribute our own brands, Kenbee, Vivita, Sunquench, Rotana and Jus-C, at retail and wholesale, and for the HORECA trade. In each market we work with one exclusive agent, and our export team gives that agent marketing support.
