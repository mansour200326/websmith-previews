The salon

Hair, colour, nails, pedicure and massage under one roof, in Shop 12 of the Office Park Building in Dubai Internet City.

“Sasalon is not just a brand; it is style, elegance and a lot of wellness.”

The welcome comes first. Before a treatment, a colour or even a simple blow-dry, we want you to have a real moment to yourself, and a little extra care that makes the visit feel like yours.

Every corner has its own job: the hair floor, the colour and wash bar, the nail bar, the pedicure chairs, a private treatment room, and a coffee corner for the wait.
