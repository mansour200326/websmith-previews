CMMA solid surface

200°C

CMMA takes heat up to 200°C for 30 minutes without changing its colour or surface.

It carries all the specifications of acrylic solid surface, and on some points it goes higher, for these reasons:

  1. Unbreakable. It is moulded under a pressure of 2,000 ton per square cm.
  2. Takes heat. Up to 200°C for 30 minutes, no change in colour or surface.
  3. Ready sections. Made for mass production, so there is less cutting and joining. It saves time and manpower.
  4. Competitive price compared with other products.
  5. Made in South Korea.
