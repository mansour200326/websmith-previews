Services

A full range of hair services, plus eyebrow and eyelash tinting and brow, chin and lip waxing. In the salon in the main foyer, or up on the ward if you're a patient.

01

Cutting and styling

Our senior stylists are fully qualified in cutting and have years of experience. Kathy, who owns the salon, is known as one of the best style-cutters in town.

  • Cutting by senior stylists
  • Advice on “styling for you”
  • Advice on hair maintenance
  • Late nights by appointment
Book a cut: 03 365 0548 →
02

Colour

Our senior stylists are fully qualified in colours, and colour is Kathy's speciality. We'll talk it through with you first and give you advice on looking after it with the right products.

  • Colour by qualified senior stylists

  • Advice on hair products
  • Joico, Nak and Clairol Professional in stock
