Kathy

Owner of New U Hair Design and a professional hair stylist. She has run the salon in Christchurch Public Hospital since 1990.

Kathy's story

  1. Hair Affair

    Where she trained

    Kathy did her training at Hair Affair.

  2. 1984

    SonShine Hair Design

    She opened her own salon, SonShine Hair Design.

  3. 1990

    Into the hospital, as New U

    Kathy moved the salon to its current spot in the main foyer of Christchurch Public Hospital, and it became New U Hair Design.

Known as one of the best style-cutters in town, with a speciality in colour.

The salon

A professional hair salon in the hospital's main foyer, open to patients, staff, visitors and the public. Our stylists know how to work with patients in a medical setting and can visit the ward if needed.
