Patients, staff, visitors and the public

The salon is open to anyone who walks through the main entrance, not only people staying in the hospital.

  1. Patients

    Our stylists are used to working with patients in a hospital setting, and we can do ward visits if you can't come down.

  2. Staff

    We're in the main foyer, Monday to Saturday. Late nights by appointment.

  3. Visitors

    Tea and coffee are on while you're in the chair.

  4. The public

    You don't need a reason to be at the hospital. Ring for an appointment like any other salon.

Can't come down? We come up to the ward.

Our stylists have experience working with patients in a medical setting. If you or someone you're visiting can't make it to the foyer, ring the salon and we'll arrange a ward visit.

Kathy

Kathy trained at Hair Affair, then opened her own salon, SonShine Hair Design, in 1984. In 1990 she moved into Christchurch Public Hospital, and the salon became New U Hair Design.

She's known as one of the best style-cutters in town, and colour is her speciality.
