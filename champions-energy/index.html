What we do

Steel and metal work for building sites, roads, events and secure areas: fabrication, supply, erection and installation, engineering and sub-contracting.

Supply

Fence panels, mesh, wire, posts, gates, barricades and bollards, from ready stock or to order. Steel for projects too: sheets, plates, pipes, beams, angles and gratings.

Fabrication

Gates, handrails, railings, cages, partitions, platforms, sheds, frames and towers, made to your drawings. Galvanised, powder coated in an approved RAL colour, or epoxy.

Installation

Erection on site across the UAE, foundations included: pre-cast concrete blocks, casting on site, or continuous concrete beam foundations.
