About us

We are a family school set in the heart of Al Ain, where we have been helping students to achieve their potential and realise their dreams since 1989. Today 2,797 students from 39 nationalities learn with us, from KG1 to Grade 12.

Vision and mission

Our vision

Al Dhafra Private Schools will enshrine educational excellence, where culture and tradition are honoured and everyone is safe. Students attending Al Dhafra Private Schools will become internationally-minded, global citizens, possessing the skills and character to thrive in a challenging and changing world.

Our mission

To provide a trusting and supportive school community, in which students are placed at the centre of learning, developing personal responsibility and self-discipline, as they become independent and creative learners and thinkers. Teaching will be inspiring and all students empowered to succeed within an innovative culture of high expectations, high standards, and outstanding quality.

What we believe in

Our six core values, as we wrote them for students, parents and staff.

  1. Transparency, fairness and justiceEveryone will know what is expected of them, and will understand how performance is judged. We will be clear, sincere, and honest.
  2. Respect and equalityWe will treat everyone with respect, value and embrace diversity, and show our appreciation for effort and achievement.
  3. Cooperation, collaboration and teamworkWe will work well together and will support each other in achieving the targets and goals of the school.
  4. Care and compassionWe will be tolerant, and will treat people as we would like to be treated ourselves.
  5. Honesty and integrityWe will always act with integrity and honesty, regardless of the circumstances, but will do so in a sensitive and thoughtful way.
  6. AccountabilityWe will accept a culture of professional and personal accountability, in which we strive for the highest standards.

Welcome to Al Dhafra

It is both an honor and a privilege to join the Al Dhafra School family as your new principal. As I take on this role, I am inspired by the school's steadfast commitment to excellence and its mission to cultivate learners who are prepared to succeed in an ever-changing world.

At Al Dhafra School, we are passionate about developing well-rounded individuals. Beyond strong academics, we place great emphasis on character, creativity, and curiosity. Our vision is to foster a culture of innovation and inclusion, where every student feels valued, supported, and inspired to discover their unique talents and contributions.

Education is a shared journey, and I am a strong believer in the power of collaboration. When students, parents, educators and the community work together, the possibilities for growth and achievement are limitless.

I encourage feedback and suggestions aimed at enhancing teaching and learning, while addressing the needs of students, educators and parents.

Dr. Salam Omar, Principal · principalpa.al@dhafraschools.com
