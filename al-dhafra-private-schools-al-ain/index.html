About the school

We are a family school in the heart of Al Ain. Children start with us in KG1 and follow the English National Curriculum to the end of Grade 8. In Grade 9 each student chooses the English or the American curriculum through to Grade 12.

School life

Rooms our students use every day.

Every child, looked after

Al Dhafra is an inclusive school. Admission is fair to every child, with assessments and interviews suited to their needs.

People of Determination

A department run by our Special Educational Needs Coordinator: an IEP for each student, adjusted materials, extra help from SEN assistants and regular progress reviews.

Gifted and Talented

Programmes drawn from the American and British curricula, with competitions through the year: storytelling, Quran recitation, Arabic calligraphy, poetry.

Health clinic

School nurses on site for health, safety and day-to-day care.

Student council

Students have their own council and a say in school life.

Fees

Tuition is paid in three instalments: by 14 September, 31 December and 31 March. Cash, cheque (to "Al Dhafra Private Schools") or bank transfer. Uniform and books are separate.

Bring these documents

  1. Two copies of the passport with a valid residence visa
  2. Two copies of the birth certificate
  3. Four passport-size photographs
  4. Immunization card
  5. Emirates ID card
  6. Transfer certificate from the previous school (Grades 2 to 12)
  7. From overseas: report card, transfer and birth certificates attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the UAE Embassy
