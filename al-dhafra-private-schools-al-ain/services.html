American stream, Grades 9 to 12

Three pathways

Every pathway prepares for graduation and university admission. Advanced and Elite add AP exams, passed with a score of 3 or more. AP results come out in July.

PathwayWhat it takesAP exams
GeneralAt least five subjects: 4 units of English Language Arts, Mathematics, Science including Physics, and History and Social ScienceNone required
AdvancedGeneral, plus AP Calculus AB, AP Biology or AP Chemistry, and calculus-based Physics2
EliteAdvanced, plus AP Physics C: Mechanics, AP Physics C: Electricity and Magnetism, and one more AP elective5

Support and school life

Al Dhafra is an inclusive school that caters to students with different needs.

People of Determination

A department run by our SENCO. Each student gets a special learning programme (IEP), adjusted materials and equipment, extra help from SEN assistants, and regular review of progress.

Gifted and Talented

Programmes drawn from the American and British curricula and matched to each student's strengths. Competitions include storytelling, Holy Quran recitation, a live art gallery, poetry and Arabic calligraphy.

Library

Almost 10,000 books, fiction, non-fiction and reference, open the whole school day. Plus an e-library for Arabic and non-Arabic speakers.

Health clinic

School nurses look after students' health and safety and work with families.

After school

Sports club and after-school activities, student council, and a published canteen menu.

For parents

The School Management System and the Dhafra mobile app (Android and iOS): weekly plans, assignments and grades, any time.
