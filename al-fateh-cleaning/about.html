Our company

Al Fateh Cleaning and Maintenance Company has been cleaning buildings across Bahrain since 1992.

We can field enough experienced staff to clean a wide range of structures: commercial offices, warehouses, industrial buildings, office parks, retail shops, malls, hotels, residences and apartments. We also do specialised cleaning for health care providers such as rest-homes, for schools and other education providers, and for government institutions.

On top of general cleaning and maintenance we do carpet, sofa and curtain shampooing, steam cleaning, office equipment maintenance, gardening and landscaping, marble restoration, and pest control and sanitizing. Our cleaners come with all the equipment and materials the job needs.

Our goal is simple: all of a customer’s commercial cleaning needs in one package. One company, one solution.

Registered management system, BS EN ISO 9001:2015, audited by Quay Audit UK Ltd (ASCB accredited).

How we work

  1. Modern equipment, fair cost

    We use up-to-date international technology in our service equipment and offer a competitive cost that suits your budget.

  2. Used to international standards

    We work with international facility management companies such as DTZ, HOCHTIEF and EMCOR, and meet their level of service requirements.

  3. Work that is checked

    A monitoring system follows our manpower’s performance on site, so the work is done properly.

  4. Trained people

    We give our staff in-house and international training.

  5. A call centre, 24 hours a day

    Our customer call centre is there for your specific needs around the clock.
