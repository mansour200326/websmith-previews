About Al Fateh

We send trained cleaning teams into offices, warehouses, malls, hotels, apartments, schools, rest-homes and government buildings across the Kingdom of Bahrain, and we bring all the equipment and materials with us.

Next to the daily cleaning we look after the building itself: A/C, electrical and plumbing work, swimming pools, gardens, marble, carpets and curtains, and pest control. One company for the whole building.
