Samira & the team

Bellissima has been owned and run by Samira Hanif since 1990, right in the heart of Sale. Over the years the salon has expanded and always looks to update its treatments with the latest trends and technology, while giving every client a warm, relaxed and friendly welcome.

Samira Hanif

Owner · Cidesco qualified · Guinot trained since 1990

“I have had Bellissima Beauty salon for over 30 years now, since 1990. I have been a qualified beauty therapist for over 30 years.”

Samira is qualified in Cidesco International and has been Guinot trained since 1990. She specialises in laser hair removal, skin rejuvenation, aromatherapy, Indian head massage, reflexology, broken capillaries and skin tag removal, electrolysis, Cosmelan, transdermic roller therapy, facial peels and the BTL machine.

Abbie Wright

Senior beauty therapist · Level 2 & 3 beauty therapy

Abbie has been at Bellissima for just over 12 years and has built up a lovely client base. She specialises in 14 day manicures, intimate waxing, semi-permanent lash extensions, Guinot facials, make-up removal, the BTL machine and Bare Minerals make-up.

Alongside Samira and Abbie there are currently four full-time and two part-time therapists at Bellissima. Ask for anyone by name when you ring.
