Car servicing

Naturally, you want the best value and performance from your car, and that's why regular maintenance matters. It helps the car run better and last longer, and it keeps the resale and trade-in value up.

We typically do three types of service:

  • Oil service
  • General service
  • Full service

We reset the service indicator on every car. A service doesn't include items like tyres, exhausts and timing belts: they don't need replacing as often as the car needs servicing, so we quote for them separately when they're due.

02

Timing belts

Timing belts are changed to the manufacturer's specification, by mileage or by age. We also listen for abnormal noises from the belt during every service. Leave it past the right interval, or carry on with the noise, and it can lead to engine failure.

03

Clutches

Clutches are replaced for four main reasons:

  • Normal wear and tear, where the clutch is worn out and slipping
  • Gears are hard to select
  • A noisy release bearing, so you hear it when you press the clutch
  • Juddering, normally caused by oil leaking onto the clutch lining
04

Suspension

The suspension's job is to keep the tyres gripping the road surface, to give steering stability and good handling, and to keep the passengers comfortable. When we service your car we report any repairs the shocks or suspension need, and replace parts as you ask.

05

Steering & tracking

Worn steering parts cause uneven tyre wear and change how the car drives. We change any steering parts needed for your safety, and re-align the steering to stop the uneven wear.

06

Tyres & exhausts

When we inspect the car at its service, we'll tell you if it needs new tyres or an exhaust. We do a price check for you, to see whether we can do the job or recommend another option.

07

Emissions

Fuel economy is always a worry for motorists. We have the equipment to check your emissions and make sure the car is running as economically as possible.

08

Car diagnostics

Modern cars have several on-board computers: engine management, ABS and airbags. When one of them finds a fault, a warning light flashes or stays lit on the dashboard. Our advanced diagnostic equipment helps us find the fault and fix it.

Questions we often get asked

Something else about servicing or looking after your car? Email us and we'll try to answer it.

My car is under the manufacturer's warranty. Will servicing it outside the main dealer affect that?

No. EU rules for the motor trade let motorists use the garage of their choice to service their cars without invalidating the manufacturer's warranty.

Can you reset my service indicator?

Yes. We reset the service indicator on all cars.

Is VAT included, and can I have a VAT receipt?

Yes. VAT is included in all prices we quote, and we issue a full report and VAT invoice when the work is done.

Do you have a loan car I can use while mine is being serviced?

Yes, but you must have fully comprehensive insurance.

How can I pay?

By Mastercard, Visa or cash.

Do you have insurance?

Yes. All of our drivers are comprehensively insured, and we have Public Liability Insurance in place.

What happens to the waste oil and oil filters?

All of our waste oil and oil filters are recycled in a responsible and environmentally friendly way.

How often should I check the levels under the bonnet?

We recommend you check your oil and water every 500 miles.
