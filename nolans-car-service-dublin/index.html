Other work

01Timing beltsChanged to the manufacturer's mileage or age

We also listen for abnormal noises from the timing belt at every service. Leave it past the right interval, or ignore the noise, and it can mean engine failure.

Ask about your timing belt →

02ClutchesSlipping, hard to select gears, noisy or juddering
  • Normal wear and tear: the clutch is worn out and slipping
  • Gears are hard to select
  • Noisy release bearing when you press the clutch
  • Juddering, usually oil leaking onto the clutch lining

Ring about a clutch →

03SuspensionShocks and suspension parts, checked at every service

Suspension keeps the tyres gripping the road, keeps the steering stable and the passengers comfortable. When we service the car we report any repairs the shocks or suspension need, and replace parts as you ask.

04Steering & trackingWorn parts replaced, steering re-aligned

Worn steering parts wear your tyres unevenly and change how the car drives. We change the parts needed for your safety and re-align the steering to stop the uneven wear.

05Tyres & exhaustsReported at the service, with a price check

We'll tell you when you need new tyres or an exhaust, do a price check, and either fit them for you or recommend another option.

06EmissionsChecked so the car runs as economically as it can

Fuel economy is always a worry. We have the equipment to check your emissions and make sure the car is running as economically as possible.

07Car diagnosticsEngine management, ABS and airbag warning lights

When one of the car's on-board computers finds a fault, a light flashes or stays on in the dash. Our advanced diagnostic equipment helps us find it and fix it.

A light on the dash? Call us →

08Valeting on requestDone the same day as the service

Give us notice and we'll have the car valeted on the day we service it.
