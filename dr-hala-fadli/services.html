Clinical dermatology

Dermatologists are trained in the anatomy, function, surgery and pathology of the skin. These are the conditions patients most often come to Dr Fadli with.

Skin examination, moles and sun damage

Sunlight is the most common cause of skin cancer, so everyone should have a full skin exam by a specialist dermatologist once a year, and more often if you have had skin cancer before. Dr Fadli checks moles, sun damage and anything new or changing, and removes moles where needed.

Book a skin check →

Acne and acne scars

Acne treatment is long term and needs commitment from both doctor and patient. The plan depends on how much scarring there is and how much the acne affects you, because even mild acne can knock your confidence. Treatment can include creams that unblock the pores, antibacterials, anti-inflammatories, and isotretinoin tablets where they suit. Scars can be improved with peels, dermaroller, PRP and other treatments.

Ask about acne →

Psoriasis

Psoriasis can't be cured yet, so be sceptical of anyone who says otherwise, but even severe cases are now very treatable. Options include creams (vitamin D, steroid, tar and retinoid), light treatment with UVB or PUVA, and tablets or injections under specialist supervision. Moisturiser, used generously and often, is the base of everything.

Cosmetic

Facial treatments done by Dr Fadli herself, after a consultation where you can look in the mirror and point out what you would like to change.

Botox

Tiny doses relax the muscles that cause lines on the forehead, between the eyes and around them (crow's feet). It takes three to five days to show and up to two weeks for the full effect; results last three to six months. Stay upright for a few hours afterwards. Botox also treats excessive sweating (hyperhidrosis) of the underarms, hands and feet. Not suitable in pregnancy or while breastfeeding.

Effect lasts 3–6 months

Ask about Botox →

Dermal fillers and lips

Fillers smooth smile lines, marionette lines and lines above the lip, and restore volume to lips, chin and cheeks. Dr Fadli uses hyaluronic acid fillers such as Juvéderm, Restylane and Teosyal, and Radiesse for longer results. Lips usually stay fuller for four to six months; expect slight bruising at most.

Hyaluronic acid: around 6–12 months

Ask about fillers →

Non-surgical nose job

A few injections of filler can hide a bump, fill a dent, raise a drooping tip or build up a flat bridge. It is done under numbing cream in about 20 minutes, and you hold a mirror and guide it with Dr Fadli. You can go back to work the same day. It is also a way to "test drive" a new nose before deciding on surgery.

About 20 minutes

Ask about the nose treatment →

Dark circles, hand filler, thread lift

Small amounts of filler under the eyes soften hollows and bags, under numbing cream, in less than 20 minutes; results can last 6 to 12 months. Fillers can also restore volume to the backs of the hands. Thread lifts are available for lifting the face without surgery.

Ask about these →

Peels and skin renewal

Chemical peels, microdermabrasion and dermaroller treatments refresh the skin's surface and help with acne scars, freckles and age spots. Facials include the sheep placenta (stem cell) facial.

Ask about skin renewal →

PRP and mesotherapy

Platelet rich plasma (PRP) uses a small sample of your own blood to treat the skin, acne scars and thinning hair. Mesotherapy delivers tiny injections into the skin or scalp.

Ask about PRP →

Soprano XL laser hair removal

Laser hair removal with the Soprano XL. Dr Fadli assesses your skin and hair at the consultation and explains how many sessions you are likely to need.

Ask about laser →

Cryolipolysis

Non-surgical fat reduction that cools stubborn areas of fat, such as the tummy and flanks, without needles or surgery.
