The practice

Dr Fadli specialised in dermatology in the UK. Her practice treats diseases of the skin, hair and nails, with an emphasis on the face: lines, volume, complexion.

She examines you, takes your medical history and gives you her candid opinion before anything is done. Then she follows you through the weeks after treatment.

Your first visit

The consultation is simply a comfortable exchange of information between you and the doctor. This is how a first appointment goes.

01

Call to book

Ring the clinic on 04 346 5542 or email manager@halafadli.com. We are open Sunday to Friday; the villa is opposite Choitram supermarket on Al Wasl Road.

02

Talk it through

You tell Dr Fadli what you would like to change or what is bothering your skin. She takes your medical history and asks what you hope to achieve.

03

Examination and her opinion

She examines you, explains the options, the risks and the limits, and tells you honestly what she recommends. The cost is explained before you decide.

04

Treatment and after

Once you decide, the treatment is booked with Dr Fadli. Her attention does not stop when you leave: she continues to look after you through the post-treatment period.

Dr Hala Fadli

MBChB, DDS, MSc, PhD in Dermatology (UK)

Dr Fadli trained in dermatology in Wales and worked at the University Hospital of Wales in Cardiff and the Royal Gwent Hospital before opening her practice in Dubai. Her cosmetic training was in private practices in Cardiff, on Harley Street in London, and in Berlin.

She comes from a family of doctors: her father is a psychiatrist and her mother a gynaecologist, both with clinics in Dubai.
