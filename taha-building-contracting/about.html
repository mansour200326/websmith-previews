About TBC

Taha Building Contracting L.L.C. was established in 2015. We are a medium-range construction company working mainly in the Emirate of Fujairah.

طه لمقاولات البناء

Our work is built on quality, construction know-how and getting projects finished without stress for the client. The team works closely together, so the building methods fit and the job is delivered on time and on budget.

We do new constructions, general maintenance projects up to G+4, interior decoration works, fabrication work and MEP works. Most of our projects are new residential villas and commercial shops.

Our skilled labour team brings more than 20 years of experience in construction, and we have completed more than 15 projects in Fujairah, handed over on time.
