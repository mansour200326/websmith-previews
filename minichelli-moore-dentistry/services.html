Treatments, in plain words

Tap a treatment to read what it is. Not sure which one you need? The front desk can help on the phone.

Dr. Minichelli at the chair, his assistant beside him.

01Composite fillingsTooth-colored, so they don't show

The decay is cleaned out of the tooth and the space is filled with a composite resin matched to the shade of your tooth. Up close, it looks like tooth, not metal.

Call to book a filling →
02Crown & bridgeFor broken, worn or missing teeth

A crown is a cap that covers a tooth that is cracked, worn down or heavily filled, so it can be used again. A bridge fills the gap where a tooth is missing, held in place by crowns on the teeth either side.

Ask about a crown or bridge →
03Implant reconstructionThe tooth that sits on an implant

An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth. Implant reconstruction is the part you see and chew with: the crown, bridge or denture that is fixed onto it. Ask us whether it suits the gap you have.

Ask about implants →
04Root canalsTo keep a tooth instead of losing it

When the nerve inside a tooth is infected or inflamed, a root canal cleans out the inside of the tooth and seals it, so the tooth can stay. A toothache that won't settle is a reason to call.

Call about a toothache →
05ExtractionsWhen a tooth can't be saved

Sometimes a tooth is too damaged to keep and has to come out. Ask us what could fill the space afterwards.

Call the office →
06Dentures & partialsFull or partial replacement teeth

A full denture replaces all the teeth on the top or bottom. A partial fills the gaps between the natural teeth you still have, and clips to them.

Ask about dentures →
07BleachingWhiter teeth

Whitening for teeth that have yellowed or stained over the years.

Ask about whitening →
08Mouth guardsFor basketball, hockey and every contact sport

A guard worn during sport protects the teeth from knocks and falls. Worth it for anyone on a team, at any age.

Ask about a mouth guard →
09Snoring appliancesWorn at night

An oral appliance worn while you sleep, which holds the lower jaw slightly forward to help keep the airway open and cut down snoring. Your partner may thank you.

Ask about snoring →
