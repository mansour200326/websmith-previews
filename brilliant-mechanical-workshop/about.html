About the workshop

Brilliant Mechanical Workshop was founded, and is run, by people who are passionate about their BMWs.

Because of that, we offer a wide range of products and services under one roof, so our customers don’t have to visit several suppliers to get what they need. Think of us as a one stop shop for your BMW or Mini, and for most other makes as well.

Whether you need a rebuilt engine, a fully customised car, a new body panel, a major or minor service or just a used window winder, we’re here to help.

When it comes to your BMW, our goal is to put you back on the road without burning a hole in your wallet. We know that keeping you on the road matters just as much, which is why we take pride in our warranties and our customer service. Our standard warranties are longer and more comprehensive than our competitors’.

We also customise cars. The aim is the car of your dreams, within the budget you set.

Please feel free to come and visit us for a chat.
