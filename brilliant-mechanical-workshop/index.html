Out of Motorplan? Bring it to Claremont.

Is your BMW or Mini out of its Motorplan? Then why pay exorbitant labour rates and excessive parts prices.

We service your car to its manufacturer’s specifications, renewing every component and fluid that is due, at prices that won’t break your bank account. No hidden costs, no unwanted costly extras.

The workshop has the latest diagnostic equipment and qualified technicians, and we’re an RMI registered workshop.
