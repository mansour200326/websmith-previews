Suspension and shock absorbers

Does your car feel “loose” on the road? Do you feel like you’re “floating” while driving? Does it make unpleasant noises over bumps? It’s time to get your suspension checked.

Most modern cars have rubber and metal suspension bushes for a comfortable, smooth ride. When the bushes or shock absorbers wear, road holding and comfort get worse, and so does your safety.

Fuel and oil in South Africa

Fuel

The quality of our petrol and diesel ranges from good to very bad. The octane rating is often lower than it should be, and fuel is often contaminated with water and other contaminants.

That can lead to expensive problems: blocked fuel injectors, corroded fuel system parts, even a car that won’t run.

Oil

Oil is oil, right? Not here. Not every oil on sale is good for your car, and using the cheapest one is usually a damaging, expensive habit.

Poor oil causes internal wear and sludge that stops critical engine parts being lubricated. We never use inferior oil, and we flush the engine out before renewing the oil.

Additives

We carry fuel and oil additives for diesel and petrol engines. They can improve fuel economy, reduce oil sludge, remove water from your fuel and clean the fuel system, at a very low cost.

Ask us about them when you bring your car in for its next service.
