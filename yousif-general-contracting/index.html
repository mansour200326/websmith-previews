Services

What we do

Construction management

We plan, coordinate and control the project from preparing for construction to handover. We look at what you need, put together the construction management plan, and help you keep good contractors within your budget.

Each job gets a team that knows that type of building, with a senior manager taking an active role.

Design and build

One company from the first sketches to the finished building, so you don't split the job between an architect and a contractor. We check our proposal against every one of the employer's requirements before any contract is signed.

About YGC

The company

Yousif General Contracting Establishment was set up in Al Ain in 1986. We are still based here, on Main Street, and we take projects all over the Emirates.

Mousa Mahmood Ali Nassar has been our General Manager since 1992. He runs a team of skilled, motivated people, and teamwork is how we get jobs done.

We work to the time and cost in the contract, and we want the client, the consultant and our own people to come away satisfied. That means honest dealing with clients, suppliers and subcontractors alike.

Al Moutared General Contracting Est. also works from Main Street, Al Ain. Its contact details are below.

Quality, health, safety, environment

QHSE

Our management system follows ISO 9001:2008, ISO 14001:2004 and OHSAS 18001:2007.

On site that means safety training and proper protective equipment for every worker, keeping visitors safe, non-polluting methods with regular checks, less waste and careful use of energy.
