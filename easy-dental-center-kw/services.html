01

Dental implants

An implant works like a natural tooth: you chew with it, speak normally with it and stop thinking about it. It is the first thing we recommend to replace a missing tooth.

Before

Every implant starts with a 3D x-ray, so the doctor sees the thickness of the bone and keeps well clear of the nerves.

During

Anaesthesia is done by computer instead of the usual needle, nitrous oxide is there if you are anxious, and a laser is used in the cases that need it to reduce bleeding and help healing. Placing an implant takes about 10 minutes; a full jaw takes about 40.

After

Most patients manage any discomfort with a simple painkiller. Swelling, if any, lasts no more than about four days when you follow the doctor's instructions.

Diabetes and blood pressure

This is the question we hear most. Diabetic, heart and blood-pressure patients can have implants safely, as long as they follow the doctor's plan.

Bone graft and sinus lift

If you were told your jawbone is too weak for an implant, a bone graft adds thickness and height, usually in the same session as the implant, in about 15 minutes. The wound heals in 7 to 10 days. When the sinus sits too low above the back teeth, a sinus lift takes about 10 to 15 minutes, also usually in the same session.

Hollywood smile

Before you come for a new smile, it helps to know the types, because the type decides the cost. We design it first on a photo of your own face, so you see the result before any work begins.

Whitening

For straight, well-lined teeth that are just not the right colour. We whiten to the shade you ask for.

A broken or missing front tooth

A tooth-coloured filling matched to your whitened teeth, or a ceramic or zirconia crown in the same shade.

Crowns and bridges smile

For heavy decay and staining on the front teeth: decay removed, a fluoride layer, then crowns and bridges in the white you choose. It also corrects a lot of crowding.

Veneers

Our cosmetic specialist prepares only the front surface of the teeth, very slightly, then fits porcelain veneers. The best-known white smile.

Lumineers

No preparation of the teeth at all. We take a measurement and send it to the lab in the United States; the shells come back to Kuwait in 2 to 3 weeks and are fitted here.

03

Crowns, bridges and dentures

There are four ways to replace or strengthen teeth. We explain each one, with its pros and cons, before you decide.

Crowns

A cap over a tooth weakened by deep decay, a fracture or a root canal. We use zirconia and E.max, which hold up well to everyday eating.

Bridges

A fixed bridge rests on the teeth either side of a gap, after light preparation, and carries the replacement tooth.

Same-day, by computer

Instead of impression paste, a scanner reads your teeth directly in the mouth. The crown or bridge is designed on screen and made in the clinic on our CAD-CAM machine, then fitted at the first visit, or the same day at the latest.

Removable dentures

Full dentures for a whole jaw, or partial ones for a few teeth. We only use them in particular cases, because implants and fixed work do the job better.

04

Root canal treatment

An inflamed nerve is a pain few people can bear. Regular check-ups catch decay before it gets that far, but when it does, this is how we treat it.

How it goes

The doctor clears the decay, cleans the infection from the root canals, removes the nerve, disinfects the canals and seals them. We work under a dental microscope, so the doctor sees inside the tooth, and the canals are prepared electronically. Instead of an hour or more in the chair, most treatments take no more than about 30 minutes.

The final filling

The permanent tooth-coloured filling can be made by computer with our CAD-CAM, in about 5 minutes.

Why a crown afterwards

A tooth that had a root canal becomes brittle. Many people skip the crown, but it is what keeps the tooth from breaking and makes the treatment last.

05

Fillings and veneers

We look after patients of every age, to keep teeth healthy, strong and white.

Cosmetic fillings

After the decay is cleaned, a tooth-coloured filling brings the tooth back to its natural shape. On front teeth they are chosen for looks; on back teeth for strength.

Broken front teeth

Come in straight away. The doctor checks the break and prepares the tooth for a permanent cosmetic filling, made by computer or as a porcelain veneer.

Fillings by computer

The tooth is scanned and the filling is made on the computer, for a precise fit and to avoid the few days of sensitivity that ordinary fillings can cause.

Porcelain veneers

No more than 1.5 mm of the outer surface is prepared, then made-to-measure veneers are fitted as new fronts for the teeth.

06

Extractions and wisdom teeth

Sometimes decay reaches the roots and the tooth cannot be saved. Before any extraction the surgeon asks about your general health, takes the x-rays needed, checks the neighbouring teeth and explains how the gap can be replaced, by an implant or a bridge.

Buried wisdom teeth

A wisdom tooth stuck in the bone can trap food, push on its neighbours, cause jaw or ear pain and crowd the front teeth. We take a 3D x-ray to be sure it is clear of the jaw nerve, numb you with computerised anaesthesia (no needle in many cases), and the extraction itself takes about 15 minutes. You leave with medicine and advice to keep swelling and pain down.

After an extraction

  1. Bite on the cotton or gauze for two hours.
  2. Change it every half hour.
  3. No rinsing, no spitting, and no drinking through a straw.
  4. Don't brush the area on the day of treatment.
  5. Hold a cold compress on the outside of the face.
  6. From the next day, use warm compresses.
  7. From the second day, brush all your teeth gently, including the area.

07

Gum treatment

The gums hold the teeth and cover the bone; if they suffer, so do the teeth. Signs you need a visit: bad breath, bleeding when you brush, or teeth that look longer than they used to.

Cleaning

A routine scale every six months, and a deep clean below the gum line when there is a problem, sometimes with a little anaesthetic. A gum treatment session takes no more than about 30 minutes. Diabetic and heart patients are more prone to gum problems and should come regularly.

Laser gum surgery and shaping

Trimming, shortening or reshaping the gums is done by laser: less bleeding, faster healing, less pain and swelling afterwards, a more precise gum line and less need for anaesthetic.

Gum lightening

For dark gums. After a check-up and a quick clean, laser lightening takes about 15 minutes, against about 30 minutes for the traditional peeling method.
