About the center

Easy Dental Center is a modern center for treating and improving teeth, in Farwaniya. We look after you from the moment you book until you leave the clinic.

8

Eight specialist clinics

The center is made up of eight specialist clinics covering every field of dental treatment. Implants, anaesthesia for nervous patients, implants and smile makeovers “in one session”, and specialist dental surgery all happen here, in one place.

We chose doctors who keep up with what is new in dentistry, one for each specialty, so whatever you need, the right person is down the corridor.
