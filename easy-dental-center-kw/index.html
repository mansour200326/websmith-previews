Most people are nervous of the dentist. We planned the clinic around that.

Our first aim is to take away the fear people bring to a dental chair: doctors trained to make the visit easier, a proper diagnosis on modern equipment, and less time lying back with your mouth open.

  1. 01

    Computerised anaesthesia

    Numbing is done electronically, by computer, instead of the usual dental needle in many cases. Ask to try it on your first visit.

  2. 02

    3D x-ray before surgery

    Before an implant or a buried wisdom tooth, the doctor checks the bone and keeps clear of the jaw nerve on a three-dimensional scan.

  3. 03

    Crowns made in the same visit

    A scanner reads your teeth instead of impression paste, and our CAD-CAM machine makes the crown or bridge in the clinic, the same day at the latest.

  4. 04

    Microscope for root canals

    The doctor works under magnification inside the tooth, and the canals are prepared electronically, so most sessions take about 30 minutes.

  5. 05

    Laser for gums

    Gum shaping, gum surgery and lightening dark gums by laser: less bleeding, faster healing and less need for anaesthetic.

  6. 06

    Nitrous oxide

    “Laughing gas” is on hand for patients who are very anxious about treatment.
