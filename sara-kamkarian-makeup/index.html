The school

Sara Kamkarian started this school more than 25 years ago. We teach makeup, hairdressing and skin therapy for film, television, fashion, salons, spas and cosmetics counters.

If you have never picked up a brush, you start with the basics. If you already work as a makeup artist, you come to sharpen your skills. Classes are small and every level is welcome. Our instructors have worked in film, television, fashion and salons, and we keep the course content up to date with the latest techniques. Students also get career advice and help finding work after the course.

Hair and skin care

Sara's products for salons, spas and clinics, also sold for home use.

Skin care

SK Skin Care

Developed by Sara Kamkarian with a team of chemists in the U.S. There are corrective products for salon treatments and a home range so clients can keep their results. Acid-balanced, with natural botanicals.

Hair treatment

SK Keratin

A keratin treatment that turns dry, frizzy, dull hair silky and shiny without changing its texture. Works on all hair types, including coloured, permed, relaxed and bleached hair.

Hair treatment

SK Straightening

Our straightening system, formulated with specialists at HBS Group in Italy. It permanently straightens curly, frizzy hair, coloured hair included, without split ends or dullness.

Tools

SK LCD Iron

A titanium flat iron with a digital display, set anywhere from 50 to 210 °C. Far-infrared and ionic heat, a 360° swivel cord, and it straightens, flips or curls. Made with an Italian partner.
