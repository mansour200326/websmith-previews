The studio

A woman-owned nail and beauty studio on the ground floor of Global Gate Tower 2, Saadiyat Marina District.

Calm, careful, unhurried

We built avery belle as a calm place where precision, care and quality come first. Each appointment is planned so nobody is rushed, and the studio is kept spotless.

We focus on the health of your natural nails and keep to strict sterilisation standards. Our nail technicians come from Ukraine and Kyrgyzstan, with experience across Ukraine, Russia, Kyrgyzstan, Turkey, Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

  • Woman-owned
  • Kid-friendly, with Little Hands and Little Feet treatments
  • Wheelchair accessible, parking outside
  • Essie polish, BIAB builder gel, Thalgo massage oils
  • Free 15-minute consultation
