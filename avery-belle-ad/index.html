Walk in with us

Five spaces, from the front door to the massage room.

01

The door

Ground floor of Global Gate Tower 2, in Saadiyat Marina District. Look for the white AB sign on the glass. There's parking outside and step-free access.

02

Reception and lounge

A plaster desk, oak shelves and a bouclé sofa under the olive trees. Come a few minutes early and choose your shade.

03

The nail bar

One long curved bar under daylight lamps, so the colour you see is the colour you leave with. Manicures, BIAB, extensions and nail art are all done here.

04

The pedicure chairs

Deep linen armchairs on a raised platform. The Smart Pedicure is waterless: feet are cared for with precise electric filing, then polished if you like.

05

The massage room

A private room for full-body massage with Thalgo oils, from a 45-minute upper body session to an hour and a half.
