Accounting & bookkeeping

From the day-to-day to planning a large transaction, accurate and consistent accounts are what every business runs on. We handle your book of accounts, your accounting software and your bank reconciliations, and make sure your company's figures are right.

We can do all of it for you, or supplement your in-house accountant. We also review how the business is run, industry trends and VAT developments, and point out where you can plan better.

Included

  • Bookkeeping
  • Preparation of statutory financial statements
  • Payroll
  • VAT compliance
  • Monthly performance reports
  • Controlling services
  • Administrative and other services

We believe an audit should give you more than a report, so we walk through the findings with the owners and key managers. We look past the financial statements at your processes and internal controls, and at the areas that could be stronger.

Included

  • Financial statutory audit
  • IFRS reporting
  • Corporate governance and compliance
  • Financial statements review
  • Audit of special purpose financial statements
  • Agreed-upon procedures
  • NGO projects audit

03

VAT & tax agency

We set you up for VAT and keep your business compliant with Federal Law No. 8 and its executive procedures. We keep full records for the Federal Tax Authority, make sure your numbers are correct and file your returns on time.

We take the time to meet you, review your situation, answer your questions and make recommendations, so that you consider VAT before you make business decisions and don't pay more than you have to.

Included

  • Tax agency services, as a tax agent with the FTA
  • Tax due diligence
  • VAT registration and set-up
  • VAT structuring, UAE and other GCC states
  • VAT return filing with the FTA

Not sure if VAT applies to you? Ask for our free VAT assessment: a short questionnaire about how your business works, then a free consultation.

04

Internal audit & control

A bad hiring decision in accounting can leave your books in trouble. Many UAE businesses struggle to keep accurate accounts because accountants change often or are new to the job. Our internal audit team takes that stress away with a regular weekly or monthly check of the work your in-house accountant does.

We make sure the accounts are accurate and follow accounting standards, and we help you put proper controls on your internal processes. Because we stay in touch through the year, control weaknesses are discussed with you as we find them.

How it works

  • Weekly or monthly review of the books
  • Control measures for your internal processes
  • Weaknesses discussed with you regularly
  • Senior management involved from start to finish
  • Outsourced or co-sourced

05

Fraud risk examination

If you suspect fraud or other wrongdoing in your company, you need to act quickly and discreetly. In a forensic audit we analyse the books and the supporting documents, and trace receivables, payables, inventory, cash and other assets to find out whether there has been fraud and how big it is.

We work with you one-on-one, respond quickly and keep it confidential. Disputes, investigations and claims need sound evidence, and our accounting and investigative work is set out in a professional consultancy report.

Areas covered

  • Accountant malpractice
  • Cash and asset misappropriation
  • Financial fraud investigations
  • Fraudulent transfers
  • Internal investigations
  • Partnership disputes
  • Shareholder disputes
  • Financial statement manipulation
  • Professional malpractice claims

Business plan & feasibility study

We help new businesses and start-ups write a business plan that tests whether the project is feasible, guides you as you start, and helps you raise money from investors and institutions.

A good plan sets out who you are, what you want to achieve and how you will get there. It matters when you talk to banks or investors, and later it is the benchmark you measure the business against.

Our plans go to

  • Prospective investors
  • UAE funds and government agencies
  • Free zone authorities, including the zones on our audit list
