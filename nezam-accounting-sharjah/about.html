About NAMC

Nezam Accounting & Management Consultants is an accounting and audit firm in Sharjah. Established in 1992, it has grown into a team of qualified accountants, auditors and consultants serving companies across the UAE.

Who we are

Our multilingual team supports over 150 clients with tax, auditing, accounting and advisory services, and knows the local laws and customs well.

Clients tell us our experience has been especially valuable to companies setting up their operations in the UAE and the GCC. Our partners, managers and consultants support both national and multinational companies.

We work in Arabic and English, and our senior management is involved in each engagement from the first meeting to the final report.

Our vision

Do whatever it takes to deliver a superior accounting experience every time, and serve our customers professionally.

Our mission

To give businesses, entrepreneurs and individuals high quality accounting, auditing, tax planning and business advisory services, on time and efficiently, through a professional team.

How we work

We review what each client needs and give a detailed estimate of the work, the cost and the time. Our quotations include all costs, so there are no hidden charges, and we work to the agreed timeline.

The team

Partners

Our people are fundamental to our business. Our accountants, auditors and consultants are the key to first-class client service.

Lutfi Al Salamat

Founder & CEO · CPA

Lutfi founded NAMC. He has more than 30 years of experience in auditing, accounting, internal control and business plans. He is a Certified Public Accountant and a board member of the Syrian Association for Certified Accountants, and has made big contributions to the Arab Association for Certified Accountants.

Yakoub Al Salamat

Managing Partner · CPA

Yakoub helps large companies in the UAE with accounting and auditing. He is a Certified Public Accountant and specialises in accounting, auditing, internal control and management consulting. He has held director positions in Syria and the UAE.

Mohammed Askar

Managing Partner · MBA

Mohammed works in international business, helping multinational companies set up and grow in the Middle East. He holds an MBA and specialises in banking, accounting, auditing, brokerage and financial firms, with director roles in Syria, Turkey, Egypt and the UAE.
