About the garage

We're a real grass roots garage. Our staff are professional mechanics, and we're known locally as a garage you can trust.

Pop down for an informal chat. We'd rather meet you and see the car than try to talk through a fault over the phone. You'll find us friendly and easy to deal with, and there are no hidden extras.
