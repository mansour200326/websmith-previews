01

Car MOT, Class 4

We've been an approved MOT test centre since 1980, testing vehicles for local residents and vehicle traders. Class 4 covers cars, motor caravans, goods vehicles up to 3,000 kg gross weight and minibuses with no more than 12 passenger seats.

  • Same-day MOTs are sometimes available. We suggest booking ahead to avoid delays.
  • If it fails, we can usually repair the defects and re-test the same day, depending on how busy the workshop is.
  • You can have the MOT up to 28 days before it's due, which gives you a 13-month MOT.

02

Motorcycles

If it's any form of motorcycle, we can't wait to MOT or service it. We're bikers too, and riders from Teignmouth and around bring us everything from super sports to classics. We can maintain, service, modify and MOT your bike.

  • Class I MOT: mopeds and motorcycles up to 199cc
  • Class II MOT: motorcycles 200cc and over
  • Class III MOT: tricycles and three-wheeled cars
  • Servicing for most makes and models, every bike road tested on completion by advanced riders
  • Mopeds, enduros, sports bikes, trikes and Harley-Davidsons
  • Most makes and sizes of motorcycle tyres, statically or dynamically balanced as needed
