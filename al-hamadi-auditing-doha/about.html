Abdulla Al-Hamadi for Auditing and Taxation, also known as Tax Services & Audit, works for individuals and institutions in Qatar. Tap a face to read about them.

How the office works

We promise our clients professional results by building strong working partnerships with them, to find out what they need and deliver it.

The cornerstone of the firm is the people we choose. Our experts' first aim is to help clients find solutions, and that lets us give a high-quality, efficient service. Our commitment is to efficiency and integrity: reliable work, and reports with effective, accurate results.

The office works in Qatar under Qatari law and the country's professional regulations, in line with the General Tax Authority's guidelines, international professional standards and US GAAP. It follows the standards of IFAC, IAASB, IAESB, IESBA, IPSASB and IASB, and is approved by many banks in Qatar and by several ministries and authorities, the General Tax Authority in particular.
