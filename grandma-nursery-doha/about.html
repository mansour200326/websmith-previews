Home awayfrom home

Watching your children grow and flourish has been an absolute delight, and we are proud to be your true Home Away from Home.

A short message from the General Manager, Grandma Nursery

Looking back on our journey since 2012, we remember the first words, the first steps, the laughter and the warm hugs. Thank you for being part of it with us.

Our mission

To provide the highest standards of safety, care and early childhood education. One pillar matters most to us: the Child Centered Classroom, built on the belief that children grow best when they are involved in their own learning. Our programme builds each child's confidence and independence, and a smooth move into preschool.

Parents play an important part too. Parents and teachers work closely together, and your ideas and suggestions help us make the best place to learn for every child.

Our vision

A safe, loving and stimulating place where every child grows with confidence, curiosity and care.

Safety and welfare

  1. A fire drill routine every month
  2. A signing in and out system for every child
  3. First aid and hygiene training for all team members, every month
  4. Facilities and equipment suitable for small children
  5. An appropriate child to adult ratio
CCTV for parentsWith our webcam you can log in on your phone or computer and see your child at play.

The Ministry of Education and Higher Education awarded all branches of Grandma Nursery the White Flag award.
