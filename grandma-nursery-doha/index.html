A day at Grandma

Outdoor play first thing, then a room for each part of learning: English at circle time, maths, the creative room, discovery, French and Arabic, and fitness, with breakfast, snack and lunch in between.

Bonjour. مرحبا.

French and Arabic, 40 minutes of each, every day, at no extra cost. Our French & Arabic room has its own letters on the wall, a peg board and an Arabic alphabet puzzle, and a hand-painted Eiffel Tower.

Inside the French & Arabic Centre →

Inside the nursery

Rooms set up for small children: a quiet room with cots for the babies, soft play inside, a sand area and water park outside, a cafeteria, and a clinic with a visiting doctor.

White Flag award

The Ministry of Education and Higher Education awarded all branches of Grandma Nursery the White Flag award. It reflects our commitment to quality education for your little ones.
