The clinic

Clinica Dental Center opened in 2016 on the 43rd floor of Al Hamra Tower, in the heart of Kuwait City. It was built to be calm, modern and properly equipped, with every kind of dental care in one place.

Seven rooms, one view

All seven of our clinics are private treatment rooms. The chairs were chosen for you to relax in, and each one looks out over the Kuwait City skyline.

There is room for whoever comes with you, whether that's a parent with a child or a friend on the day of a bigger treatment.

How we work

A consultation for everyone

Every treatment starts with your medical history and a thorough consultation. We take our time to understand what you want, then explain the options.

Care after treatment

We want the result to last. After treatment you get a follow-up and check-up timetable based on your own dental risk, and all the work we carry out is under warranty.

Clear costs

The cost depends on the extent of your treatment plan. We are open about it, so you can choose what's right for you.

Friendly, clean, organised

Good treatment needs a good setting. Friendliness, hygiene and good organisation are how we work every day.
