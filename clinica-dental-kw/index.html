Since 2016, on the 43rd floor

Clinica Dental Center opened in 2016 in Al Hamra Tower, in the middle of Kuwait City. We do the full range of dentistry under one roof: check-ups and cleaning, root canals, braces, implants, whitening and oral surgery.

All seven of our clinics are private rooms. The chairs are made for you to relax in, there is room for whoever comes with you, and every one of them looks out over the city.

“Clinica Dental Center is not just a dental clinic. We want you to trust our care. Our patients are valued family members.”

Mohammad Alattabi, General Manager

Treatments

Every treatment in detail →
01Dental implantsReplacing missing teeth, with new teeth in one day where it suits

An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth, and a crown goes on top. We check your bone and gums first, then tell you whether a same-day tooth is right for you.

Implants in detail →

02Root canal treatmentSaving a tooth when the nerve is infected or inflamed

Endodontics is Dr. Al Safy's speciality, with a master's from the University of Warwick. The nerve is cleaned out under local anaesthetic, the canals are sealed and the tooth is kept.

Root canal in detail →

03OrthodonticsStraightening teeth and correcting the bite

For teeth and jaws that are out of line, in children and adults. We look at how your face, teeth and jaws are growing and recommend the right way to align them.

Orthodontics in detail →

04Whitening and cosmetic workThe Complete Whitening Pack, and work on the look of your smile

Whitening is one of the simplest ways to change how your teeth look. For more than colour, we talk through cosmetic options with you at the consultation.

Whitening in detail →

05Cleaning with Air FlowRemoving plaque and surface stains

A fine jet of air, water and powder lifts plaque and stains from the teeth and along the gum line. Part of a regular check-up.

Cleaning in detail →

06Children's dentistryFrom a child's first dental visit through the teenage years

Children aren't small adults, and they can't always sit still for an exam. We take the first visit slowly, and offer fissure sealants to protect the back teeth.

Children's dentistry in detail →

07Oral surgery and sedationWisdom teeth, surgical extractions, and care for nervous patients

Removing teeth surgically is the most common oral surgery, wisdom teeth above all. If you are afraid of the dentist, ask us about sedation when you book.

Surgery and sedation in detail →

How a visit works

The same four steps, whether you come in for a filling or a full set of implants.

01

Consultation

We start with your medical history and a proper consultation. We take the time to hear what you want, then explain your options.

02

Your plan and its cost

The cost depends on the plan. We show you both before we begin, so you choose with the numbers in front of you.

03

Treatment

In your own room on the 43rd floor, with space for whoever came with you. Nervous? Ask about sedation.

04

Follow-up

We set your check-ups around your own dental risk, and all the work we do is under warranty.
