01

Dental implants

An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth, and a crown is fixed on top so it looks and works like your own. We list it the way we always have: new teeth in one day, where your case allows it.

  • A full check of your bone and gums first
  • We tell you at the consultation if a same-day tooth is right for you
  • Follow-up visits planned around you, and the work is under warranty
Call about implants →
02

Root canal treatment

When the nerve inside a tooth is infected or inflamed, a root canal saves the tooth instead of taking it out. The nerve is cleaned out under local anaesthetic, the canals are sealed, and the tooth is rebuilt.

This is Dr. Mohammad Al Safy's speciality. He holds a master's in endodontics from the University of Warwick.

  • Ask for Dr. Al Safy by name when you book
  • Usually done under local anaesthetic
Call about a root canal →
03

Orthodontics

Orthodontics corrects teeth and jaws that sit in the wrong place, and looks at how the face, teeth and jaws are growing. It is for children and adults.

  • An assessment of the bite and the jaws
  • Modern ways of straightening teeth, explained at the consultation
  • Regular check-ins while the teeth move
Call about orthodontics →
04

Whitening and cosmetic dentistry

Whitening is one of the most asked-for treatments because it changes how your teeth look quickly. Our Complete Whitening Pack is the place to start.

If you want more than a lighter colour, we talk through cosmetic options at the consultation and show you the plan and cost first.

Call about whitening →
05

Cleaning with Air Flow

Cleaning removes plaque, the soft layer that leads to decay and gum problems. With Air Flow, a fine jet of air, water and powder lifts plaque and surface stains from the teeth and along the gum line.

  • Part of a regular check-up
  • Your next visit is set by your own dental risk
Book a cleaning →
06

Children's dentistry

Children are not small adults, and they can't always sit still for an exam. We look after teeth, gums and mouth from a child's first dental visit through the teenage years.

  • A gentle first visit, taken at the child's pace
  • Fissure sealants to protect the back teeth
  • Room in the treatment room for a parent
Book a child's visit →
07

Oral surgery and sedation

Oral surgery covers problems of the jaw and mouth that need a surgical approach. The most common is removing teeth surgically, wisdom teeth above all.

If you are afraid of the dentist, tell us when you book. We offer sedation dentistry, so the visit is easier on you.

Call about surgery or sedation →
