About the center

A multi-specialty medical center for the families of Al Jimi, opened in October 2015.

Why we opened in Al Jimi

Al Jimi has a lot of homes and a lot of families. Our chairman's idea was simple: give the people who live here one place with specialty clinics, modern equipment and well-qualified doctors, under one roof.

We believe healthcare isn't only about diagnosing and treating. It's about helping people live a healthy life, and that includes spreading medical awareness in the community we serve.

The center opened to patients in mid-October 2015 after approval from the Abu Dhabi health authority. One by one, most of the leading insurance companies then approved us to accept their health cards. Today more than 10 doctors and 40 support staff work here.

  • October 2015Opened to patients after Abu Dhabi health authority approval.
  • After openingApproved by most of the leading health insurance companies.
  • Today17 departments, a pharmacy on the ground floor, X-ray and ultrasound, and a home care team.
