Nu's is a family-owned shop. We've been doing affordable car repair in San Diego since 1987, and we fix all cars, from Toyota to Mercedes Benz. Foreign cars too.

Body work, electrical repair, engine and transmission rebuilds, brakes and regular maintenance: bring it in and we'll take care of it. Our goal hasn't changed: great repair service at the best price.
