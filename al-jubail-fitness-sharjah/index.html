On the floor

The gym takes the M floor of the building: cardio by the windows, machines and free weights down the middle, and a turf lane for sled work.

01 / 04

Cardio and the turf lane

Stair climbers, recumbent bikes and a ski erg along the windows. At the end, a green turf lane with a sled and battle ropes.

02 / 04

Bikes and treadmills

Spin bikes up front and a full row of treadmills behind them, facing the windows.

03 / 04

Machines

Cable stations and plate-loaded machines for every muscle group, with mirrors on the columns.

04 / 04

Benches and dumbbells

Adjustable benches, a full dumbbell rack and barbells on rubber flooring, under the same LED lines as the rest of the gym.
