Services

Eight trades under one company, from our yard in Al Ghail Industrial Area.

Take us for the whole job, from drawings to handover, or for one package: the civil works, the steel, the MEP or the finishes. Our own engineers, manpower and machinery do the work.

Turnkey projects

One contract from the drawings to the keys. We run the construction and the project management, bring in our own teams for each trade, and arrange the third-party inspections with recognised organisations in the UAE.

  • Residential villas
  • Buildings up to G+5
  • Construction management and project management
