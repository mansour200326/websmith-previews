About us

Asian Building group, Ras Al Khaimah. Since 2004.

Asian Star Building Contracting is part of the Asian Building group of companies in Ras Al Khaimah, a privately owned group working in civil construction and steel structures with turnkey projects.

The company

Asian Building Contracting LLC was founded in 2004 and is privately owned. We offer construction, construction management and project management, and we take on demanding projects whatever their size or value.

Our focus is industrial warehouses, residential villas and buildings up to G+5. We have our own manpower, machinery and equipment, and a team of managers, engineers and skilled labour backed by the infrastructure to support them.

A safety department works across our premises, and our HSE policy covers every activity and every employee. A procurement team with good contacts in the market sources the materials, and third-party inspections are done by recognised organisations in the UAE.

Our quality policy

We are committed to complete satisfaction for our customers at competitive prices. We measure how well we meet their requirements and work with them to keep improving the service.

  1. Keep an effective, efficient quality management system.
  2. Monitor our work processes and objectives through internal management reviews and internal audit.
  3. Monitor customer satisfaction and keep re-aligning our objectives to improve.
  4. Find the cause of any complaint and act so it doesn’t happen again.
  5. Make sure the resources for the core work are available and competent.
  6. Comply with every regulatory and legal requirement.
