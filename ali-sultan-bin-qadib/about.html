Aboutthe office

Ali Sultan Bin Qadib Advocates and Legal Consultants is a 100% Emirati team of advocates and legal advisers, working from Sharjah and Dubai for clients across the UAE.

A team chosen case by case

Our team was chosen carefully, to find the best legal answer for each client. Our record shows steady success in criminal and recovery cases, and we also handle commercial cases, civil disputes, personal status, labour and administrative cases, and the drafting of legal contracts.

We read the file, understand it, and look for the answer that fits the client's interest, rather than the usual routine. We have pleaded, defended and signed contracts for companies, government and private institutions and individuals in all seven emirates.
