Servicing, repairs & air-con

Whatever make or model of car you have, call us when it needs servicing or repairing. Regular servicing does two things: it keeps the car safe and reliable, and it holds its value. A fully stamped service book reassures the next buyer.

If a warning light comes on, bring the car to us. We use the latest diagnostics equipment for quick fault finding, and we carry out the full range of electronic repairs, from ABS to air conditioning.

Air-con: if yours blows cool rather than cold, it may need a repair or a re-gas. Tip: recirculation mode is best for summer.

MOT preparation

Worried your car or van will fail its MOT? A failed MOT costs you time and money. We test everything the MOT covers before it goes in, so you know it will sail through.

If we find a fault that would fail it, we'll repair it with your permission, to a higher standard than the MOT asks for.

Does the car sit unused for long spells? That's hard on the battery and alternator. We look after batteries, alternators and starter motors too.

Brakes & clutches

Does the car pull as you slow down, or do you hear a screech? Those can be signs of brake damage. Don't risk it: ring us straight away. Our brake repairs won't break the bank.

A worn clutch makes a big difference to the car's power and to changing gear. We supply and fit clutches at good prices.

We recommend an inspection of the brakes and clutch once a year. It can save you an expensive repair later.
