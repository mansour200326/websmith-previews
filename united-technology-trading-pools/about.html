About us

United Technology & Trading Co. and its sister company, United Pools Contracting LLC. Pools and water features in the UAE since 1976.

How it started

1976
Navin Asarpota founds United Technology & Trading Co.
1998
We open in Abu Dhabi.
2010
The landscaping division starts. Meydan's 22-metre jet goes in for the Dubai World Cup.
2011
Ibn Battuta Gate Hotel's lap pool wins Time Out Dubai's "Best Hotel lap pool".
Today
Head office in Bay Square, Business Bay. Showroom on Kuwait Street. Office in Abu Dhabi.

Mr. Navin Asarpota founded the company in 1976. He is still its Managing Director, and still involved in the work every day.

We design, supply, install, commission and maintain swimming pools and water features. Since 2010 we have also done hard and soft landscaping, and the work around it: decking, saunas and steam rooms, misting systems, water playgrounds, musical and dry fountains.

We are also the people to call when something is wrong. Finding M.E.P. faults and structural leaks, then waterproofing and re-tiling, is a big part of what we do.

Our strength is our people: more than 60 qualified engineers, 35-plus CAD operators, over 200 skilled and 250 unskilled workers. That is what lets us take on jobs like the ones below.

We work with suppliers from around the world, including Pentair, AstralPool, Hayward, PEM Fountains and Calorex, and we trade their products from our showroom.

Al Zeina, Abu Dhabi · Aldar Properties

Two pools on springs

Two big communal pools, roughly 25 metres by 8, had to sit on anti-vibration springs so the tenants below would not hear the splashing. The whole pool body is suspended. Main contractor Aldar Laing O'Rourke, consultants Pell Frischmann.
