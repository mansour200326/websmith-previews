Swimming pools

Commercial and residential pools, built for the UAE for more than three decades. Hotels, compounds, schools, villas, and pools for horses, camels and dogs too.

Whether you are an architect, a consultant, a developer or the owner, our design team can guide you through every stage, from bare ground and excavation to filling and commissioning. We also work on a design-and-consult basis next to your own architects and contractors.

  • New construction, overflow and infinity-edge pools
  • Waterproofing, guniting and decorative tiling
  • Underwater lighting and underwater speakers
  • Massage jets, counter-current jets and slides
  • Acrylic panels for pools and water features

02

Fountains and water features

From a small courtyard fountain to the features in Dubai Mall, Festival City and Terminal 3.

The hard part of a water feature is the part you don't see: the trough that has to be level, the stone fixed at the right angle, the pumps and filters sized so the water falls the same way every day. We design these as consultants too.

  • Dancing and musical fountains, dry-deck fountains
  • Waterfalls, water walls and string water features
  • Water features with fire
  • Mechanical and dry fixing of stone to vertical water walls
  • Misting and fogging systems

05

Maintenance

Every swimmer deserves clean water. Without a routine, any pool or pond fills with algae and bacteria that smell.

pH and chlorine are only the start. We kill off bacteria, remove algae and visible dirt, and look after lakes and ponds too, even ones with koi. Annual maintenance is available for everything we build.

  • Pools, jacuzzis and water features
  • Lakes and ponds, including koi ponds
  • Annual maintenance contracts

06

Refurbishment and leaks

A new look for an old pool, or a longer life. And the leaks that neither the owner nor the company who built the pool could explain.

We trace M.E.P. problems and civil structural leaks, then waterproof and re-tile. We have done this for several commercial pools with major leaks.

  • Skimmer pools changed to overflow or infinity edge
  • Re-waterproofing, re-tiling and re-grouting
  • Depth reduction and pool extensions
  • Add counter-current jets, music, slides, laminar jets, massage
  • Leak detection and repair
