Four jobs that were harder than they look

Water does what it likes. Most of our work is getting it to fall evenly, stay off the people watching, and come on when it should.

01 · Meydan Boat House, 2010

A 22-metre jet in 48 hours

For the 2010 Dubai World Cup, Meydan asked us to install a 22-metre illuminated fountain jet at the Boat House, in a record 48 hours. We did it.

02 · The Dubai Mall

The Diving Men waterfall

If the basalt cladding is off by a degree, the water throws itself off the wall and onto the shoppers. The trough at the top had to be set almost dead level too, or the fall would not be even along its whole length.

03 · Dubai Festival City

A waterfall that carries the stairs

The main feature holds the stairs from the ground floor to level one, and its top is not built evenly. We still got a consistent flow across all four of its water bodies.

04 · Millennium Hotel expansion

A glass-clear line between two pools

The hotel wanted the kids' pool divided from the main pool by an acrylic wall. We built it with BKIO Acrylic from Malaysia, our second job with heavy acrylic work inside a pool.

Something wrong with your pool?

Call us for a free visit by one of our technicians. If any of these sound familiar, we have seen it before.

  • Water turning green, black or yellow
  • Eyes, skin or nose burning after a swim
  • The chlorine smell is too strong
  • Cloudy water, or sand that the filter won't clear
  • Water or electricity bill running too high
  • Tiles falling off, or grout turning black
