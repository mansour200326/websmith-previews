About Life Care

Life Care Medical Centre was the first medical center in Dibba, Fujairah, and has served the community for over 16 years. Our doctors, nurses and staff look after General Medicine, Emergency care and Dental Care, with a fully integrated pharmacy in the same building.

We accept most major insurance providers, so patients can get care without financial barriers. Our mission is to promote the health and well-being of our community.
