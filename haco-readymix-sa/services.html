Ready mix concrete

خرسانة جاهزة

HACO ready mix concrete is made of high quality cement, selected aggregates, a calibrated dosage of additives, and water.

We tailor the concrete to each client's needs. Changing the proportion of water, aggregates and cement changes its strength, how easy it is to work and its finish. Additives let us set the mix for the drive from our plant to your project, the weather on site and the project's specification.

Range
From high-performance to fluid-fill concrete, specially designed for the job
Standards

Aggregate

كنكري

HACO produces high quality aggregate from its numerous crushers, catering to the different needs of clients' mix designs.

Aggregates are the crushed stone, sand and gravel used in virtually all forms of construction: in roads and streets, and in concrete, where they make up 60% to 70% of the total volume and strongly shape how the concrete behaves fresh and hardened.
