Since 1976

Al-Houssain & Al-Afaliq Company (HACO) is the first ready mix concrete supplier in Al-Ahsa. Over the years we have built a name for reliability, fast service and high quality products.

Our history

1976

The company opens in Al Ahsa, making 50 cubic metres of ready mix an hour.

800 m³/h

Production capability at present, which made HACO the pioneer and leading supplier of ready mix concrete, masonry and insulated blocks, and aggregate in the area.

Jubail

After the opening of the Al-Jubail plant, HACO was approved by the Royal Commission for Al-Jubail & Yanbu', SABIC and Marafiq.

HACO played a major role in the construction and development of the Al Ahsa area, supplying products that conform to the internationally accepted standards and specifications, for big and small projects alike: commercial and industrial buildings, schools, palaces, mosques, hospitals and housing projects.

For more than two decades HACO has been an approved vendor of Saudi Aramco, the Saudi Electricity Company (SEC) and the Ministry of Communications in the area, and has been approved by the Kingdom's ministries since the company opened.
