Our policy

Providing the very best supply of drinking water which God has provided, with the best use of available resources, under the highest hygiene, to the GCC/UAE standard for water.

The safety of the customer is our top priority. Any complaint is investigated thoroughly and followed up as per our procedure.

“Don’t waste water even if you were at a running stream.”

Certification

BSCIC Certified, HACCP Registered, ISO 22000:2005 and ESMA certified. The company has been audited and found to be in compliance with the requirements of the Codex Alimentarius Commission's recommended international code of practice.

Building & facilities

The plant sits in a clean location with no stagnant water around it, and a clearly marked perimeter kept free of waste and unused equipment. The approach to the processing area is kept neat so wind-blown dust stays out.

Partitions, air flow and enclosed systems keep operations apart and prevent cross contamination. Storage for edible product is separate from other materials, and food handling areas are used for nothing else.

External doors close themselves and have air curtains and plastic strip curtains against insects. Processing halls are fully air conditioned at a constant temperature, with fly screens that come off for cleaning, protected lights, smooth crack-free walls and ceilings, and floors that drain against the flow of production.

Production

All production runs under strict sanitation, in one shift, under a qualified and experienced production in-charge. Equipment is built for easy cleaning and to avoid contamination by lubricants, fuel or metal; utensils are stainless steel, plastic or other non-corrosive material.

Separate storage is kept for raw materials, finished products, sanitising agents, toxic compounds, packaging materials, and consumables and additives. Packaging is stored in two rooms, one for corrugated cartons and one for flexible laminates, stacked on pallets away from walls and floor.

Inspection

Product inspection is carried out in three stages:

  1. 01Incoming raw materials, bought on specification from reputed suppliers
  2. 02In-process, at every stage, by the on-line supervisor, recorded in the in-process QC report
  3. 03Finished product, sampled for organoleptic and bacteriological checks, recorded in the finished products QC report

When a result is inconclusive, we sample again at double the scale to confirm it.

Glass & wood policy

No glass and no wood in the process area. Lights have non-breakable sleeves, glass-front gauges are covered with plastic film, glass thermometers are replaced with probe thermometers, and even drinking from a glass is banned inside the processing areas. Workers are trained on the glass policy.

Stock rotation

Everything is marked with its production and expiry date. We follow First In First Out (FIFO) and First Expire First Out (FEFO) for raw materials and finished product, and the production in-charge checks the rotation every month.
