Dr. Firas Joumma

Founder and implantologist. An Emirati dentist with more than 30 years in practice, who leads the team at Marina Center.

01

Implants, often in one visit

Dr. Firas has placed implants for more than 20 years, and has spent the last 10 on immediate loading. Where the bone allows, you leave with a temporary crown on the same day.

02

Laser dentistry since 2005

He took his Master's in Laser Dentistry at Aachen University in Germany in 2005, and was one of the first in the UAE to use Er,Cr:YSGG and Er:YAG lasers on teeth and gums.

03

Our own lab, and surgeons at hand

A digital workflow and our own lab technicians make crowns and prosthetics in house. Complex cases are planned with our maxillofacial surgeons and prosthodontists, and with colleagues abroad such as Prof. Alessandro Pozzi in Italy.
