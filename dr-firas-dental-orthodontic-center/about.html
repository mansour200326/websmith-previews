Dr. Firas Hussein Joumma, DDS

Founder and owner of Dr. Firas Dental & Orthodontic Center. Implantologist, and one of the people who brought laser dentistry to the UAE.

An Emirati national with more than 30 years of experience, Dr. Firas works mainly in implantology and advanced cosmetic dentistry.

Implants

He has more than 20 years in implantology, and 10 of them with immediate loading. That technique lets a temporary crown go in on the same visit as the implant, for fast, efficient care.

Laser dentistry

He earned his Master's Degree in Laser Dentistry from Aachen University, Germany, in 2005, and was one of the first in the UAE to use Er,Cr:YSGG and Er:YAG lasers for treatment of hard and soft tissue.

One team, one building

The center runs a fully digital workflow with experienced lab technicians in house, so crowns and prosthetics are made close to the chair. Dr. Firas works side by side with maxillofacial surgeons and prosthodontists on each case.

Colleagues abroad

For the most complex cases he can call on a network of international experts, including Prof. Fouad Khoori in Germany and Prof. Alessandro Pozzi in Italy.

Reem Hospital and ADNOC

Dr. Firas oversees dental care at Reem Hospital and at ADNOC Headquarters in Abu Dhabi.

To reach him directly: direct@firasdoc.com
