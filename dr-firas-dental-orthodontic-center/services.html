Implants and bone grafting

An implant replaces a missing tooth from the root up. Implantology and bone augmentation are what the center was built on: Dr. Firas has placed implants for more than 20 years.

When the jaw has lost bone, our oral and maxillofacial surgeons rebuild it first with a graft. For the last 10 years Dr. Firas has used immediate loading, so where the case allows, a temporary crown goes in on the same visit as the implant. The final crown is made with our own lab technicians.

Braces and Invisalign

Braces are easier to live with than they used to be. We fit traditional metal braces, clear and tooth-coloured brackets, and heat-activated wires that need fewer adjustments.

Some patients can skip braces altogether with Invisalign: clear, removable aligners with no brackets or wires. The orthodontist will tell you at the first visit whether it suits your bite.

Laser treatment

A dental laser uses light energy to cut or remove tissue, prepare a cavity for filling, and treat the gums and soft tissue in the mouth.

Dr. Firas holds a Master's in Laser Dentistry from Aachen University (2005) and was one of the first in the UAE to use Er,Cr:YSGG and Er:YAG lasers on both teeth and gums.

Root canal treatment

The pulp in the centre of a tooth holds its nerves and blood vessels. When decay or infection reaches it, a root canal clears it out and seals the tooth, so you keep it instead of losing it.

Our three endodontists focus on root canals. Many are finished in a single session, and the team is used to patients who are nervous in the chair.

Veneers and smile makeovers

Veneers are thin shells fitted over the front of the teeth to change their shape, colour and line. With today's cosmetic dentistry, a smile makeover can be quick and painless.

Cosmetic dentistry is one of the center's specialities. The plan is made with you, so the result still looks like your own smile.

Check-ups and cleaning

Regular check-ups and cleaning help prevent cavities, gum disease, enamel wear and bad breath, and help you keep your teeth as you get older.

Your dentist will tell you how often to come back. Our hygienists also teach you what to do at home between visits.

Children's dentistry

Children fall and bump their faces more than adults do, and a knock can damage baby teeth and the teeth growing behind them. Our children's department sees young patients for check-ups and for injuries.

After a fall, call us straight away and bring your child in.
