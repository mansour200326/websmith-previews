About Excel.

Excel International School used to be Zakher Private School, British Division. Today it is one of the AJ Group of Schools, a group established in 1989.

The group also runs Al Ain Juniors School (British and Indian systems), Al Ain Juniors Nursery and the House of Colours Nursery. Excel shares the AJ Club for after-school sport with Al Ain Juniors.

What we aim for.

"At EXCEL, we aim to nurture our young people to be achievers of extraordinary determination."

From the school's vision. The motto is "Ready for Life".

Our pledge

  1. Establish high educational standards
  2. Help every student reach their dream, knowing their skills and challenges
  3. Grow a lifelong love of learning
  4. Build self-confidence, humility and love for self and others
  5. Keep the school innovative and creative
  6. Keep children and staff healthy and safe at all times

How the school feels.

  • Children take pride in their work, the work of others and their school.
  • Each child should feel challenged, not daunted.
  • We celebrate children's talents and achievements.
  • Everyone appreciates the value of laughter.

Along the way.

  1. 1989

    The AJ Group of Schools is established. The group marked its 35th anniversary in 2024.

  2. 2024–25

    Excel starts the Pearson Edexcel IGCSE programme for Grade 9 and Grade 10.

  3. 2025

    ADEK rates the school Good. Inspection report (PDF)

  4. 2026
