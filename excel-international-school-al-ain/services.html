Stages and subjects.

01Early Years, FS2From age four. Learning through planned and spontaneous play.

A carefully planned and structured classroom, with activities that are fun, relevant and motivating. Children learn through planned and spontaneous play that helps them connect, reflect and grow.

  • ADEK age rule: four by 31 December 2026
  • House of Colours Nursery children get first priority for FS2

02Key Stages 1 and 2The UK National Curriculum, and the Discovery Forest outside.

A broad and balanced education following the UK National Curriculum. Arabic is compulsory for every child, with French, Tagalog or Urdu as optional languages.

  • English
  • Maths
  • Science
  • History
  • Geography
  • Art and Design
  • PE, with swimming
  • ICT
  • UAE Social Studies
  • Arabic
  • Islamic Studies (Muslim students)
  • Moral Science (non-Muslim students)

The Discovery Forest is their outdoor classroom: plantation beds, a rain area, a market area for role play, a sandbox, mushroom stools for reading and a small amphitheatre for drama and art.

03Key Stage 3Curiosity Labs and Maker's Labs.

Key Stage 3 is where most learning habits are set. The Curiosity Labs and Maker's Labs are for students' own how, why and what-if questions: they find the answers by doing, investigating, observing and experimenting.

  • Collaborative projects
  • Critical thinking exercises
  • Digital literacy
04IGCSE, AS and A LevelPearson Edexcel IGCSE from Grade 9, then the sixth form.

Since 2024–25 Grade 9 and Grade 10 students take the Pearson Edexcel IGCSE programme, followed by AS and A Levels. Excel is a Pearson Edexcel Approved Centre.

Alongside exam preparation, Key Stage 4 students follow the Life Skills Training Track:

  • Communication and interpersonal skills
  • Decision-making and problem-solving
  • Creative and critical thinking
  • Emotional intelligence, self-awareness and empathy

Admissions.

  1. Book a school tour

    Register your interest and the admissions team gets in touch to arrange your visit.

  2. Apply online

    On the Edunation admissions portal: choose the academic year, enter your child's details and upload the documents, then the guardian's details. The guardian's email becomes the login.

  3. Follow the application

    A username and password arrive by email so you can track it. Once submitted, the form can't be edited. If the year group is full, it joins the waiting log and families are called in order.

  4. How places are given

    Siblings get priority, complete applications are considered first, and the baseline assessment is taken into account. The Head Teacher's decision is final.

Documents you'll need

  • Original Emirates ID of the student and both parents
  • Student's passport copy with visa page
  • Sponsor's passport copy with visa page
  • 4 passport-size photos of the student
  • Birth certificate (attested if issued outside the UAE)
  • Vaccination card
  • Attested report card for Year 3 / Grade 2 and above, if issued outside the UAE
  • Attested original transfer certificate, if issued outside the UAE
  • MoE equivalency certificate for transfers from outside the UAE (Grades 2–11)
  • GL Assessment results (Year 5 / Grade 4 and above) and/or CAT4, if any
  • Proof of address: water and electricity bill, tenancy or ownership contract

The full list with attestation steps is on the admission procedure page. The registration fee is non-refundable.
