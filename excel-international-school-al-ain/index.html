Excel International School Al Ain: Ready for life.

A British-curriculum school for boys and girls in Falaj Hazza, Al Ain. From FS2 to A Levels in Year 13, rated Good by ADEK.

Book a school tour → WhatsApp the school

One school, FS2 to Year 13.

Children can start with us in FS2 at four and stay until their A Levels.

We follow the British curriculum. From Grade 9 students take the Pearson Edexcel IGCSE programme, then AS and A Levels in Years 12 and 13. Excel is a Pearson Edexcel Approved Centre.

The school used to be Zakher Private School, British Division. It is run by the AJ Group of Schools, established in 1989, alongside Al Ain Juniors School and the House of Colours Nursery.

What each stage looks like.

Four stages on one campus, each with its own spaces. The full subject list is on the Learning page.

01 · Early Years, FS2

Learning through play

A carefully planned classroom where children learn through planned and spontaneous play: activities that are fun, relevant and get them talking.

ADEK's age rule: four years old by 31 December to join FS2.

02 · Key Stages 1 and 2

The Discovery Forest

The UK National Curriculum: English, Maths, Science, History, Geography, Art and Design, ICT, and PE with swimming. Arabic for everyone, Islamic Studies or Moral Science, and UAE Social Studies.

Outside, the Discovery Forest: plantation beds, a rain area, a sandbox, mushroom stools for reading and a small amphitheatre for drama.

03 · Key Stage 3

Curiosity Labs

The Curiosity Labs and Maker's Labs are for the how, why and what-if questions: students find answers by doing, investigating, observing and experimenting.

04 · Key Stage 4 to Year 13

IGCSE, AS and A Level

Pearson Edexcel IGCSE in Grades 9 and 10, then AS and A Levels. Alongside the exams, the Life Skills Training Track: communication, decision-making, problem-solving, critical thinking and emotional intelligence.

Joining Excel.

  1. Book a school tour

    Register your interest and the admissions team gets in touch to arrange your visit.

  2. Apply online

    On the Edunation admissions portal: choose the academic year, enter your child's details and upload the documents. The guardian's email becomes the login.

  3. Follow the application

    A username and password arrive by email so you can track it. If the year group is full, the application joins the waiting log and families are called in order.

  4. How places are given

    Siblings get priority, complete applications are considered first, and the baseline assessment is taken into account.
