Hotel and restaurant supplies, and a joinery workshop, in Doha.

Arabian Coast Qatar supplies hotels, restaurants and catering companies with everything that goes on the table and into the room: chinaware, glassware, flatware, buffetware, linen, towels, amenities and more. Our carpentry and joinery division makes wardrobes, doors, panelling and furniture for offices, villas, hotels and apartments.
