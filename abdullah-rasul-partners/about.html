The firm

Established by Emirati lawyers. Founded in 2001 by Attorney Abdulla Mohammed Rasoul Al Harmodi, with offices in Abu Dhabi, Al Ain and Dubai.

Abdullah Al Harmodi

Founder · Lawyer

His experience covers all fields of law and legal practice.

It started in the legal departments of government and private bodies, extended across the courthouses and state arbitrators, and is supported by a long series of studies and academic research.

He graduated from the Faculty of Law, U.A.E University. Since founding the firm he has been inspired by the vision of the late Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan: to see the UAE take part in the making of human civilisation, and that everyone has the right to justice.

The firm's vision

“To be at the forefront of excellent offices in the U.A.E and to be trusted in the community.”

We keep our clients fully informed at each stage of their claim, and explain each part of the litigation process.
