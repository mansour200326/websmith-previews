Practice areas

Thirteen areas, for individuals and companies, in Arabic and English, set out in three groups.

In court and in arbitration

Cases before the UAE courts, arbitration and mediation, sports disputes up to FIFA and CAS, and collecting what you are owed.

01Lawsuit

We represent our clients before the UAE courts, civil, criminal, commercial, labor and family, at every stage. We attend the expert meetings, prepare all the documents and memos required, and represent companies in any dispute with a local sponsor.

02Arbitration & Mediation

We represent clients in resolving legal disputes through arbitration, whether civil or commercial: we help the client choose the arbitrator, and sign all the documents and minutes of meeting.

03Litigation and Arbitration in Sports Matters

We appear, plead and act for litigants in sports issues before the National Association, the Federation and the competent committees of FIFA, and before the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) in Lausanne, Switzerland.

04Collection of Debts

Collection through amicable settlement or through the courts. This division also provides property management, with accurate, regular reports on all matters related to the property managed, and debt collection where there is no supply agreement.

About a case or a dispute: Abu Dhabi 02 222 2515 Dubai 04 295 6661 Al Ain 03 780 3033

Companies, contracts and rights

Setting up, signing, registering and protecting: the paperwork behind a business, local or foreign.

05Business Law

Legal services for forming companies: we bring both contractual parties to a mutual agreement and finalise the establishment procedures in line with the applicable laws and regulations.

06Contracts & Agreements

All types of commercial, civil and industrial contracts, in Arabic and English, under local or foreign laws and regulations. We also send legal notices to defaulting parties.

07Commercial Agencies & Franchising

We draw up commercial agency and distribution contracts and register them with the Ministry of Commerce, to protect the rights of the local agents.

08Foreign Investment

We prepare contracts within the foreign investment registration and licensing procedures, and review contracts and investments under the system in force in the United Arab Emirates.

09Protection of Intellectual Property

Protection of intellectual property rights at local and international level, including registration of patents, copyrights and brands, and the legal protection they need.

10Copyright Protection

We register literary works with the competent authorities and represent the client in protecting his rights from abuse. Legal data is kept secret and private by the legal team.
