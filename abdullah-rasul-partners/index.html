2001

A firm established by Emirati lawyers.

Attorney Abdulla Mohammed Rasoul Al Harmodi founded the firm in 2001. He graduated from the Faculty of Law at United Arab Emirates University, and his experience started in the legal departments of government and private bodies before reaching the courthouses and state arbitrators.

From the start he was inspired by the vision of the late Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan: to see the UAE take part in the making of human civilisation, and that everyone has the right to justice.
