British Early Years Foundation Stage

Our daily programme follows the EYFS framework and its seven areas of learning. The first three matter most at this age: they build curiosity, the ability to learn, and the ability to form relationships.

  1. Communication and Language
  2. Personal, Social and Emotional Development
  3. Physical Development
  4. Literacy
  5. Mathematics
  6. Understanding the World
  7. Expressive Arts and Design

How we teach it

The mobile classroom

Children use the whole building during the day, moving between themed spaces, and have a say in the direction of their learning through free flow.

A creative focus

Painting, drawing, drama and role play. Hands-on activities with a strong focus on art and self-expression.

Small groups

Smaller, more intimate groups mean more individual attention, and children who feel confident to speak up.

Specialist lessons

Music, Arabic and French are taught as specialist lessons. English is our main language of teaching.

How you’ll know how it’s going

Our teachers observe each child to understand where they are, what interests them and how they learn, and use it to plan lessons. You’ll see the progress through the Learning Journal, regular parent-teacher meetings and weekly newsletters with ideas for home.

A full-day facility

The learning doesn’t stop at the end of school hours. Every class can stay until 6pm. Ask our registrar about the after-school option.
