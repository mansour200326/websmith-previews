About us

Learning Tree grew out of a wish to give young children the best care and education, in a rich, stimulating place. It was planned and set up by experienced educators, all grounded in the British Early Years Foundation Stage.

The Learning Tree Philosophy

Fundamentally, The Learning Tree is a neighbourhood nursery. Like a tree, it grows from our neighbourhood and shapes it, as it caters to the needs of our community.

Dubai, and Executive Towers in particular, is an exciting place for families. We have drawn on that to give children room to be active learners, intrepid explorers and creative inventors, all within the safety of their school.

Many families here are part of a fast-changing expat community, and still want to belong somewhere. Here every child is welcomed as a member of the family and as a friend. An international education for an international community, in a local neighbourhood.

Our children are not simply targets of instruction. They deserve to be inspired, encouraged, and to grow.

Our third teacher

Children learn best in spaces that respond to them. Every room, and the way the rooms are used, was planned around the outcomes of the EYFS.

Outdoors, in private

Our own outdoor play area, with an age-appropriate play gym on Astroturf under a shady canopy. Sand and water play, and PE games.

Safety first

The premises were designed with the children’s safety and security as the highest priority.

Equipment from the UK, US and Germany

Our Early Years furniture, toys, learning resources and play equipment come from leading international suppliers.

Our team

Our teachers are experienced, qualified educators. English is our main language of teaching, and we chose our staff to reflect the wonderful mix of a Dubai community.
