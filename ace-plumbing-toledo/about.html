AboutJosh

Ace Plumbing is run by Josh Barfield, a master plumber who has spent well over a decade fixing the plumbing in Northwest Ohio homes and businesses.

Prompt calls, good work, and the price before we start.

Ace Plumbing has a simple goal: to give you the best plumbing service we can. Josh Barfield, Master Plumber, has been taking care of families' plumbing for many years, and the promise hasn't changed: excellent workmanship, prompt calls, and personal service.

Our service techs go through ongoing training, so we're ready for whatever you've got, from a dripping faucet to a flooded basement. We try to keep it affordable and get it done on time.

We take house and business jobs, from Toledo, Sylvania, Perrysburg and Maumee down to Bowling Green and up into Temperance, Lambertville and Bedford, Michigan. Every job starts with a free estimate, and we stand 100% behind our work.
