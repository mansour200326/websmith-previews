Before the first freeze

Ohio winters split pipes every year. Here is what Josh tells customers to do in November, before the cold comes.

  1. Shut off the outdoor faucets

    Close the shut-off valve on the pipe that feeds each outdoor faucet.

  2. Drain them

    Then open the outdoor faucets so the water left in the line runs out.

  3. Let warm air in

    For indoor faucets that freeze now and then, leave the cabinet doors open, or let a slow trickle run on the coldest nights.

  4. Wrap what's exposed

    Insulate pipes in unheated places like basements and garages, and any that catch the wind.

  5. Going away?

    If the house will sit empty over winter, drain the water pipes before you leave.
