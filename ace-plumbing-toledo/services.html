Sump pumps

The most common reason a sump pump fails is the switch. The pump can move around in the basin, and the float that works the switch gets lodged against the side. Sometimes it's debris. Making sure the switch and float arm move freely is most of the job.

We maintain and replace pumps, and fit a backup so the basement stays dry when the power goes out.

  • Battery backup sump pumps

Frozen pipes

When a line freezes, we come out and thaw it, then fit heat tape so it stays open. For a business that couldn't be without water, we ran a bypass line through the freeze.

"Your quick response and reasonable pricing was greatly appreciated," wrote Bolan after that January job.

To stop it happening: close the shut-off valves to the outdoor faucets and drain them, leave cabinet doors open on cold nights, and insulate pipes in basements and garages.

Questions people ask us

Something else? Call 419-461-1944 or email sales@aceplumbingusa.com.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. You get a free estimate before we perform any work, on house and business jobs alike.

What is the most common reason for sump pump failure?

A switching problem. The pump can move around inside the basin, and the float that operates the switch lodges against the side. Sometimes debris is to blame. Make sure the switch and float arm move freely.

Why won't my sink hold water?

Hair and other matter build up in the pop-up drain or strainer over time, or the pop-up or basket strainer is worn. Clean out the build-up, tighten the nut connecting the lift rod, and replace the assembly if it's worn.

How do I stop my faucets freezing in winter?

Before the freeze, close the shut-off valve on the pipes to your outdoor faucets, then open the outdoor faucets to drain them. For indoor faucets, leave the cabinet doors open or let a slow trickle run. Insulate pipes in unheated areas, and drain the pipes if the house will be empty for the winter.

The plunger didn't clear the toilet and the water is low. What now?

Fill a bucket with warm water and pour it into the bowl from waist height or higher. If the level stays low, repeat. Still stuck? Call us.
