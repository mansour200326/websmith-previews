Services for every BMW

Routine servicing as well as problem-solving diagnostics, whatever BMW you drive. We'll tell you what's needed, what can wait and what it's likely to cost before we start.

01

Servicing

Depending on the age and model of your car, condition based servicing (CBS) may apply, from 2005 onwards depending on the exact model. CBS gathers information from modules and sensors around the car to lengthen service intervals and make the most of each part, so prices are less fixed than an old-style service.

Older cars use a service interval system, based on time and mileage rather than condition.

  • BMW approved oil every time. Cheaper oils risk premature engine wear.
  • Genuine BMW or OEM parts, whichever is the best solution for the job.
  • Genuine parts are the same ones a main dealer uses, with the trade discount applied.
  • Every service is logged on the BMW database and written to the car's electronic service book.
