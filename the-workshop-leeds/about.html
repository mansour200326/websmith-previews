Johannesburg to Leeds

The man behind The Workshop Leeds is Anthony Rouse, a qualified Senior BMW Technician with over twenty years' experience within the main BMW dealer network. This is his story, in his words.

“I will never tell you something needs doing when it doesn't.”

“I was born in South Africa and in 1994 I came to the UK, reversing the journey my father had taken 50 years earlier when he sailed to SA during the war and fell in love with the country. When I arrived in the UK I had already completed my BMW apprenticeship in Johannesburg and qualified as a BMW Technician in Cape Town. In the UK I gained my Senior Technician accreditation.

Originally, I came to Europe to travel but after an amazing trip round the continent in a classic VW Campervan, I decided to stay and make England my home. I've been here ever since and I love the British way of life, if not always the weather!

Complete customer satisfaction is fundamental to the way I work. My aim is to take care of your BMW to the standard you expect at a fair price. I will do what is needed to keep your car in prime condition but I will never tell you something needs doing when it doesn't. I will discuss the options with you and answer any questions you may have so you have all the information you need to help you decide how to proceed.

I see this as a shared endeavour: you want to take care of your BMW and I want to take care of you as a customer.”
