About us

Glow on Call Beauty & Spa is a home-service beauty and spa team in Abu Dhabi. We come to you, with everything we need.

A small team that travels

We are a team of practitioners working across Abu Dhabi, seven days a week from 10:00 to 21:00. Nails, lashes and brows, threading, waxing, massage, facials and hair, all in your own home.

Our setup goes where we go: a mirror case with lights, a portable wash basin, our folding chair, a treatment bed for facials and massage, a lash bed with its lamp, and a trolley bag with our name on it.

The products we work with

Our facials use Skeyndor. For hair, we offer K18 and Kerasilk treatments.
