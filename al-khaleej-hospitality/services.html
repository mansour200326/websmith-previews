Weddings and koushas

The setting has to be the highlight of the occasion. Our designers know local and international design, from interior decor and flower arrangements to the kousha, the wedding stage. If you already have a picture in mind, we will design it with you and build it.

  • Kousha (wedding stage) design and build
  • Flower arrangements and centrepieces
  • Interior decor for the hall
  • Dinner for the guests from our kitchens

Buffets and catering

Food and drink for every kind of event: local, Arabic and international, with menus for every taste. Everything is prepared in the company kitchens to ISO 22000:2005 and HACCP, following the requirements of the Abu Dhabi Food Control Authority. Our kitchens can prepare about 10,000 meals a day.

  • Food buffets
  • Breakfast
  • VIP lunches and dinners
  • Local, Arabic and international menus
