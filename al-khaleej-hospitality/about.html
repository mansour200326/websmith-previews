About us

Al Khaleej Hospitality is a national company in the UAE that organises events, weddings, meetings and conferences, and provides the food and beverage service for them, with qualified people who know what our clients expect.

Our vision

To lead the way in keeping our customs and traditions alive in a modern form, by giving the best hospitality service to every kind of event, food and beverage included, at the highest degree of luxury.

Our mission

To provide hospitality for events and activities in our own way, with a balance between the modern and the authentic, and to build bridges of cooperation with institutions and with the people of our community.

From the General Manager

We at Al Khaleej Hospitality are confident in how we manage and organise events for all occasions, with an eye for detail. Our staff are qualified and trained, with the experience to give the finest level of service.

We will keep earning this success through the trust of our clients, and we look forward to delivering whatever you wish for your event, personal or corporate.

Our people

We only hire people with real experience in this specialised field. The management keeps developing every employee through internal and external recognised training programmes, so our people always know the market as it grows.

Food safety

Our food is prepared in the company kitchens to ISO 22000:2005 and HACCP, and to the requirements of the Abu Dhabi Food Control Authority (ADFCA). The kitchens can prepare about 10,000 meals a day.
