From the first meeting to the last guest

How an event comes together with us.

01

We meet

You sit with our sales team and tell us about the occasion, the guests and what you have in mind. A coordinator stays with your event from here on.

02

You choose

Furniture, settings and decor are on show in our showrooms, so you can see them and pick what suits you before the day.

03

We design and build

Our designers draw the kousha, the flowers and the hall, or take your own idea and make it real.

04

The kitchen cooks

Your menu is prepared in our own kitchens, local, Arabic or international, to ISO 22000 and HACCP and the Abu Dhabi food control requirements.

05

On the night

Our team sets up, serves and stays to the end, so you can be with your guests.
