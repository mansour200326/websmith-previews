Vinayak Interiors is a Dubai fit-out company. Our designers draw the space, our team builds it, and the same project manager stays with you from the first drawing to handover.

We have done hundreds of projects across the Middle East and Asia: souvenir stores in Dubai Mall, an Indian restaurant, nail bars, showroom offices, villa pergolas and living rooms.

We take care of procurement, the budget, the MEP work and the approvals, so you can keep running your business while we get the new space ready.

What we do

01

Interior design

Our in-house designers plan the layout, choose the finishes and show you the space in 3D before anything is ordered.

02

Complete fit-out works

Partitions, ceilings, flooring, lighting, paint and signage. We hand over a shop, restaurant or office that is ready to open.

03

Customised joinery

Display units, counters, wall panelling and slat screens made to our own drawings, like the skyline shelving at Discover Dubai.

04

MEP works

The electrical, plumbing and air-conditioning changes the new layout needs, done by the same team.

05

Project management consultancy

Procurement, budget and programme looked after for you, through to delivery and installation.

06

Authority approval

We prepare the drawings and follow up the approvals so work can start, and the doors can open, on time.
