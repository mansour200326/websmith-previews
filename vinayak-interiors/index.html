Who we are

A fit-out and joinery contractor, built from the workshop up.

Vinayak Interiors is based in Al Quoz, Dubai. We design and build interiors for shops, restaurants, showrooms, offices and private homes — our in-house designers draw the space, our own joinery shop builds the counters, panelling and fixed furniture, and our site teams run the civil, MEP and finishes through to handover.

We've carried out projects across Dubai and the northern Emirates, for clients who come back for the next fit-out once the first one is running. Small renovation or full store build, the approach is the same: draw it properly, price it straight, and stay on site until it's done.

What we do

Services

Six services, run by one team, so the drawing, the joinery and the site work don't belong to three different contractors.

01

Interior Design

We draw the space before anyone builds anything — layout, finishes, lighting and material boards, signed off before work starts on site.

02

Complete Fit-out Works

Partitions, flooring, ceilings, paint and signage — we run the whole site from shell handover to opening day.

03

Custom Joinery Work

Our own joinery workshop in Al Quoz builds counters, shelving, reception desks and panelling to the drawing, not off a catalogue.

04

MEP Works

Electrical, air conditioning, plumbing and fire fighting, coordinated with the fit-out so nothing gets opened up again after painting.

05

Project Management Consultancy

One team tracking programme, budget and subcontractors from kick-off to handover.

06

Authority Approval

Drawings and permits through the relevant municipality, civil defence and mall or free zone authority, so the site isn't waiting on paperwork.
