Who we are

Most of what we build ends up under the road. We lay the sewers, water mains and TSE lines, push pipe through the ground by micro-tunnelling where a street can't be dug up, then build the road, the lighting and the signals on top.

Grade A Local Civil Contractor for roads and drainage on the Ministry of Finance list, and qualified for Ashghal's Local Roads and Drainage Programme since 2013.

ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001. One management system for quality, health and safety and the environment, on every site and in every department.

How we grew

From house connections for the water authority to Doha's biggest sewer tunnels.

1992

House connections

We started with small contracts from the PWA O&M House Connection Unit, then took on subcontract work.

1999

First main contract

Our first pipeline and drainage project as main contractor. Two years later we started on roads, as a subcontractor.

2003

Micro-tunnelling

We brought in trenchless pipe-jacking so pipes could go under live roads without digging them up. Other contractors now call us in for it.

2004

Roads as main contractor

Our first road project as main contractor, followed by road improvement works in Doha, Rayyan, Al Khor and Zones 55, 56 and 67.

2013

Doha Industrial Area

Qualified for Ashghal's Local Roads and Drainage Programme, and awarded Roads and Infrastructure in Doha Industrial Area, Package 1. In 2016 came LIS-01, the Lateral Interceptor Sewer.
