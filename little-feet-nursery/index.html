Little Feet Nursery, SharjahLittleFeet

A Montessori nursery in Sharjah for children from 1½ to 4 years. Three branches, in Al Khan, Al Khalidiya and Al Jazzat, all with the same teachers' training and the same day. We've been here since 2003.

Book a visit on WhatsApp or call 06 545 4296

Since 2003, more than 2,000 children have taken their first school steps with us and gone on to big school ready for it.

23years in Sharjah, grown from a single branch to three

We teach the Montessori way, inside the British EYFS framework, with STEAM projects mixed in. Every floor has its own Montessori lab and its own indoor play area, and the children learn in Arabic and English.

Our teachers are Montessori-trained and SPEA-certified, and trained in first aid, fire safety and hygiene. Sharjah nurseries are regulated by the Sharjah Private Education Authority, and so are we.

A morning at Little Feet

What the day looks like for a preschooler, from the gate to home time. Times are the same at all three branches.

7:00 am

Early drop-off

The gate opens at seven for parents who start work early. A teacher is there to take your little one in. Our air-conditioned bus, with a supervisor on board, also collects from set areas of Sharjah.

8:30 am

Class and the Montessori lab

The main routine starts: circle time, then work in the lab with real Montessori materials. Pouring, sorting, sounds and letters, counting beads.

Mid-morning

Play, inside and out

Each floor has its own indoor play area, and outside there's a big turf play area with slides, cars and tunnels. Water play, the vegetable patch, and a lot of running.

All day

A nurse and a speech therapist

We have a clinic room and a nurse on the team. A speech therapist works with children who need a little extra help with their first words.

12:00 – 2:00 pm

Home time, or daycare

Class ends at noon. Children in daycare stay on for lunch, rest and play until two.

Five corners of the Montessori lab

Our labs are painted in coloured bands, one for each area of the Montessori curriculum. This is what happens in each.

The Montessori lab at Little Feet. There's one on every floor.

Practical life

Pouring, buttoning, washing hands, laying the table. Real tasks that make them independent.

Sensorial

Size, shape, texture, loud and soft, hot and cold. Sharper senses and steadier hands.

Language

Sounds linked to letters, step by step, towards reading and writing, in English and Arabic.

Math

Real objects from day one, so a number always means a quantity they've held.

Cultural

Nature, science, art and music, and the world around them.

  • EYFSThe British Early Years Foundation Stage, side by side with Montessori.
  • STEAMHands-on projects in science, technology, building, art and maths.
  • Arabic & EnglishStories, songs and everyday talk in both languages.
  • Summer campCrafts, experiments, stories and outdoor games at Al Khalidiya.
