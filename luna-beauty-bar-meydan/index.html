A nail and lash studio inside Azizi Riviera 46.

Nails are most of what we do. We work with The Gel Bottle BIAB and CND hard gel for extensions, and we take our time over the prep: shaping, cuticle work, then the colour. Weak or thin nails? Ask for the strong base gel manicure.

Lashes and brows have their own quiet room: classic, hybrid and volume sets, infills, an LVL lash lift, and HD brows that last up to eight weeks. Threading and waxing too.

It's adults only, there's parking, and there's a drinks menu while you sit.
