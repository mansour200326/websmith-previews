Since 1984

From the firm's own archive: newspaper cuttings and prints from four decades of Dubai business.

1977 – 1984

From Mumbai to Dubai

Kiran Sangani, chartered accountant and law graduate from Mumbai, joined a Dubai trading group as chief accountant in 1977 and became its finance manager within four years. In 1984 he opened his own firm, Sangani & Co.

Cutting: "New team to lead Overseas Indians' Forum". Kiran Sangani was the forum's honorary secretary.
1985 – 2000

The Dubai chapter

A founder member of the Dubai chapter of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India: its treasurer in 1986–88 and honorary secretary in 1998–2000. Fellow of the Institute from 1985.

Cutting dated 8 May 1999: "ICAI Dubai Chapter holds forum".
1998 – 2002

At the table

As honorary secretary of the Overseas Indians Economic Forum, Kiran Sangani sat on panels with the people who were building Dubai's economy.

Print: the forum welcomes H.E. Mohamed Ali Alabbar, then Director General of the Department of Economic Development. Kiran Sangani, far left.
2007 – 2012

The next partner

Pratik Sangani joined as partner in 2007, after KPMG in Dubai and PwC in the USA. The firm became an approved audit firm with the DIFC in 2009 and the DFSA in 2012, and opened its SAIF Zone branch the same year.

Photo: internal training in the firm's boardroom.
2014 – 2016

Thirty years on

ACCA Approved Employer at Gold level in 2014, the year the firm turned thirty. Full member of Kudos International Network in 2015, and a bigger office in Business Bay in 2016.

Photo: Kiran Sangani receives The Golden Award 2014 from the Indian Business & Professional Council, Dubai.
