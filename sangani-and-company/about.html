The firm

Sangani & Co. was opened in Dubai in 1984 by Kiran Sangani. Today it is run by partner Pratik Sangani and by Shabbir Patanwala, who joined in 1988.

About the firm

We are a firm of chartered accountants doing statutory and internal audits, bookkeeping, company formation, legal agreements, feasibility studies and project reports, for medium-sized private companies across the UAE.

Our clients include banks, retailers, gold and jewellery groups, hotels, exchange houses, shipping and construction companies, traders, manufacturers, travel companies, schools and associations. We follow international accounting standards.

We're set up for small and medium-sized companies. Whether you are an international group or an entrepreneur looking for good professional service at a fair price, we can help.

Besides the Dubai office, the firm has one branch, in SAIF Zone, Sharjah, under the name Sangani Chartered Accountants & Financial Consultants.

The founder

Kiran Sangani opened the firm in 1984 and led it as managing partner.

The late Kiran Sangani

B.Com, FCA, LLB · Founder and Managing Partner

Sydenham College, Mumbai. Chartered accountant and law graduate.

He trained with a Mumbai firm of chartered accountants, auditing manufacturing, banking, government and trading clients. In 1977 he moved to Dubai as chief accountant of a well-known trading group, and within four years was its finance manager, reporting to the president.

In 1984 he opened his own firm, Sangani & Co., and built a portfolio of more than 100 clients across a wide range of industries, adding bookkeeping, company formation and business consultancy to the audit work.

He was a founder member of the Dubai chapter of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, its treasurer in 1986–88 and its honorary secretary in 1998–2000; honorary secretary of the Overseas Indians Economic Forum; and vice president of the Indian Business & Professional Council.

Running the firm today

Ask for either of them by name.

Pratik Sangani

Partner · B.Com, FCA, CPA, CIA, CISA

Qualified as a chartered accountant in 2002, ranked 32nd and 42nd in India at the Foundation and Intermediate stages; Fellow of the ICAI since 2010. Also a US CPA and Certified Internal Auditor.

KPMG Dubai 2002–05, external audit across retail, broadcasting, construction, trading, manufacturing, travel, share broking and ship management. PwC USA 2005–07, including Sarbanes-Oxley work for a major public client. Partner since 2007, responsible for the firm's assurance work.

Shabbir Patanwala

Director, Business Consultancy · B.Com, Mumbai University

With the firm since 1988. Audited hotels and hotel apartments, exchange houses and jewellery groups, did special audits in the finance sector, and was part of the team on investigations for the Central Bank of the UAE.

Set up the company formation department: LLCs and partnerships, free zone FZE and FZC, offshore companies, and their liquidation. Also runs the firm's administration.
