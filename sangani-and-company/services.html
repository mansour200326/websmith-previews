Audit

We are registered auditors with the Ministry of Economy and the Department of Economic Development, so we can sign audits under the Commercial Companies Law and other regulations. We are also an approved audit firm with the DIFC (since 2009) and the DFSA (since 2012).

External (statutory) audits

Required by law for every LLC in the UAE and for free zone establishments and companies. An audited set of accounts keeps you compliant, gives you a track record, and gives your partners, suppliers and banks confidence in the numbers. Along the way we point out weak controls and places to cut costs, for the owner's attention.

Internal audits

While the owner is busy growing the business, risks go unnoticed and controls get neglected. Our internal audit team looks at every process, tells you whether the controls are enough, and reports to management only.

  • Statutory audits under the UAE Commercial Companies Law
  • Free zone audits
  • Independent audits for banks and financial institutions
  • Audits for insurance claim settlements
  • Audits for court cases
  • Sarbanes-Oxley compliance audits
  • Stock audits
  • Revenue audits of retail outlets

Bookkeeping & accounts

When a company starts out, the books are often the first thing to slip: no time, or no qualified person to do them. Without them you can't judge your own decisions or see how the business is doing.

We keep the books in the format and to the deadline you need, prepared accurately for the statutory requirements, and we'll suggest the accounting software that fits your business.

  • Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or annual bookkeeping
  • Setting up the accounting system and financial policies
  • Maintaining client records and books
  • Advice on accounting and MIS packages

04

Company formation

First we ask why you are forming the company and what it will do. Then we point you to the right legal form and the right zone, weighing cost and your future plans, and handle the paperwork.

Mainland

Limited liability companies under the UAE Companies Law, branch or representative offices, and professional licence companies, in any of the seven emirates.

Free zones

Free Zone Companies (FZC), Free Zone Establishments (FZE) and branches, in JAFZA, SAIF Zone, Dubai Media City, Dubai Internet City and others.

Offshore

For holding investments, bank accounts, contracts or property: JAFZA Offshore, RAK Offshore, BVI, Mauritius. We are a registered agent for JAFZA Offshore (since 2007) and RAK Offshore (since 2010).

  • Choosing the right legal structure
  • Name approval
  • Memorandum and articles of association
  • Legal documentation
  • Government procedures, start to finish
