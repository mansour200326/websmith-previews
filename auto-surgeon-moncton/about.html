Jeff Hurd

Mechanic and owner. ASE Certified Master Technician, Licensed Journeyman (Interprovincial Standard), with eleven certificates in automotive electrical and mechanical repair.

“I am very thankful to my customers for the positive feedback and the opportunity they have given me to work in the automotive industry. I love my job.”

Jeff has been fixing cars for a living since 1979. He worked for Ford dealers as a general mechanic, including electrical, and for Chrysler, AMC, Jeep and Renault dealers as electrical specialist, drive train and general mechanic. He also worked in independent repair shops, with specialized training in electrical fault diagnostics, emissions, fuel systems and drive trains.

He opened Auto Surgeon in March 1994 and bought Custom Muffler in October 2005. He welcomes the challenge of keeping up with new car technology, which is why so much goes into equipment and information systems.

On the track: two first place awards at Speed Sport, with an 11.32 second quarter mile.

Mission statement

Word for word, as Jeff wrote it.

  1. Serve every customer the way we would like to be served.
  2. Provide clear and accurate estimates for recommended service or repair.
  3. Perform no repairs without the customer's authorization.
  4. Perform every service and repair professionally using top quality parts and materials.
  5. Prioritize recommended services and repairs in this order: #1 Safety, #2 Progressive damage, #3 Recommended maintenance.
  6. We will not recommend services or repairs that are without benefit to our customers and their vehicles.
  7. We will stand behind our work and honor all applicable warranties.
