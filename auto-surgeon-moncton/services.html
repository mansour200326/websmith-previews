02

Oil and fluid service

Lube, oil and filter with a preventive maintenance inspection, and manufacturer approved maintenance on new cars. We're an authorized new car service facility for North American, Asian and European vehicles, so the warranty stays good.

The other fluids matter as much as the oil. Changing them on time saves money on costly repairs:

  • Coolant. Old antifreeze turns acidic and eats head gaskets and water pump seals.
  • Transmission. Nearly nine in ten transmission failures come from overheating and dirty fluid. A normal service changes only about 25% of it; our flush equipment removes all of the old fluid.
  • Brake fluid. It absorbs moisture, which lowers its boiling point and can turn it acidic enough to damage lines and ABS modules.
  • Power steering. Heat and hard turns break the fluid down until sludge wears out the rack or pump.
  • Fuel injection flush. Clears deposits that hurt economy, emissions and performance.

Battery, alternator, starter

The most common sign of a weak battery is a slow crank. When the engine starts to turn over slowly, have it checked right away.

  • Hard or slow cranking, worse in the cold and first thing in the morning: battery or alternator. When it's hot: often the starter.
  • Dim or flickering lights, especially with the brakes, wipers, heater or rear defrost on: the alternator can't keep up.
  • Battery light on the dash: that light is for the alternator, not the battery. It's over charging, under charging or not charging. Don't wait.
  • Clicking: a bad or weak battery, an alternator not charging, wiring, loose bolts or the starter.

The Midtronics EPS 1000 HD tests battery, charging and starting and prints the result. No guess work.

05

Brakes

Brakes are one of the most important safety systems on your vehicle. Winter and the salt on Maritime roads rot unprotected rotors, sometimes in under a year. The coated rotors we supply are EPA approved and carry up to three years warranty.

CeramicBest for cars, crossovers, SUVs and light trucks. Quiet, almost no dust, last longer and easy on rotors.
Semi-metallicA bit cheaper, noisier and dustier. Right for extreme cold, constant braking or loaded work trucks.
OrganicCheap and easy on rotors, but they fade with heat and wear out quick. Jeff doesn't recommend them.

Also: ABS and ETS diagnostics, disc and drum brakes.

06

Timing belts

Timing belt replacement is a must to avoid engine damage. Most engines today are belt driven, overhead cam, interference fit: if the belt fails, the valves can hit the pistons. In Jeff's experience 80% do, and the repair costs three to four times more, plus the belt.

Most manufacturers recommend a new belt at 100,000 km; some are good to 160,000 km. If your vehicle is close to 100,000 km, call and Jeff will check the recommended interval at no cost.
