The taste of the real tea

كومو كافيه، رأس الخيمة القديمة

Kumo Cafe, in Old Town, Ras Al Khaimah. Tea and karak made on charcoal, fresh milk with saffron or zaatar, and the old plates people grew up on: balaleet, khabees, harees and luqaimat. Sit in on the sadu benches or take it away as a parcel.
