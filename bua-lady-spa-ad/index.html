Inside Bua

Bua is the Thai word for lotus, and you'll find one in every room: painted across the pedicure lounge, in gold on the marble at reception, laid in mosaic and lit in blue. The hair room sits under a ceiling of plants.

A team of seven-plus therapists, nail technicians and hair stylists.
