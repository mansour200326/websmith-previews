Getting a quote

  1. Tell us the job

    Cars, buses or parts? The size of your bay, and whether you have power and diesel on site. A WhatsApp photo of the space helps.

  2. We match a model

    We send the spec sheet of the booth or prep station that fits, with the price. Custom sizes are possible.

  3. Ducting and dispatch

    Our team handles the ducting work and stays in touch with you until the booth is dispatched.

For tools, lifts and spares, just call or WhatsApp the model number and the quantity.
