Since 1980

Future Contracting Co is a privately held construction company in Dubai. We build commercial, residential and industrial projects across the UAE, and we've completed more than 60 of them.

We work from a building we built

Our headquarters is in the City Pharmacy Building, 22 A St, Deira: offices on basement, ground, mezzanine and two floors. The consultant was Khatib & Alami; the contractor was us.

Four things we hold to

Ethics

We are uncompromising about integrity, honesty and fairness, on every project we take on.

Expertise

Experience is the knowledge and skill you only get from decades of practical work in construction.

Quality

There is no such thing as quality by accident. Every stage goes through quality checks, from casting concrete to fitting door locks.

Safety

Safety is one of the main pillars at FCC. We keep a safe and healthy environment for our workers at every level.
