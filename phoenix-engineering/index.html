What we do

Six trades under one group, most of them run from our own yard and workshop. Open a line for the detail, or see the full services page.

01Insulation and claddingHot, cold, cryogenic, acoustic and fire-protection insulation, finished in aluminium, galvanised or stainless steel.
  • Rock wool, glass wool, cellular glass, calcium silicate
  • PU and PIR foam, elastomeric rubber, phenolic, microporous
  • Sprayed or injected polyurethane foam on site
  • Removable pillows and jackets up to 1000°C
  • A30 and A60 fire-rated bulkheads and decks
  • At the dry dock, at anchorage or under way

Four sectors, one crew

The marine yards set our standard for schedules, quality and safety. We carry the same standard into plants, towers and cooling networks.

01Marine

LNG and LPG carriers, reefers and trawlers, VLCCs, conversion vessels, barges and rigs, at the dry docks, at anchorage or while sailing.

02Industrial

Refineries, oil and gas terminals, power and desalination plants, cement, aluminium, lubricant and chemical plants.

03Commercial MEP

Malls, hotels, towers, schools, hospitals, airports, exhibition halls and palaces, for MEP contractors such as Drake & Scull, EFECO and Khansaheb.

04District cooling

Distribution networks and chilled water plants. We were one of the first in the region to insulate thermal energy storage tanks.
