Phoenix Engineering & Marine Co. was set up in Sharjah in 1998, to be a company with a difference. Today it is a group of four companies, with offices in two emirates, three storage warehouses and a 10,000 m² yard in Hamriya Free Zone.

Why Phoenix

The phoenix turns up in the stories of old civilisations on three continents: a bird that stands for discipline and control, and that keeps rising again, better than before. We took the name because we wanted to build a company people could rely on for products and services that meet the best standards of safety, quality and professionalism.

We are led by a Managing Director with over 45 years of managerial experience in the GCC. The team is a mix of youth and experience: over 130 technicians, backed by estimation, quality and safety staff and a group of engineers and managers.

Our Quality Management System has been certified to ISO 9001 since 2001. Our clients and our safety record are the best proof of it: we supported Dubai Aluminium on a maintenance contract for over ten years without a Lost-Time Incident.
