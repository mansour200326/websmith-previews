Insulation and cladding

Insulation (lagging) and cladding is one of our biggest lines of work. We do thermal insulation, hot and cold; personnel protection against burns and freezing; cryogenic, acoustic and fire-protection insulation. Over it we put aluminium, galvanised or stainless steel cladding, or GRP coatings, reinforced vapour barriers and burn guards where the job calls for them.

For things that are opened often, like mufflers, turbo-chargers, turbines, valves, pumps and expansion bellows, we make removable, reusable insulation pillows, pads and jackets, in fabrics that take up to 1000°C, water or chemicals. We are also one of the few insulation contractors in the region who spray or inject polyurethane foam on site.

Materials we install

  • Stone (rock) wool and glass wool
  • Cellular glass and ceramic fibre
  • Calcium silicate
  • Polyurethane and polyisocyanurate foam
  • Flexible elastomeric (rubber) foam, polyolefin, polystyrene
  • Phenolic foam, microporous, ceramic insulating coatings

Where we work

  • Marine: cryogenic repairs on LNG and LPG carriers, reefers and trawlers, A30/A60 fire-rated bulkheads and decks, at dry dock, at anchorage or under way
  • Industrial: refineries, terminals, power and desalination, cement, aluminium and chemical plants
  • Commercial MEP: malls, hotels, towers, hospitals, airports and palaces
  • District cooling: networks, chilled water plants and thermal energy storage tanks

Ship repair and structural steel

The marine industry keeps raising our standards: tight schedules, strict quality and safe practice are the norm in marine steel work. Our steel team is led by a manager with over 20 years hands-on in the industry, with skilled fabricators, class-certified 6G welders (electric arc and CO2) and experienced supervisors.

We fabricate, erect, repair and build new structural steel, deck fittings, ducting and piping, in our 1,250 m² covered shop in Hamriya Free Zone or on site. With the same marine standard we build platforms, ladders, railings, walkways, light posts, small tanks and skids for industrial and commercial clients.

Guniting and shotcreting

Gunite (dry mix) and shotcrete (wet mix) is concrete sprayed through a hose at high speed, so it is placed and compacted in one go, on any shape of surface, vertical or overhead. How good it turns out depends on the people holding the nozzle. Our engineers and nozzlemen have over 20 years in the Middle East.

We run two dry gunite machines (UK made), two wet shotcrete machines (UK and US made) and two compressors to match. We are an Overseas Associate and Member of the Sprayed Concrete Association, UK. Jobs include a swimming pool on the 65th floor of a Dubai tower and gunite repairs under a bridge on Sir Bani Yas Island.

New construction and protection

  • Domes, retaining walls, piled and diaphragm wall facings
  • Tunnel linings
  • Fire protection to structural steel
  • Refractory linings, impact protection to steel supports

Repair, free forms and water

  • Concrete damaged by rebar corrosion: housing, bridges, jetties, silos
  • Landscaping, sculptures, water features, water parks, zoo enclosures
  • Sea walls, reservoirs, aqueducts and swimming pools

Refractory lining

Refractory lining works like insulation, holding the heat in, but with materials that work at up to 2400°C. It matters most in kilns, boilers, incinerators and furnaces, offshore and onshore.

We line in brick, mouldable and castable refractory, with the backing it needs: insulating slabs and bricks, ceramic wool filling. We have the equipment to pressure-spray refractory for speed and low waste. At the Fujairah Refinery turnaround in 2007 we replaced about 31 MT of refractory in boilers, economiser, furnace, desalter and stack, inside a limited timeframe.
