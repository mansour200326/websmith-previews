About the school

We opened on 25 August 2003 in Al Azra and still teach the way we started: the British curriculum, in English, with written and spoken English given special attention at every level.

Our students come from many different parts of the world. Classrooms are child friendly and use modern teaching alongside good old chalk and talk. Teachers, students and parents work as partners at every step, and learning goes well beyond the pages of the textbook.

Learning

From FS2 to Year 9

Three stages under one roof, all following the British curriculum. Children start in FS2 and can stay with us until the end of Year 9.

01 · FS2 and Year 1

Pre-Primary

We follow the Early Years Foundation Stage: seven areas of learning, from talking and listening to numbers, art and music. Children learn through structured play, and regular field trips are part of the year.

  • Communication and Language
  • Physical Development
  • Personal, Social and Emotional
  • Literacy
  • Mathematics
  • Understanding the World
  • Expressive Arts and Design
02 · Years 2 to 6

Primary

Activity-based and hands-on. We build a strong foundation in reading, creative writing, science and maths, and a second language starts from Year 3.

  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Arabic
  • Islamic Education
  • Social Studies
  • Moral Education
  • ICT
03 · Years 7 to 9

Middle School

Three years where the work gets deeper: the same core subjects plus a second language, with term exams and continuous assessment. Arabic, Islamic Education and Social Studies finals for Years 5 to 9 are set by the Ministry of Education.

Admissions

Joining Star

Admissions are in progress for FS2 to Year 9. Call 06 522 6111 and the office will take you through places, fees and transport.

Age guide: FS2 (KG 1) for children born 1 September 2020 to 31 August 2021. Year 1 (KG 2) for 1 September 2019 to 31 August 2020. Nursery experience is required for both.

Call 06 522 6111 → Email the office

  1. Fill in the admission form

    Online, or ask the office for help. We send you the date for the assessment once it's in.

  2. Pre-admission assessment

    Every child has a short assessment or observation. Applications with missing documents can't be booked in.

  3. Bring the documents

    The child's Emirates ID, passport, birth certificate, vaccination record and photo, the father's Emirates ID and passport, and the transfer certificate and report card for children moving school.

  4. Confirm within 15 days

    After registration the school holds the place for 15 days while you complete the formalities and pay the fees.
