Learning at Star

The British curriculum, FS2 to Year 9

Lessons are taught in English, and we pay special attention to written and spoken English at every level. Arabic, Islamic Education and Social Studies are taught throughout, with French or Urdu as an additional language.

01 · FS2 and Year 1

Pre-Primary

The aim at this stage is to build thinking and motor skills and let curiosity and imagination run free. Learning is child-centred, through structured play, with regular field trips.

In FS2 and Year 1 we use the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) framework, organised in seven areas of learning.

Prime areas

  • Communication and Language
  • Physical Development
  • Personal, Social and Emotional Development

Specific areas

  • Literacy: phonics and storytelling
  • Mathematics: numbers, shapes, space, measures
  • Understanding the World
  • Expressive Arts and Design

Recent trips on our site include Green Belt Park in Sharjah and the Book Fair at Expo Centre Sharjah.

Assessment

How we assess and promote

We use continuous assessment: informal, regular and meant to help the child improve, not just a test at the end.

FS2 and Year 1

Continuous assessment all year. Parents receive a report on their child's work at the end of each semester.

Years 2 to 9

Continuous (formative) assessment plus term exams (summative) at the end of each term. Teachers share the details before each assessment.

  • Promotion: 30% of the first term, 35% of the second and 35% of the third.
  • Pass mark: 50% in each subject, and 80% in behaviour.
  • Arabic, Islamic Education and Social Studies are compulsory. For Years 5 to 9 their annual exams are set by the Ministry of Education.
  • Promotion follows SPEA rules and criteria.

At the end of the year we give proficiency prizes to the best students, with special mentions for the best athlete, the promising artist, the born leader and the all-rounder.

Navy and yellow

School uniform

Navy blue and yellow, with the school logo. The school store stocks everything in the right material and shade, though you're free to buy it elsewhere.

  • Pre-Primary: navy and yellow round-neck T-shirt with the logo on the sleeve, track pants, white socks, plain black trainers.
  • Girls, Years 2 to 5: navy pinafore with logo, yellow half-sleeve shirt, white socks, plain black leather shoes.
  • Girls, Years 6 to 9: navy full-length skirt, yellow full-sleeve shirt (white scarf optional), white socks, black leather shoes.
  • Boys, Years 2 to 9: yellow half-sleeve shirt with the logo on the pocket, navy trousers, white socks, black leather shoes.
  • PE days: house-colour tracksuit, white socks, white trainers.

No gold or flashy jewellery, hair colouring, nail polish or henna.

Optional

School transport

Buses are fully outsourced and optional. We offer them to most areas and tell you at admission if your area isn't covered.

  • Pick-up and drop-off can take more than an hour before and after school, so follow the times the transport team gives you.
  • Buses don't wait at the stop. A Pre-Primary or Year 2 child is only dropped off if a parent is there; otherwise they come back to school for collection.
  • To stop the bus, tell the Admin Officer at least a week before the start of the month. Transport can't be stopped in December, January or June.
  • Driving your own child? Drop off inside the campus by 7.45 a.m. and collect from the waiting rooms by 2.45 p.m.

Questions parents ask

Anything else? Call the office on 06 522 6111, 8.00 a.m. to 1.00 p.m.

Which curriculum do you follow?

The British curriculum, taught in English, from FS2 to Year 9. FS2 and Year 1 follow the Early Years Foundation Stage.

How old does my child need to be for FS2?

For FS2 (KG 1), born between 1 September 2020 and 31 August 2021. For Year 1 (KG 2), born between 1 September 2019 and 31 August 2020. Nursery experience is required for both.

Is there an entrance test?

Yes. Every student has a pre-admission assessment or observation. We give you the date once the admission form is in.

What are the school hours?

FS2 and Year 1: 7.30 a.m. to 12.30 p.m. Years 2 to 9: 7.30 a.m. to 2.30 p.m. The office is open 8.00 a.m. to 1.00 p.m. and the fee counter 8.00 a.m. to 2.00 p.m.

How can fees be paid?

The year's fee can be paid in instalments, by post-dated cheques. Fees are due by the 10th of the month. The office will explain the current schedule when you register.
