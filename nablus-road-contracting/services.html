Services& fleet

Road works and earthworks with our own machines and our own crews. Over 70 units, from excavators to asphalt pavers, in Dubai since 1995.

01

Earthworks & excavation

Cutting, filling and digging out before the road goes in. We load and cart the material away ourselves, so there is no waiting on hired trucks.

  • Machines:
  • excavators
  • wheel loaders
  • tipping trucks
02

Grading

Shaping the sub-grade and road base to the levels on the drawings, layer by layer, ready for compaction.

  • Machines:
  • motor graders
03

Compaction

Rolling each layer to density, with water tankers on site to keep the material at the right moisture.

  • Machines:
  • vibratory rollers
  • rollers
  • water tankers
04

Asphalt paving

Laying the asphalt with our own pavers and finishing it with our own rollers.

  • Machines:
  • asphalt pavers
  • rollers
05

Haulage & water supply

Our own tipping trucks and water tankers move material and water around the site and to and from it.

  • Machines:
  • tipping trucks
  • water tankers
