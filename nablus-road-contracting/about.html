AboutNablus

A Dubai road contractor since 1995, started by Samer Arafat and Mohammad Sherif Dar with one small machine.

We started with one small machine and no full-time employees.

Nablus Road Contracting was founded in 1995 by civil engineer Samer Arafat and Mohammad Sherif Dar.

What we had was dedication, hard work, efficiency, honest dealing and work done to a high standard every time. Twenty years and over a thousand projects later, Nablus employs 150 people full time and owns a fleet of over 70 units.

We have worked on the Meydan Hotel, Palm Jumeirah, DIFC, JAFZA, Dubai Healthcare City and many more, for private companies and government entities alike.

  2. 1,000+Projects, from Sheikh Zayed Road to Dubai Police stations.
  3. 70+ / 150Machines in the fleet, and people on the payroll today.
