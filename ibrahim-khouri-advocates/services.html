Arbitration & dispute resolutionالتحكيم وحل النزاعات

Our principal field, with litigation. In arbitration the parties put their dispute to one or more arbitrators and settle it privately, outside court. In conciliation a neutral conciliator helps both sides reach an agreement. When neither works, we take the case to court.

  • Arbitration before one or more arbitrators
  • Conciliation
  • Negotiation and mediation
  • Settlement agreements
  • Litigation and appeals

Insolvency & debt settlementالإعسار وتسوية الديون

Under the UAE insolvency law, a person with debts can ask the civil court where they live to declare insolvency. The court appoints financial experts who set a payment plan; while the plan runs, legal action stops.
