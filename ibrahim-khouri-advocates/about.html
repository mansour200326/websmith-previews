The firm and the team

Started in Dubai in 2008

Ibrahim Ali Karam Khouri founded Ibrahim Khouri Advocates & Legal Consultants in 2008 and has led it since. He holds a bachelor's and a master's degree in law, with further courses in national and international law, and has given legal advice across everything that falls under UAE federal law.

The firm grew from Dubai to offices in Abu Dhabi, Al Ain and Sharjah. Its main work is dispute resolution and litigation, with mediation and settlement, for clients in the UAE and abroad. Our lawyers and consultants speak Arabic, English, Hindi and Russian.

“Our core values are viewing each case from an emotional perspective, and solving the disputes abiding all the laws of the country giving prime importance to truth and justice.”

We deal with individuals and families as well as companies and institutions: commercial needs, company governance, licensing, employment, contracts, funding and procurement.
