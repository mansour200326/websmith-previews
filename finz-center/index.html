About FinZ Center

We're a Kuwaiti dive centre on the fifth floor of Building 186 in Jabriya. We teach people to dive, from a first breath underwater in the pool all the way to Divemaster, then take them out at the weekend to Kubbar, Arifjan Reef and Taylor's Rock.

2006The year we opened. Kuwaiti owned and operated ever since.
600+Open Water divers trained, plus divers at every level up to Instructor.

PADI Five Star Dive Center. Every instructor on our team is an authorised PADI professional.

From the pool to Divemaster

Pick where you start. Kids begin with a pool session; adults can try a single Discover Scuba day or go straight into Open Water. The theory can be done online with PADI eLearning.

Out on the boat

Our leisure trips go out at the weekend. Certified divers come for the dives; non-divers come snorkelling, fishing or out to the islands.

  • Day dive tripsBoat dives for certified divers.
  • Snorkelling tripsFor non-divers, kids and anyone who wants to stay at the surface.
  • Island trips and cruisesA day out to Kuwait's islands.
  • Fishing cruisesRods out instead of fins.
  • Dive holidaysDiving trips further afield with the FinZ team.
  • Trip membershipSpecial packages for regular divers.
