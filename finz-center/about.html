Dive is Life

FinZ Center is a Kuwaiti owned and operated dive business, running since 2006 in Kuwait. We teach diving, take divers out at the weekend and on dive holidays, and run a dive shop.

Taught at home, recognised everywhere

Our staff are authorised instructors with PADI, the Professional Association of Diving Instructors, and FinZ is ranked a PADI Five Star Dive Center. That means you can earn an internationally recognised certificate here in Kuwait, close to home.

We've trained over 600 Open Water divers, and divers at specialty and leadership levels up to Instructor. We serve non-divers, snorkellers and certified divers alike.
