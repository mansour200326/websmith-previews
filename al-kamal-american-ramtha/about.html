About the school

Al Kamal American opened in Al Ramtha in 2003. Today it teaches the American curriculum from KG1 to Grade 12, is approved and rated Good by SPEA, and holds full Cognia accreditation.

A letter from the principal

Ms Jessica Griffin
Principal

Dear parents,

Assalam Alaikum Wa Rahmatullah

It is with great pleasure and enthusiasm that I introduce myself to you as the new principal of Al Kamal American School, Al Ramtha.

I am honored to join this esteemed institution with over 22 years of experience in the UAE as School Principal and prior to that in the UK.

I am deeply committed to fostering an environment where every student feels valued, supported, and inspired to reach their fullest potential. My approach to education is centered on collaboration, innovation, and a relentless pursuit of excellence. I believe in the power of partnership between educators, parents, and the community to ensure the success of our students.

As a parent myself, I understand the importance of trust and communication between home and school. I am dedicated to maintaining open lines of communication and working closely with you to address any concerns, celebrate achievements, and create a positive and inclusive school culture.

I am excited about the opportunities that lie ahead and look forward to getting to know each and every one of you. Together, I am confident that we can continue to make Al Kamal American School a place where every student can thrive academically, socially, and emotionally.

Please feel free to reach out to me with any questions, suggestions, or just to say hello. I am here to serve the needs of our students and community.

Warm regards

Vision & values

To build lifelong learners who can demonstrate 21st-century skills for a productive global citizenship.

Our mission: to empower every student to become a responsible, productive and engaged citizen within the peaceful global community.

Integrity

Acting with honesty at all times and taking responsibility for our own actions. Always doing the right thing, especially when no one is looking.

Inclusion

Making sure everyone feels welcome and valued, and can be their true self. Celebrating the diversity of our community so that everyone has a voice.

Excellence

High aspirations and expectations for ourselves and those around us. Valuing expertise, and taking every chance to grow what we know.

Accreditation

Cognia (formerly AdvancED) awarded the school Candidacy Status in 2025 and, in less than a year, full accreditation in 2026 under its new standards, with an IEQ score of 307, higher than average. The school is approved by SPEA, the Sharjah Private Education Authority, which rates it Good.

The campus

Advanced IT and innovation labs, science laboratories, a library, the Secret Garden outdoor learning space and sports grounds. Classes are separate for boys and girls from Grade 5 to Grade 12.

Trips are planned through the year, to places like factories and museums, so students build their social skills outside the classroom too.
