The campus

Labs, a library, sports grounds and a lot of room to play. A few corners parents ask about.

01

Play

KG has its own soft-play room with a ball pit, and a shaded playground with slides. For small children, play is as much a part of learning as the classroom.

A covered football pitch, an outdoor court for basketball and PE, and a sports hall. Classes also go out on trips, like the visit to the fire station.

Registering for 2026–27

Four steps. The registrar will walk you through them on the phone or at the school.

  1. Ask about a seat

    Call or WhatsApp 06 538 3136 with your child's grade for next year.

  2. Gather the papers

    Passports with visas, Emirates IDs, birth certificate, vaccination card, transfer certificate and last report. All 12 documents

  3. Register

    The AED 500 registration fee is taken off the tuition later. Email the documents to info@akaisramtha.com or bring them in.

  4. First instalment

    Paid in cash with books and uniform, bought at school. Two cheques cover the rest of the year.

WhatsApp the registrar Call 06 538 3136
