How to register

The registrar's office handles new families. Call first and they'll tell you if there's a seat in your child's grade.

  1. Ask about a seat

    Call or WhatsApp 06 538 3136 with your child's grade for next year.

  2. Gather the papers

    The full list of 12 is just below. Incomplete documents mean the registration isn't done.

  3. Register

    Pay the AED 500 registration fee and send the documents to info@akaisramtha.com, or bring them to the registrar.

  4. First instalment

    In cash, with books and uniform. Two cheques cover the rest of the year.

The 12 documents

Bring originals where it says so. New students bring the original Emirates IDs on the first visit.

  1. Copy of your child's passport with a valid residence visa page
  2. Two copies of your child's birth certificate
  3. Copies of the father's and mother's passports with valid residence visa pages
  4. Copies of the Emirates ID, both sides, for father, mother and student
  5. Copy of the family book (UAE nationals only)
  6. Six passport photos of your child
  7. Copy of the vaccination certificate
  8. Health file from the previous school
  9. Original transfer certificate from the previous school
  10. The most recent original school report
  11. Financial clearance letter from the previous school
  12. Original Emirates IDs (father, mother, student) for new admissions

Tests your child will sit

Outside tests show how your child is doing against students in the UAE and around the world.

MAP

Grades 3–9, two or three times a year

NWEA's computer-adaptive test in reading, language, maths and science. Teachers use it to pitch lessons to each child and track growth over the year.

CAT4

Grades 3, 5, 7 and 9, term 1

Shows how a child thinks: shapes and patterns, words, numbers and spatial reasoning. It helps us see their potential, not just their marks.

ACER IBT

Non-Arab students

International Benchmark Tests in English, maths, science, Arabic and reasoning, compared against students worldwide.

TIMSS, PIRLS, PISA

TIMSS Grades 4 and 8 · PIRLS Grade 4 · PISA at 15

The big international studies in maths, science and reading that the UAE takes part in.

PSAT 10, SAT

Grades 10–12

The SAT suite for students heading to university.

EmSAT

Upper grades

The Emirates Standardized Test, used by UAE universities for admission.

TALA & Mubakkir

Arabic, twice a year

Standardised Arabic tests from KG to Grade 11.
