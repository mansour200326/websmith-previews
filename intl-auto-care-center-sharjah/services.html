PPF paint protection film

أفلام حماية PPF (جلاد مخفي)

A clear film laid over the paint, what people here call the invisible shield (جلاد مخفي). Stone chips, small scratches and the daily wear of Sharjah roads hit the film instead of the paint.

  • Good forNew cars, and any car you want to keep looking the way it does now.
  • Send usA photo of the car, the make and model, and which parts you want covered.
