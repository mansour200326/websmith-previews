What we do

خدماتنا

PPF, nano ceramic, polishing inside and out, regular and steam washing, and deep cleaning, all done in our bay in Industrial Area 13, Muwaileh. Tap one to ask the price on WhatsApp, with a photo of the car and its make and model.

  1. 01PPF paint protection filmجلاد مخفيA clear film over the paint, the invisible shield. Stone chips and scratches hit the film, not the paint.→
  2. 02Nano ceramic coatingنانو سيراميكA protective layer on the paint for gloss that lasts.→
  3. 03Polishing, inside and outتلميع داخلي وخارجيBrings the original shine back to the paint outside and the finishes inside.→
  4. 04Washing, regular and steamغسيل عادي وبخارA normal wash, or a steam wash when the car needs more than water and soap.→
  5. 05Deep cleaningتنظيف عميقA thorough clean that gives the car back that new feeling.→

Inside the bay

Grey marble walls and a ceiling full of LED panels. Under that light every mark on the paint shows, so you can see the finish for yourself when you collect the car.
