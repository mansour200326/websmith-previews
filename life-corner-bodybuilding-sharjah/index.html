About the gym

A bodybuilding gym in Hay Al Qasimiah, near Emigration Road. Dumbbell racks along the mirrors, benches and Olympic bars, plate-loaded and cable machines, and a row of treadmills. Open for gents until 2:30 at night, with ladies-only hours from 10 to 5.
