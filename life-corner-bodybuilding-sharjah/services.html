Training

Everything happens on one floor in Hay Al Qasimiah: free weights, machines and cardio. Pick what you're after below and send us a message about it, or just call.

01

Bodybuilding

The core of Life Corner. Racks of dumbbells, fixed bars, bench stations and machines, with mirrors down the walls so you can check your form.

  • Rubber hex dumbbells on racks along the mirror wall
  • Fixed-weight barbells and EZ curl bars
  • Bench press stations with Olympic bars and plates
  • Plate-loaded and cable machines on the main floor

02

Personal training

Want someone to write your plan and train with you? Life Corner offers personal training. Message us and we'll match you with a trainer.

  • Tell us your goal: muscle, strength or weight loss
  • Say which session suits you: morning, evening or late night
  • Ask about trainer availability and the monthly price
Ask about a trainer
03

Weight loss

A row of treadmills and upright bikes next to the weights, so cardio and strength happen in the same visit. The gents session runs until 2:30am, so there is time after work.

  • Treadmills and upright bikes facing the glass wall
  • Weights and machines alongside the cardio
  • Gents evening session from 5pm to 2:30am
Ask about weight loss
04

Ladies hours

From 10am to 5pm the gym is for ladies only. Same floor, same equipment: weights, machines and cardio.

  • Ladies-only: 10:00am to 5:00pm
  • Full access to the free weights, machines and cardio
  • Sunday is evening only (5pm to 10pm), so check before you come
