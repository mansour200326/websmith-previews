How every session works

In our own words: just simply book, pose and post.

  1. Book online or on WhatsApp. Reserve your private studio in a few clicks.
  2. Arrive, and the studio is yours for the session.
  3. Pose freely: up to 35 minutes of unlimited self-photography.
  4. Use the props in the room: birthday sashes and crowns, number balloons, sunglasses.
  5. Get your photos the same day* and post your favourites.
*Terms apply. Ask us when you book.

Questions people ask us

Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How much is a session?

Sessions start at AED 99. There is more than one package, so send us a WhatsApp and we will reply with the current list and what is in each.

How do I book?

Book online with our booking link (bit.ly/BookHappySnapsAE, the same link as in our Instagram bio), or WhatsApp us on 050 876 0922 with the day, the time and how many of you are coming.

How long do we get in the studio?

Up to 35 minutes of unlimited self-photography. The Christmas package is a 40-minute shoot.

When do I get my photos?

The same day, so you can post them that evening. Terms apply; ask us when you book.

When are you open, and where are you?

Every day, 10am to 1pm and 4pm to 8pm, in Abu Dhabi. WhatsApp us and we will send the location pin.
