Our history

Promotech Gulf Industry LLC was established in 2010 and then started its own manufacturing in the UAE, becoming a pioneer of electroluminescent (EL) technology in the region. We were the first sole manufacturer of EL products in the UAE.

The company is run by people with years of experience in design, fabrication, installation and lighting, leading a team of technicians and engineers. Our thinnest EL light is called Promo-Light, and it's the base of almost everything we make: tape, animated displays and back-lit panels.

Around the lighting we design and build exhibition stands, interiors, displays and kiosks, delivery motorbike boxes, and do tile, digital, garment and silk screen printing.
