What EL light is

Electroluminescent (EL) material gives off light when an electric current passes through it. No bulb, no glass, no heat problems. You can stick a panel on a wall, a shop window, a car door or a jacket, and it lights evenly across the whole surface.

0.5mm

  • ThicknessAbout 0.5 mm, thinner than a credit card and light enough to wrap around a finger.
  • ShapesCut to any shape: logos, lettering, lines, full posters with animation.
  • ModesSteady or flashing, in sequence or selectively, indoors and outdoors.
  • VisibilityStays readable in smoke, fog, rain and sandstorms.
  • Power85–90% less energy than neon and cold cathode.
  • SafetyNo mercury, no glass to break, no gas to escape.

Promo-Light, made in Ajman

Three products come out of our production unit. Everything else we do is built around them.

01

Promo‑Light Tape

A strip of EL light in any colour that bends around any surface and runs continuously for long lengths. It works for interiors and exteriors, steady or flashing.

  • Wraps around a finger, thinner than a credit card
  • Seen in bright artificial light, dim light and fog
  • Used for ceilings, shop fronts, furniture and vehicles
02

Promo‑Light Animated Display

A full-colour printed poster with parts of it lit and flashing in sequence. It catches the eye like a screen but fits in a normal poster frame, chiller header or kiosk.

  • High-resolution full digital image with animation
  • Multi-channel sequences and flashers
  • 0.5 mm thick, light, easy to install
03

Promo‑Light Back‑Lit

Replaces the big light box with bulbs behind frosted glass. The panel itself is the light, so the graphic is lit evenly with no box at all.

  • Stick or paste it anywhere, plug and play
  • Easy to clean, remove, move and reuse
  • Steady or flashing, indoor or outdoor
