EL product 01

Promo-Light Tape

The thinnest, longest and most flexible EL lamp we make. Think of a light in any colour that you can bend around any surface and run continuously for hundreds of feet. It costs a fraction of what bulbs or other lighting systems cost to run, and works flashing or steady, indoors or outdoors.

  • For interiors and exteriors
  • Wraps around a finger, thinner than a credit card
  • Seen in bright artificial light, dim light and fog
  • 93% less energy than traditional lighting, by our figures
  • No mercury, no glass, no gas

EL product 02

Promo-Light Animated Display

A high-quality, full-colour printed image where chosen parts light up and flash, one after another or selectively. Because the panel lights evenly across its surface, you get a full digital poster with animation, in a frame as thin as a business card.

  • 0.5 mm thick and light
  • Multi-channel sequences and flashers
  • 85–90% energy saving against neon and cold cathode
  • Little to no maintenance, long working life

EL product 03

Promo-Light Back-Lit

Our back-lit units replace the traditional light box: the big container with bulbs and a pane of frosted glass behind the graphic. The panel itself gives even light, with no extra material or strips.

  • Stick or paste it anywhere
  • Easy to install, clean, remove and transport
  • Plug and play, and the panel can be reused
  • Flashing or steady, indoor or outdoor
