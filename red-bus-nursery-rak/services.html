Classes & care

Five classes from 8 weeks to the Foundation Stage, all following the British EYFS. Around them: a Breakfast Club, an After School Club, Holiday Camps, weekly music and daily Arabic.

8 weeks to walking

Ladybird, the baby class

Ladybird follows the Early Years Foundation Stage, with a wide mix of experiences and activities to help each baby develop. The teacher keeps regular notes on what your baby is achieving and plans around their likes and dislikes.

Babies spend time around our play areas and in their own homely classroom, full of toys for their age, from playmats to stacking bricks. The sleep room is laid out so your child can nap just as they do at home.

Your routine, not ours

We follow your home routine for milk, snacks, meals and naps. What you tell us in the "All About Me" form is displayed in the classroom for every member of staff. If the routine changes at home, just let us know.

Walking to 2 years

Bumblebee, the toddlers

Bumblebee cares for a small group in a homely setting, with activities chosen for the needs and interests of each child. Every day there's sensory play, both indoors and outside in our safe, secure garden.

There's time each day for creative and imaginative play, singing, rhymes and music, books and free play. Naps follow your child's usual home routine in the sleep room, and we start with your routine for milk and meals too.

2 to 2½ years

Dragonfly

Two-year-olds are building social skills, growing their vocabulary and testing their independence. Dragonfly works in small groups so each child's needs are seen, with plenty of chances to explore.

This is where toilet training starts, along with handling frustration and learning to do things for themselves.

  • Mark makingNumber and letter tracing
  • Book cornerPuppets, video and audio books
  • ConstructionTowers, K'nex and Lego
  • Role playA role-play area that changes with the topic

2½ to 3 years

Caterpillar

Caterpillar carries on in small groups, built around each child, with lots of time to explore and learn about the world around them. The day has a set routine that helps the children settle quickly.

Outdoors there are parachute games and Wet Wednesdays: sprinklers, bubbles and lots of water.

3 years, Foundation Stage

Butterfly

Butterfly children have a busy year from the very first day. Activities happen inside and outside the classroom, are planned around each child, and are so much fun they hardly feel like learning.

The UAE Ministry of Education allows children in licensed nurseries until age 4, so this class gets your child ready for school, with phonics, pencil control and early writing.

Clubs & extras

6:30Breakfast Club6:30am to 9am, every day or now and then

For parents who need an earlier start. The children eat breakfast together (packed from home for now), then do crafts and activities until class begins. Staff on duty organise breakfast and the activities, and there's always at least one qualified first aider on site.

Email for Breakfast Club fees →

1:00After School Club1pm to 6pm

For parents who can't collect at 1, and for older children after their own school. Our qualified staff run crafts and activities from 1 to 4pm; the younger children nap in our cosy sleep room first, then join in.

Snacks and dinner are part of the afternoon but come from home. We can heat evening meals and keep food in the fridge. We don't supply formula or bottled milk.

Email for After School Club fees →

7:00Holiday Camps7am to 4pm in the school holidays

Sign up for full or half days, whole weeks or just a few days. Every camp runs on a theme and is lots of fun. Hours are different in the Holy Month of Ramadan.

WeeklyMusic & MovementA specialist session every week, no extra fee

Music is part of every day at Red Bus. On top of that, our experienced teachers give a specialist session once a week, linked to the topic the class is working on.

DailyArabic TimeIn every class, from Ladybird up

The earlier a child meets a language, the easier it comes. From the baby class, we blend Arabic into the children's everyday learning through play and music, and each class has Arabic Time during the day.

Uniform, if you like

For children aged 2 and up we offer a Red Bus polo shirt. It's optional: you choose whether your child comes in uniform or their own clothes.
