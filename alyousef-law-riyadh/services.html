02 · Stage by stage

How a case runs

“We will carefully examine every angle of your case, and we will argue your case at every turn.”Yasir S. Alyousef
  1. 01Negotiating surrender

    Arranging how and when you present yourself, with your lawyer beside you.

  2. 02Arraignment

    With you when the charges are put to you.

  3. 03Pretrial proceedings

    The file, the evidence and the procedure, reviewed before the first hearing.

  4. 04Pretrial negotiation

    Talking to the other side before trial, where that serves you.

  5. 05Trial

    From simple to complex litigation.

  6. 06Post-verdict proceedings

    Following the judgment through, step by step.

  7. 07Appeal

    Objecting to the judgment before the Court of Appeal.

  8. 08Cassation

    The last stage, before the Supreme Court.
