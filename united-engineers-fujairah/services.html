Services

Roads, buildings, transport and materials. Four lines of work that share one fleet, one set of engineers and one office in Fujairah.

01 / 04

Roads and streets

We have built and widened roads for the Ministry of Public Works, Fujairah Municipality and the oil terminals: Khorfakkan–Shis Road, Fujairah–Masafi Road, the Kalba and Khorfakkan truck ring roads, Fujairah Airport Road and internal roads in Madhab, Al Jurf and Al Mudafi.

We do the whole road: setting out, earthworks, sub-base and base, asphalt, kerbs, crossings and service roads.

What's included

  • New roads and road widening
  • Internal roads, service roads and truck ring roads
  • Earthworks, sub-base and base course
  • Asphalt paving and compaction
  • Kerbs, crossings, car parks and petrol-station access

02 / 04

Building contracting

All kinds of building contracting: villas, warehouses, public buildings and their refurbishment, renovation and maintenance. Our work includes the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque in Fujairah, the Dhaid Wild Center, Fujairah Mall works and villas in Sharjah.

Civil and structural works sit with one contractor, so there's one programme and one point of contact for the consultant.

What's included

  • Villas and residential buildings
  • Warehouses and workshops
  • Public and government buildings
  • Refurbishment, renovation and maintenance
  • Civil and structural works

03 / 04

Transport

We are one of the larger transporters in the Eastern Region, with our own fleet of trucks and trailers. They are driven by our own drivers and kept by our own engineers and technicians.

Our trucks carry our own material to our own sites, and are available to other contractors and suppliers.

What's included

  • Tipper trucks and trailers
  • Delivery to sites across the UAE
  • Fleet maintained in-house
  • Material moves for other contractors

04 / 04

Building and road materials

We supply a wide range of building and road construction materials to the local industry. Materials are tested every 3 to 6 months at Fujairah Municipality or a third-party lab, and delivered by our own fleet.

Tell us the material, the quantity and the site, and we'll send a price.

What's included

  • Road construction materials
  • Building materials
  • Regular lab testing (3–6 months)
  • Delivered by our own fleet
