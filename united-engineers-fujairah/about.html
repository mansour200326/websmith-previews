About

A Fujairah contracting company, established in 2009 for construction, refurbishment, renovation and maintenance in civil, structural and building works.

How we work

Our aim on every job is simple: finish in the time in the contract, to the specification, the current code of practice and the latest standards, and leave the client and the consultant satisfied.

We listen to our clients and keep a professional, direct line with them. Clients can talk to our site staff or straight to the company's principals.

Our engineers run a service-assurance plan: our performance is measured on every project and compared with past jobs and industry benchmarks, so we know what to keep and what to fix.

Quality control

All tests required for building materials are carried out regularly, every 3 to 6 months, at Fujairah Municipality or at a third-party lab whenever required.

Clients, consultants and contractors get full document backup from our engineers, technicians and staff, from the raw materials and production to transport to the site.

Safety first

Our safety engineers visit each project site and carry out regular safety checks. A safety meeting is held at the start of every project so the safety requirements are met before work begins.

We follow the OHSAS 18001 health and safety management system.
