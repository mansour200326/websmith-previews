Since 1993

Al Habbai Contracting was started in Dubai in 1993 by the Al Habbai family and is still privately owned, with Mr. Ali Al Habbai as CEO.

We plan and run each job so the owner ends up with a building that pays back, built with integrity.

Our projects are in Downtown, Business Bay, Media City, Palm Jumeirah and the Marina, and in neighbourhoods like Al Satwa, Al Barsha and Arjan. We hold a permit for unlimited-storey construction and are approved to ISO 9001 and ISO 14001.

We buy from the UAE's established suppliers and have our plant checked regularly by specialists, so nothing mechanical holds up a handover. Several of our projects have been finished a few months ahead of schedule.

Developers come back to us: New Dubai Properties for three hotels, and SRG companies for a mosque, a 28-floor apartment building, a community mall and now a tower in JVC.
