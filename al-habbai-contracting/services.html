How we run a site

Safety is everybody's duty and responsibility.

Safety

Every site gives safe access, safe exits and a safe place to work. Our HSE manual is written from international practice, with zero tolerance, and every worker's safety record is checked regularly.

Quality

Work to the company's and the authorities' standards, on budget and on time, with as little waste as possible. Quality objectives are set and reviewed for every level of the company.

Plant and suppliers

We buy from the UAE's established suppliers and have our equipment checked by specialists, so a breakdown never holds up a handover.
