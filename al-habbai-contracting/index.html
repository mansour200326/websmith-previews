From the pit to the roof

One job, start to finish. P157, a 22-floor residential and retail building in Al Barsha South 4th, photographed by our team as it went up, March 2017 to February 2020.

01Mar 2017

The pit

We start below ground: excavation, shoring and waterproofing, then the raft reinforcement for the basement. This is P157 in Al Barsha South 4th in its first weeks.

022017

Substructure

Basement walls and columns go up in formwork until the building is back at street level. Everything the tower stands on is poured here.

032018

The frame

Four podium levels for parking and shops, then 22 residential floors, slab by slab, with our cranes and our own site team.

042019

Blockwork and facade

While the top floors are still being cast, the lower floors close in: blockwork, facade, then services and finishes.

05Feb 2020

Handover

Basement, ground, 4 podium levels, 22 floors and roof, handed over to Al Sondos Real Estate on 16 February 2020.
