01

Structural steel

We started the steel division because so many of our contracts had steel in them: as an extra element (supports for MEP equipment, hybrid structures, restoration work) or as the main structure itself: industrial buildings, shades, warehouses.

For those we deliver turnkey: the structure, the skin of the building and most of its standard equipment. When the design isn't ours, our engineers often propose improvements to the connection details, the bracing and the foundations. We take coatings seriously too, so the steel lasts.

  • Corrugated sheets, tensile fabric, PU sandwich panels
  • Steel doors, roof vents, overhead cranes, mezzanines
  • Skylights, bridges, catwalks, roof additions

02

Concrete works

Concrete is our stronghold. Our record is over 6,300 m³ poured, as slabs, cores, columns, retaining walls and industrial floors, often in hard-to-reach places or sensitive surroundings.

Around the pour we handle the related trades as well:

  • Coring
  • Special concretes: chemically modified facing, waterproof, lightweight, fibre-reinforced
  • Concrete repair, restoration and protection
  • Old-to-new jointing and dowel insertion
  • Finishing: polishing, surfacing, special coatings

03

MEP works

MEP is one of the trades we're proudest of. We have supplied and installed over 6,000 KVA of industrial power and over 500 tons (6,000,000 BTU) of cooling.

Electrical: from sub-station installation and MV to LV, to power distribution and lighting networks for industrial and domestic buildings, and control panels for BMS and fire safety.

Mechanical: air conditioning of all kinds (packaged units, AHUs), plus turnkey networks of industrial compressed air, kitchen and industrial air filtration, and stand-alone sewage systems.

Civil works

06

Historic building restoration

Restoration is usually the flagship of a contracting company, because it needs real know-how in every construction trade. Our references:

  • Banque Pharaon-Chiha historic headquarters, Nejmeh Square, Beirut Central District
  • Bustros Palace, The Metropolitan Club, Achrafieh, Beirut
  • Boutique Hotel Bloc C, Souq Waqef, Doha, supervised by the Private Engineering Office

At Bloc C the scope was full external facade propping and preservation, demolition and replacement of the whole building inside, joining the new structure to the existing facade, restoring the original finishes, and non-destructive cleaning of the old materials.

07

Shop fabrication

Every day brings a different design, so the shop needs skill across many trades. What sets ours apart is the equipment:

  • CNC cutting table, press brake and shear for accurate cutting, forming and bending
  • Band saws for heat-free cutting, which keeps the steel's performance
  • Wet and dry sandblasting to prepare for coating
  • Airless and air-assisted spraying for durable coatings

We keep updating our methods, and the client is always the first to benefit.

08

Stainless steel

Stainless steel needs a craftsman's approach, like fine cabinet-making: careful people with fine tools.

It also needs its own workshop, with tools, equipment and workers kept only for stainless, so no other metal contaminates it and spoils the finish or its life in corrosive conditions. We built that workshop at Sayliyah, with passivating and pickling baths.

  • Handrails and balustrades
  • Staircases
  • Chandelier structures
  • Decorative stainless for interior projects
Ask about stainless work on WhatsApp →
09

Special welding, oil & gas

We weld carbon steel, stainless, aluminium, copper and bronze every day. Our welders are skilled on the equipment for each process:

  • GTAW (arc) for sturdy structural welding
  • TIG for fine, accurate welds, mainly stainless and aluminium
  • GMAW (MIG-MAG) for production welding
  • Gas welding for copper, bronze and other metals

For oil and gas fields we developed our own Welding Procedure Specifications (W.P.S.), so we can work in our workshop (manifolds, valves, gauge installation) and in situ on the fields.
