About ARD

An engineering company from Lebanon that became a general contractor in Qatar. We take on medium-sized projects of all trades, especially the ones that need advanced engineering, accurate work, a precise schedule and close follow-up.

How we got here

1997

Founded in Lebanon

A small engineering company. We handled significant parts of major Lebanese government infrastructure projects, as sub-contractor for MEP and concrete works. Our restoration work in Beirut includes the Banque Pharaon-Chiha historic headquarters in Nejmeh Square and Bustros Palace, The Metropolitan Club, in Achrafieh.

2003

ARD Engineering Qatar

Set up under the wing of SIDDIQI International Group as a medium-sized general contractor, to take part in Qatar's building boom.

2005

Independent

As the business and its logistics grew, ARD Engineering & Development W.L.L. became a fully independent company, in partnership with our original sponsor.

2010

Steel Structures Division

A new facility in the Sayliyah area, equipped to serve both steel buildings and the oil and gas sector in Qatar.

2011

Stainless steel & special welding workshop

Added at Sayliyah, for custom-made products for building finishes and decoration.

Our secret is no secret: a systematic plan for every step of the project, personal attention to the smallest detail, and a craftsman's care in the work.
ARD Engineering & Development

Hard projects

In Qatar we have taken on non-standard projects that most contractors avoided: complex engineering, a difficult site, or a very tight schedule. Over time that became our speciality, with highly skilled engineers and staff, high quality standards and competitive costs.
