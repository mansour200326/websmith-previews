Taj Fitness is a gym in Al Bustan, Ajman, in KM Trading Building across from Lulu. Men and women train here. It's one big open floor: dumbbells along the mirror wall, plate-loaded racks, pin machines, treadmills and bikes, and a strip of turf for warming up.

What's on the floor

It's all one open room, so you're a few steps from dumbbells to cables to cardio. Tap a line to see that area on the gym page.

  1. 01Free weightsDumbbell racks along the mirror wall, benches, plates and bars
  2. 02Racks & cablesSmith machines and cable towers for pulls, presses and rows
  3. 03MachinesPin-loaded and plate-loaded machines for every body part
  4. 04CardioA row of treadmills and spin bikes facing the floor
  5. 05Turf stripGreen turf by the cardio row, for warm-ups and stretching
  6. 06TrainersOn the floor to show you a machine and check your form
