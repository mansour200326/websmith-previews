Taj Fitness is a gym in Al Bustan, Ajman, in KM Trading Building across from Lulu. Men and women train here. It's one big open floor: dumbbells along the mirror wall, plate-loaded racks, pin machines, treadmills and bikes, and a strip of turf for warming up.
