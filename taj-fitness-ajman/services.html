The gym floor

One open room in KM Trading Building, opposite Lulu. Here's what's on it, area by area, and how to join. For prices and timings, ask us on WhatsApp and we'll send the current options.

Free weightsRacks & cablesMachinesCardio & turfTrainersHow to join
01

Free weights

Dumbbell racks run the length of the mirror wall, with benches in front and plate trees at the end. Ask a trainer if you need a spot.

  • Dumbbells in pairs on two-tier racks
  • Flat and adjustable benches
  • Weight plates and plate trees
  • Straight and EZ bars
  • Lifting belts on the rack
Ask about free weights →
02

Racks & cables

Smith machines and multi-station cable towers sit side by side, so presses, pulls and rows are all in one corner.

  • Smith machines
  • Cable crossover towers
  • Lat pull-down
  • Cable rows and handles
Ask about the racks →
03

Machines

Pin-loaded and plate-loaded machines cover legs, back, chest and shoulders. Good if you're new and want a guided movement.

  • Shoulder and chest machines
  • Leg machines
  • Back machines
  • Plate-loaded stations
Ask about the machines →
04

Cardio & turf

A row of treadmills and spin bikes faces the floor, with a strip of green turf next to it for warming up and stretching.

  • Treadmills
  • Spin bikes
  • Turf strip for warm-ups and stretching
Ask about cardio →
05

Trainers

Our trainers are on the floor. Ask one to show you how a machine works, watch your form, or help you work toward a goal.

  • Machine set-up for beginners
  • Form checks
  • Support toward your goals
  • Ask about training with a coach
