The building is in the heart of the city, so the town's commercial and business centres are easy to reach. Every apartment is set up for travellers and business guests alike: a fully equipped kitchen, a dining table, a sitting room and a separate bedroom.

Reception is open 24 hours, there is security round the clock, and the Rosso restaurant on the mezzanine will send meals up to your apartment.
