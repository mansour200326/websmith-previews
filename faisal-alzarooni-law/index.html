The firm

We do one thing: criminal defence. From the first police statement and the Public Prosecution, through the Court of First Instance and the Court of Appeal, up to the Federal Supreme Court. Our lawyers, advocates and researchers work every case as a team, led by Faisal Alzarooni.

Why only criminal law? A legal system that is foreign to many can be daunting. By staying with criminal law alone we keep up with every change in the penal code and give you current, precise answers about your case.

Case results

Two acquittals, in our own words

AcquittedFinancial crime10 July 2018

Banker acquitted in a half-a-million dirham bribery case

A stockbroker who had moved to Dubai was charged with taking a bribe to sell unliquidated shares. We were up against a larger firm that tried to bury us in paperwork. Countless hours on financial records and the expert's mandate later, the expert report showed the bank had profited and the shares were liquid.

The court agreed with the expert report and acquitted our client.

AcquittedViolent crime10 July 2018

Acquittal on a kidnapping charge

The defence centred on the prosecution witnesses. Their statements did not match each other or the prosecution's arguments, and the relationship between the complainant and the parties pointed to a motive behind the complaint.

The court found the witness statements doubtful and unrealistic. The prosecution did not appeal.
