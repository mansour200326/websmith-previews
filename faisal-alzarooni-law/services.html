Practice areas

01

Financial crimes

Also called white-collar crime. The rules change often and there is a lot of room for misunderstanding, so these cases are won in the documents.

Embezzlement

Taking money or property you were trusted with, usually at work. Often charged against managers and executives, as a misdemeanour or a felony.

Credit card fraud

Using someone else's card, often a stolen one, without the owner's knowledge. We defend people under investigation, charged or arrested.

Money laundering

Investigations can lead to seized cash, cars and property. We have obtained dismissals of money laundering charges and the return of seized property.

Bank and loan fraud

Lenders, loan agents and investors under investigation since the 2008 property crash. We work with real-estate and lending experts on the defence.

Bribery

In 2018 we won an acquittal for a banker charged in a half-a-million dirham bribery case, on the strength of the expert report.

02

Drug crimes

The penalty depends on the drug, the amount, any intent to sell and your record. Drugs held for personal use are often charged as possession with intent to sell, so the charge itself is the first thing we look at.

Possession and personal use

We work to have charges reduced or dismissed, whether it is a first case or not.

Drug trafficking

Trading, selling or exchanging drugs. A felony charge with very heavy sentences; we have defended clients in Dubai and federal courts.

Drug transporting

Moving a controlled substance by car, air, sea or any other way. Drugs brought in from abroad face the most severe treatment.

03

Violent crimes

We investigate the charge from day one, protect any evidence from the scene, and work to keep pre-trial detention to a minimum.

Assault and battery

Charged as a misdemeanour or a felony, with effects on your job and record. We look at every part of the complaint.

Kidnapping

Simple and aggravated kidnapping. In 2018 we won an acquittal by showing the prosecution witnesses contradicted each other.

Homicide and manslaughter

Intentional and unintentional killing, including vehicular manslaughter. We review the circumstances with you and explain the options.

04

Theft crimes

Taking someone's property to keep it from them: larceny, burglary, shoplifting and more. Simple theft and aggravated theft are treated very differently.

Grand and petty theft

We have defended both throughout our careers, from a first complaint to a court hearing.

Forgery

Making or changing a document to pass it off as real, most often passports and cheques. If you are charged, stay silent and call a lawyer.

Identity theft

Using another person's ID, card or banking details, including online. A federal matter; we offer a free evaluation to see how we can help.

05

Other crimes

Several of these are not crimes in the UK or the US, and people new to the UAE find themselves under investigation without understanding why. We explain it plainly and defend it.

Insulting and defamation

Words said or posted about someone, even if true. A defence needs a lawyer who knows both the federal system and local culture.

Cyber crimes

Hacking, online fraud, phishing, extortion and what people view or share online. Deleted files can be recovered, so call early.

Alcohol-related charges

Cases where a police stop leads to a charge. We guide you through it and work for a good outcome.

Consensual relations and sex crimes

Sensitive cases, handled in confidence. People are sometimes wrongly accused; we challenge every part of the allegation.

Juvenile crimes

Children make mistakes. We work to have charges reduced or dropped, and ask for counselling or rehabilitation instead of detention.

Domestic violence

If you have been accused or charged, take advice before you respond. We defend these cases in the Dubai courts.

06

Appeals and records

Our work does not stop at the trial court. We have won appeals in the Dubai courts and at the UAE Federal Supreme Court.

Direct appeals

Appealing a conviction or a sentence, with the research and written argument it needs.

Post-conviction petitions

Challenging the legality of a sentence or conviction after the fact.

Cleaning up a record

Setting aside or reducing convictions, ending probation, and applying for certificates of rehabilitation or pardons.
