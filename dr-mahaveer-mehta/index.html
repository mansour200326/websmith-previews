Our lasers, and what each is for

Dr. Mehta was one of the first to bring medical lasers to the Middle East. Today the clinic has a different machine for each job, so the treatment fits your skin type rather than the other way round.

01Pico LaserTattoos, pigmentation, melasma, birthmarks

Ultra-short pulses in four wavelengths (532, 585, 650 and 1064 nm), so it can take out black, blue, green and even red and purple tattoo ink, and brown pigment from any part of the body. The fractional handpiece works on melasma with no downtime.

Read more →Ask on WhatsApp
  • Multi-colour tattoos
  • Freckles and age spots
  • Nevus of Ota
  • Dark underarms, knees, elbows
02TitanSkin tightening without surgery, face and body

Infrared light heats the deeper skin while the surface is kept cool, so collagen tightens and keeps rebuilding over the following months. No pain, no downtime. Dr. Mehta has done it for more than 2,800 patients.

Read more →Ask on WhatsApp
  • Face and neck
  • Abdomen
  • Thighs
  • Underarms
03Fractional CO2Acne scars, wrinkles, sun damage, stretch marks

The 10,600 nm CO2 laser fired as a fine grid of micro-shots: new collagen forms, damaged skin shrinks, and healing is quicker and less painful than old-style resurfacing. Dr. Mehta has presented his results with it at congresses in the USA, Europe and Dubai.

Read more →Ask on WhatsApp
  • Post-acne scars
  • Surgical and injury scars
  • Fine lines
  • Skin texture
04RF Micro-needlePores, scars, skin tightening, excess sweating

Gold-plated micro-needles carry radio-frequency energy to an exact depth in the skin. It suits darker skin because the surface stays cool, and it is one of the longest-lasting options for sweaty underarms, palms and soles.

Several lasers under one roof (CoolGlide 1064, LightSheer diode, IPL, ProWave 770 and Q-switched YAG), chosen for your skin tone and hair, from fair skin to dark and fine hair to coarse. A lady technician treats lady patients.

A 1064 nm Nd:YAG laser for leg veins, down to the deeper ones, and LimeLight IPL for redness, tiny face veins and light brown spots on the face, neck and chest.
