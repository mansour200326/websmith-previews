Dr. Mahaveer Mehta, MD

Chairman and medical director of his own medical center in Dubai since May 1990, and one of the doctors who first brought lasers to the Middle East.

Before Dubai he headed the dermatology departments of the Qatar Armed Forces medical unit and International Hospital Bahrain, and taught at Al Fateh University in Tripoli and S.M.S. Medical College in Jaipur. He has treated patients from more than 140 nationalities and is invited to chair and speak at dermatology and laser congresses around the world.

Outside the clinic: tennis, golf, table tennis, travel and photography. On 23 April 2013 he scored a hole in one at Dubai Creek Golf & Yacht Club.

Giving back

“The essential in life is not in having but in giving.”

Dr. Mehta has run free skin camps for low-income workers with the Indian Consulate and Indian Ladies Club, free consultations for students and staff at the University of Wollongong in Dubai, and skin health talks in schools, clubs, on television and radio. He supports the American Academy of Dermatology's Camp Discovery for children with chronic skin conditions, and the Rotary Club of Dubai's End Polio and Ramadan campaigns.
