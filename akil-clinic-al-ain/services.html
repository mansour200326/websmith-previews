01Implant dentistry

Dr. Mohamed Akil is a Certified Oral Implantologist and a Member of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID). He completed the AAID's postdoctoral MaxiCourse® fellowship in implant dentistry in 2011.

1.1Dental implantsReplacing one or more missing teeth

Missing teeth cause more than a gap in your smile: they make biting, chewing and speaking harder, and the teeth next to the gap can move. An implant replaces the missing tooth and can hold a crown or a bridge. We'll look at your mouth and tell you plainly whether an implant, a bridge or a denture suits you best.

03Children's dentistry

We want children to grow up liking the dentist. Infants, children and teenagers get a friendly, upbeat visit, whether it's a check-up or a filling.

3.1The first visitWhen the first tooth comes in, and by age one

We get to know you and your child, then explain the chair and the instruments in a fun way. The visit includes a thorough exam, X-rays if needed, a cleaning and fluoride. Afterwards the dentist goes through any problems, how to prevent decay, and your questions. Your child leaves with stickers, a prize, a toothbrush and floss; you get a summary of their teeth.

For everyone's safety and privacy, brothers and sisters who aren't being seen wait in reception with an adult.

Book your child's first visit →

3.2Check-ups and treatmentPrevention first, and fillings when needed

We look after your child's teeth however complex the need. Bigger treatments such as fillings are done at a later visit, once your child is used to the clinic. If your child has a toothache, tell us when you book: treatment can start at the first appointment if time and behaviour allow.

04Cosmetic dentistry

Whiter teeth, chips repaired, gaps closed. We work out the simplest, most cost-efficient way to get the smile you want.

4.1Porcelain veneersThin porcelain shells over the front of the tooth
What are they good for?
Covering chips or cracks, deep stains and gaps, or reshaping teeth so they look even.
How long do they last?
With regular maintenance, they could last a lifetime.
Will they look natural?
Yes. They're coloured to match your other teeth, and resist stains from coffee and smoking.

Ask about veneers →

4.2Teeth whiteningOne visit of 45 to 60 minutes
Is it right for me?
It works best on healthy teeth without many fillings, and on yellowed teeth more than greyish stains. We check your teeth and gums first.
How often?
A thorough whitening every few years.

Book whitening →

4.3Dental bondingUsually one visit

A simple, economical way to repair chipped or cracked teeth, cover stains, fill small gaps or change a tooth's shape. Bonding doesn't last as long as crowns or veneers and stains more easily, so we'll tell you honestly if it's the right choice.

5.3Root canal treatmentSaving a tooth with an injured or infected nerve
What is it?
We remove the damaged nerve and replace it with a sterile, inert material, so you keep the tooth.
Is it painful?
With today's anaesthetics, most patients report practically no discomfort during the procedure.
How many visits?
Root canal treatment often takes more than two visits; with our equipment we can save you some of them.
Anything after?
Most teeth that need a root canal are then sealed with a crown.

Call about a toothache →

5.4Gum treatmentPeriodontal care

Gum disease ranges from inflamed gums to damage to the bone that holds your teeth. Watch for bad breath that won't go, swollen or bleeding gums, painful chewing, and loose or sensitive teeth. Caught early, it can be reversed; left alone, it can lead to tooth loss. Regular check-ups and cleaning, and daily brushing and flossing, stop it early.

06Crowns, bridges & dentures

Restoring broken, weak or missing teeth so you can bite, chew and smile normally again.

6.1All-porcelain crownsA "cap" over the whole tooth
When do you need one?
To protect a weak, decaying or cracked tooth, hold a bridge, cover an implant or a discoloured tooth, or for cosmetic corrections.
How is it fitted?
We numb the area and take impressions so the crown matches in size and colour, then cement it in place. It feels like your own tooth.
How long does it last?
With good care, a very long time: 50 to 80 percent of crowns last 15 to 20 years.

Ask about crowns →

6.2Dental bridgesWhen an implant isn't possible

A bridge is a false tooth held between two crowns on the teeth either side of the gap. It's less invasive than an implant, made to match your own teeth in colour and shape, and with proper care it is strong and lasting.

Ask about bridges →

6.3DenturesFull or partial

Removable replacement teeth for anyone who has lost a few or all of their teeth. Dentures restore the shape of your face and make eating pleasant again. Getting them is a big step: we help you get used to them and show you how to look after them.
