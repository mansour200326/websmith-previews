Divisions and branches

Rock and landscape is what most people know us for. It sits alongside four other lines of business.

Established 1994 · Commercial Register 11378 · Commercial Permit 4224 · Export Import License 3513

  1. 2001Rockworks Division, from February

    Rockworks Division

    Waterscapes, wall decoration, external plastering, waterfalls, stone face, artificial rockworks, stamped concrete and exposed aggregate. Our men were trained by American companies and have completed major projects in Morocco, the UAE, Oman and Qatar.

    We consider concrete work a form of art. We apply decorative concrete chemicals (grouts, admixtures, adhesives, concrete repair and treatment) and are the exclusive distributor of AMANCRETE products in Qatar.

  2. 2001Founded the same year as Rockworks

    Commercial Division

    Air handling units and quality filters. We are the sole representative in Qatar of BerlinerLuft KlimaTechnik GmbH of Berlin (air ventilation, air conditioning, noise control, fire protection and metal construction), and the sole representative in Doha of Volks-Air filters, formerly McLeod Russel.

    Through this division we contracted with Hamad Medical Corporation for air handling units and a water filtration plant.

  3. TimberCommercial Permit 12176

    Doha Arabesque Timber

    Interior decoration, carpentry, ceramic and aluminium works, and furniture made for homes, businesses and hospitals. Our main line here is project furnishing.

  4. GalleryRetail furniture

    Galerie La Marquis

    A furniture store run by Doha Arabesque Timber, from classical to contemporary, and sole agent of LINEA VERDE fabrics and curtains, with up to 25,000 fabric samples in stock.

  5. SEDARAgent

    Agent of SEDAR

    Venetian, vertical, wooden Venetian, pleated and roman blinds, folding doors, false ceiling strips and tiles, fabric curtains and umbrellas.
