01

Rock & waterscapes

Our Rockworks Division started in February 2001. It builds waterscapes, waterfalls, artificial rock, stone face, wall decoration and external plastering, plus the stamped concrete and exposed aggregate around them. Our men were trained by American companies and have finished major projects in Morocco, the UAE, Oman and Qatar.

We are also the exclusive distributor of AMANCRETE products in Qatar: decorative concrete chemicals, grouts, admixtures, adhesives, concrete repair and treatment.

What goes into a rockwork

  • Plaxit CP-150 cement plaster render
  • Fosroc Brush Bond waterproofing
  • Jotun paints
  • A-Z Super Sealer
  • Ready-mix, slump 100, 20N OPC
  • 8 mm steel bar, angle bars, chicken wire mesh
  • Carving tools and texturing mats
  • PFT G5 mixing pump

What we need on site

  • Free access, no vehicles or other trades in the area
  • Single and 3-phase power
  • Water on site at all times
  • Approved shop drawings

02

Stamped concrete

Textured concrete is a slab cast in place, coloured and imprinted with a pattern and a texture while it is still plastic. We offer over 100 patterns and unlimited colours, so a courtyard, a driveway or a pool deck can look like slate, cobbles, brick, timber or tile, in one piece.

How we lay it

  • Sub-grade, forms and screeds set, reinforcement where needed
  • Concrete placed, then colour hardener applied
  • Release agent, then the imprinting tools in your pattern
  • Washed by water jet once the concrete has cured
  • Contraction joints cut
  • Two coats of sealer

To specification

  • Portland cement to ASTM C150
  • Aggregates to ASTM C33, max 9 mm
  • 3,000 psi in non-freeze areas
  • No calcium chloride admixtures
  • Job-site sample for the architect to approve, on request

03

Exposed aggregate

Concrete floors with exposed aggregate are slabs cast on the job site. While the concrete is still plastic we work in a dry-shake mix of granite granules, fractured stone or marble chips with coloured powder, float and trowel it by hand or by power machine, then expose the stone to the texture you want.

The system

  • Sub-grade prepared and compacted to level
  • Shuttering and reinforcement where needed
  • Slab usually 10 cm or more
  • Polypropylene fibre in the mix, 0.75 kg per m³
  • Colour powder floated in once the bleed water is gone
  • Aggregate shaken on at 7.5 kg per m², in three or more passes
  • Joints saw-cut
  • Two coats of acrylic sealer
