Rock & water

Built, not quarried

Every rock in these photos was made on site. Blockwork and steel first, then the pipes for the water and the cables for the lights. Two coats of Plaxit CP-150 render, Fosroc waterproofing, then a thick final coat that our men carve by hand while it is still soft.

It cures for at least ten days, then it is coloured with Jotun paint and sealed. At night, with the lights on, it reads as stone.

From our method statement

How a rock waterfall is built

  1. 01

    Ground and base

    We clear and lay out the area, compact the sub-grade and pour 50 mm of concrete blinding.

  2. 02

    Blocks and steel

    Hollow blocks mark the edge of the lagoon. The steel structure follows the approved shop drawings, with green mesh as a backing for the render.

  3. 03

    Water and power

    Pipes for the waterfall, conduits for the underwater lights, the pump, the filter and the switches, all laid before the floor is poured.

  4. 04

    Render and waterproofing

    Two sprayed coats of Plaxit CP-150 cement render, two coats of Fosroc Brush Bond, then render again over the waterproofing.

    Sprayed with a PFT G5 mixing pump
  5. 05

    Carving

    The last coat goes on thick and is carved and textured by hand with tools made for rock cracks. Then at least ten days of curing.

  6. 06

    Colour and seal

    A Jotun base coat, the final rock colours sprayed on for a natural look, and a sealer to protect the paint.
