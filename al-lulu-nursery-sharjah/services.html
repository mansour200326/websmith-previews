Foundation classroom

The foundation classroom has what children need to take part in every activity, in a room that's fun to be in. It's set up for early reading, writing and numbers.

Multi‑purpose hall

Alongside the classroom activities, the hall gives a well-balanced programme of extra-curricular activities. It helps children try new things, see what they can do, build confidence and grow their gross motor skills.

There's a wide choice of equipment: balls, hula-hoops, soft climbing shapes, beanbags, activity mats, balance beams and parachutes. We set physical challenges for each child and for small groups, and watch how every child gets on.

The hall is also used for music and movement, drama, ballet and gymnastics, with trained sport coaches.

The EYFS curriculum

We follow the British Early Years Foundation Stage. It has seven areas: three prime areas and four specific areas. Each child gets an individual development plan, and we share it with you.

Prime areas

  1. 1Personal, social and emotional development
  2. 2Physical development
  3. 3Communication and language

Specific areas

  1. 4Literacy
  2. 5Mathematics
  3. 6Understanding the world
  4. 7Expressive arts and design
