We are your home away from home.

We took care over every part of the nursery: planning it, fitting it out, the toys and books we chose, and the people we hired. Experienced, qualified and kind practitioners who keep your child's best interest at heart.

Children move around the nursery at their own pace. Every area is designed to explore, and the good things stay out all day, so a child can come back to an idea and take it further.

Learning through play

What children learn matters. How they learn it matters even more.

01

Play comes first

Play is the language of childhood. It's how children pick up new skills, make sense of things and show what they can do. Through it they learn to choose, reason, imagine and feel for others.

02

A focused activity every day

Our practitioners plan short, adult-led activities each day that open something new. The rest of the time children lead their own play, with a grown-up close by.

03

A plan for each child

We follow the British Early Years Foundation Stage and write an individual development plan for every child: physical, emotional, social and thinking skills, step by step.

04

You're part of it

We work closely with parents and share what we see in your child, so home and nursery pull in the same direction.

Admission in three steps

Admission is open for children from 3 months to 4 years. Call or WhatsApp us and we'll send the forms.

  1. 1

    Registration form

    Fill in the registration form. We'll send it to you, or pick one up at the nursery.

  2. 2

    A play session with a teacher

    Once the form is with our Registrar, your child spends a relaxed, playful session with one of our teachers, so we can see they're ready for nursery.

  3. 3

    Enrolment

    After the session our Registrar calls you and offers a place. You get an offer letter, then confirm with the documents on the list.

What to bring

  • Child's Emirates ID, passport and visa (copies)
  • Mother's passport and visa (copies)
  • Father's passport and visa (copies)
  • Birth certificate (copy)
  • Immunisation record (copy)
  • Health insurance (copy)
  • Registration form, filled in and signed
  • Medical form, filled in and signed
  • 4 colour passport-size photos
