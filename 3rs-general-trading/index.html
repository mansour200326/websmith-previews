About 3RS

3RS General Trading LLC was set up in Dubai in 2007 to supply adhesive application and quality assurance equipment.

Today we are the authorised ValcoMelton distributor for five countries. Alongside ValcoMelton, we supply compatible parts for the other hot melt systems you find on factory floors in the region, so one call covers most of the glue line.
