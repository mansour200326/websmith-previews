About the office

Our lawyers have the right of audience before all the courts of the country, from the Court of First Instance to the Federal Supreme Court. We write the briefs, plead the case and follow the judgment through to execution. And we keep your file to ourselves.

Who comes to us
Government departments, banks, companies across the Emirates, and individuals with civil, criminal, family and labour cases.
Languages
Arabic and English, for advice, contracts and translation of documents.

From the office archive

Thirty-odd years of the office, in the photographs it kept.

1989

Two names on the door

The office opened as Al-Zahmi and Al-Yousef Advocates and Legal Consultants, founded by Counselor Yacoub Yousef Al-Hammadi and lawyer Saeed Mubarak Al-Zahmi, the first lawyer to practise in Fujairah.

Opening of the office. Archive photograph.
25 Feb 1991

The official opening

A year and a half later H.H. Sheikh Saleh bin Hamad Al Sharqi, Head of the Fujairah Department of Industry and Economy, opened the office officially. Government departments and banks were among the first clients.

Mr. Saeed Al Zahmy honoured by H.H. Saleh bin Mohamed Al Sharqi.
In the papers

A voice on the law

Interviews with Al Bayan, Al Khaleej and Emirates Today, the chair of an appeal committee, and our counsel explaining cheques, powers of attorney and online defamation on Sabah El Kheir Ya Watan.

With H.H. Saleh bin Mohamed Al Sharqi.
