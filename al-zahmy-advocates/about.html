The first law officein Fujairah

Al Zahmy Advocates and Legal Consultants is one of the oldest law offices on the east coast. It was started in 1989 by Saeed Mubarak Al Zahmi, the first lawyer to practise in Fujairah.

How it started

The office opened in 1989 as Al-Zahmi and Al-Yousef Advocates and Legal Consultant, founded by Counselor Yacoub Yousef Al-Hammadi and lawyer Saeed Mubarak Al-Zahmi.

A year and a half later, on 25 February 1991, it was opened officially by H.H. Sheikh Saleh bin Hamad Al Sharqi, Head of the Fujairah Department of Industry and Economy.

From the start the office drew clients from the public sector and government departments, major companies in the Emirates and many banks. It works with advocates in Egypt and the UK on matters abroad.

The head office is still on the 6th floor of the Fujairah Insurance Building, opposite Fujairah Tower on Hamad bin Abdullah Road. The Abu Dhabi branch is on Airport Road.

How we work

Plainly

Transparency and credibility were set as the rules of the office by Mr. Saeed Al Zahmy from the first day. You will know what the case involves, what it may cost you and what we will do next.

In confidence

Your information and your case stay with us. We never discuss one client's matter with another, as the charter of the legal profession requires.

Up to date

Our counsel keep learning: research, conferences and every new local and federal law, so that advice protects the client before a dispute starts.
