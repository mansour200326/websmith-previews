01

Criminal cases

القضايا الجزائية

We represent clients in violations, misdemeanours and felonies before the courts of first instance, appeal and cassation: traffic courts, municipal courts, the public prosecution, state security and military courts.

We also act for victims who bring a civil claim inside the criminal case, so that the damage is compensated in the same proceedings.

  • Bounced cheques, fraud, breach of trust, misappropriation
  • Electronic crimes: messages, chat apps, hacking
  • Libel, slander, assault
  • Serious traffic offences
  • Immigration and residence violations, absconding reports
  • Municipality and building code violations

02

Family matters

الأحوال الشخصية

We represent husbands, wives and parents before the family courts in the UAE, Muslims and non-Muslims, whether the case runs under UAE law or the law of their home country.

Family cases often need quick temporary orders before the final judgment. We file them, and we tell you when the court needs you there in person.

  • Divorce, including at-fault divorce
  • Maintenance for the wife and the children
  • Custody and visiting rights
  • Travel bans on children, and lifting them
  • Guidance on proving your case at each session

Labour cases

القضايا العمالية

We act for employees and for employers: companies, associations, government and semi-government bodies, before the labour courts at first instance, appeal and cassation, and before the experts the court appoints.

For employers we work out what is really owed, defend unfounded claims, and claim for breaches of confidentiality or serious misconduct.

  • Unpaid salary and allowances
  • Arbitrary dismissal
  • End of service gratuity and accrued leave
  • Overtime and public holiday pay
  • Bonuses and commissions
  • Notice period compensation

Authentication of documents

التصديقات

Our certified lawyer authenticates documents issued abroad and documents that foreign authorities ask for. We check the papers and where they came from, the person signs in front of the lawyer after showing their passport, and you collect a certified copy.

Typical cases: property sales in the US, the UK and other countries, offshore companies, bank accounts abroad, migration and travel files.

  • Passports and utility bills
  • Proof of residence address for people and companies
  • Trade licences and memoranda
  • Agreements and declarations
  • Signature verification

Commercial companies

الشركات التجارية

Federal Law No. 2 of 2015 sets the forms a company can take. If a company doesn't fit one of them, the incorporation is void and the people behind it are jointly liable. We advise investors on the form and the scope that suit their market, and prepare the papers.

  • Joint liability company
  • Limited partnership
  • Limited liability company (LLC)
  • Public and private shareholding companies
