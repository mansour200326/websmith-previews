The sign on our wall

If you’re satisfied, please tell others.

If you’re not… please tell us.

It’s been on the wall in the shop for years, under “This business operates on the basis of service.” The best compliment we get is a customer who comes back.

Bringing your car in

No app, no portal. A phone call, a front room with a coffee, and the person who fixed your car telling you what they found.

01

Call or email

Phone 204-888-0935 or write to appts.nessautoservice@shaw.ca, tell us what the car is doing and we’ll find you a time, Monday to Friday.

02

Wait in the front room, or leave the keys

There are leather couches, a TV, coffee and water. If it’s a quick job, you’re welcome to wait for it.

03

Talk to the person who worked on it

You hear what was wrong, what we did and what can wait, from the mechanic who had the car on the hoist.

More than repairs

Certified Safety Station

Manitoba safety inspections for light passenger vehicles and medium trucks, done here.

Licensed vehicle dealer

We sell used vehicles too. See our listings on Kijiji (search “Ness Auto Service”), or ask Brian to help you look for something. Financing on approved credit, and warranties available.

MPI tire financing

We’re a tire retailer for MPI’s tire financing program. Ask Brian how it works.

Accessories and customizing

Cold air intakes, lift kits, tires, rims, headlights and taillights, supplied and installed.
