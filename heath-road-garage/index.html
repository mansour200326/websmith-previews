How we work

No one here is on bonus or commission.

So no one gets paid to sell you parts you don't need.

That's from our purpose statement, and it's how the garage has run since 1996: listen to what you ask for, look at what the car actually needs, and give you an honest answer that's right for you.

We aim to give everyone the right price first time. Rich or poor, young or old, male or female, the price is the same.
