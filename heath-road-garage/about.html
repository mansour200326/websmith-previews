About &the team

A family-run garage out on Warbraham Farm, Burwell. Friendly, honest and reliable, from tyre fitting to computer diagnostics.

1996the year the garage opened

Heath Road Garage has been family-run since 1996. We've grown by giving an honest, friendly, quality service at affordable prices, and we want every customer to leave happy to recommend us to friends, family and colleagues.

Stuart owns the garage and has been in the motoring and tyre trade since 1989. Dave runs the office. Out in the workshop there are MOT testers, a Master Technician who came from Renault, and tyre technicians who are training up as vehicle technicians.

We work on cars, vans, minibuses, horseboxes and camper vans up to 3.5 tonnes, and do the odd tractor battery and wheelbarrow puncture along the way.
