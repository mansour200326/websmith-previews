MOT testing

Petrol and diesel, tested while you wait. If something fails we can do the repair here, and the retest is free up to 10 working days after the original test.

Classes we test

  • 4Quads, cars and vans up to 3,000kg, people carriers up to 12 passengers, camper vans
  • 5LMinibuses with more than 12 passengers, up to 16 passenger seats
  • 7Vans from 3,000kg to 3,500kg gross vehicle weight, and horseboxes

Good to know

  • Petrol and diesel tested
  • While you wait
  • Repair service available
  • Free retest up to 10 working days after the original test
  • Free local collection and return from your workplace

Bringing your car in

01

Ring the office

Call 01638 741 070 or email info@heath-road-garage.co.uk. Dave in the office books you in and aims to give you the right price first time.

02

Drop it off, or we fetch it

Bring it to Unit 5, Warbraham Farm. For MOTs, if you work locally, we'll collect it from your workplace and bring it back, free.

03

In the bay

Qualified technicians and MOT testers do the work. Nobody here is on bonus or commission, so nobody is paid to sell you parts you don't need.

04

Failed? Retest free

If the car fails its MOT we can do the repairs here, and the retest is free up to 10 working days after the original test.
