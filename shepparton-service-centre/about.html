Aboutthe workshop

Locally owned, on Mitchell Street, and committed to the Goulburn Valley community. Our dedicated team pride themselves on their work.

Who we are

We're committed to giving you the best possible service, getting you back on the road faster, and making sure your vehicle performs at its best.

We understand how important it is to get straight advice from specialists you understand. We only use quality parts and lubricants on your vehicle.

Our friendly, fully qualified technicians do regular training to keep up with the latest vehicles and industry changes. We're trained suspension specialists, so we understand how to set up your vehicle's handling and performance, whatever your needs.

Shepparton Service Centre offers more than just great products. We offer you great people too.
