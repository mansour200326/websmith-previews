How to book

Three things and you're on the list. Parts cost depends on the make, model and year, so that's the first thing we'll ask.

  1. Call the workshop

    Ring (03) 5831 2866, Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm.

  2. Tell us the vehicle

    Make, model and year. Parts can cost more or less depending on all three.

  3. Tell us the job and a time

    What needs doing, and the day and time that suits you to drop it in.

  4. Drop it at Mitchell Street

    57 Mitchell Street. If you'd rather wait, there's a clean, comfortable customer lounge inside.
