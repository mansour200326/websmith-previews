01Check-ups and children’s teeth

Most dental problems are painless at first. If you don’t see a dentist regularly, you may not know about a problem until it has done real damage. We suggest a check-up every six months, more often if you are at higher risk.

Regular check-up

We look at every tooth and the gums, screen for signs of abnormal tissue, and for children we check how the mouth and teeth are growing.

For children

  • Fluoride treatment
  • Pit and fissure sealants: thin coatings on the chewing surfaces of the back teeth that protect them from decay
  • Space maintainers: if a milk tooth is lost early, they keep the gap open so the other teeth don’t drift and crowd

02Fillings, root canals and crowns

When a tooth is decayed, broken or worn, we repair it so you can bite and chew properly again. Sensitivity from lost enamel often improves or goes once the right filling is in.

What we do

  • Silver and tooth-coloured fillings (composite and porcelain)
  • Root canal treatment: the infected pulp is removed, the canals cleaned, shaped and sealed so the pain does not come back
  • Post and core, to rebuild a tooth before a crown
  • Crowns and bridges: a fixed bridge replaces one or more missing teeth and cannot be taken out like a denture
  • Partial and complete dentures

03Cosmetic dentistry

Treatments you choose to improve how your teeth and smile look. Dr. Hafsa is certified in Lumineers and Cerinate smile designs.

Treatments

  • Tooth whitening, for stains from smoking, coffee, tea and food
  • Porcelain veneers and laminates, to close gaps or cover chips and discolouration that whitening can’t fix
  • All-porcelain (metal-free) crowns
  • Tooth-coloured fillings and composite bonding for chipped or discoloured teeth
  • Smile makeover: a full assessment and a plan that may combine several of these

Tooth jewellery

A small crystal or gold design bonded onto the tooth, a simple 15-minute procedure. No drilling, no pain, and it can be removed or changed at any time.

04Braces (orthodontics)

Crooked or crowded teeth and a poor bite can be corrected at any age. Orthodontic treatment is with Prof. Dr. Sandeep Shetty, our specialist orthodontist, and you can pay for braces in easy instalments.

Options

  • Fixed braces, metal or ceramic
  • Lingual braces, fixed behind the front teeth
  • Removable clear aligners (Invisalign), made for you
  • Functional appliances for growing children
  • Retainers to hold the teeth after treatment

How long

Treatment usually takes about two years, sometimes longer, and adults often take a little longer than children. We will tell you what to expect for your own case at the consultation.

05Gums and sensitive teeth

Bleeding or swollen gums are usually the first stage of gum disease. Treated early, it is simple to put right.

What we do

  • Scaling and polishing: a thorough cleaning that removes plaque and tartar from around the teeth and under the gums
  • Gum disease treatment, planned around how far it has gone
  • Tooth sensitivity: we find exactly which tooth reacts to cold or air and treat it
  • Custom mouth guards for sport
  • Night guards for teeth grinding and jaw pain

06Laser dentistry

A dental laser uses a very narrow beam of light to remove or shape tissue precisely.

Why a laser

  • In some procedures, no drill and no anaesthetic
  • Less bleeding during treatment
  • Fewer bacteria in gum pockets and cavities
  • Often a quicker, more comfortable recovery

After your treatment

The short version of the care notes we give you at the clinic. If anything worries you, call us.

After an extraction
  • Don’t chew for two hours, or until the numbness has gone.
  • Don’t rinse on the day. Don’t use a straw or spit hard: the blood clot must stay in place.
  • An ice pack, 20 minutes on and 20 off, for the first six hours.
  • From the next day, rinse gently with warm salt water 2–3 times a day for a week.
  • Soft food for the first 48 hours. No smoking.
  • Call us if the bleeding continues or gets worse.
After a root canal
  • Don’t eat or drink for half an hour while the temporary filling sets.
  • Don’t bite on anything hard until the permanent filling or crown is in.
  • Have the permanent filling or crown placed within one month.
  • Some soreness for 2–3 days is normal. Call us if the temporary filling falls out.
With a temporary crown
  • Avoid sticky and hard foods: chewing gum, toffee, nuts, popcorn.
  • Floss carefully and pull the floss out from the side.
  • If it comes off, put it back on and call us to fix it.
After a filling
  • White fillings are hard straight away. Silver fillings stay soft for 24 hours, so chew on the other side.
  • Some sensitivity to hot, cold or chewing can last a few weeks and should ease.
  • If your bite feels high once the numbness has gone, call us so we can adjust it.
After whitening
  • For 48 hours: no coffee, tea, dark soft drinks or coloured lipstick.
  • No smoking for 48 hours, or the teeth will darken again.
  • No coloured toothpaste or mouthwash for 48 hours.
After an implant
  • No smoking. Keep the area clean and free of food.
  • Rinse gently with warm salt water from the day after surgery.
  • Ice pack 20 minutes on and 20 off for two days. Soft food on the first day.
  • Call us if there is unusual swelling or pain after five days.
