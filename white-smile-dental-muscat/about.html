Dr. Hafsa Banu Abid

Managing director · General and aesthetic dentistry

Dr. Hafsa completed her Bachelor of Dental Surgery at Yenepoya Dental College, India, in 2003. She did her vocational training in a government hospital and in the private sector in India, then worked in a private dental clinic in Oman for four years.

In 2010 she opened White Smile Dental Speciality. Her aim is simple: a wide choice of treatment, done to a high standard in a sterile clinic. With her team she also runs dental check-ups in schools, to raise awareness of dental problems.

Prof. Dr. Sandeep Shetty

Specialist orthodontist · MDS in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics

Prof. Dr. Sandeep earned his Bachelor of Dental Surgery at the A.B. Shetty Institute of Dental Sciences, Mangalore, and his master’s in orthodontics at Yenepoya Dental College, Mangalore, where he joined the teaching faculty in 1999. He is a member of the Indian Orthodontic Society and the Indian Dental Association.

He has lectured and examined widely, and published in international journals including the Latin American Journal of Orthodontics and the World Journal of Orthodontics. He has treated many kinds of malocclusion, including cleft cases after surgery.

Techniques

  • Pre-adjusted edgewise
  • MBT
  • Begg
  • Functional
  • Lingual
  • Incognito (certified)
