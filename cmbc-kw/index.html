About cmbc

We're a Kuwaiti firm, established in 1998. We build complete projects: commercial and residential buildings, industrial projects, warehouses, shops, and high-end interior finishes.

Because design, building and management sit with one company, the drawings and the site stay in step, and the building is worked out against the client's budget and needs. Our in-house design division handles interior design and image creation for shops and brands. In 2010 we launched a roads and infrastructure division.

We comply with Kuwait health & safety regulations and work towards current international standards.
