About cmbc

Construction Management and Building Company is a Kuwaiti firm, established in 1998. Our head office is in the Bobyan Complex, Dhajeej.

What we do

1998
Established in Kuwait as a building contractor.
2010
Roads and infrastructure division launched.
In-house
Design division for interior design and image creation.
Dhajeej
Head office: Suite 48, Bobyan Complex, Farwaniya.

We build commercial and residential buildings, industrial projects, warehouses, retail shops and stores, and high-end interior finishes.

We're equipped to handle all construction disciplines, with design, build, operation and management capability for turn-key projects. One company from design to construction keeps the two stages in step, and lets us offer a well-coordinated, value-engineered building that fits the client's budget and needs.

Our in-house design division specialises in interior design and image creation, for each customer's needs and budget. In 2010 we launched our roads and infrastructure division.

Our workforce is multinational, led by a qualified, professional management team.

Health & safety

Health, safety and the protection of the environment come first in our everyday work. Our policy spells out the company name.

We comply with Kuwait health & safety regulations, and we work to make sure the company and its workers meet current international standards.

  1. C

    Commitment

    That every employee works in a safe and healthy environment, in line with all safety and health laws and regulations, and is informed, trained and given the proper tools to do the job safely.

  2. M

    Management accountability

    Every manager, supervisor and subcontractor is held accountable for the company's safety objectives, and those objectives are made clear to everybody.

  3. B

    Believe

    We believe all incidents are preventable, and we take every measure to prevent job-related injuries and illness.

  4. C

    Carrying out

    Regular safety inspections, and an accident prevention programme that finds the cause of an accident and puts it right.

At cmbc, "Quality is a policy and a culture."

We keep strict quality control and keep working to improve our performance and our relationships with clients: better coordination, higher productivity and a greater focus on business objectives.

Kitchell International

cmbc-Kitchell International brings cmbc together with Kitchell, an American construction company, to serve clients across the Middle East & North Africa. Its MENA headquarters is in Kuwait.

Together we provide construction and complete program management services for government and private projects, from initial programming and budget through to occupancy and property management.

  • Development program management
  • Project management
  • Construction management
  • Value engineering
  • Claims analysis & dispute resolution
  • Other engineering & architectural services
