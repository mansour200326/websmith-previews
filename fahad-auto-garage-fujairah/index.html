A respray, start to finish

Three stages from our paint booth, straight from the reels we post on Instagram.

Stage 1

Masking

Before any paint goes on, everything that isn't being painted gets covered: glass, lights, mirrors and trim, taped edge to edge.

Stage 2

Primer and sanding

The repaired panels get primer, then they're sanded flat so the colour goes on smooth and the repair doesn't show through.

Stage 3

Paint and polish

Colour coat, then the machine polisher, until the new paint sits level with the rest of the car.
