The first days

All children are different and settle in at their own pace. Here's how we make the start as smooth as we can.

Day one

Short and sweet

Leave your child for an hour or two in the first couple of days, then build it up. Please be strong and positive when you let go. Some children get upset, but they all settle in.

While you wait

The parents' room

Parents don't stay in the classroom, it only makes settling harder. Have a tea or coffee in our parents' room instead. If your child is really distressed, we'll come and get you straight away.

Every day

Their routine, not ours

Tell us their snack times, nap times, likes, dislikes and any fears. For the younger ones we follow the routine they have at home, and keep you posted as it changes.

Our people

Our staff are great. We have lots of them, all trained and dedicated. Many are mums, and we all love children!

Our staff to children ratios are among the best in Qatar. Every teacher holds a first aid and CPR certificate in childcare, and we keep spare teachers on the team so that if your child's teacher is off, someone they know steps in.

We also employ a registered nurse and a nutritionist, and a paediatrician visits every month. If a child gets ill during the day, they stay with the nurse until you arrive.

Health and safety

The rules we keep, written down in our parent handbook.

  • Security guard, intercom entry and CCTVCovering the entrance, every classroom and the play areas.
  • Every accident and incident written upYou see and sign the report the same day. Any bump to the head, we call you at once.
  • Medicines given only by our nurse or managerWith your signed consent form and the medicine in its labelled box.
  • Monthly fire drillsEquipment checked every quarter, plus Civil Defence inspections.
  • No nuts and no pork in lunch boxesFor allergies, and out of respect for every family.
  • No trips off the premisesIn line with Ministry guidelines.
