Classes

Tiny Town follows the British Early Years Foundation Stage, the curriculum that sets the standards for learning, development and care of young children in the UK. Children are grouped by age and ability and learn through classroom activities, games and play.

Ask about a place →Ages, days and hours
01

Learning through play

It's amazing what children can absorb and understand while having fun. Play can be structured and supervised, and during free time their imagination takes them to exciting new worlds.

02

Literacy, numeracy and science

To children this may start off as simple letters, numbers, shapes and colours. We know these are the keys to reading, writing and understanding the world we live in.

03

Expressive arts and crafts

We encourage children to use their artistic talents and enjoy the pleasure, satisfaction and reward of making something all their own.

04

Communication and language

From circle time and singing to interactive reading and real conversation, we encourage children to express themselves and talk with us and with each other.

05

Personal, social and emotional development

We teach good social skills, good manners and respect for others. This builds independence and confidence, so each child can reach their own potential.

06

Kitchen fun and healthy eating

We teach children why eating well matters for a healthy mind and a healthy body. We also have lots of fun making healthy treats, and bake a few cookies too!

07

When I grow up

From role play to visiting professionals like the dentist, the vet and paramedics, children hear first-hand about real jobs, then dress up and act out what they've learned.

08

Arabic and Islamic studies

What a wonderful opportunity to learn about the country you're living in. Our dedicated teacher gives a gentle introduction to the Arabic language and culture.

Playtime!

We never lose sight of the importance of good, old-fashioned fun. After all, they are just children!

Ages, days and hours

Everything you need to know before you call us.

Who it's for
Children from 3 months to 4 years.
Days
Two, three or five days a week. Fees are paid monthly, or by term with a 5% discount. Ask us for this year's fees.
Hours
Sunday to Thursday, 6.30am to 3.30pm.
Meals
Breakfast 9.00–9.30am and lunch 12.00–12.30pm, from the lunch box you pack. Babies keep their own routine. We can warm food in the microwave.
What to pack
  • A healthy snack (fruit is best) and 2 drinks
  • Spare clothes
  • Nappies, a small towel and wet wipes, if needed
  • Swimwear and a towel, when asked
  • A sun hat for the playground
Summer
We run a Summer Camp in July and August. Ask us for this year's dates.
Language
English is spoken in class, with Arabic lessons from our own teacher.
