From the Tiny Town welcome

Your children's home from home

Somewhere your little ones can laugh and learn in a loving, relaxed, happy and secure place of their own.

Our classrooms and playgrounds are bright, colourful, clean and spacious. Our staff are great too. We have lots of them, all trained and dedicated. Many are mums, and we all love children!

Our teacher to children ratios are among the best in Qatar (from our parent handbook), and every child is grouped by age and ability, then looked at again as they grow.

Our door is open You are very important as well. We appreciate the trust parents place in us, so Tiny Town has an open door policy: visitors are always welcome. So come on down to Tiny Town!

A day at tiny town

We open early for working parents and close at 3.30. Here is how the day goes.

6.30am

Drop-off

Our security guard is on the door, and the intercom lets you in. In the first days, leave your child for an hour or two and build it up. You're welcome to wait with a tea or coffee in the parents' room.

Morning

Learning through play

Letters, numbers, shapes and colours, circle time and singing. It's amazing what children take in while they're having fun.

No nuts.Lunch at 12.
9.00am

Breakfast

You pack a healthy snack and two drinks; we keep the water bottles topped up. Lunch is at 12.00. Tiny Town is a no-nut nursery.

Outside

Two shaded playgrounds

Ball games, stepping stones, ride-ons and water play in season. We watch the heat and keep everyone drinking.

Till 3.30pm

Making things, a nap, home

Arts and crafts, kitchen fun, library time and a rest in the sleeping room. Collection is only by the adults you list on your consent form.

What we teach

All classes in detail →
01Learning through playStructured, supervised and free play

Sometimes structured and supervised, and sometimes free time when their imagination takes them somewhere new.

02Literacy, numeracy and scienceLetters, numbers, shapes and colours

To children it starts as simple letters, numbers, shapes and colours. We know they are the keys to reading, writing and understanding the world.

03Expressive arts and craftsMaking something all their own

Painting, cutting, sticking and building, for the pleasure of making something all their own.

04Communication and languageCircle time, songs and stories

From circle time and singing to interactive reading and conversation, we help children express themselves to us and to each other.

05Personal, social and emotionalManners, friends and confidence

Good manners and respect for others, so each child grows in independence and confidence.

06Kitchen fun and healthy eatingAnd a few cookies too

Why eating well matters for a healthy mind and body, plus lots of fun making healthy treats, and baking a few cookies too.

07When I grow upRole play and real visitors

Dressing up and role play, and visits from real professionals like the dentist, the vet and paramedics.

08Arabic and Islamic studiesA gentle start with our own teacher

A wonderful chance to learn about the country you're living in. Our dedicated teacher gives a gentle introduction to Arabic language and culture.

09Playtime!Good, old-fashioned fun

We never lose sight of good, old-fashioned fun. After all, they are just children!

Safe hands

What we have in place every day, so you can get on with yours.

  • A security guard on duty whenever we're openPlus an intercom entry system and CCTV over the entrance, classrooms and play areas.
  • A registered nurse on siteWith a nutritionist and a paediatrician who visits every month (from our parent handbook).
  • Staff trained in first aid and CPRAnd spare teachers, so a class is never left short.
  • A fire drill every monthFire equipment and escape routes are checked every quarter.
  • No nuts, everAnything with nuts in the snack bag goes home with a reminder.
  • Collected only by the adults you nameAnyone new needs your written OK, and we may ask for ID.

How to join

Children from 3 months to 4 years, two, three or five days a week. Places are subject to availability.

  1. 1

    Come and look around

    Call 4483 4553 and pop in. See the classrooms, meet the teachers, ask anything.

  2. 2

    Ask for the information pack

    It has this year's fees, the term dates and the registration form. Paying by term gives you 5% off.

  3. 3

    Bring the papers

    • Copy of your child's passport and visa
    • Copy of the birth certificate
    • 2 passport photos of your child
    • Copy of the sponsor's passport and visa
    • Up-to-date immunisation record
    • HMC medical card, if you have one
