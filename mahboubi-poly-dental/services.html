01

Check-up and consultation

With Dr. Marie V. Riveros, Dr. Nuno Sarmento

Every treatment starts here. The consultation tells us what problems there are, what treatment you need and how we'll plan it.

On your first visit you fill in a short form at reception; your details stay confidential and are used only for your treatment. The doctor checks your teeth, gums and jaw joints, and takes X-rays if needed to see between the teeth and under old fillings. Then we talk through any treatment, and you can ask whatever you want.

We recommend a check-up and cleaning at least twice a year.

02

Scaling and polishing

Scaling removes the hard tartar that brushing can't, plus trapped food and plaque, which hold the germs that cause tooth decay and gum disease. Polishing takes off the stain that's left.

03

Orthodontics

With Dr. Bayan H. Mahboubi, Senior Specialist Orthodontist

Our first speciality. Orthodontics treats crooked teeth and bites that don't meet properly, and guides the growth of the face and jaw in children.

  • Crowded teeth, gaps between teeth
  • Overbites and underbites
  • Fixed braces to move the teeth into line
  • Retainers to hold them there afterwards

04

Cosmetic dentistry

With Dr. Nuno Sarmento, General & Cosmetic Dentist

For discoloured, chipped, gapped or badly shaped front teeth, and old fillings that no longer look right.

  • Tooth whitening for teeth stained by age, medicines, coffee, tea or smoking.
  • Porcelain veneers: thin porcelain shells over the front teeth to change colour, shape and size, close gaps and straighten mildly crooked teeth.
  • Composite veneers: tooth-coloured resin shaped directly on the tooth and hardened with a light.
  • Lumineers: ultra-thin porcelain veneers.
  • Tooth-coloured fillings in composite resin, also to replace old silver fillings.
  • Inlays and onlays in porcelain or gold for larger cavities.

05

Root canal treatment

When a crack or deep cavity reaches the nerve, the pulp inside the tooth gets damaged. That causes toothache, swelling, an abscess or bad breath. Once, the tooth would have come out. A root canal treatment cleans out the damaged nerve and seals the tooth, so you keep it.

06

Dental implants

With Dr. Kamran A. Khorrami, Implantologist, Oral & Maxillofacial Specialist

Only an implant replaces the whole tooth, root and crown. A gap left open lets the teeth next to it drift and puts extra load on the rest. An implant takes that load and, unlike a bridge, doesn't need the teeth either side cut down.

Dr. Kamran places and restores implants. Call 04 345 8686 to book a consultation and hear which options suit you.

07

Crowns, bridges and dentures

For missing teeth, or teeth badly damaged by trauma, neglect or a condition you were born with. From one or two teeth to a full set.

  • Crowns (caps) cover and protect a broken or badly chipped tooth.
  • Bridges, at least three crowns joined, fill a gap between healthy teeth.
  • Dentures, partial or complete, in acrylic or acrylic and metal.
  • On implants: crowns, fixed bridges or overdentures held by implants.

08

Gum treatment

Periodontics treats gum disease: gingivitis, an inflamed gum, and periodontitis, when it reaches the bone around the teeth. Smoking, some medicines and hormone changes in puberty and pregnancy all play a part.

Oral surgery

With Dr. Kamran A. Khorrami, Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon

Surgery of the mouth, jaw and face, decided by our oral surgeon according to what each patient needs.

  • Wisdom tooth removal, the most common one
  • Dental implants
  • Sinus lift and ridge augmentation
  • Nerve repositioning

10

Jaw joint pain (TMD)

Pain and stiffness in the chewing muscles and the joints that hold the lower jaw to the skull. It isn't dangerous, but it can wear you down when it goes on, so it's worth having it looked at early.
