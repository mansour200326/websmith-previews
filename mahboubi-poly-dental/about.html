Since 1950

Mahboubi Dental Clinic was founded in Dubai in 1950 by Dr. Hussain Mahboubi. Today his son, Dr. Bayan H. Mahboubi, heads the clinics.

The first in the Gulf

Dr. Hussain Mahboubi opened Mahboubi Dental Clinic in Dubai in 1950. It was the first modern dental clinic and dental laboratory in the Gulf region.

He believed in good dental treatment, high standards of hygiene and the wellbeing of every patient. He brought local anaesthesia, permanent fillings, crowns and bridges to his patients, using materials from Germany and the USA.

Patients from across the Emirates

Dr. Hussain Mahboubi was the dentist of the late Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan. Patients came to him from all over the Emirates and the neighbouring countries, among them many of the country's best-known families.

The clinics today

Today the clinics are headed by his son, Dr. Bayan H. Mahboubi, an orthodontist who graduated from the University of Gothenburg in Sweden. He works with a team of doctors from many countries, and between us we speak English, Arabic, Spanish, Persian, Portuguese, Urdu, Hindi and Filipino.

After more than 65 years we are still a family practice: professional, caring, and in a comfortable clinic. You'll find us at Villa 176 on Al Wasl Road in Jumeirah 1, and in Deira.
