Where we started

Dr. Hussain Mahboubi opened his clinic and dental laboratory in Dubai in 1950, the first modern one in the Gulf. His son, Dr. Bayan Mahboubi, runs the clinics today.

Since 1950 we have run it the same way: a family practice, careful hygiene, and good materials, with time to explain each treatment. Read our history →

Our story

Dr. Hussain Mahboubi brought local anaesthesia, permanent fillings, crowns and bridges to his patients in Dubai, with materials from Germany and the USA.

  1. 1950

    Dr. Hussain Mahboubi opens in Dubai

    The first modern dental clinic and dental laboratory in the Gulf.

  2. Then

    Dentist to Sheikh Zayed

    Dr. Hussain Mahboubi was the dentist of the late Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan. Patients came from across the Emirates and the countries around.

  3. Today

    Dr. Bayan Mahboubi

    His son, an orthodontist trained in Gothenburg, heads the clinics with a team from many countries.

  4. 2 clinics

    Jumeirah 1 and Deira
