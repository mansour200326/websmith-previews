About

Prime Technologies LLC started in the Middle East in 1993. Today we work from Al Quoz Industrial Area 4 in Dubai, with an office in Doha and Kitchen House in Riyadh, and projects in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and India.

Who we areEngineers, technicians, traders

Mechanical, electrical, plumbing

We cover the main disciplines of building services: mechanical, electrical, plumbing, acoustics, TAB, commissioning management, and kitchens and laundries, plus specialised MEP products and services.

Our management team have each run commissioning management projects themselves, and stay responsible to the client from the start of a job to its completion.

Growth is life

Our motto. We take on complicated engineering problems and hand back simple, workable solutions, on time and at a sensible cost.
