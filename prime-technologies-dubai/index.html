We prove the building works

Prime Technologies is a third-party, independent commissioning management company. Our technicians test, adjust and balance the air and water systems, live-test the electrical services to the IEE 17th Edition, and witness the contractor's commissioning for the client and consultant. Formal commissioning is also a requirement for LEED and Estidama.
