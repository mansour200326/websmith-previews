Testing & commissioning

A third-party, independent commissioning management company. We work for the client and consultant on complex construction projects, from the commissioning plan through to post-construction inspection.

HVAC testing, adjusting & balancing

An established team of specialist technicians dedicated to hands-on commissioning of mechanical services.

  • Low velocity air and VAV systems
  • Kitchen hood and smoke exhaust
  • Stair, lift and lobby pressurisation
  • Chilled water and package AHUs
  • Chilled, condenser and glycol water systems
  • AHU and coil performance tests, sound levels

ICA, MEP commissioning management

A formal commissioning management system is vital to any project and a requirement for LEED and Estidama certification.

  • Complete commissioning plans
  • Static test and commissioning witnessing
  • Performance testing and statutory demonstrations
  • O&M documentation and client training
  • Commissioning Authority (CxA) for LEED and Estidama
  • Design commissioning appraisal

Electrical, ELV & automation testing

Specialist electrical technicians who live-test electrical services to the IEE Regulations 17th Edition.

  • Building fabric
  • Electrical services and ELV systems
  • Mechanical services and public health
  • Specialist systems

Third-party verification, witnessing & approval

Commissioning validation and verification, using our hands-on and management experience.

  • Commissionability studies
  • Review of programmes and method statements
  • Witnessing of static, system and plant load tests
  • Thermal imagery
  • Review and collation of test results

Condition survey & troubleshooting

Brings maintenance and risk concerns to light, prevents expensive repairs and reduces the cost of any remedial work.

Specialised testing

  • Laboratories: fume hoods, venturi valves, exhaust snorkels, air leakage
  • Cooling and power redundancy tests, black building testing
  • Industrial process cooling: pressure drop, temperatures

Energy audit

We evaluate a building's energy use to find inefficiencies and recommend improvements that cut consumption, running costs and the carbon footprint.

Retro-commissioning

We compare the approved handover documents with how the systems run today, retest and rebalance (Re-TAB), and suggest energy-saving measures.

Mechanical products

A channel partner for MEP makers for more than two decades. Our products are approved by the region's major engineering consultants, and went into jobs like Dubai Metro, Burj Khalifa, Dubai International Airport, Emirates Palace and New Doha International Airport.

Fan coil units · King Air

340 to 3000 CMH, cooling up to 12,800 watts. Horizontal concealed or exposed, standard or high static, low-flow versions for district cooling.

Fans · Arem

Axial fans for general and high-temperature use, belt or direct drive, with or without venturi.

Sound attenuators · Vibro-Acoustics
