01Cleaning & gum care

Brushing at home takes off the soft plaque. The hard deposits (tartar) need a scale and polish in the chair, and that is what keeps cavities and gum disease away.

If your gums are red, swollen, bleed, pull back from the teeth, or a tooth feels loose, don't wait. Gum disease starts in the gums and, left alone, moves into the bone that holds your teeth. The earlier we treat it, the less damage.

  • Scaling and polishing
  • Root planing and curettage
  • Flap surgery
  • Gum grafts and bone grafts

03Root canal treatment

Decay left alone eats through the tooth until it reaches the pulp, the soft nerve inside. That's the toothache. At that point a root canal is the way to save the tooth.

We remove the infected tissue inside the tooth and seal it with an inert filling. The tooth stays, the pain goes. Dr Amal holds a fellowship in advanced endodontics from the University of Turin.

04Braces & aligners

Orthodontics, whatever your age. Crowded or crooked teeth, overbites, underbites, deep bites, gaps, or straightening before veneers, crowns or implants. Our specialist orthodontist, Dr Anil Krishnan, looks at your case and tells you which option fits.

  • Metal and ceramic braces. Smaller and more comfortable than they used to be; ceramic is tooth-coloured.
  • Damon braces. Self-ligating brackets: shorter treatment, easier to keep clean, less discomfort, often no extractions. Metal or clear.
  • Clear aligners such as Invisalign®. Worn 20 to 22 hours a day, out to eat and clean.
  • Children. When the adult teeth come in is the right time for a check, to stop problems getting worse.

05Hollywood smile

Thin porcelain veneers bonded to the front of your teeth. They change the colour, shape and line of your smile, mend chipped teeth and close small gaps where braces aren't the answer.

We plan it around you: your skin tone, your gum line, the size and colour of your front teeth, and how your upper teeth sit with your lower lip. It takes at least two visits. The first to prepare the teeth and match the shade, the second to fit them. Before anything is bonded, you see the veneer on your tooth and say yes.

11X-rays & OPG

OPG is one wide X-ray of the lower face showing every tooth in both jaws, including ones that haven't come through yet, plus the jaw joint. We use it to plan braces, check wisdom teeth or get the full picture.

Close-up X-rays find what the eye can't: hidden decay, bone loss, and anything that shouldn't be there.
