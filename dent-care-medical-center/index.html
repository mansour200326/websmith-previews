Treatments

Whether it's a routine check-up or cosmetic, restorative or implant work, it's done here in the clinic.

Not sure what you need? Call 06 747 8910, tell us what's wrong, and we'll book you in.

  1. 01

    General dentistry

    Routine check-ups, and repairing damaged or decayed teeth so they work as they should again.

  2. 02

    Implants

    Replacing missing teeth with implants, to get back a natural, healthy smile.

  3. 03

    Dental surgery

    Including buried wisdom teeth. We check the teeth and jaw on our panoramic X-ray first, then plan the treatment.

  4. 04

    Gums

    Receding gums and loose pockets around the teeth. We use a laser device for gum work: less pain and quicker recovery.

  5. 05

    Alignment and braces

    Straightening teeth and correcting the bite, for children and adults.

  6. 06

    Whitening

    Flash whitening, the German technique: a whiter smile in about one hour, with less sensitivity.

  7. 07

    Prosthesis

    Replacement teeth for when a tooth can't be kept.

  8. 08

    Teeth protection

    Looking after healthy teeth before problems start.

The children's section

Most children grow up with one idea about the dentist, and it's not a good one.

dentist = pain

We've worked to change that. Our children's section has its own play corner, with a play house, a slide and toys. The aim is simple: the child leaves seeing the doctor as a friend.

Bring them for a check-up, call 06 747 8910.
