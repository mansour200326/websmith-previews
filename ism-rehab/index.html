Where does it hurt?

Whether you've been in a car accident, had a fall, train at a high level or just want less pain from your daily work, start with the area that's bothering you.

  • Low backDisc issues, sciatica, spinal stenosis, facet pain, postural pain
  • NeckWhiplash, strain, arthritis, headaches and migraines
  • ShoulderFrozen shoulder, rotator cuff tears, AC joint separation, labral tears
  • ElbowTennis elbow, golfer's elbow, radial head fracture
  • Wrist & handCarpal tunnel syndrome, fractures, repetitive strain
  • HipHip bursitis, arthritis, joint replacement, piriformis syndrome
  • KneeOsteoarthritis, ACL and meniscus injuries, patellofemoral pain, IT band
  • Ankle & footPlantar fasciitis, Achilles tendon, sprains, heel pain, bunions

We also treat concussion, dizziness and vertigo, balance after stroke, MS or Parkinson's, chronic pain, and rehab before and after surgery.

Your first visit

New patients can call, email or book the first appointment online. Save time by filling in the intake form at home.

Physiotherapy intake form (PDF) ↓ Massage therapy intake form (PDF) ↓
What happens at the first appointment?

Your physiotherapist takes a detailed history, then does a physical assessment. Afterwards they explain what they found and your treatment plan.

Do I need a referral from my doctor?

No. Unless your insurance company requires a referral for coverage, you don't need one.

What should I wear?

Whatever you'd wear to the gym. You'll be moving.

How do I pay?

We accept extended health benefit plans and private payment, direct bill most insurers, and accept motor vehicle accident claims. We can't accept WSIB or OHIP claims at this time.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking at the building is free. We're on the fifth floor, Suite 506.
