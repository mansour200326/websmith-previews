Physiotherapy

One on one, with a registered physiotherapist or physiotherapy assistant.

We focus on not just treating your condition but on giving you the right tools to become part of the solution. Your first visit is a detailed history and a physical assessment, then your physiotherapist explains the plan. You'll be shown your exercises and supervised doing them, so you can carry them on at home.

What a session can include

  • Soft tissue and manual techniques, spinal and peripheral joint mobilization and manipulation
  • Personalized exercise programs for range of motion, strength and function
  • Acupuncture
  • Ultrasound, interferential current, TENS, muscle stimulation, heat and ice
  • Off-the-shelf and custom brace fitting
  • Cardiorespiratory techniques, including airway clearance
  • Education, consultation and prevention

Conditions we treat

Low back
Compression fractures, disc issues, facet joints, postural pain, spondylolisthesis, degenerative disc disease, radiculopathy, stenosis, sciatica
Neck
Osteoarthritis, strain, whiplash, radiculopathy, stenosis, headaches and migraines, TMJ
Shoulder
Frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis), rotator cuff tears and tendinosis, dislocations and instability, AC joint separation, labral tears, thoracic outlet syndrome
Elbow, wrist & hand
Tennis and golfer's elbow, biceps ruptures, radial head fracture, carpal tunnel syndrome, post-fracture care
Hip
Trochanteric bursitis, osteoarthritis, fractures, dislocations, joint replacement, piriformis syndrome
Knee
Osteoarthritis, ACL tears, meniscal injuries, patellar tendonitis, IT band syndrome, patellofemoral pain, high tibial osteotomy
Ankle & foot
Achilles tendonitis, tendinosis and tears, plantar fasciitis, flat feet, high arches, bunions, heel pain, metatarsalgia
Whole body
Concussion, dizziness and vertigo, balance and mobility after stroke, MS, ALS or Parkinson's, fall prevention, fibromyalgia, chronic pain, running and sports injuries, repetitive strain, before and after surgery

Registered massage therapy

Tully Holman, RMT. Licensed by the College of Massage Therapists of Ontario (CMTO).

Massage works on the muscles, soft tissues and joints. Some people come for a therapeutic reason, others for relaxation and stress. Tully trained at the Canadian College of Massage and Hydrotherapy and uses acupressure, meridian therapy and massage to ease pain and help you relax.

People come to us for

  • Myofascial and muscle pain
  • Rehabilitation after a car accident
  • Soft tissue injuries
  • Recovery after a fracture
  • Relaxation and stress management

We'll work with you to make the most of your insurance coverage for massage therapy. Free parking at the clinic.

Custom orthotics & bracing

Dispensed by Amanda Murphy, Canadian Certified Pedorthist, C. Ped (C).

Orthotics, bracing and footwear advice to help your muscles work efficiently when you're active and recover properly when you rest, whether you play recreational sport or train for something specific.

Amanda has an HBSc in Kinesiology from McMaster, started as a pedorthic lab technician with DK Orthotic Solutions in 2019, completed Western University's Diploma in Pedorthics and became a Certified Canadian Pedorthist in 2022.

What we make and fit

  • Custom foot orthotics
  • Custom ankle-foot orthosis (AFO)
  • Ankle gauntlets
  • Off-the-shelf and custom braces
