How we treat

Unani medicine works in four ways, the four Ilaj. Most plans use two or three of them together.

Ilaj bil DawaHerbal and mineral medicine
  • Remedies from herbs, minerals and animal-based substances
  • Mixed for your condition and your temperament

People come to us for digestive complaints, hormonal imbalance, skin problems, joint pain and tiredness.

Ilaj bil GhizaDiet therapy
  • A food plan for your temperament
  • Adjusted to the nature of your illness
  • Changes for each season

Patients ask us about weight, digestion and liver health.

Ilaj bit TadbeerRegimental therapy: Hijama, Fasd, Dalak
  • Hijama: dry and wet cupping
  • Fasd: controlled venesection
  • Dalak: Unani therapeutic massage

Patients come for long-standing pain, arthritis, migraine and tiredness.

TashkheesDiagnosis and health check
  • Pulse examination (Nabz)
  • Physical examination and temperament check
  • Referral for lab tests or imaging when needed

Classical Unani methods next to a normal medical check, so the plan starts from the right place.

Women & familyCare for women and families
  • Hormonal balance
  • Irregular periods
  • Fertility consultations

Natural Unani care for women and the whole family, from children to grandparents.

Your first visit

  1. 1

    Book a time

    Message us on WhatsApp or call. Bring any reports, scans and the medicines you take now.
