Treatments and prices

Every treatment here starts from your temperament (Mizaj) and your condition. Our work follows the Department of Health Abu Dhabi rules for traditional, complementary and alternative medicine (TCAM). Prices are per session.

Hijama (cupping)

1 h 30 minAED 400

Book Hijama →

Part of Ilaj bit Tadbeer, regimental therapy. We do dry cupping and wet cupping, by a licensed practitioner.

Good to know

  • Dry cupping or wet cupping, as the doctor advises
  • A session takes about an hour and a half
  • Book ahead, especially on the Sunnah days
  • Fridays evening only, Sundays morning only

People come to us for

Neck and back pain, stiff joints, migraine, sciatic pain and general tiredness. Many book on the Sunnah days. This month’s Hijama days →

Senior doctor consultation

1 hourAED 200

Book a consultation →

Tashkhees wa Taqdeer, diagnosis and health assessment. We use the classical Unani checks next to a normal medical examination.

  • Pulse examination (Nabz)
  • Physical examination and temperament evaluation
  • Referral for lab tests or imaging when needed
  • A written plan: diet, herbal medicine and therapy

Bring your reports, scans and the medicines you take now. Adults and children are both welcome.

Spine & therapeutic massage

30 minAED 300

Book a massage →

Unani massage (Dalak) and hands-on work on the spine and the muscles around it. Treatment is usually for neck or low back pain.

  • Manual techniques: mobilisation, soft tissue and reflex work
  • Exercises to do at home
  • Advice on posture, food and daily habits
  • Referral to another specialist when it’s needed

More of what we do

Herbal medicine Ilaj bil Dawa

Natural remedies from herbs, minerals and animal-based substances, mixed for your condition and temperament. People come to us for digestive complaints, hormonal imbalance, skin problems, joint pain and tiredness.

Diet therapy Ilaj bil Ghiza

A food plan built on your temperament, the nature of your illness, and the season. Patients ask us about weight, digestion and liver health.

Women’s health & family care for the whole family

Hormonal balance, irregular periods and fertility consultations. Natural Unani care for women and families, from children to grandparents.

Staying well Asbaab-e-Sitta Zarooriya

Lifestyle advice built on the six essentials: air, food, movement, mental balance, sleep and elimination. General surgery (Ilaj bil Yad) is part of Unani medicine, but we don’t practise it at the Abu Dhabi centre.

Your safety

Every therapy is given by licensed practitioners in a clean, regulated clinic. We follow strict hygiene rules and the DoH Abu Dhabi guidelines for traditional medicine.
