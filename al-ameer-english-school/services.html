Curriculum and languages

We follow the CBSE (Delhi) curriculum, approved by the Ministry of Education, UAE. English is the medium of instruction.

Arabic
Compulsory from Grade 1 to Grade 9, as the UAE Ministry of Education requires. Special Arabic in Grades 8 to 10.
Second language
Grades 1 to 7: Hindi, Malayalam or Urdu. Grades 8 to 10: Arabic (as a second language), Malayalam or Hindi.
Islamic Studies
For Muslim students. Ethical Science is offered to non-Muslim students.
Moral Education
Grades 1 to 12. Moral, Social and Cultural Studies for Grades 1 to 9.
Computer Education
Part of the curriculum from Grade 1.
Assessment
No formal tests in Kindergarten. In Grades 9 and 10, the Board exam carries 80 marks and periodic assessment 20.

After the textbook

Co-curricular activities run alongside lessons. These are the ones the school offers.

Brain Gym

Started by the Student's Advisory and Welfare Committee on 2 September 2015. A short series of exercises, every day after recess, for concentration, memory and coordination.

Abacus

Part of the regular syllabus, to sharpen students' computing skills. Abacus and mental arithmetic for Grades 1 to 5.

Dance

Mainly Indian classical dance, along with group dances, Oppana, Margam kali, Thiruvathira kali and other folk dances, taught by the school's own dance faculty.

Karate

Strength, flexibility and balance, and the discipline, respect and patience that come with regular practice.

Football & Cricket

An inter-house Football Sevens tournament for the senior section, and a school team for inter-school competition.

Swatantara

A student committee formed under guidelines from the Consulate General of India, Dubai, raising awareness against tobacco, alcohol and drugs.

Yoga · Art and Craft · Horse riding

Yoga, art and craft sessions, and horse riding as an outdoor activity.

Admissions

Admission is from KG through Grade XII, and the academic year begins in April. In general, applicants take an entrance test; places depend on performance and the seats available.

Documents to bring

  1. Recent passport-size photographs (4)
  2. Passport copy with valid visa page (2 sets)
  3. Vaccination card / child's fitness certificate (2 sets)
  4. Copy of birth certificate
  5. Emirates ID, original and copy (2 sets)
  6. Child sponsor's passport copy with valid visa page
  7. Health card and insurance card copy, if available
  8. Original attested Transfer Certificate (TC)
  9. Copy of mark sheet / progress card
  10. Copy of 10th CBSE Board exam mark list (for Grade 11)
  11. Copy of 9th / 11th CBSE registration card (for Grade 10 / 12)
  12. Copy of 10th CBSE migration certificate (for Grade 11)
  13. Original conduct certificate (for Grades 9 to 12)

Transfer Certificate attestation, from Grade 2

From a school outside the UAE: local attestation (AEO/DEO/CBSE), then the respective consulate in the UAE, then Foreign Affairs in the UAE. From a school in another emirate: the TC is attested by the previous school and the respective Ministry of Education.
