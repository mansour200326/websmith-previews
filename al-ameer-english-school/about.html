About the school

Al Ameer English School, Ajman was set up in 1991, in a few villas in Rumaila. The school moved to its present premises in Al Zahra in 2004.

Vision, mission and core values

Al Ameer aims at giving opportunity to all children to accomplish their full potential and to develop responsibility.

From the school's vision

  • To develop the ability for critical thinking and fellow feelings.
  • To maintain a rapport among students, teachers and parents.
  • To embrace diversity and unique talents.
  • To instill in our students value based education.

Core values

Empathetic humans
Nurture the ability to understand and share the feelings of others.
Global citizens
Recognise their role in a world community and help strengthen its shared values.
Futuristic learners
Apply their learning confidently to educational and real-world challenges.
Responsible leaders
Show a strong sense of social responsibility, shaped by the values they uphold.
