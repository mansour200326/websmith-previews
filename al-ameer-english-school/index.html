Our story

We started in 1991 in a few villas in Rumaila, Ajman. In 2004 the school moved to its present campus in Al Zahra, and it has grown a great deal since. Lessons are in English, on the CBSE curriculum, with Arabic and Islamic Studies taught as the UAE Ministry of Education asks.

1991

Al Ameer English School opens in villas in Rumaila, Ajman.

2004

The school moves to its present premises in Al Zahra.

Today

KG 1 to Grade 12. The academic year begins in April.

From KG 1 to Grade 12

Every stage has its own section head. From Grade 1, boys and girls each have theirs. Reporting time is 7:40 every morning.

  1. KG 1–2

    Kindergarten

    From age 4. No formal tests: children are assessed on how they do in class. 7:40 to 12:00, Monday to Thursday.

    Mrs Shaher BanuSection Head (Kindergarten)

  2. 1–4

    Primary

    Arabic from Grade 1, and a second language: Hindi, Malayalam or Urdu. Computer Education starts in Grade 1.

    Mrs Mini Mathew · Mrs Beena HamsaSection Heads, girls · boys

  3. 5–8

    Upper primary and middle school

    Hindi, Malayalam or Urdu as second language up to Grade 7. From Grade 8: Arabic as a second language, Malayalam or Hindi.

    Mrs Sujatha Prakash · Mrs Riffat TariqSection Heads, girls · boys

  4. 9–12

    Secondary and senior secondary

    CBSE Board exams in Class X and XII. Science and Commerce in the senior years. 7:40 to 2:25, Monday to Thursday.

    Mrs Geetha Narayanan · Mrs Shermila UnnikrishnanSection Heads, girls · boys
