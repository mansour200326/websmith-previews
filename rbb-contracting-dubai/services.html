Services

Villas are what we are known for. Around them we run our own MEP and aluminium work, take on renovation, and build mosques, hospitals, warehouses and civil works.

01

Villas, palaces and buildings

Residential and commercial buildings, villas and palaces, from concept to completion. Most of our list is family villas in Dubai: the main house over ground and one floor, often with a roof floor, a service block, a driver room, a majlis and the compound wall.

  • G+1 and G+1+roof villas, single or in groups
  • Service blocks, driver rooms and majlis
  • Compound and boundary walls
  • Built to your engineering consultant's drawings
