A family nameon Dubai villas

His Excellency Rashid Bin Butti Bin Humaid Bin Bishr started the company in 1991. Since then we have built villas for families across Dubai, from Al Khawaneej and Al Mizher to Jumeirah and Al Warqa, and we take on renovation and refurbishment as well as new houses.

We work to your consultant. Every villa on our list was built with a named engineering consultant, from Arabisc in 1997 to Render Engineering in 2023.

Our own office, our own team. Administrators, engineers and technical experts, run from Office M05 in Bin Bishr Building, Abu Hail.
