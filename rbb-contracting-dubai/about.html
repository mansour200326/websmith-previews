Since 1991

Rashid Bin Bishr Contracting LLC was founded by His Excellency Rashid Bin Butti Bin Humaid Bin Bishr in 1991, and is known as one of the area's most experienced villa builders.

How we work

Our work starts with understanding what the client needs. We offer complete designs and a wide range of services alongside architecture, structure and engineering firms, and we advise from start to finish until the project is complete.

We have a record of finishing projects on time and within budget, and we handle renovation and refurbishment as well as new projects.

The team is administrators, engineers and technical experts, in the office and on site. The full team stays with a project from the start of work until it is finished. Whatever the size or location of the project, the team sees it through.

What we aim for

To build to the client's requirements on quality, time, safety and the environment, while keeping costs under control. To run the company with good management, steady growth and proper work ethics.

And to keep moving forward: to be one of the leading engineering and construction firms in the UAE, delivering projects that meet international standards.

We are committed to running a sustainable business, balancing the social, environmental and economic sides of what we do.
