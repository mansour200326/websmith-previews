A history of service since 1973

Industrial Equipment & Services Co. started in Doha in 1973 selling industrial and construction supplies. It still does, from a head office on B Ring Road, three showrooms and a warehouse in the Industrial Area, and it now supplies across the region and beyond.

What you get from us

Twelve points we have always set out for our customers, whatever the size of the order.

  1. Quality products

    Industrial and construction supplies and equipment sourced worldwide, to international quality standards.

  2. Technical support

    Professional advice, plus product certificates and testing when your job needs them.

  3. Stock in Doha

    A computerised warehouse in Doha and the Industrial Area, centrally placed to serve you.

  4. Wholesale prices

    As a regional wholesaler for a wide range of brands, we keep our prices competitive.

  5. Three showrooms

    All in Doha, so you can see the product and collect it quickly.

  6. Sales engineers

    People who know the products, so you get the right item at the right time for the right price.

  7. Free delivery

    Our own fleet of trucks and delivery staff.

  8. Experience since 1973

    Enough years to handle difficult supply projects and unusual requests.

  9. Service and repair centre

    For power generators, load testing, power tools, forklifts, compressors, water pumps and cleaning equipment: warranty service and repairs.

  10. Online priced catalogue

    More than 300,000 priced products.

  11. ISO 9001 certified

    For consistent sales, service and follow-up.

  12. Worldwide sourcing

    If it isn't available in Doha, our buyers handle it from A to Z.
