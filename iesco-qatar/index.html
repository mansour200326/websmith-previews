The stock sits in our own warehouse, our sales engineers help you choose, and our trucks deliver.

1973

Where it started

IESCO began selling industrial and construction supplies in Doha in 1973. Our Founding Chairman set the rule we still work by:

“Our corporate philosophy focuses on developing mutual trust with our customers and suppliers through adhering to the highest ethical standards & principles.”Mr. Mahmoud Akel, Founding Chairman
Today

Head office, showrooms, warehouse

The head office is on B Ring Road / Najma Street, opposite Al Handasa Baladiya. Behind it are a computerised warehouse, three showrooms, sales engineers who know the products and our own fleet of delivery trucks.

Beyond Qatar

From Doha to Alaska

From Doha we supply across the Middle East and North Africa and into Kenya, and we have shipped to operations and projects in Japan, Guam, Korea, Niger and Alaska. We exhibited at Project Qatar every year from 2010 to 2015.
