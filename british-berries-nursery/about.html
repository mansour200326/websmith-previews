About BBN

We know what it means when parents hand us their child for the day. Our aim is to make these early years as enjoyable, rewarding and positive as they can be, so children have fun at nursery and learn through play.

What we aim for

  • A caring, enriching place to be
  • Education through play
  • Curiosity and questions encouraged
  • Creativity and imagination
  • Each child developed in their own way
  • Every child reaching their potential

Built for the youngest

British Berries was planned as a school for the youngest children, with the needs of infants and toddlers first. We want it to feel loving and warm to everyone who comes through the door.

Children do best when they feel comfortable, confident and safe. We believe the building blocks of learning are play, curiosity, creativity, questions and imagination, and that a school built on them does the most for a child's social, emotional, thinking and physical growth.

How children learn here

Children are curious by nature. They learn by doing, with other people, in a room full of things to try.

That is the idea behind every classroom at BBN. Rooms are rich in materials, and children are encouraged to test their own theories of how things work. Much of the day is spent in small groups where each voice is heard, ideas are explored and the group agrees on results together.

Alongside the usual thinking, movement and social skills, children build problem solving, their own ideas and the confidence to stand by them. Teachers observe and keep notes, then shape the room around what the children are interested in. Each class works like a small community of mutual respect and care.
