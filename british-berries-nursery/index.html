What we offer

Four things parents ask us about first. Tap one to read more, or see the full programmes page.

01Pre-Nursery to FS2British EYFS

We follow the British Early Years Foundation Stage. Children learn through play: maths, early reading and thinking skills grow out of their own curiosity, in small groups where every child gets heard.

How a class works →
02DaycareBirth to 6 years

Care for babies, toddlers and young children in the same villa, with the same teachers and the same yard. Parents tell us pick-up and drop-off are flexible; WhatsApp us and we will work out what fits your day.

Daycare details →
03Inclusion, speech & behaviour therapyOn site

We are an inclusion setting. Speech and behaviour therapy happen here at the nursery, and teachers plan around each child's own needs, so a child with a speech delay is supported inside the normal day, not taken out of it.

Inclusion at BBN →
04Outings & field tripsSmall groups

Walks in the neighbourhood, parks, nearby restaurants and hypermarkets, the Zoo and KidZania. Children go in small groups, are counted at all times, and only go with a permission slip signed by a parent.

About outings →
