Pre-Nursery to FS2

We teach the British Early Years Foundation Stage from Pre-Nursery through FS1 to FS2. The idea is simple: children are naturally curious, so we use that curiosity to teach early maths, reading and thinking skills.

Classes work a lot in small groups, where each child can be heard, ideas get tried out and the group settles on an answer together. Teachers watch, write down what they see, and change the room to fit what the children are interested in.

  • Learning through play
  • Small group work
  • Curiosity and questions
  • Creativity and imagination

Inclusion, speech & behaviour therapy

British Berries is an inclusion setting. Speech therapy and behaviour therapy are offered here at the nursery, and teachers plan around each child's own needs.

One father wrote on Google that his son, born in the Covid years with a speech delay, can now follow complex instructions and is starting to speak. That is the kind of progress we work for, one child at a time.

Outings & field trips

Trips are planned often and kept to small groups, so the children get more out of them and get to know each other. They range from walks in the neighbourhood and visits to parks, to nearby restaurants and hypermarkets, the Zoo and KidZania.

Children are supervised closely and accounted for at all times. The class teacher tells families about each trip in advance, and a child only goes with a permission slip signed by a parent.

Qualified staff

Our teachers are hired to the Ministry of Social Affairs requirements and qualifications as a minimum, and we look beyond the certificate: experience, dedication and whether they share how we work with children.

We commit to ongoing training for every member of staff, beyond the minimum the EYFS asks for. One parent wrote on Google: “Ms Sonia is an amazing teacher doing a lovely job!”
