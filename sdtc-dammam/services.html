Check-ups, cleaning & fillings

الفحص والتنظيف والحشوات

Most visits start here. The doctor checks every tooth, cleans off the tartar and fixes small cavities before they turn into big ones. If something needs a closer look, the 3D X-ray is here in the clinic.

  • Dental cleaning
  • Fillings of all kinds, including cosmetic (tooth-coloured)
  • Treatment for gum inflammation
  • 3D X-ray for diagnosis and planning

02

Root canals under the microscope

علاج العصب بالميكروسكوب

When the nerve of a tooth is infected, a root canal can save the tooth instead of pulling it. We do root canal treatment with a dental microscope, so the doctor can see the canals clearly.

  • Root canal treatment with a dental microscope
  • Laser-assisted treatment
  • Diagnosis on the 3D X-ray

Dr Alaa Aldeen Alhzwani · Diploma in root canal treatment with microscopes, laser courses, 10+ years in major dental centres in Saudi Arabia.
