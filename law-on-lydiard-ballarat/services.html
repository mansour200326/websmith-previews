Employment law

Paul Cott has been assisting employers and employees in enforcing their rights for more than 15 years, giving practical, legally sound advice that leaves people far better equipped to make their own decisions. After all, your business, or your job, is a key part of your life.

Recognised by members of courts and tribunals as a straight shooter, with an empathic ear for clients going through significant challenges.

Having advised so many people over so long, Paul can deal with employment disputes as they arise, and help with the work that stops them arising in the first place.

  • Unfair dismissal matters
  • General protection disputes
  • Workplace bullying, disciplinary issues and redundancies
  • Reviewing and drafting employment contracts
  • Restraint of trade clauses: where you can work after leaving a business
  • Representation, wherever you are in Australia
