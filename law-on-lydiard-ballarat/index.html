Practice areas

Most of our work is disputes: about money, about a business, about a house being built, about a job. We also write simple Wills and look after probate.

Everything we handle
  1. 01Employment law→Unfair dismissal, general protection disputes, redundancies, disciplinary matters, employment contracts.
  2. 02Civil litigation→Business disputes and all types of disputes involving money. Lengthy experience in domestic building disputes.
  3. 03Building contracts→Price-competitive contract reviews for owners about to sign with a builder.
  4. 04Business disputes→Disputes between partners, about money in the business, or about the way it is being run.
  5. 05Property disputes→Co-owner disputes, disputes about buying or selling property, easements.
  6. 06Wills & probate→Mainly simple Wills, with care and attention to detail. Probate and estate matters.
  7. 07Insolvency & bankruptcy→Advice for individuals and liquidators: unfair preference claims, liquidations, bankruptcy notices.
  8. 08Professional disciplinary→Mostly at VCAT, where nurses, psychologists and medical practitioners face misconduct allegations.
  9. 09VCAT matters→Help with most matters scheduled to be heard at VCAT.

Trouble at work?

Paul has been helping employers and employees enforce their rights for more than 15 years. Practical, legally sound advice, so you can make your own decisions about your job or your business.

He can also help stop a dispute before it starts: reviewing and drafting employment contracts, and advising on restraint of trade clauses, so you know where you can work after leaving a business.

  • Unfair dismissal
  • General protection disputes
  • Redundancies and workplace bullying
  • Employment contracts and restraint of trade
